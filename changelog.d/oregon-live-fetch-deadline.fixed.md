A live Oregon Revised Statutes page fetch now gives each of its two attempts explicit 10-second connect and 20-second read timeouts and gives up on the page after 25 seconds, whatever the server does. It goes through the new `bounded_http.get_bytes`, which runs the attempts on a worker thread that the caller waits on for at most the deadline, so a host that drops connections or sends its headers or body a byte at a time cannot hold the caller longer. A worker still reading when its caller gives up counts against a process-wide cap of 16; while the cap is reached, new fetches wait for room within their own deadline and otherwise fail without a request, so chapter discovery against a trickling site cannot pile up open threads. Before, two 30-second connect timeouts to www.oregonlegislature.gov outlasted CI's 60-second per-test limit, so `test_extract_oregon_ors_real_source_smoke` failed the required `test` job instead of skipping (CI run 40042942746). An extraction that fetched no chapter now names the fetch errors in its "no Oregon ORS provisions extracted" message, so the smoke test also skips when the landing page answers and the chapter page does not.
