A live Oregon Revised Statutes page fetch now gives each of its two attempts explicit 10-second connect and 20-second read timeouts and gives up on the page after 25 seconds, whatever the server does. It goes through the new `bounded_http.get_bytes`, which runs the attempts on a worker thread that the caller waits on for at most the deadline, so a host that drops connections, redirects late, or sends its headers or body a byte at a time cannot hold the caller longer. No request, a redirect's included, is started once the deadline has passed (a DNS lookup, which has no timeout, can still hold one started just before it past it), and every response, connection and connection pool a worker opens is closed by the time it ends, unless closing one itself fails: that failure is noted on the error raised, or, if nothing else failed, fails the attempt. Once an answer has arrived, any failure but an interrupt is reported as a failed fetch, an answer `requests` cannot handle included (a redirect whose Location is not UTF-8, say, or, under a warnings-as-errors policy, a cookie `http.cookiejar` warns about); a `KeyboardInterrupt` is raised unchanged. At most 32 such workers make requests at once, process-wide, counting those still reading after their caller gave up; a fetch that finds them all busy waits for one within its own deadline and otherwise fails without making a request. Oregon chapter discovery now counts a candidate chapter as absent only when the site answers 404 or 410. A candidate whose page could not be fetched is reported as a skipped chapter with its cause, so a batch run no longer counts as a success, where before it was dropped as absent without a word. Before, two 30-second connect timeouts to www.oregonlegislature.gov outlasted CI's 60-second per-test limit, so `test_extract_oregon_ors_real_source_smoke` failed the required `test` job instead of skipping (CI run 40042942746). An extraction that fetched no chapter now names the fetch errors in its "no Oregon ORS provisions extracted" message, so the smoke test also skips when the landing page answers and the chapter page does not.
