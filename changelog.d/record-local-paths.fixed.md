Run notes, closure work, reasoning logs and source manifests no longer publish paths on the machines that wrote them. 121 files under `docs/ingest-runs/`, `docs/coverage/`, `.axiom/reasoning-logs/` and `manifests/` recorded paths such as `/Users/<name>/axiom-corpus/data/corpus`, `~/axiom-corpus-worktrees/<name>` and `/private/tmp/...`. They now name repository files by their repository-relative path and other directories by a placeholder such as `<main-checkout>`, `<worktrees>/<name>`, `<rulespec-us>`, `<axiom-cache>` or `<scratchpad>`. The `local_path` fields of the Bolivia, Mozambique and New York TANF manifests name the retained copies where the corpus locks them, so `corpus fetch` supplies them on any machine, and the adapter manifests' `download_dir` is `../data/corpus/downloads/...` like the rest.

`tests/test_record_local_paths.py` fails CI on any record under those directories that holds a local path, in prose and markup as well as commands, unless `.axiom/record-local-paths.yaml` grandfathers it. Only a record an ingest manifest attests in `reasoning_logs` can be listed, because its bytes cannot change without a re-sign. Each entry pins the record's path and bytes and the manifests that attest them, stale entries fail, and no entry can be added. Five are listed until the hub scrubs them and re-signs their manifests (axiom-corpus#896).
