`extract-ecfr --include-appendices` takes an appendix to a subchapter, which the eCFR places in the subchapter's last part: 41 CFR chapter 101's "Appendix to Subchapter D" (in part 101-21) is `us/regulation/41/101-21/appendix-to-subchapter-d`, legal identifier "41 CFR chapter 101, subchapter D, appendix". It is read only inside the subchapter it names, and it was the last appendix shape that stopped whole-title-41 appendix runs.
