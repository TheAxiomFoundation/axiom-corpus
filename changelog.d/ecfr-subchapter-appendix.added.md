`extract-ecfr --include-appendices` takes an appendix to a subchapter, which the eCFR places in the subchapter's last part. It writes 41 CFR chapter 101's "Appendix to Subchapter D" (in part 101-21) at `us/regulation/41/101-21/appendix-to-subchapter-d`, with the legal identifier "41 CFR chapter 101, subchapter D, appendix". It reads such an appendix only inside the subchapter the appendix names. As of the 2026-10-12 eCFR differential, only this shape still stopped whole-title-41 appendix runs.
