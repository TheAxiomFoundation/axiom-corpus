`extract-ecfr --include-appendices` takes an appendix to a subchapter in the part that holds it. The eCFR places 41 CFR chapter 101's "Appendix to Subchapter D" in part 101-21, that subchapter's last part. The command gives it the citation path `us/regulation/41/101-21/appendix-to-subchapter-d` and the legal identifier "41 CFR chapter 101, subchapter D, appendix". It reads such an appendix only inside the subchapter the appendix names. As of the 2026-10-12 eCFR differential, no other appendix shape still stopped whole-title-41 appendix runs.
