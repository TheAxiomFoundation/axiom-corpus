A `drop_lines` or `drop_line_patterns` entry that matches a bare number (a line of digits only, such as `^\d{1,3}$` written for page numbers) now drops that line only where a page number prints: in the page's margin, outside the text the filters keep, or at the height where matched bare numbers print on at least 30% of the document's pages. Table cells, list numbers and citation titles that are bare numbers stay in the text, where the content-only filter removed them from released rows (Colorado Works §§ 3.606.1 and 3.609.2, Colorado Rule 39-22-2003, 16 Del. Admin. Code 3000, 4000 and 9000, Indiana IHCPPM chapter 3000, 106 CMR 364, 12 CSR 10-2 and Wisconsin Tax 2 and 3). A bare number the page cannot place, OCR text or text that does not run left to right, is dropped as before. Existing scopes do not change until their documents are extracted again.
