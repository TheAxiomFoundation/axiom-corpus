Refined the release content check to `content-check-v2`. A body with no word with a letter now carries text when it is a table of figures (at least five numbers, dates, amounts or rates, making up at least half its tokens), so a benefit table of dates and amounts no longer leaves its root hollow, while date stamps on scanned pages still do. `manifests/reviewed-empty-nodes.yaml` lists the 72 document rows the 2026-10-13 hollow-root audit found correctly empty (tables of contents, link indexes, cross-reference stubs, reserved, deleted and repealed items), each with its reason; they are no longer `hollow_document_root` findings, validation reports them as a `reviewed_empty_document_roots` notice, and `content-check --check-baseline` fails on a listed row that is not a hollow root. The navigation threshold stays at 0.7, with the measurement in `docs/ingest-runs/2026-10-13-content-check-v2.md`; `docs/source-coverage-check.md` proposes a source-coverage check for partial loss. The content baseline is rebuilt: 1,444 findings in 185 of 2,533 scopes (the v1 rules on the same inputs: 1,517 in 186).
