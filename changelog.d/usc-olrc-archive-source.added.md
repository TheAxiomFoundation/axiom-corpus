Harden `extract-usc --source-zip`: a retained OLRC zip is refused when it exceeds the zip size cap, when a member name is unsafe (absolute, drive-letter, backslash, `.` or `..` segment, or a NUL byte in the raw header), when a member is a symlink, encrypted or duplicated, when the member inflates past the member cap, or when its compressed data is corrupt. Release validation reruns these checks on every retained `olrc/*.zip` source in a US statute scope.
