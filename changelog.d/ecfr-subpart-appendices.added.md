`extract-ecfr --include-appendices` takes subpart appendices (`Appendix II to Subpart C of Part 404`, the unnumbered `Appendix to Subpart B of Part 404` and the collective `Appendixes to Subpart C of Part 404` note) at `us/regulation/<title>/<part>/subpart-<S>/appendix-<d>`, `.../appendix` and `.../appendixes`, as children of their subpart, with roman-numbered appendices ordered by value. A subpart appendix outside the subpart it names, or with an ambiguous designator, fails validation. Appendix bodies now keep table captions and break words at `<br/>`.
