Decode typed text in fonts MuPDF has no Unicode for when `ocr: true` reads a scanned page by OCR: when a font's character codes are a constant shift from ASCII (Minion Pro 31 below it, Arial and Times New Roman 29 below on CMS state-plan forms) and the OCR text reads some of the font's lines so decoded, the font's other lines are decoded too, so typed dates, names and notes OCR missed are kept instead of dropped as garbled, and garbled lines without a control character ("1(:" for "NEW") are no longer appended. Characters with a Unicode mapping are never changed.
