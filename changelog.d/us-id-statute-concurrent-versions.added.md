The `idaho-statutes` adapter keeps every dated rendition an official section page prints. The rendition in force on the expression date keeps the plain path; each other rendition is a same-number variant row at `<section>--effective-YYYY-MM-DD` (or `--effective-until-YYYY-MM-DD`), with `variant`, `canonical_citation_path`, `effective_note` and `status` metadata. Title 63 chapter 30 is re-extracted that way from the retained 2026-09-14 official pages as `us-id/statute/2026-09-27-income-tax-concurrent-versions-us-id-title-63-chapter-30`, which adds the text of Idaho Code §§63-3022E and 63-3025D effective January 1, 2027 beside the existing rows.
