Kept the Israeli OpenLaw notes that state legal effect in the provision body instead of deleting them with the project's apparatus. A repeal, deletion or expiry marker on a limb of a section that is still in force, a parenthesised colon-terminated qualifier saying which version a text is or when it applies, and a parenthesised note inside a table cell now stay where the source prints them and are recorded in metadata.statutory_notes. Without them 147 rows misstated the law: NII לוח י׳ printed its two §337(א)/§340(א) contribution-rate tables back to back under identical headers with nothing to tell them apart, NII §348 and §158 ran a permanent version straight into its temporary-order replacement, ITO §11's confrontation-line credits read as permanent, and 30 section bodies held a bare enumerator where a deleted limb had been — ITO §5 read "(1) (2) (3) (4) (א) (ב) שר האוצר…". Amendment history, OpenLaw's indexed-amount glosses, its footnote letters and its comparison lead-in still never reach a body, and a block whose only content is a status line still becomes that section's body with operative: false.
