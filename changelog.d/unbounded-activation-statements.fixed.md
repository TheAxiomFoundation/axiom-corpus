Activation and its preview no longer die on the Management API session's statement timeout for large releases. Run 34866210267 (activating the 761-scope `us-rulespec-2026-09-13-federal-and-plans-union`) was cancelled with `57014: canceling statement due to statement timeout` about five minutes into `activate_corpus_release`'s per-scope counts: the function's own `SET statement_timeout = 0` cannot lengthen a timer armed when the outer statement began. The activation and preview statements are now sent with `SET statement_timeout = 0` as the leading statement of the same request; because multi-statement text cannot carry bind parameters, the upload id, release name and digests are inlined as validated plain literals and the preview's release identity as a dollar-quoted jsonb literal. Client timeouts rise to 30 and 15 minutes and the `activate-release` jobs to 60 minutes.
