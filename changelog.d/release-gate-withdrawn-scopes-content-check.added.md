Added a release gate for withdrawn scopes and page-chrome content. `manifests/withdrawn-scopes.yaml` lists the 19 scopes the July recovery audits and the release decisions of 2026-09-27, 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-11 took out of later cuts, each with its replacement and run note; `validate_release` refuses a new selection of one and grandfathers the tracked selectors that already carry it, pinned by selector digest. A content check (`axiom-corpus-ingest content-check`) finds page-navigation bodies, chrome-prefixed bodies, hollow document roots and LegInfo version pickers; `manifests/release-content-baseline.json` records every locked scope's findings, so existing cuts validate as before and a new finding fails. Grandfathered and recorded conditions are reported as notices that leave the attested counts unchanged. `scripts/recover_ingest_batch.py` now reads bodies when it reconciles recovered citations and no longer lets a scope's own rows resolve an unparsed document. See `docs/release-gate-policy.md`.
