OpenLaw's vulgar-fraction markup (`4<sup>1</sup>⁄<sub>2</sub>`) no longer flattens into a digit run that glues the whole number to the numerator ("41⁄2"): a mixed number renders as `4 1⁄2` and an ordinary fraction as `1⁄4`. Six Income Tax Ordinance rows (§§21, 38, 39, 40, 41, 66) re-rendered; regressions cover mixed and ordinary fractions, a lone superscript, and the committed §§21/40/66 rows.
