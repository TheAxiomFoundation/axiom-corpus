The opt-in PDF keys `amendment_markup` and `sort_blocks` now read the glyphs PyMuPDF's `rawdict` leaves out: glyphs whose boxes have no area, such as a zero-advance period or a combining mark, when they make up a whole line or the last span of a line. A page with such glyphs used to stop extraction; it now extracts with the characters of the default text. Pages where `rawdict` leaves nothing out extract as before.
