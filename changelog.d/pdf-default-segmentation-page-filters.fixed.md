PDF extraction under the default per-page segmentation now rejects the page filters `page_windows`, `start_page`, `end_page`, `start_after_pattern`, `drop_lines`, `drop_line_patterns` and `page_header_patterns`, naming each one set. Only the `single_block`, `numbered_sections` and `labeled_sections` segmentations read them; before, the default segmentation ignored them and emitted every page. Two Belgian manifests relied on that: `be-cir92-fisconetplus-current-code-2025-income` (pages 182 to 186) and `be-vat-royal-decree-20-moniteur-original-pdf` (pages 18 to 20). Their ignored keys are now comments, and their extraction is unchanged.
