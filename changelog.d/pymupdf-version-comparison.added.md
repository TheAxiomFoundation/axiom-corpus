`scripts/compare_pdf_extraction_versions.py` measures what a PyMuPDF version change does to PDF extraction across the corpus: it re-extracts every committed `source_format: pdf` source document and reads every PDF page by page in two environments, then compares the two with each other and with the committed provisions. `docs/dependency-lock.md` records its measurement of PyMuPDF 1.26.7 (the lock) against 1.28.2 on 2026-10-13: 612 of 11,630 documents differ, and of 245,329 committed rows 3,135 are reproduced on 1.26.7 only and none on 1.28.2 only, so the lock stays on 1.26.7 until the version for published extraction is decided. CONTRIBUTING no longer installs the pytest plugins by hand (the `dev` extra has them) and says when an extraction library may move in the lock.
