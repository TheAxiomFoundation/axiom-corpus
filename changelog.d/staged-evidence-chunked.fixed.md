Publication no longer asks Supabase for staged evidence of a whole release in one request. `fetch_staged_release_scope_evidence` now calls the per-scope evidence RPC in chunks of 32 scopes, splits a chunk the gateway rejects (for example a 504 after the request ran too long) in half until it fits, and merges the rows under the same exact-identity checks. The 556-scope follow-up publish (run 34784069026) failed in this phase after 125 s with HTTP 504 once R2 staging itself fit the job.
