`extract-ecfr --include-appendices` takes roman-numbered and unnumbered part appendices (`Appendix II to Part 200`, `Appendix to Part 720`, and the collective `Appendixes to Part X` note) at `us/regulation/<title>/<part>/appendix-<d>`, `.../appendix` and `.../appendixes`, as children of their part, with a part's roman numerals ordered by value. Numbered and lettered part appendices keep their paths, ordinals and metadata, with two exceptions no eCFR identifier meets: a single letter I, V, X, L, C, D or M reads as a roman numeral when the part also lists a reserved multi-character numeral, and part appendix identifiers must be ASCII, as subpart appendix identifiers are.
