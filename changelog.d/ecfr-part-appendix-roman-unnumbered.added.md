`extract-ecfr --include-appendices` takes roman-numbered and unnumbered part appendices (`Appendix II to Part 200`, `Appendix to Part 720`, and the collective `Appendixes to Part X` note) at `us/regulation/<title>/<part>/appendix-<d>`, `.../appendix` and `.../appendixes`, as children of their part, with a part's roman numerals ordered by value. Numbered and lettered part appendices keep their paths, ordinals and metadata.
