`is_file_url` now applies the URL parser's preprocessing before it tests the scheme. ASCII tab and newline are removed, and leading C0 controls and spaces are stripped. So the ingest-manifest local-path guard and `scripts/repair_local_file_download_urls.py` also catch a `file:` URL split or prefixed by control characters. `redact_file_urls` now redacts an unencoded `file:` path whole, through the end of its quoted or line context, instead of stopping at a space or apostrophe.
