Read New Jersey statute `metadata.status` only from a heading that is itself a status note ("Repealed by L.2005, c.83, s.20, ...") or from the final clause of the section's last source-history line ("...; repealed R.S. 46:38A-57 ...", "...; per s.4, expired April 19, 1993."). Live sections whose text only uses the words "repealed", "expired" or "omitted", including repealer sections, are no longer marked repealed, expired or omitted.
