Harden the opt-in PDF keys `amendment_markup`, `sort_blocks` and `ignore_actual_text` (#741). The layered path no longer doubles a line's newline, and every layered page is checked against the default page text. `sort_text`, an empty `amendment_markup` mapping, page values that are not positive integers, a `start_page` past the last page, and non-boolean `sort_blocks` or `ignore_actual_text` are rejected. Rules with zero opacity are ignored, a stroked rectangle's thickness includes its stroke, and near-threshold and thick rules draw a `PdfAmendmentMarkupWarning`. A `typographic_underlines` phrase counts only as whole words and only where it is exactly an underlined stretch. `scripts/compare_pdf_amendment_markup_with_html.py` compares one label per character in both directions, so labels swapped between words or inside a word fail, even behind spelling differences.
