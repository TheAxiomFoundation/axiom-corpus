CLML MathML without `alttext` keeps its structure in provision text: fractions render as `a/b`, sub- and superscripts as `x_1`, `x^2` and `x_1^2`, roots as `√(a + b)` and `root(x, 3)`, and fences with their delimiters and separators, with any operand longer than one name or number in parentheses. Universal Credit Regulations 2013 reg 73(2A), which rendered as `N × ( M × 12 365 )`, reads `N × ( (M × 12)/365 )` on its next extraction. Flat rows render as before, so the Tax-Free Childcare Regulations 2015 reg 9 products stay `M x W` and `M × W`. A MathML element with no notation keeps its tokens as a plain row and is named in the row's `metadata.unrendered_mathml`; `<semantics>` annotations are no longer read into the text.
