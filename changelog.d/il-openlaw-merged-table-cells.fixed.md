OpenLaw tables are rendered over their expanded grid: a `rowspan` repeats the cell's text in its column on every row it covers and a `colspan` repeats it across every column it covers, so each row of the National Insurance Law's income-limit schedule (לוח י״א) carries its item number and insured category, and the contribution schedule's `טור ג׳` heads each of the six rate columns it spans. Four rows change; regressions cover a rowspan/colspan table and the committed לוח י״א and לוח י׳ rows.
