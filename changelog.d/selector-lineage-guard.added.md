Refuse, in the required test job, a release selector that selects no version of a (jurisdiction, document_class) pair its predecessor carries, names a predecessor another selector already succeeds or a fork branch its line did not continue through, or names no tracked predecessor without a record that it is the first cut of a line. The one drop, six forks and 100 first cuts already on main are recorded in `manifests/selector-lineage-baseline.yaml`, each pinned to its selector digest. Entries are append-only and dated: a later entry for a recorded fork adds successors or branches the line continues through. A fork or ended-branch finding names the newest selector of the line to cut from when the check finds exactly one.
