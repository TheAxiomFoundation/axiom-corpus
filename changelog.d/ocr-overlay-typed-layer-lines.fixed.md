Keep the typed text of a scanned page's text layer when `ocr: true` reads the page by OCR: a layer line is now appended unless the OCR text holds the whole line, so a typed date whose day and year occur in another date, a typed word OCR misread, and the signing official's name and title are no longer lost. Invisible recognition layers that disagree with the OCR text almost everywhere, and their one- and two-letter fragments, are no longer appended.
