Keep a Minnesota, Nebraska, Washington, District of Columbia, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Utah or Wisconsin statute container whose table of contents cannot be fetched, is a maintenance, empty or wrong-shape page, or lists no children as an inventory gap with no provision, so coverage and strict release validation report the run as incomplete instead of silently dropping every section under it. For DC this also covers a section file the title index lists but that is missing or cannot be parsed. A gap for a page that could not be fetched is sourced from the listing that names it; a gap for a page that was read but is unusable is sourced from that page. Containers that list nothing by law stay complete: Washington chapter pages that name their chapter and carry no sections, DC containers and titles marked "[Reserved]", Rhode Island containers and South Dakota titles and chapters whose caption, heading or catchline says reserved, repealed, transferred or superseded as a whole word, and Utah index pages whose children are all out of force on the expression date. In Rhode Island, South Dakota, Utah and Wisconsin one failed table-of-contents page used to abort the whole run (a failed section page still does in Rhode Island and Utah); in South Dakota, Utah and Wisconsin an interrupt during a pooled fetch surfaced as a RuntimeError, and now stops the run as KeyboardInterrupt. A run that writes no provisions still fails.
