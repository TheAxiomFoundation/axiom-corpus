Israeli OpenLaw provisions read from a supplement fragment now carry that fragment's digest in `metadata.verified_source_sha256`, with the primary render's digest under `primary_source_sha256`; schedule items are named the way their schedule's own heading names it (`תוספת ראשונה א׳ פרט 1` in the Ordinance, `לוח י׳ פרט 3` in the National Insurance Law) instead of a fixed לוח; and the document extraction report binds to the primary render and lists every fragment.
