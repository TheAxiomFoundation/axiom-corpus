Stream provisions, inventories and R2 objects through release validation, release content, staging and navigation instead of holding them whole. Each phase now keeps compact per-row metadata rather than provision bodies: on a 257 MB, 70,000-row synthetic scope the projection digest, navigation build, deep validation and release-content projection peaked at 91 to 188 MB of RSS on macOS, against 1.26 to 1.37 GB before, with byte-identical digests and reports. This is the first step toward publishing the 756 MB May whole-eCFR base layer (#728).
