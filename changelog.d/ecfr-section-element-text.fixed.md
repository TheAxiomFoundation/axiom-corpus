`extract-ecfr` section and appendix bodies keep the text of every block element in document order. Before, they dropped the own text of any element other than `P`, `PSPACE`, `FP`, `FP-*`, `HED`, tables and formulas (and `HD1`–`HD3` in appendices): headings such as `HD1`–`HD5` and `HED1` inside sections, leader-dot worksheets (now one `label | figure` line per entry), `SCOL2` lists (one item per line), the `FP` and `P` paragraph variants such as `FP1-2`, `P-2` and `FRP`, section and paragraph authority citations, OMB approval statements and signature names. An unfamiliar element's text renders as its own block instead of vanishing. Image captions (`TCAP`, `BCAP`) render as `[Image caption: …]` blocks, which paragraph-anchor inference masks like table captions; the anchor extractor now stamps `provision-anchors/1.0.2`, and the regenerated committed anchors change only that field. The eCFR's own amendment links (`XREF`) stay out of the body. Locked scopes keep their exact text until successor extractions replace them.
