Read eCFR sections that are numbered for a part other than the one holding them. The committed title 43 fixtures place § 3809.1 in subpart 3809 of part 3800, with no part 3809 in the structure fixture. Such a section keeps its citation path (`us/regulation/43/3809/1`), but a run scoped to part 3800 now includes it instead of dropping it. Its inventory parent is now the subpart that holds it (`us/regulation/43/3800/subpart-3809`), replacing `us/regulation/43/3809/subpart-3809`, which is absent from the fixture inventory. Its source URL now uses part 3800 instead of part 3809, and `metadata.enclosing_part` records part 3800. New inventories and XML parsing reject duplicate citation paths: an inventory with two items under one path fails, a retained XML whose `--section` selector names two sections fails before writing, and fresh or reparsed XML holding two elements under one selected path fails even with `--allow-incomplete`. Complete cached provisions whose pinned source hashes match are reused as before; their XML is not re-checked for duplicate paths. A fatal XML validation error preserves source snapshots, graphics, inventory, provisions, and coverage across every title in the run. Ordinary resumes verify cached source hashes and reparse missing or changed snapshots.
