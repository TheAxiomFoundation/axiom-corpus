The eCFR adapter now takes sections whose identifiers carry parenthesised Code-subsection groups (26 CFR 1.401(k)-1, 31.3121(a)(1)-1): the inventory no longer skips them, the XML pass no longer truncates them to a colliding path, and `--section` accepts the official form. Citation paths fold each group into hyphens (`us/regulation/26/1/401-k-1`); labels and identifiers keep the official text.
