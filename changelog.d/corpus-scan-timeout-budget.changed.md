Scale the pytest-timeout budget of the three tests that parse the whole provision corpus (the citation-path grammar module, the provision identity-convention check and the claims evidence-span check) with the size of the provision JSONL: `tests/corpus_scan.py` allows 1 s per 10 MB, never below the suite's 60 s. Before the grammar scan streamed rows, CI's flat `--timeout=60` errored all eight corpus-dependent grammar tests on slower runners (axiom-corpus#736). The scans now take 8–23 s on the 2.7 GB corpus, and the scaled budget keeps corpus growth from bringing that limit back.
