## Extraction

`labeled_sections` with `section_heading_requires_bold: true`, as for Pub 100-01 and 100-24. Both publications print
Pub 100-01's bold "10.1 - Title" headings in the great majority of sections (849 of 880 bold headings in 100-02,
3,600 of 3,734 in 100-04), but individual chapters print the same headings as "100.1 Definition of AKI" (no dash),
"40.7- Individuals ..." and "230.2.1– Chronic ..." (dash attached to the label), "20.3 -End Stage ..." (dash attached
to the heading) and "20.4.3 -- TPNIES ..." (double dash), all bold and all followed by their transmittal line, so the
publications share one pattern that accepts every dash form and no dash:
`^(?P<label>\d{1,3}(?:\.\d+)*)(?:\s*[-–—]+\s*|\s+)(?P<heading>[A-Z(].*)$`. The heading must start with an upper-case
letter or "(" (so "10-foot", "1-800-MEDICARE", "100-01 - General Information" and bold enumerations "1. Content: ..."
never match) and the top label has one to three digits (Pub 100-04 chapter 1 opens with "01 - Foreword", "01.1",
"02.1.1"; four-digit years "2014 - ..." and HCPCS codes "99406 - ..." in bold tables do not match). The generator
narrows the top label to two or three digits for every chapter whose TOC lists no single-digit section (all but
100-02 chapter 1 and 100-04 chapters 18, 33, 37), which keeps bold table cells such as "1 - Community Early" /
"2 - Low" (100-04 chapter 10), "1 - Paper Claims" (chapter 1) and "6 –Blend Year 1" (chapter 3) out. Three chapters
whose bold tables still collide with the pattern and four whose labels are misprinted carry explicit overrides,
recorded in each document's `metadata.extraction_override`:

| Pub | Ch. | Override |
|---|---|---|
| 100-02 | 7 | `text_replacements` "30.2. 8 - Facsimile Signatures" → "30.2.8 - ..." (the body prints a space inside the label; the TOC-listed section was otherwise lost) |
| 100-02 | 11 | `text_replacements` "100.6.1. Dialysis Modality" → "100.6.1 - ..." (trailing dot on the label) |
| 100-02 | 15 | heading optional + `label_only_heading_pattern: ^[-–—]\s*[A-Z(].*$` + `label_only_requires_heading: true`: section 320.7.1 prints its bold label alone with "- Determining Qualifying Home Infusion Drugs" on the next line; a bare bold "2" on p. 59 is not followed by a dash and stays in the body |
| 100-04 | 12 | `text_replacements` "40.8. - Claims for Co-Surgeons and Team Surgeons" → "40.8 - ..." |
| 100-04 | 26 | dash required: the place-of-service code table prints bold "01 Pharmacy (October 1, 2005)" ... "99 Other Place of Service" rows; all 16 section headings use a dash |
| 100-04 | 27 | spaced dash required: the CWF response-code table prints bold "50 -Not in file", "61 -Cross reference/database problem" rows and a bold "837 Professional Claims" caption; all 60 section headings use " - " |
| 100-04 | 32 | label 23.17 excluded: MSN message 23.17 is printed bold in English and Spanish under the surgical-error denial instructions (a message number, not a section; the two lines would otherwise have become a section and a dropped duplicate) |

`text_replacements` are applied by the extractor to both the styled span text and the page text, so the bold flag
stays aligned. The table of contents is dropped with a per-chapter `start_after_pattern` on the chapter's last TOC
line, computed by the generator. The body-start rule was extended: the body starts at the first repeated heading
label (100-01/100-24 rule) or at the first bold heading followed by its "(Rev. ...)" transmittal line with no
heading-shaped line in between, whichever comes first, because Pub 100-02 chapter 17's TOC omits its first section
("10 – OTP General Information" appears only in the body; the old rule would have started the body at section 20
and dropped section 10 into the discarded TOC) and because recently added TOC entries are bold (100-04 chapters 1, 6,
18, 20). A first version of the rule looked three lines ahead without the heading guard and took 100-04 chapter 6's
bold TOC line "120.6 - Default Billing" as the body start (the next heading's transmittal line was within reach),
producing an empty duplicate `chapter-6/120.6`; the guard fixed it and both scopes were re-extracted. The TOC
transmittal regex now also reads "(Rev. 12421; Issued; 12-21-23)" (100-02 ch. 6), "(Rev. 12425 Issued: 12-21-23)"
(ch. 7, no separator; that transmittal is the later one), "(Rev. 198, 11- 06-14)" (ch. 14) and the header line
"Transmittals Issued for this Chapter" (ch. 8). Wrapped headings are joined with the 100-01
`heading_continuation_pattern` in a variant whose next-heading guard also rejects the dashless and attached-dash
heading forms (`(?!\d{1,3}(?:\.\d+)*(?:\s*[-–—]|\s+[A-Z]))`), so a container heading with no transmittal line never
absorbs the next heading; the transmittal line stays as the first body line.
