## Citation-path ratchet

The seven page rows join the `page_n` irregular family that
`scripts/validate_citation_paths.py` caps. Against the unraised schema the
validator (586,315 records, 434,904 unique paths) reports
`page_n 150600 / 150593  <-- REGRESSION` and fails; `--update-baselines`
raises that one baseline in `schema/citation-path.v1.json` from 150,593 to
150,600 and nothing else moves (`block_n` 75,770, `space_segments` 196,
`endash_segments` 1,652, `uppercase_segments` 16,280, `truncated_segments` 53,
`collection_roots` 10 all stay at baseline; the identity-drift list is
unchanged). With the raised baseline it passes. A branch that merges after
another baseline raise must recompute the count on the merged tree rather than
take either side of the conflict.
