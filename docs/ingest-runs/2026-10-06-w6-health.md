## Family 1: agency, legislature and county documents (official-documents manifests)

Method. `scripts/build_w6_health_manifests.py` holds one static entry per work-order row that was fetched and read
(publisher, title as printed, edition or effective date where the document prints one, citation path, content selector)
and writes one manifest per (jurisdiction, document class): `manifests/<jur>-health-w6-<class>.yaml`, version
`2026-10-06-w6-health-<class>-<state>`. `source_url` is the bundle address exactly where that address answered (the bundle
generator joins by URL; redirect targets are in `metadata.final_url`); where the official text sits at another address
(OFFICIAL-SOURCE rows and the DHCS wp-content copies) the bundle's address is in `metadata.bundle_url` and in the decisions
row's `id`, and `official_url` names the address fetched. PDFs use the extractor's default page granularity with
`ocr: true` as a fallback (no page needed it); HTML pages use a per-publisher `html_content_selector` (`main`, `article`,
`div.region-content`, `.page-content`, `#mainContent`, `div.MRSSection`, `div.norm`); the BadgerCare handbook topic drops the
RoboHelp header and mini-TOC as the 2026-09-10 SSI scope does. Document classes follow the precedents: agency pages, charts,
notices, letters, testimony and fiscal notes `guidance`; CMS approvals, 1115 applications and state-plan pages `policy`;
eligibility-manual chapters and handbook topics `manual`; administrative-code chapters and rules `regulation`; statutes,
enrolled bills and session laws `statute`; the MC 250A application `form`.

Editions. Where the publisher still serves the superseded edition the bundle names, that edition was taken at a dated
sibling path and the note names the current held edition: CT CHOICES quick guides 2023 and 2024 (2026 edition also taken),
CT HUSKY chart March 2025 (March 2026 held in the CHIP scope), MS EPM chapter 101 (2017 file; September 2025 revision held),
VA M03 (7-1-24 file; 4-1-26 held), OR OAR 461-155-0250 history (current rule held), CO CHP+ charts April 2022 and April 2023.

Notable reads: AL 560-X-25 is the Alabama Medicaid Agency's eligibility chapter (54 pages); the WA session law is the
2023-25 operating budget (1,409 pages); MACPAC Exhibit 36 (2024 and 2025) is a federal multi-state exhibit kept under
`us-ga` because the Georgia layer names it; the Santa Clara County handbook page is a county agency's (the county administers
CCHIP); Connect for Health Colorado is the state-created marketplace (C.R.S. 10-22-104) and Florida Healthy Kids Corporation
the statutory KidCare administrator (s. 624.91 F.S.), so both are official publishers; the DE `dhcpflyer/` address is now an
empty WordPress attachment page (ABSENT).
