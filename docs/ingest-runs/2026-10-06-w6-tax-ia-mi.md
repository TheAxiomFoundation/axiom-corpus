## Method

1. **Held already?** Every row's `bundle_url` (and the check's final URL) was matched against every `source_url` and
   `download_url` in the inventories of the 11 jurisdictions on disk, every `bundle_path` and its ancestors against the
   citation paths, and every statute row was resolved to its section and looked up in the held chapter scopes (the
   Louisiana `Law.aspx?d=` ids through the Title 47 inventory), and against the lock files of the scopes committed on
   main (`.axiom/corpus-locks/<jur>/<class>/<version>.json`, whose bytes are not all in the local root: four locked
   scopes of these jurisdictions are not on disk, and two of them hold rows of this order, TIR 02-21 and M.G.L. c. 62,
   s. 2). 85 rows are ALREADY-HELD (statute sections in the wave-4/5 chapter scopes, IAC 701 rules, 86 Ill. Adm. Code
   100, the locked 2026-09-23 TIR 02-21 and c. 62 s. 2 scopes, the wave-5 2025 MI-1040 Book, whose SHA-256 equals
   today's fetch). Two cited scopes are on the local root only (wave 5, unmerged): the c. 62 chapter scope (9 rows; s. 3,
   4 and 6 are also in the locked `2026-07-13-recovery`) and the 2025 MI forms scope (1 row). TIR 02-21 was first
   taken here too; on the lock check it was dropped from the Massachusetts guidance scope (re-run under the same w6
   version, its stale source file removed) and recorded ALREADY-HELD.
2. **Probe.** One GET per remaining address with the extractor's own client (corpus user agent, Accept headers,
   certifi plus the committed `data/certs/` intermediates), one thread per host so no host saw parallel requests,
   0.6 s spacing; on failure one retry with the extractor's built-in browser user agent, and for `michigan.gov` and
   `mass.gov` (documented fallbacks) one chrome120 retry. Every body was saved and its first page and printed year read
   (pages, text length, first 500 characters). 318 addresses, then about twenty targeted reads of the publishers' own index and search pages to find moved documents.
3. **Manifests.** `scripts/build_w6_tax_ia_mi_manifests.py` holds the static tables (one row per document: address,
   citation path, title with the printed year and revision, tax year, subtype, HTML content selector) and writes one
   `extract-official-documents` manifest per jurisdiction and class (`manifests/us-xx-w6-income-tax-{forms,guidance,statute}.yaml`,
   30 manifests), the decisions CSV (`--work-order ... --decisions ...`) and the queue blocks (`--queue --base ...`).
   `source_url` is exactly `bundle_url` for every FETCH row taken. Before any extraction every manifest was run offline
   through the extractor's own block functions against the saved bodies (selector misses, empty or banner-only bodies
   fixed), and every form's tax year was checked against its text (two corrections: `revenue.iowa.gov/media/2650` is the
   2021 IA 1040 instructions, revised 01/03/2023, not 2022; the 2022 Form 4884 Section C worksheet prints no year and is
   dated by the publisher's TY2022 folder and index).
4. **Extract.** `uv run axiom-corpus-ingest extract-official-documents --base <corpus> --version <manifest version>
   --manifest <manifest> --source-as-of 2026-10-06`, one state's manifests in sequence, states in parallel (no host is
   shared between states). Indiana Code Title 4 through the `indiana-code` adapter (below).
5. **Check.** Every scope `complete: true`, 0 missing, 0 extra, 0 duplicate citations; every document has a body
   (smallest: the Michigan withholding FAQ, 90 characters, which is the page's whole text, and two Kentucky news
   releases, 425 and 482); `scripts/validate_citation_paths.py` over the 31 new provision files: 4,962 records, 4,962
   unique paths, `RESULT: OK`; no new citation path exists in any other provisions file of the 11 jurisdictions on disk
   (all classes, including the other wave-6 agents' scopes); every source file under the new scopes is inventoried
   (the Indiana adapter's release ZIP aside).

Citation paths follow the wave-4 slug rules and the jurisdictions' own conventions: forms `us-xx/form/<agency>/ty<year>/<form>`
(agency segments as the TY2025 scopes: `idr`, `istc`, `idor`, `kdor`, `dor`, `ldr`, `comptroller`, `mrs`, `treasury`),
guidance `us-xx/guidance/<agency>/<slug>`, session laws and bills `us-xx/statute/session-laws/<year or legislature>/<bill>`
(the GA/OR/CO precedent), code sections the held scopes' shape (`us-id/statute/57-1110`, `us-in/statute/4-10-22-4`). The
EXTRACT-MANIFEST rows of Idaho, Maryland and Michigan carried May 2026 URL-derived paths with underscores
(`us-id/form/tax_forms/tax.idaho.gov/.../efo00086_09-15-2021`), which the grammar rejects; they were re-pathed
(`us-id/form/istc/ty2021/form-24-efo00086-2021-09-15`) and the decisions rows record old id and new path. The Iowa
and Kansas guidance rows kept their manifest paths (33 decisions rows carry the old-to-new path note).
