## Method

1. **Held already?** Every row was checked before any request: the bundle path (PATH-ONLY rows) against every
   `citation_path` of the 159 local `us/statute` provisions files (every one of the 158 `us/statute` lock files on
   `main` has its bytes on the controller's disk), and the two IRS and the ACF addresses against every local
   `us` provisions and inventory file, every lock file and every manifest outside `manifests/program-bundles/`. None
   was held. The only hits were the tax agent queue (`manifests/tax-agent-queue.yaml` lists `rp-21-45` and `rp-22-38`

   scopes (the page itself was never taken). The held title 8 and 20 sections (8 U.S.C. 1612, 1613, 1641; 20 U.S.C.
   1070a, in `2026-07-13-recovery-...`) and the title 38 scope (38 U.S.C. 1521 and the title row) do not hold the
   requested sections. Open pull requests were read for claims on the same documents or versions: none (#771 takes
   title 52 only).
2. **One probe per address** with the corpus user agent (`curl`, TLS verified), one request at a time per host;
   the printed title and edition of both PDFs read from page 1 (`pdftotext`).
3. **Extraction**: `extract-official-documents` for the three documents (one manifest, one scope) and `extract-usc
   --source-zip` for the eight sections (one scope per title, the adapter appends `-title-<n>`), then
   `scripts/self_contain_usc_scope.py` on each U.S. Code scope. Coverage read back for every scope.
4. **Decisions** from `scripts/build_w7_federal_decisions.py`, which re-checks every PRESENT row (the path is a row
   of the named scope, and the scope's coverage is complete with no missing, extra or duplicate rows) before writing.
   A dry run of `scripts/apply_wave_decisions.py` against a scratch copy of `joins.yaml` adds exactly three
   `document_paths` (the two IRS addresses and the ACF bundle address); the eight U.S. Code rows join by their own
   path.

## The two container references (SKIPPED: the controller's decision)

The brief says: for `us/statute/26/subtitle-A` and `us/statute/42/chapter-7`, check first whether a served scope holds
the sections; then the gap is the container node, not the text. Checked against the 34 `us/statute` scopes of
`us-rulespec-2026-10-07-w6-bundle-gaps-union` (the 2026-10-11 release carries the same 34) and the section lists of the
retained OLRC release point 119-103 zips (`xml_usc26@119-103.zip`, `xml_usc42@119-103.zip`, no new request):

| container | sections in it | current | served (any row of the section) | not served |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 26 U.S.C. Subtitle A (income taxes; `/us/usc/t26/stA`) | 890 | 764 | 117 | 647 |
| 42 U.S.C. chapter 7 (Social Security Act; `/us/usc/t42/ch7`) | 628 | 591 | 102 | 489 |

So the precondition does not hold: the served corpus has 15% of Subtitle A's current sections and 17% of chapter 7's, and it has no node for
either container (`extract-usc` places sections directly under the title, `us/statute/26/<section>`, so no corpus
path starts with `us/statute/26/subtitle-A/`). Neither row can be closed by a join alone, and neither was extracted:

- Taking the rest would be two closure scopes of 647 and 489 whole sections (tens of thousands of rows: Subtitle A is
  the whole income tax, chapter 7 includes all of Medicare), and the bundle row would still need a path move to the
  title (`us/statute/26`, `us/statute/42`), after which the CHIP, Medicaid and SNAP full tiers count every served
  provision of those titles. That is a decision about what the three bundles measure, not an ingestion gap.
- The bundles already name the sections they use one by one (26 U.S.C. 2, 21, 61, 62, 152, 199A, 1222, 1402, 3401,
  7703, 36B; 42 U.S.C. 601, 1320b, 1382, 1382c, 1396 to 1396u-1, 1397aa to 1397mm), and every one of them is served.

Both rows are SKIPPED with this finding in the note. The controller's options: (a) drop the two container rows from
the CHIP/Medicaid/SNAP bundle plans (their `plan` and `screener` sources), or replace them with the sections they mean;
(b) extract the 1,136 remaining sections from release point 119-120 into closure scopes (exclude every section any
local scope holds, whole or in part) and add `path_moves` to the title nodes.
