# BFH decision source recovery

The pending DA-KG discovery snapshot contains 82 post-2009 BFH case queries, including one duplicated case with a conflicting date. Exact case-number searches on the official BFH service resolved them to 81 unique decision pages, each linking a complete official PDF. This manifest captures those complete PDFs through the native single-block adapter. It preserves decision date separately from court publication date and records the discovery member IDs. No adapter changes or manual body editing.

The official page for III R 21/12 gives 8 May 2014. One discovery seed says 8 November 2014; another already gives 8 May. The capture uses the court's date and retains both originating member IDs. The consumer must disposition this identity discrepancy explicitly.

This is source recovery, not a legal disposition or an exhaustive decision census. The BFH service describes its online coverage as decisions since 2010. Older cases, BStBl issue/page verification, and complete registry discovery remain outstanding. Each recovered decision stays pending until its bearing is read and its computational rules are encoded or a text-bound exclusion is established. No certification claim.
