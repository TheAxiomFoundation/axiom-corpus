## Reproducible local extraction

The capture was copied unchanged into `<capture-dir>/act/public/2026/54/`
under its original filename. Directory input preserves this source layout and
the publisher's unpadded document URL; a bare `--source-xml` input defaults to
`act/public/2026/0054/wholeof.xml`.

```bash
uv run --extra dev axiom-corpus-ingest extract-nz-legislation \
  --base data/corpus \
  --version 2026-10-20-rulespec-nz-pco \
  --source-dir <capture-dir> \
  --source-pattern act_public_2026_54_en_2026-09-03.xml \
  --source-as-of 2026-10-20 \
  --expression-date 2026-10-20
```

The source-as-of and expression dates are the requested corpus observation
date, 20 October 2026. The official XML's assent and version date remain
3 September 2026. Operative commencement is established by section 2.

## Result and operative provisions

The scope contains one official XML snapshot and 26 normalized records with
26 matching inventory entries: one document, 15 sections, two schedules, two
schedule parts and six schedule clauses. Coverage is complete, with zero missing
or extra entries. The scoped artifacts are:

- `data/corpus/inventory/nz/statute/2026-10-20-rulespec-nz-pco.json`
- `data/corpus/provisions/nz/statute/2026-10-20-rulespec-nz-pco.jsonl`
- `data/corpus/coverage/nz/statute/2026-10-20-rulespec-nz-pco.json`

Required citation paths:

- `nz/statute/act/public/2026/0054/section/2` — commencement. The Act comes
  into force on 2 November 2026, except that “Part 2 comes into force on
  1 April 2027”. Section 15 is in Part 2.
- `nz/statute/act/public/2026/0054/section/15` — replaces Social Security
  Act 2018 Schedule 4 Part 7 clauses 4–6A with clauses 4, 4A, 5, 5A, 6,
  6AA, 6A and 6B. The general homeowner rows 4, 5 and 6 require qualifying
  costs that “exceeds 40% of the base rate”; the specified-person rows 4A,
  5A and 6AA retain “exceeds 30% of the base rate”. Clause 6B defines
  “specified person”. All replacement table rows are retained within section 15.

The XML's Part 2 `date.first.valid` attribute says `2026-11-02`; the explicit
commencement exception in section 2 establishes `2027-04-01`. No effective-date
inference or RuleSpec encoding was added to this source-text ingest.
The adapter also defaults normalized `metadata.stage` to `in-force`; that
inherited field does not establish commencement. Downstream encoding must use
the operative section 2 text. An independent comparison confirmed that every
nonempty section 15 table cell survives extraction after whitespace
normalization, including the spanning cells for clauses 6A and 6B.
