# California R&TC 17024.5 and 17052 as amended by SB 1435

This intake captures the current official text of California Revenue and
Taxation Code sections 17024.5 (Internal Revenue Code conformity) and 17052
(California Earned Income Tax Credit). The PolicyBench California root causes
r17/r08 (CalEITC) and r11 (itemized-deduction conformity) need both sections
for tax year 2025.

## Why a new scope

Both sections already resolve in corpus main, in
`us-ca/statute/2026-09-14-income-tax-chapter-us-ca-sections-4e26e6efabc3f0c7`
(and 17052 also in
`us-ca/statute/2026-07-06-ca-rtc-pit-core-us-ca-sections-rtc-17041-rtc-17043-rtc-17045-rtc-17052-rtc-17054-rtc-17073.5`).
Those records hold the text from before SB 1435 (Stats. 2026, Ch. 236). The
Governor approved SB 1435 and it was filed with the Secretary of State on
September 14, 2026, the day the chapter scope was captured. On 2026-09-23 the
leginfo pages for both sections carry these history notes:

- 17024.5: "(Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 236, Sec. 1. (SB 1435) Effective
  September 14, 2026. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after
  January 1, 2025, provided in Section 46 of Stats. 2026, Ch. 236.)"
- 17052: "(Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 236, Sec. 2. (SB 1435) Effective
  September 14, 2026. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after
  January 1, 2025, provided in Section 46 of Stats. 2026, Ch. 236.)"

In the 2026-09-14 scope the history notes read "Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch.
231, Sec. 1. (SB 711)" (17024.5) and "Amended (as amended by Stats. 2022, Ch.
72, Sec. 1) by Stats. 2022, Ch. 482, Sec. 5. (AB 1766)" (17052). Because the
amendments apply to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2025, the
tax-year-2025 oracle needs the amended text.

Text differences between the 2026-09-14 bodies and the bodies in this scope
(both produced by the same adapter):

- 17052(d): "substituting “$3,400” for “$2,200.”" now reads "substituting
  “$3,400” for the amount prescribed in that provision."
- 17052(j)(1): "In accordance with Section 41 of the Revenue and Taxation
  Code" now reads "In accordance with Section 41".
- 17052(j)(2): the report recipient "Senate Committee on Governance and
  Finance" now reads "Senate Committee on Revenue and Taxation".
- 17024.5(b): former paragraph (5), "A foreign investment company, as defined
  in Section 1246(b) of the Internal Revenue Code", is deleted, and former
  paragraphs (6) to (14) are renumbered (5) to (13). For example, the
  personal-exemption exclusion (Section 151) moves from (b)(12) to (b)(11).
- The history notes, as quoted above.

No other lines differ. In particular the specified-date table in
17024.5(a)(1) is unchanged: subparagraph (Q) gives January 1, 2025 as the
specified date for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2025 in both
texts. Sections 17041, 17073.5 and 17076 were fetched on the same day and
their extracted bodies equal the 2026-09-14 bodies, so they are not captured
again.

## Generation

The scope uses the same adapter as the two existing R&TC section scopes, with
`--preserve-tables`. The sections were first fetched on 2026-09-23; the
provisions were then rebuilt from the retained bytes (copied into a download
cache laid out as `california-leginfo-sections/RTC-<section>.html`), so the
retained sources, inventory and coverage are unchanged:

```bash
uv run --extra dev axiom-corpus-ingest extract-california-code-sections \
  --base data/corpus \
  --version 2026-09-23-ca-rtc-sb-1435 \
  --source-as-of 2026-09-23 \
  --expression-date 2026-09-23 \
  --section RTC:17024.5 \
  --section RTC:17052 \
  --delay-seconds 1 \
  --download-dir <cache of this scope's retained california-leginfo-sections> \
  --preserve-tables

uv run --extra dev python scripts/self_contain_usc_scope.py \
  --base data/corpus \
  --jurisdiction us-ca \
  --document-class statute \
  --version 2026-09-23-ca-rtc-sb-1435-us-ca-sections-rtc-17024.5-rtc-17052
```

The adapter names the scope
`2026-09-23-ca-rtc-sb-1435-us-ca-sections-rtc-17024.5-rtc-17052`. It emits
each section with `parent_citation_path: us-ca/statute/rtc`, a container this
scope does not carry. The second command detaches that parent in the same way
as the 2026-09-14 chapter scope: `parent_citation_path` and `parent_id` become
null, and `metadata.detached_parent_citation_path` and
`metadata.self_contained_root` record the omitted container. Coverage is
complete: 2 inventory items, 2 provisions, 2 matched.

## Table fidelity

Without `--preserve-tables`, the adapter collects each `<p>` block, including
every table cell, and drops any block whose text repeats an earlier one. In R&TC
17052(b) that removed the second column of both tables (e.g. `$3,290 | $3,290`
became one `$3,290`) and the row labels of the second table, which repeat the
first table's labels. With `--preserve-tables` each table row becomes one
`cell | cell` line in reading order and no block is dropped. Where LegInfo wraps
a table in a paragraph, as it does for the six tables in 17052(m), (n) and (o),
the paragraph's own text is emitted around the rows and the table only once. The
option is off by default so existing scopes rerun unchanged. In 17024.5, whose
table wraps each taxable-year label across several rows as leginfo's HTML does,
the rows now read `... ........................ | January 15, 1983`; no text is
added or lost.

`tests/test_us_ca_rtc_sb_1435_sections.py` checks that both bodies equal a
table-preserving re-extraction of the retained bytes, that every 17052(b) row is
present, and that each body's words (with the ` | ` separators removed) are the
section's visible LegInfo text without its section-number heading, in order.
Run over all 1,045 LegInfo section pages retained in the corpus, the
table-preserving path meets that word-for-word check on every page.

Follow-up, not changed here: the default path drops text from 16 section
records in three merged scopes, whole repeated paragraphs as well as table
cells:

- `2026-06-25-ca-wic-calworks-…`: WIC 11450.
- `2026-07-06-ca-rtc-pit-core-…`: RTC 17041 and 17052.
- `2026-09-14-income-tax-chapter-…`: RTC 17041, 17052, 17052.6, 17053.5,
  17053.73, 17276, 17955, 18628, 18648, 18662, 19025, 19141.5 and 19183.

For example, the chapter scope's 17053.5 (renter's credit) keeps only one of
the two word-for-word identical passages that give its $250 and $500 amounts.
The FTB 3514 booklet records' primary `statutory_corpus_version` pointer names
the chapter scope, whose 17052 is one of these records. Re-extracting these
scopes with `--preserve-tables` needs new versions.

## Release boundary

Nothing is published, loaded or activated. The two citation paths also exist
in the 2026-09-14 chapter scope, which the tracked selectors
`us-rulespec-2026-09-14-wave4-union` and `us-rulespec-2026-09-14-wave4-r2-union`
select. `validate_release` under the `complete-expression-dates-v1` profile
(the profile those selectors use) passes with zero issues for a selector
holding this scope and `us-ca/form/2026-09-23-ca-2025-ftb-3514`. A selector
holding this scope and the 2026-09-14 chapter scope fails with two
`duplicate_release_citation` errors, one for each path. Serving follows one
active release per (jurisdiction, document class), so a release cannot serve
this scope in place of the chapter scope without dropping the rest of the
chapter.

A future release that should serve the SB 1435 text therefore needs a
refreshed income-tax chapter scope, captured once leginfo shows the SB 1435
text (as it does on 2026-09-23), in place of the 2026-09-14 chapter scope (see
the next section).
Until then this scope keeps the official amended text and its provenance in
the corpus. The eight FTB 3514 booklet records in
`us-ca/form/2026-09-23-ca-2025-ftb-3514` name this scope in
`statutory_superseding_corpus_version`, beside their primary
`statutory_corpus_version` pointer to the 2026-09-14 chapter scope.
`scripts/deduplicate_release_selector.py` keeps the canonical scope's row when
a citation appears in both a canonical and an added scope, so running it with
this scope on the added side would discard the amended text.

The signed ingest manifest
(`.axiom/ingest-manifests/us-ca/statute/2026-09-23-ca-rtc-sb-1435-us-ca-sections-rtc-17024.5-rtc-17052.json`)
covers the source snapshot, inventory, provision JSONL and coverage report, with
this note as its reasoning log.

## Other SB 1435 sections in the chapter scope

SB 1435's enacted title amends Sections 17024.5, 17052, 17077, 17085, 17091,
17156.1, 17201.3, 17225, 17260, 17276, 17276.1, 17276.3, 17276.4, 17276.7,
17276.21, 17276.22, 17276.24, 17302, 17551, 17560.5, 17737, 18622.5, 18624,
18666 and 19311, repeals Sections 17132, 17279, 17865 and 18044, and repeals
and adds Section 17250, among the Personal Income Tax Law and administration
sections (it also amends Corporation Tax Law sections). All 30 of those Part 10
and Part 10.2 sections are in the 2026-09-14 chapter scope, and none of their
history notes there mentions Stats. 2026, Ch. 236. This intake refreshes only
17024.5 and 17052, the two sections the PolicyBench targets name. The other 28
need a refreshed chapter scope.
