## Part A: the 34 EXTRACTABLE cells

Rule 2 of the preamble (confirm the document is not held before extracting) decided most of this group: 22 of the 34
cells are check-pattern misses over text the selected scopes already hold, 3 are closed by the new scopes, 4 are ABSENT
on the publisher's side and 5 stay EXTRACTABLE with the carrying family named. Every ALREADY-HELD row in the decisions
file cites the held scope and citation path and says why the check's regex missed it. The recurring causes, worth
folding into the next `check_snap_wic.py`:

1. `\$\s?\d{3}` never matches an amount with a thousands separator, so every income-limit table ($1,305, $1,696 ...)
   misses (KY MS 5200, MI RFT 250, and the same family of misses across the REVIEW column).
2. State vocabulary: Georgia "LSUA", Massachusetts "Non-heating SUA", Michigan "Heat and utility"/"Non-heat electric",
   New Hampshire "Utilities-Only", Indiana "NON-HEATING AND COOLING (SUA 2)", Maine "NHUA", West Virginia "NHCS",
   Minnesota "Heat/Air Standard Utility Deduction", Iowa "little standard utility allowance".
3. A word between the two anchors: "Maximum SNAP Allotment" (NH, ND), "maximum excess deduction" (NC), "Basis of
   Coupon / EBT Issuance" (MA), "certified for 12 months" (ND), "income Eligibility Standards" (HI).
4. The 600-character window between "broad-based categorical" and the first state name is 315 characters too short
   for the BBCE chart's introductory paragraph (snap_g12).
5. Version names containing `tanf` are excluded by `OTHER_PROGRAM_RE`, which drops Hawaii's selected SNAP rules scope
   `2026-05-27-hi-snap-rules-r2026-07-15-self-contained-r2026-09-13-tanf-consolidated` (a consolidation) from every
   Hawaii check; 20 of Hawaii's 34 REVIEW cells are carried by that scope.
6. PDF ligatures: Tennessee's manual prints "simpliﬁed" with U+FB01, so `simplified report` never matches.

## Part B: the reading pass (337 REVIEW cells)

Only one of the 337 REVIEW rows cites a provision (MS snap_s32); the other 336 are "no match" rows (the regex did not
hit and the family index was recorded complete) or "family not inventoried" rows (wic_s30 x34, wic_s31 x50, snap_s32
x12), so "reading the cited provision" meant re-searching the scopes the check named with looser patterns (thousands
separators allowed, state vocabulary, wider windows) and reading the hits, plus the Hawaii scope the check had excluded.
Cells read: 236 (every REVIEW row with a scope to search; under the 250 cap) plus the 34 Part A state cells. The 96
not-inventoried rows were not read: they have no corpus text (see below).

Method: `scratchpad/partb_search.py` (not committed; the loose patterns are listed per element in the decisions
notes) ran 60 element patterns over the exact scope lists in each row's note, printed up to three snippets per cell,
and every cell was decided by hand from the snippets, with a targeted second search where the first was ambiguous
(48 cells). Results by class:

- **PRESENT (81)**: the scope carries the element; the decisions row cites scope, citation path and the text. Most are
  the same regex causes as Part A (thousands separators, vocabulary, ligatures), so the PATTERN-CONFIRMED verdict of
  the preamble applies to the elements below rather than to any one state's wording.
- **ABSENT (130)**: the state's complete selected scope does not carry the element. Two large groups: (a) optional
  elections the state does not make (snap_s37 state-funded non-citizen food, snap_s40 restaurant meals, snap_s53
  state-funded minimum supplement, snap_s59 SSI cash-out, snap_s33 TBA, snap_s39 CAP/ESAP, snap_s07 mandatory SUA,
  snap_s10 standard medical deduction) where the manual restates only the federal rule; (b) dollar amounts the state
  keeps in a standards chart outside the manual (AL, DE, ID, MS, NE, NJ, OK, TX, UT), each with the carrying family
  named.
- **OUTREACH (37)**: MA (12), VT (22) and WI (3) WIC cells whose only scope is the State Plan; the manuals are not
  published (WIC queue), and the MA plan itself refers to a "Program Manual (PM)" that mass.gov does not post.
- **EXTRACTABLE (3)**: WA snap_s03 and snap_s38 (the EA-Z manual cross-references WAC 388-414-0001 and 388-442-0010;
  neither WAC chapter is in the corpus) and MN wic_s31 (Minnesota Rules chapter 4617).
- **REVIEW (85)**: 83 wic_s30/wic_s31 rows with no corpus family (a state-by-state survey of administrative codes and
  state-plan postings is a separate discovery task, not a reading) and two ambiguous cells (HI snap_s41 interview mode,
  IL snap_s38 drug felony) left open with what was found.
