## Why

CMS issued State Medicaid Director letter SMD# 26-006, "Optional Hold Harmless Period for Community
Engagement Implementation", on 2026-11-13. It offers states an optional period through June 30, 2027
during which a state that elects it may suspend denials and disenrollments when applicable individuals
do not demonstrate compliance with the community engagement requirement of section 1902(xx) of the
Social Security Act. A RuleSpec encoding of that option reads the letter through the release
rulespec-us pins, so the letter needs a signed corpus scope first. The release selector that adds this
scope is in the same change (`docs/ingest-runs/2026-11-14-us-rulespec-cms-smd-26-006.md`).

- Hold Harmless Period (pages 2 and 3): CMS will not take enforcement action against a state that

  relevant application and renewal. The period applies only to the requirement at section 1902(xx)(1)

  deny or terminate when an applicable individual has not met the requirement, following 42 C.F.R.
  § 435.558. States request the period in their mitigation plans; states that intend to use it from
  January 1, 2027 are asked to notify CMS by December 11, 2026.
- State Mitigation Plans (page 3): CMS approval is required before a state implements the period or
  other mitigations. Determinations made during an approved period are not errors for PERM, MEQC or
  other CMS eligibility audits.
