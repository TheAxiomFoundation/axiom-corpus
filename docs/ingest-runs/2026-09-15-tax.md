Method. Per state the selected statute scopes (09-14 selector) were scanned section by section with element-specific
patterns (`statute_scan.py`, scratch), the hits were read and each cell decided: ALREADY-HELD when a held section

carrier sits in a companion selected scope, e.g. the 2026-07-13 recovery scope, that scope is cited), EXTRACTABLE
when the element lives outside the held chapter (local income taxes, withholding/estimated-tax and property-tax
chapters), REVIEW when the whole chapter is held but the element was not located by pattern or is a candidate N/A
(the note says which), OUTREACH for the Lexis-only states. Three states lacked a whole chapter:
