## Method

1. Held already? Every row's `bundle_url`, `check_final_url` and `id` were normalised and matched against every
   inventory `source_url` / `download_url` under `data/corpus/inventory/us*/` (509,339 items), every `bundle_path`
   against every provisions JSONL of its jurisdiction, and (after the first pass) against the inventories of every
   **locked** US scope in `.axiom/corpus-locks/` read from git blobs (1,343 inventories): five EXTRACT-MANIFEST
   documents (CA ACL 06-31, 13 DE Reg 1550, IL MR 23.22, MA DTA child support, VA 22VAC40-601) are committed as
   2026-09-23 locks whose bytes are not in the local `data/corpus`; the first pass had re-extracted them and those
   duplicates were dropped (manifests deleted, artifacts removed). Rows whose text sits in a held scope under another
   address (mirror rows, dead links, older editions) were searched by citation path and by body text.
2. One plain probe of every remaining official address with the extractor's own client (corpus user agent, the
   extractor's built-in browser user-agent retry on 403/404/406, TLS verified with certifi plus `data/certs/`), one
   request at a time per host, 1.5 s apart. The body of every document was read for its title, edition and date before
   it was listed.
3. Manifests: one per (jurisdiction, class), `manifests/<jur>-benefits-w6-<class>.yaml`, version
   `2026-10-06-w6-benefits-<class>-<st>`, written by the generator; EXTRACT-MANIFEST entries are copied from the
   manifest on main. Adapter scopes use the existing extractors (`extract-illinois-admin-code`,
   `extract-maryland-comar`, `extract-texas-tcas`, `extract-indiana-code`, `extract-state-statutes` with the
   `alabama-code` and `arizona-revised-statutes` adapters).
4. Every scope: coverage `complete: true`, 0 missing, 0 extra, 0 duplicate citation paths; every document row checked
   for a non-empty, non-navigation body (selectors added for KY, ME, MO, NY, WA and MD pages after the first pass; OSCN
   challenge pages removed, see below); `scripts/validate_citation_paths.py` over the 56 new provisions files:
   13,117 records, 13,117 unique paths, 0 pattern failures (`RESULT: OK`, after the Texas fix below); collisions
   checked against every other provisions JSONL of the jurisdiction on disk and every locked scope's inventory.
5. Version strings all carry `w6-benefits`; no other agent's manifest was edited; nothing under `manifests/releases/`
   or `manifests/program-bundles/` was touched; nothing was signed, loaded or pushed to R2/Supabase.
