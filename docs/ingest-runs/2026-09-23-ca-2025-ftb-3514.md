# California 2025 FTB 3514 booklet (California EITC)

This intake preserves the California Franchise Tax Board's official 2025
California Earned Income Tax Credit booklet, FTB 3514: the instructions, the
Step 6 California Earned Income Tax Credit Worksheet, form FTB 3514 and the
2025 Earned Income Tax Credit Table. It supplies the administrative
computation that sits beside the statutory credit in
`us-ca/statute/rtc/17052`.

## Source

Official source: <https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-3514-booklet.pdf>

Retrieved 2026-09-23. The 32-page PDF (695,014 bytes) has SHA-256
`9096ff0ec8a240717753f3f0345f2fa85a7bfaba4a8bcb194f316394a4e15538`. The FTB
server reported `Last-Modified: Wed, 04 Mar 2026 14:11:41 GMT`; the PDF's own
modification date is February 19, 2026.

Each root carries one body-bearing child, `<root>/document-1`.

PDF pages 11, 12 and 21 read only "THIS PAGE INTENTIONALLY LEFT BLANK" with
the page footer and web address. PDF pages 17-20 are the booklet's second copy
of form FTB 3514; their extracted text is identical to PDF pages 13-16, with
and without a position sort. Those seven pages are not captured.

## Extraction choices

`single_block` with `page_windows` keeps each extracted PDF line on its own
line (lines joined by a newline, pages by a blank line). Without a page
filter, `single_block` joins consecutive non-blank lines into one paragraph,
which would merge worksheet lines and table rows.

The bodies were extracted with PyMuPDF 1.26.7, the version `uv.lock` pins,
and every slice records it in `text_extractor` metadata.

The instructions, worksheet and form slices use the PDF content-stream order.
On the two-column instruction pages a position sort (`sort_text: true`)
interleaves the two text columns line by line, and on PDF pages 1-10 and
13-16 it returns fewer non-whitespace characters than the stream order
(between 34 and 570 fewer per page); on PDF pages 31-32 the two orders carry
the same characters. For every one of these slices, the whitespace-separated
tokens in the extracted body equal the tokens PyMuPDF 1.26.7 returns for the
same pages in stream order.

The EITC table slice uses `sort_text: true`, which keeps each printed table
line as one line. On PDF pages 23-30 the sorted and stream orders carry the
same characters. On PDF page 22 they do not: the position sort returns 2,410
non-whitespace characters against 2,822 in stream order (see the next
section).

## Text layer

The PDF's tagged structure attaches ActualText to some glyphs, and PyMuPDF
substitutes it for the printed glyphs. The bodies therefore differ from the
printed pages in these ways, each listed with counts in the slice's
`extraction_note`:

- The word "Bullet" stands where the page prints a bullet: 30 times in the
  instructions, 3 in the worksheet and 4 in the form.
- On PDF page 1 the cover title ("2025 California Earned Income Tax Credit
  Booklet. 3514 California Forms & Instructions") appears four times where
  the page prints it once; the fourth copy starts at the end of the "Form FTB
  3514, California Earned Income Tax Credit" line.
- Instruction headings repeat: the Step 1-5 and 7-11 headings two or three
  times, on PDF page 5 the Worksheet 1 heading three times and the Worksheet
  2 heading twice, and on PDF page 9 the Part I heading twice and the Part
  III heading three times. The worksheet's Part III heading appears three
  times.
- The Step 7-11 and line 22, 30, 31 and 41 headings spell EITC, YCTC and
  FYTC with spaces ("E I T C").
- On PDF page 13 the form title ("TAXABLE YEAR 2025 California Earned Income
  Tax CreditFORM 3514") appears five times, each Part I-X heading twice, and
  on PDF page 15 the line 23 text twice, where the form prints each once.
- On PDF page 22 PyMuPDF returns ActualText strings (for example "And your
  number of qualifying children is 0, Your credit is") in place of the
  printed column labels, and with the position sort it returns 412 fewer
  non-whitespace characters than in stream order. The body's page-22 header
  keeps "Your credit" and the labels 0, 1, 2 and 3 once each and has no
  "looking up from" or "But Not", although the page prints both column
  blocks' labels. The page-22 rows are complete, and the headers on PDF
  pages 23-30 carry the labels once per printed column block.

The pipeline removes and replaces no words: each body keeps every token
PyMuPDF 1.26.7 returns for its pages. Other PyMuPDF releases read the
ActualText differently. PyMuPDF 1.28.2 returns different tokens for the
instructions, form and table pages (the worksheet page's tokens are the same),
so re-extracting with it does not reproduce these bodies. CI installs the
package with `pip install -e .[dev]`, which resolves `pymupdf>=1.25.0` to a
newer release than the lock (the `test (3.14)` job of main CI run
35886119992, at 942e138e7, installed PyMuPDF 1.28.2), so the test that
re-extracts every slice and
compares tokens runs only under PyMuPDF 1.26.7 and is skipped otherwise.
The other checks read the committed bodies or the PDF's word positions and
run under either version: under PyMuPDF 1.28.2 the two test files give 17
passed and 1 skipped.

## Worksheet guards

The worksheet body keeps each line guard as printed. Line 1 stops when
California earned income from form FTB 3514, line 19 is zero or less. Line 2
reads the credit from the EITC Table and stops when it is zero. Line 3 takes
federal AGI from federal Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11b. Line 4 skips line 5
when line 1 and line 3 are the same. Line 5 is left blank when line 3 is less
than $4,661 (no qualifying children), $6,998 (one qualifying child) or $9,823
(two or more qualifying children); otherwise it reads the EITC Table at line
3, and line 6 takes the smaller of line 5 and line 2 and carries to form FTB
3514, line 20. In the PDF content stream the worksheet lines come before the
Step 6 introduction, and the body keeps that order.

## EITC table

Each lookup row is six whitespace-separated values: at least, but not over,
and the credit when the number of qualifying children is 0, 1, 2 and 3. On
PDF pages 22-29 each printed line carries two lookup rows, the page's left
column block followed by its right column block, and the left block precedes
the right block in ascending lookup order. PDF page 30 prints one block.

Rebuilding the rows from the committed body gives 658 contiguous $50 brackets
from $1-$50 to $32,851-$32,900, with no gaps. The largest credits are $302,
$2,016, $3,339 and $3,756 for 0, 1, 2 and 3 qualifying children.
`tests/test_us_ca_2025_ftb_3514_booklet.py` rebuilds the rows from the
committed provision body and checks these properties.
