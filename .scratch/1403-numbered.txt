11	1	CCCAP MISSION AND APPROPRIATIONS
12	1	A.
13	1	Mission
14	1	The purpose of CCCAP is to provide eligible households with access to high quality, affordable child care
15	1	that supports healthy child development and school readiness while promoting household self-sufficiency
16	1	and informed child care choices.
17	1	B.
18	1	Appropriations
19	1	Nothing in these rules shall create a legal entitlement to child care assistance. Counties shall not be
20	1	required to expend funds exceeding allocated state and federal dollars or exceeding any matching funds
21	1	expended by the counties as a condition of drawing down federal and state funds.
22	1	When a county can demonstrate, through a written justification in its county CCCAP plan, that it has
23	1	insufficient CCCAP allocations, a county is not required to implement a provision or provisions of rule(s)
24	1	enacted under statutory provisions that are explicitly “subject to available appropriations.” The county is
25	1	not required to implement that or those rules or statutory provision(s) for which it has demonstrated
26	1	through its annual CCCAP plan that it has insufficient CCCAP allocations to implement, except for the
27	1	entry income eligibility floor referenced in rule section 3.105.1.H.
28	1	As part of its demonstration, the county shall include a list of priorities reflecting community circumstance
29	1	in its county CCCAP plan that prioritizes the implementation of the rules and/or provisions of statute that
30	1	are “subject to available appropriations.”
31	1	If the Colorado Department of Early Childhood (Department) determines the county CCCAP plan is not in
32	1	compliance with these rules and/or provisions of statute, the Department will first work with the county to
33	1	address the concerns. If a resolution cannot be agreed upon, the Department reserves the right to deny
34	1	the county CCCAP plan. If the Department denies the county CCCAP plan, the county and the state shall
35	1	work together to complete a final approved county CCCAP plan that is in compliance with these rules and
36	1	statute.

38	1	PROGRAM FUNDING
39	1	A.
40	1	The Colorado Child Care Assistance Program will be funded through annual allocations made to
41	1	the counties. Nothing in these rules shall create a legal entitlement to child care assistance.
42	1	Counties may use annual allocation for child care services which includes direct services and
43	1	administration.

51	2	B.
52	2	Each county shall be required to meet a level of county spending for the Colorado Child Care
53	2	Assistance Program that is equal to the county's proportionate share of the total county funds set
54	2	forth in the annual general Appropriation Act for the Colorado Child Care Assistance Program for
55	2	that State fiscal year. The level of county spending shall be known as the county's maintenance
56	2	of effort for the program for that State fiscal year.
57	2	C.
58	2	The CCCAP allocation formula shall be applied uniformly across all counties and must be based
59	2	on the relative cost of the program. The allocation formula must take into consideration:
60	2	1.
61	2	The eligible population for each county using the federal poverty level (FPL) as outlined
62	2	in rule section 3.105.1.H; and
63	2	2.
64	2	Reimbursement rates set by the state as informed by the market rates study.
65	2	3.
66	2	If not already taken into consideration in the initial allocation formula as stated in rule
67	2	sections 3.102.C.1 and 2, the following factors must also be included:
68	2	a.
69	2	A measure of cost of living, which may include market rates; and,
70	2	b.
71	2	The cost of high quality child care programs.
72	2	4.
73	2	If not already taken into consideration in the initial allocation formula, the formula may
74	2	include the following factors:
75	2	a.
76	2	A statewide adjustment to the allocation formula for geographic differences within
77	2	counties or regional differences among counties in order to improve access.
78	2	b.
79	2	A statewide adjustment to the allocation formula for drastic economic changes
80	2	that may impact the ability of CCCAP to serve low-income families.
81	2	c.
82	2	A statewide adjustment to mitigate significant decreases in county allocation
83	2	amounts due to changes in the factors considered in the initial allocation formula.
