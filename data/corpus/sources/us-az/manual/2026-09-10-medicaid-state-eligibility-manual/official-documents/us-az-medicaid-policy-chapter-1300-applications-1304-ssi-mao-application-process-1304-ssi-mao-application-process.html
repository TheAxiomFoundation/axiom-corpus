1304 SSI-MAO Application Process

 

 

 

Revised 11/29/2018

Overview

This section describes application requirements that are specific to the Supplemental Security Income - Medical Assistance Only (SSI-MAO) program: 

  • Screening for one of the three specialty groups within SSI-MAO; and

  • Disability determinations.

 

General information about the application process can be found in MA1301 and MA1302.
