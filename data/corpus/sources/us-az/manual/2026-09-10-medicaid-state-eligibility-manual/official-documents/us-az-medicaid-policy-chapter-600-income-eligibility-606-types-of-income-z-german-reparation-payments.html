Z German Reparation Payments

Policy

German Reparation Payments are excluded for all Medical Assistance programs.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

German Reparation Payments

Payments made to certain survivors of the Holocaust by the German Government.

 

Proof

Because this income is excluded, only the source of the income is verified. Types of proof include, but are not limited to:

  • Award letter;

  • Copy of check stub; or

  • Collateral contact with the agency providing the income; or

  • By contacting the German embassy in Los Angeles at:

 

The Consulate General of the Federal Republic of Germany

6222 Wilshire Blvd. Suite 500

Los Angeles, CA 90048

Tel: (323) 930-2703

Fax: (323) 930-2805
