 F Three Bi-Weekly Payments Received In a Month Example

 

Program

Manual Section

ALTCS

SSI-MAO

MSP

FTW

MA604.E

 

The customer applies in November. He is working and is paid every other week. The customer gets three bi-weekly checks in November. The total income from the three checks received in November is more than the income limit. In December and for the next few months, the customer will get two bi-weekly checks. The total income from the two checks he normally gets is less than the income limit.

The customer will be denied in November for excess income, and approved for December and ongoing. The worker uses the customer’s pay schedule to figure out the next month he will get three checks and sets an alert for the month before to review his case.
