Definitions

Term

Definition

In-kind income

Any non-cash item including food, clothing, or shelter provided in return for labor or services rendered. This is also called bartering income.

Example:

A plumber does repair work for a dentist in exchange for dental services.

In-kind Support and Maintenance (ISM)

Food, clothing or shelter received by an individual that was paid or partially paid by someone else.

 

Proof

For ISM, the person’s statement can be used for proof unless it is questionable. For In-kind income, types of proof include:

  • Written statement on a from the person or source providing item or service the in-kind income;

  • Receipts showing the value of the item;

  • Collateral contact with source providing the in-kind income or ISM.
