Definitions

Term

Definition

Individual Development Account (IDA)

IDAs are special savings accounts that match the deposits of low income people.

IDAs are offered through partnerships between financial institutions such as banks and credit unions, and local nonprofit organizations or program sponsors.

There are two types of IDAs:

  • TANF IDA; and

  • Demonstration Project IDA.

 

Proof

Because this income is excluded, only the source of the income is verified. Types of proof include, but are not limited to:

  • Written statement from the sponsor;

  • Collateral contact with the sponsor; or

  • Statement from the financial institution.
