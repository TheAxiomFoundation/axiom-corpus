608 Income Deeming

 

Overview

For some AHCCCS Medical Assistance (MA) programs, part of another person's income may count as income to a customer, whether or not that income is actually contributed to the customer.

Deeming applies to:

  • Parental deeming; and

  • Sponsored non-citizen deeming.
