V Reverse Mortgage Payments

 

 

Revised 01/01/2018

Policy

Payments from a reverse mortgage are a conversion of a resource from equity in real property to cash and are counted resources. 

When a customer has a line of credit as the result of the reverse mortgage, the conversion does not occur until the funds are actually advanced.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Reverse Mortgage Payments

A special type of mortgage which allows homeowners age 62 and older to borrow against the value of their home.

 

Proof

Proof of reverse mortgages and payments may include one or more of the following:

  • Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) documents like;

    • Home Equity Conversion Deed of Trust;

    • Home Equity Conversion Note

    • Home Equity Conversion Mortgage contract.

  • “Home Keeper Mortgage” contract; or

  • Account statements or ledgers from the lender.
