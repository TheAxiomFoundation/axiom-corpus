NF 2026-017M 

Arizona Health Care Cost Containment System

Processing Active FTW, SSI-MAO and MSP When Denying ALTCS Update

08/25/2026

 

This NEWS FLASH is being issued to inform staff...

Program Impact: ALTCS

MAP1303 Processing ALTCS Applications [Section B] Dispositioning SSI MAO, FTW, and MSP for ALTCS Applications

  • “Processing Active FTW, SSI-MAO and MSP when denying ALTCS” step/action table updated:

    • Added a new step to determine if the customer has other AHCCCS eligibility when ALTCS is denied.

    • Clarified processing requirements for ALTCS denials due to missed financial appointments or voluntary withdrawals.

    • Added clarification on the appropriate denial, approval, and redetermination actions for ALTCS, SSI-MAO, FTW, and MSP eligibility.  

    • Removed “Application Taken in Error” for SSI-MAO, FTW, and MSP when no eligibility changes were identified.

 

  • Note to staff: Do not use “Application Taken in Error” for the SSI MAO, FTW or MSP.
