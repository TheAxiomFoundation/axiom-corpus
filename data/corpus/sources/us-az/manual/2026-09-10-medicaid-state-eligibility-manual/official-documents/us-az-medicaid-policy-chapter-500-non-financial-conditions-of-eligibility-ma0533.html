533 Valid Application

 

 

Revised 08/15/2023

Policy

A customer must submit a valid application. A valid application contains at least the following information:

  • Applicant's name;

  • Address or location where the applicant can be reached;

  • Date the application was signed.

  • Signature under penalty of perjury (MA1301A.2)

Definitions

Term

Definition

Application

A method for a person to apply for MA benefits. The application may be written, online or telephonic, and must be approved by AHCCCS.

Monoymous person

A person who has one name.

 

Proof

An application is submitted containing all of the required information.
