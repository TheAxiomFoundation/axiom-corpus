E Alaska Native Corporation and Settlement Trust Payments

 

Policy

Alaska Native Corporation and Settlement Trust payments are excluded for all Medical Assistance programs.

 

Definitions

 

Term

Definition

Alaska Native Regional and Village Corporation Payments

Distributions of cash or dividends on stock received from a Native Corporation to an:

  • Individual Alaska Native; or

  • Descendant of an Alaska Native.

 

Proof

Because this income is excluded, only the source of the income is verified. Types of proof include:

  • Copies of check stubs;

  • Letter from the agency providing the income; or

  • Collateral contact with the agency providing the income.
