603 What is Not Income

 

 

 

Revised 07/06/2017

 

Policy

The following are not considered income when determining eligibility:

  • Money from the sale of a resource;

  • Money transferred between financial accounts that belong to the same person;

  • Money a person receives to cover someone else’s expenses and then uses to pay the expenses is not considered income to the customer;

  • Replacement income;

  • Return of incorrect payments; and

  • Vendor payments.

 

Exceptions for vendor payments:

  • Payments made directly to a vendor when the money is legally owed to the person (such as alimony) are considered income. For example, the person’s ex-husband pays her rent with the alimony that he normally sends her. This is income; not a vendor payment.

  • For ALTCS, payments made directly to a vendor from a Special Treatment Trust for shelter expenses are considered income, not a vendor payment. See MA803E for details.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Replacement Income

A payment that replaces income which was lost or stolen. 

NOTE     The original payment would be counted as income when received.

Return of incorrect payments

A payment returned by a person who is aware that he is not entitled to the payment.

If there is a delay in the return of an incorrect payment beyond the month following the month of receipt, the reason for the delay must be documented.

See Example - Return of an incorrect payment

Vendor Payments

Payments for a customer’s expenses made directly to the vendor by a third party.

See Example - Vendor Payment

 

Proof

Proof may be needed if the transaction appears questionable. Types of proof include, but are not limited to:

  • Receipts;

  • Bills; and

  • Phone call to the source of the payment.
