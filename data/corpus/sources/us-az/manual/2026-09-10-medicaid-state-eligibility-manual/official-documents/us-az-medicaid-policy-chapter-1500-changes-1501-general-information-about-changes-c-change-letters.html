C Change Letters

 

 

Revised 03/29/2022

Policy

A written notice is required if a change in circumstance causes:

  • A customer to lose eligibility;

  • A decrease in services;

  • An increase in the customer’s share of cost; or

  • An increase in the customer’s premium amount.

 

See MA1604 for information regarding written letters.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Change in Circumstance

Something that happens to a customer that may impact their Medicaid eligibility, enrollment, share of cost, or premium amount, or ability to be contacted or receive mail.
