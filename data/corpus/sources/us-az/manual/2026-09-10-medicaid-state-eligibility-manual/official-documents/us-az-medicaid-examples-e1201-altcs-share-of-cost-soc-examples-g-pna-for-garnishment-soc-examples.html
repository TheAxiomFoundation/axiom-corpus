G PNA for Garnishment SOC Examples

 

Programs

Manual Section

ALTCS

MA1201C

 

The customer is on ALTCS and has total income of $1000.00 per month. He lives in a nursing facility. His PNA for August 2026 is $149.10.

In September 2026, a court-ordered child support garnishment of $100.00 per month takes effect. The $100.00 garnishment is added to his PNA, increasing it to $249.10.
