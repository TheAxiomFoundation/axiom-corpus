1603 Non-Discrimination

 

 

Revised 02/20/2019

Policy

An AHCCCS customer must be treated fairly and equally regardless of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, age, political beliefs, or disability.

AHCCCS does not discriminate on the basis of disability in admission to, access to, or operation of its programs, activities, services, or in its employment practices. AHCCCS complies with the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990.

If the customer is visually or hearing impaired and needs an accommodation or a different format to complete an application, the customer may submit a request by speaking to the Benefits and Eligibility Specialist assigned to their case, or by calling 1-855-432-7587.
