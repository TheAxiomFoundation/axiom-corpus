 B EPD ALTCS Health Plan Enrollment Process

 

 

 

Revised 04/29/2025

Policy

Only customers who are age 65 or older (elderly) or have a physical disability (EPD) whose county of fiscal responsibility (MA1104E) is Maricopa County, Gila County, Pinal County or Pima County may choose their ALTCS Health Plan. Other fiscal counties do not have multiple ALTCS Health Plans. Enrollment in an ALTCS Health Plan is determined according to the following policy:

  • All ALTCS customers who have a developmental disability are enrolled with the Division of Developmental Disabilities (MA1104C).

  • EPD American Indians that are within the bounds of a tribal nation are enrolled with the tribe or Native American Community Health (NACH) if the tribe is not an ALTCS Health Plan (MA1104D).

  • EPD customers whose county of fiscal responsibility is Maricopa County, Gila County, Pinal County or Pima County may choose their ALTCS Health Plan (MA1104E). 

  • All other EPD customers are enrolled with the ALTCS Health Plan that serves their county of fiscal responsibility.

  • An EPD ALTCS Health Plan is determined based on the customer’s county of fiscal responsibility and their Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (DSNP) with Medicare, if the customer has one.

 

ALTCS Health Plans for EPD Customers Not Enrolled with a Tribal Contractor

When a customer does not choose an ALTCS Health Plan before the application is approved, AHCCCS automatically assigns an ALTCS Health Plan and enrolls the customer. A Freedom of Choice letter is mailed to customers informing them of the ALTCS Health Plan they were enrolled in and giving them a 90-day period to choose a different ALTCS Health Plan if they wish to change. If the customer does not contact AHCCCS to choose a different ALTCS Health Plan, the customer will remain enrolled with the auto-assigned ALTCS Health Plan.

When customers need assistance selecting an ALTCS Health Plan, they may

  • visit www.azahcccs.gov/altcschoice; or

  • contact the AHCCCS Benefits and Eligibility Specialist for choice counseling.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Choice Counseling

Provides information and services to help customers make enrollment decisions. It includes answering questions and identifying factors to consider when choosing an ALTCS Health Plan. Choice counseling does not include making recommendations for or against enrollment into a specific ALTCS Health Plan.

Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (DSNP)

A type of Medicare Advantage (Part C) plan designed for persons who are eligible for both Medicare and Medicaid.
