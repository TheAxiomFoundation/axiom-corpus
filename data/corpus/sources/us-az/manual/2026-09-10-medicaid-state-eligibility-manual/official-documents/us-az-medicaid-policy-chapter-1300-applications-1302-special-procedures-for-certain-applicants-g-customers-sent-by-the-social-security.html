G Customers Sent by the Social Security Administration

 

Policy

Applications sent to AHCCCS electronically by the Social Security Administration (SSA) may not have the customer’s contact information or all the information needed to determine eligibility. These applications meet the conditions in MA533 and are considered signed when they are sent to AHCCCS.

When an application is sent electronically by SSA but does not have the customer’s citizenship status, the customer’s attestation is not needed. AHCCCS will request proof when the customer’s citizen or qualified noncitizen status cannot be verified electronically.
