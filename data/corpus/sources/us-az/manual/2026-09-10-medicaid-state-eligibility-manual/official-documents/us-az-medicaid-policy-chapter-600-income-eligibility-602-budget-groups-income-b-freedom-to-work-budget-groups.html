B Freedom To Work Budget Groups

 

Policy

The budget group includes only the person applying for or receiving Freedom to Work.

NOTE     If the applicant or recipient qualifies for long-term care services, the income of other household members may be used to determine their share of cost. See MA1201 for more information on Share of Cost.

 

Proof

Only the earned income of the applicant is used to determine income eligibility. A person will be asked to provide proof of income as needed. See MA605 for more details on proof of income.
