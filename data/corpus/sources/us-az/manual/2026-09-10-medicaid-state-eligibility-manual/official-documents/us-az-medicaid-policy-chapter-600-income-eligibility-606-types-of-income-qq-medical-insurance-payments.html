Definitions

Term

Definition

Flat Rate Policy

Cash from any medical insurance policy, which pays a flat rate benefit to the recipient without regard to the actual charges or expenses incurred (ex., per diem hospitalization or disability insurance, cancer or dismemberment policies or long-term care policies).

Long-Term Care Insurance

Helps cover the cost of assistance with activities of daily living, like bathing and dressing, when you need it, whether at home, in a nursing home, or another facility.

NOTE     Payments from  a long-term care Insurance policy that would be received had they been claimed, are constructively received income. When the benefit is received it may be assigned to a Special Treatment Trust (STT).

Medical Insurance Payments

Payments from Medicare or private medical insurance policies.

Reimbursement for Medical Expenses

Reimbursement received from medical insurance (Medicare and private medical insurance policies).

 

Proof

Income is first obtained through the Federal and State Data Services Hubs. If needed, other proof includes:

  • Collateral contact with payment source;

  • Check stub;

  • Statement from payment source; or

  • Request for Verification of Long-Term Care Partnership Insurance Policy (DE-243) if payments are being made from a Long-Term Care Partnership Insurance Policy.
