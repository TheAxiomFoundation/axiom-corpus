Policy

The treatment of census income depends on whether the person is:

  • An employee of the Census Bureau; or

  • A temporary census taker.

Definitions

Term

Definition

Census

The U.S. Census Bureau gathers and analyzes data on the U.S. population every ten years.

Census worker

A person employed to assist the U.S. Census Bureau with collecting data during the census.

 

Proof

Income is first obtained through the Federal and State Data Services Hubs. If needed, other proof includes:

  • Pay stubs;

  • Notification of Personnel Action (SF-50) from the employer;

  • Written statement from the employer; or

  • Collateral contact with the employer.
