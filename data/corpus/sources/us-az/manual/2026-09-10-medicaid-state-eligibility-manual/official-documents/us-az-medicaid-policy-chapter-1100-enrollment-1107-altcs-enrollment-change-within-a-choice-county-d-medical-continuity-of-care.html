D Medical Continuity of Care

 

Policy

Special health plan changes may be approved on a case-by-case basis to ensure the customer’s access to care. These situations generally involve existing conditions at the time of enrollment as opposed to new conditions that develop after enrollment.

Approval requires consent of the Medical Directors of both health plans or approval by the AHCCCS Chief Medical Officer.

The ALTCS Health Plans determine the effective date of the enrollment change.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Medical Continuity of Care

Special health plan changes to ensure the customer’s access to care. This is made on a case-by-case basis.
