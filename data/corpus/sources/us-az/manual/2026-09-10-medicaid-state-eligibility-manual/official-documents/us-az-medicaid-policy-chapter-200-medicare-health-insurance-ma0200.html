200 Introduction

 

In this chapter, you will learn about:

  • What is Medicare Health Insurance;

  • What are beneficiaries’ medical cost responsibilities;

  • How Medicare and Medicaid work together;

  • Medicare entitlement;

  • Medicare enrollment; and

  • State Buy-In and Buy-Out.

For each section in this chapter, you will find:

  • The policy for the requirement;

  • Any definitions needed to explain the policy; and

  • A list of the federal and state laws that apply to the requirement by program.
