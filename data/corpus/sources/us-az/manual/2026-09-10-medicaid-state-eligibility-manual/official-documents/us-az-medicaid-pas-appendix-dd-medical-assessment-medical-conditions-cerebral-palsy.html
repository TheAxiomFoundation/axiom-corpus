Cerebral Palsy

 

Overview

Cerebral Palsy is a condition that affects a person’s ability to move and maintain balance and posture. It is marked by impaired muscle coordination or other disabilities, typically caused by damage to the brain before or at birth. Cerebral Palsy is the most common motor disability in children.

Related Conditions

Diplegia

Paralysis of corresponding limbs on both sides of the body. For example, paralysis of both arms or both legs.

 

Hemiplegia

Paralysis on one side of the body. For example, the left arm and left leg.

 

Quadriplegia

Paralysis of all four limbs.

  • Infantile Quadriparesis

  • Tetraplegia

Paraplegia

Paralysis of the legs and lower body.

 

Cerebral Palsy Type Not Specified

Any type of Cerebral Palsy not described above.

  • Ataxic

  • Athetoid

  • Congenital

  • Infantile

  • Monoplegia

  • Spastic
