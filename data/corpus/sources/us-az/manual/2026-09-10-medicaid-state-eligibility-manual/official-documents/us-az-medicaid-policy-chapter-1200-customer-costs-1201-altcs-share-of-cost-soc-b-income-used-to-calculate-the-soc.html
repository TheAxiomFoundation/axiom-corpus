B Income Used to Calculate the SOC

 

 

Revised 12/27/2022

Policy

The income used for the Share of Cost (SOC) calculation is generally the same as the income used for income eligibility, but some income types are counted differently when calculating SOC. 

The following table describes how to count these incomes for SOC:

If the customer...

Then…

Meets all of the following:

  • Receives SSI Cash;

  • Lives in a certified Long-Term Care (LTC) medical facility; and

  • Has more than 50% of their cost-of-care paid for by ALTCS

When the customer has no additional income, the counted SSI Cash amount is $30.00.

When the customer’s additional income is equal to or more than $50.00, the counted SSI Cash amount is $0.00.

When the customer’s additional income is less than $50.00, the counted SSI Cash amount is the difference between $50.00 and the additional income. Countable SSI Cash cannot exceed $30.00.

Meets all of the following:

  • Is a veteran or the surviving spouse of a veteran;

  • Has no spouse or dependents; and

  • Is a resident of an Arizona State Veteran Home

Count all Veteran’s Assistance (VA) benefit amounts including:

  • Aid and Attendance (A&A); and

  • Unusual Medical Expenses (UME).

See MA606RRR for more information about VA benefits.

Has income assigned to a Special Treatment Trust

See MA803E.2 for instructions.

Is eligible for FTW - ALTCS

Gross earned and unearned income is counted.

Was determined eligible using Community Spouse policy

Only the customer’s income is counted.

 

Definitions

Income amount before any deductions like taxes or insurance.
