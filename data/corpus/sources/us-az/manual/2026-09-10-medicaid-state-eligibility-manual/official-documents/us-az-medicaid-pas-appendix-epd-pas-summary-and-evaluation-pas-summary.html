PAS Summary

 

Overview

The PAS Summary gives an overview of the customer’s medical conditions, functioning and needs. It should not include opinions about eligibility, placement, or need for institutionalization.

 

Things that must be included in the PAS Summary

Include the following items:

Factor

What to include

Introduction

  • Names of any people that gave information during the PAS interview.

  • Any corrections to Medicare or other insurance information.

Living situation and living arrangement

  • Where the customer is living.

  • Who the customer is living with.

NOTE     When the PAS is conducted in person, include observations noted during the interview.

Falls, hospitalizations, and ER visits

  • Date of each incident.

  • Reason for ER visit or hospitalization.

  • Cause and description of any falls, if known.

  • Any injuries caused by falls.

Medical conditions

A brief description of the customer's current major medical conditions and related problems.

ADL performance and continence

  • A summary of the customer's functional limitations and abilities.

  • What help is provided to the customer and who provides it.

  • For any ADL scored based on need, explain the reasons assistance is needed but not received.

  • A description of the customer's level of bowel and bladder continence.

Communication and sensory status

A note of any significant impairment in hearing, communication, and vision.

Level of orientation and behaviors

  • Whether the customer is oriented to person, place, and time.

  • A description of the customer's behaviors, including any reported behaviors that were not scored.

  • Any interventions provided for the behaviors.

Services and support

  • Any supportive services such as Meals on Wheels.

  • A description of informal services or support provided by relatives, neighbors, or friends.

  • Any services or treatments under "Other" in the Services/Treatments battery.

  • Any history with the Department of Developmental Disabilities or Adult Protective Services.

Social determinants of health (SDOH)

A description of how the SDOH impacts the customer's health or functioning.

NOTE    Reviewing SDOH is NOT part of the PAS interview. If an impact or risk due to a SDOH comes up during the interview or is observed during an in-person interview, include it in the PAS summary.

Examples of SDOH include:

  • Housing and utilities

  • Access to enough food or healthy food

  • Transportation

  • Safety

  • Financial strain

  • Education

NOTE     Document that the referral resource list was given to the customer. If the customer asked about or was given information about specific resources, include that information in the summary.

Previous PAS information

Summarize findings from prior PASs.

If the PAS was completed over a year ago, provide the following information:

  • The date of the PAS

  • The reason for reapplication.

  • How the customer’s condition has changed since the prior PAS.

 

If the PAS was completed within the last year, also include:

  • What scores have changed since the prior PAS.

    • For example, “For the PAS completed on 8/2023, transferring, bathing, and dressing were scored a “0”. The caregiver now reports the customer needs hands-on assistance for all these tasks and are scored a “2”. No behaviors were reported on the previous PAS, but the caregiver reports the customer has become more confused and is now resistive to bathing and taking medication believing he has already done so.”

NOTE     If there is more than one application completed in the past year, only the most recent requires notation of what scores have changed. The others only require the dates of the PAS. 
 

Other necessary information

  • Statements made by customer or caregiver about the reason they have applied for ALTCS, or any specific services they want.

  • A statement that a description of the ALTCS program requirements for nursing facility (NF) level of care was provided.

NOTE     Do NOT include statements that reflect personal opinions, judgments, or biases.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Social determinants of health (SDOH)

Conditions in the places where people live, learn, work, and play that affect a wide range of health, functioning, and quality-of-life outcomes and risks.

There are five key areas of SDOH:

  • Economic stability

  • Education

  • Social and community connection or support

  • Health and health care

  • Neighborhood and environment

Resources that enhance quality of life also influence health outcomes. Examples include safe and affordable housing, access to education, public safety, availability of healthy foods, local emergency and health services, and environments free of harmful toxins.

Adverse conditions or a lack of the resources noted above can impact a person’s health and functioning, as well as risk of institutionalization.
