Physician Review

 

Overview

A Physician Review (PR) may be requested when the final PAS score may not accurately reflect the customer's need for a Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF) level of care. A Benefits and Eligibility Manager must review the PAS and records before requesting a PR to make sure the PR process is necessary.

There are situations when a PAS Analyst Review Consultant (PARC) can complete an eligibility review. Both Physician and PARC reviews are completed on the Physician Review screen in HEAplus.

NOTE     See Eligibility Review for guidance on when a PR is mandatory or a PARC review is appropriate. See below for PRs that are not mandatory.

 

Physician Reviews that are NOT mandatory

A PR can be requested when the assessor and Benefits and Eligibility Manager agree that the customer appears to be at immediate risk of institutionalization. Some examples of PRs that can be requested, but are not mandatory, include customers who:

  • Are already in a Nursing Facility, but:

    • The PAS score is less than 56 points; AND

    • The assessor and Benefits and Eligibility Manager believe the need for a SNF level of care is still there.

  • Are very elderly and frail, but the PAS score is less than 56 points;

  • Had multiple hospitalizations for chronic medical conditions in the past six to twelve months;

  • Live alone and lack the resources to live safely;

  • Appeal an ineligible PAS decision;

  • Have a traumatic brain injury with behavioral concerns;

  • Receive services that include extensive and complex medical care.

 

Physician Reviews Information

The Physician review screen is completed by both the assessor and the reviewer. After the assessor completes their portion of the screen, the PAS is sent to the reviewer's work management list. The reviewer completes their portion of the screen. When finished, it is sent back to the assessor or Benefits and Eligibility Manager in HEAplus. The Assessor and Benefits and Eligibility Manager review the decision prior to completing the PAS.

Medical records should be reviewed by the assessor and Benefits and Eligibility Manager and scanned into the document storage system BEFORE requesting a PR. More information about medical records is below.

Medical Records

The assessor provides the reviewer with current medical records, if available and relevant to the customer's condition. The medical records should CLARIFY the current medical condition and functional needs.

Medical records may include:

  • History and Physical;

  • Discharge summary for hospitalizations;

  • Consultations by specialists. For example, psychology, psychiatry, neurology, or cardiology reports;

  • Therapy notes;

  • Nursing notes that address a specific incident or condition;

  • SIGNIFICANT test results such as laboratory results. For example, HgA1C for diabetics, viral titers, T-cell or CD4 counts for customer's diagnosed with AIDS, EEG, EKG or MRI results;

  • Physician Progress Notes relating to current conditions or need for long-term care; and

  • Minimum Data Set (MDS) if the customer is in SNF.

NOTE     For PAS reassessments, medical records also include the ALTCS health plan's two most recent long-term care assessments, when the ALTCS health plan reports the customer's overall functioning has improved.
