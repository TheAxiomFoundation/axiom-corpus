B Agent Fees

 

Policy

Agent fees are considered as countable income for all programs. The income is counted towards the agent.

NOTE     Agent fees are categorized as unearned income for non-MAGI coverage groups.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Agent Fees

Income that a person has been authorized to keep as a:

  • Payee;

  • Guardian; or

  • Other agent for another person.

 

Proof

Proof of the amount of fees includes:

  • Accounting statements;

  • Copies of check stubs; or

  • Legal document that authorizes the payment.
