Definitions

Term

Definition

Insurance Awards and Legal Settlements

Payments for financial loss, pain and suffering, loss or earning ability or for many other reasons. Payment may be received in a lump sum, periodic payments or both.

 

Proof

Income is first obtained through the Federal and State Data Services Hubs. If needed, other proof includes:

  • Written statement from the insurance company;

  • Copy of award letter;

  • Court documents; or

  • Collateral contact with the insurance company.
