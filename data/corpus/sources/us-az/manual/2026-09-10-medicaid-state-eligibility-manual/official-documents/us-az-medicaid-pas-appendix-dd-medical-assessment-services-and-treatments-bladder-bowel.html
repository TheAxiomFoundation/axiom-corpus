Bladder/Bowel

 

Overview

This section is used to record bladder and bowel treatments the customer is currently receiving. The three types of Bladder and Bowel treatments are:

  • Catheter Care

  • Ostomy Care

  • Bowel Dilation

 

Definitions

Treatment list in HEAplus

Definition

Catheter Care

The regular cleaning of urinary catheters and the genital area, emptying and changing draining bags, and disinfecting catheter supplies that are designed to be used more than once. 
A urinary catheter is a tube placed in the body to drain and collect urine from the bladder. Urinary catheter types include:

  • Condom;

  • Indwelling; and

  • Intermittent self-catheter

Ostomy Care

Maintaining the health of an artificial opening or stoma to the bowel or bladder, which includes cleaning the area around the stoma, changing the stoma ring, and changing the ostomy bag.
An ostomy is a surgical procedure that creates an opening, called a stoma, from the bowel or bladder to the outside of the body for the passage of urine or stool.

Bowel Dilation

The expansion of the anal opening to allow or help stool passage, which includes the use of suppositories.
