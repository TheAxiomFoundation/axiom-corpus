A General Information on Approval of Applications

 

 

Revised 10/29/2015

Policy

In general, eligibility for AHCCCS Medical Assistance is determined on a month-by-month basis. A customer may be eligible or ineligible for any specific month. See MA1311 for more information.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Approval

A determination that a person is eligible for Medical Assistance benefits.

Decision Letter

Notifies a customer of approval or denial of AHCCCS Medical Assistance program eligibility. 

Eligibility Begin Date

Effective date a person is eligible for AHCCCS Medical Services.
