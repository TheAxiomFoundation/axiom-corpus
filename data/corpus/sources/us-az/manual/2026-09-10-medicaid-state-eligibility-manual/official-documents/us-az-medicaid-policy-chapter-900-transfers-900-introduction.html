900 Introduction

 

This chapter explains how to evaluate transfers of income or resources that could impact a customer’s eligibility to get long-term care services.

For each eligibility requirement in this chapter, you will find:

  • The policy for the requirement;

  • Any definitions needed to explain the policy;

  • What proof is needed; and

  • A list of the federal and state laws that apply to the requirement.
