A Overview

 

 

Revised 03/28/2023

Policy

The Share of Cost (SOC) is a monthly amount a customer must pay toward the cost of long-term care services. Customers who qualify for ALTCS or FTW - ALTCS may have a SOC. However, the maximum SOC is limited to the monthly capitation rate for the customer.

NOTE     Customers who get ALTCS Acute Care only, and FTW-ALTCS customers who live in an HCBS setting do not have a SOC.

The SOC amount is based on the customer’s:

  • Counted income;

  • Living arrangement; and

  • Allowed SOC deductions.

 

The SOC is recalculated each time there is a change in the customer’s income or amount of the SOC deductions. 

The ALTCS health plan is usually responsible for collecting the SOC from the customer. However, there are exceptions as described in the table below:

If the customer...

Then...

Is eligible, but not enrolled during a month

The ALTCS health plan is not responsible for collecting the customer's SOC for that month.

Changes ALTCS health plans during the month

Each health plan is entitled to a portion of the monthly SOC based on the number of days the customer is enrolled with that health plan.

The health plan with whom the customer is first enrolled during the month is responsible for collecting the SOC, figuring each health plan’s share, and transferring the prorated SOC amount to the receiving health plan.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

ALTCS Acute Care only

The customer qualifies for the ALTCS program but cannot receive long-term services.

Capitation rate

A fixed rate paid to the health plan for the delivery of services to each customer enrolled with that health plan, regardless of the amount of medical services the customer receives.
