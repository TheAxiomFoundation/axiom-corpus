Definitions

Term

Definition

Vaccine Injury Compensation Program (VICP)

Payments made by the federal government to compensate individuals, or families of individuals, who have been injured by adverse reactions to mandated childhood vaccines.

 

Proof

Income is first obtained through the Federal and State Data Services Hubs. If needed, other proof includes:

  • Court document; or

  • Copy of check stub.
