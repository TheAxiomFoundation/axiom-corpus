A Enrolled with an AHCCCS Complete Care (ACC) plan at Time of ALTCS Approval Example

 

Programs

Manual Section

ALTCS

MA1104

 

The customer applied for ALTCS on March 25th. On April 10th she applied for AHCCCS Medical Assistance programs other than ALTCS. On April 20th, she was approved for SSI-MAO and enrolled with a health plan effective April 1st. 

On May 7th her ALTCS application was approved effective March 1st. Because her ALTCS eligibility begin date is before her enrollment started with the AHCCCS Complete Care (ACC) plan, she is enrolled with the ALTCS Health Plan beginning March 1st.
