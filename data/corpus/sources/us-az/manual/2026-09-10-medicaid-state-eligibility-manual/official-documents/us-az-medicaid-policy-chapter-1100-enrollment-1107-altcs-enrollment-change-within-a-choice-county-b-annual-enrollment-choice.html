B Annual Enrollment Choice

 

 

Revised 05/26/2026

Policy

The Annual Enrollment Choice (AEC) is a two-month process. The customer’s enrollment choice period is held annually based on the member’s most recent enrollment date. The customer’s enrollment anniversary date is based on the customer's most recent eligibility renewal date. The Annual Enrollment Notice is mailed to the customer or representative approximately two months prior to the customer’s anniversary date. 

The first month of the AEC period is the customer’s choice month. During this month, the customer has the option to change to a different health plan. The customer does not need to do anything to remain with the same health plan. If the customer would like to change to a different health plan, the Annual Enrollment Notice provides instructions to call the Communications Center or return the form to the Communications Center in Central Office by the end of the month. At the end of the month health plans are notified of any enrollment changes.

The second month is the health plans’ transition month. This period allows health plans to arrange for the transition in case management and providers. The health plans must be allowed a minimum of one full calendar month to arrange for the transition in case management and providers so there is no interruption in the customer’s medical care and services.

 

1) Requests Received During the Transition Month

AHCCCS accepts an annual enrollment change request through the last day of the transition month. However, the enrollment change will be delayed by one month to provide the required transition period.

 

2) Requests Received Outside the Annual Enrollment Period

After the annual enrollment period ends, the Communications Center refers all enrollment change requests received on or after the customer’s anniversary date to the ALTCS local office for resolution.

NOTE     Customers that are enrolled in an EPD Health Plan need to have their enrollment aligned to their Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (DSNP) with Medicare. When a customer wants to change their Health Plan that is aligned with their DSNP, they first need to change their DSNP with Medicare. The Annual Enrollment Notice will provide the customer with information to complete this alignment when needed.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Anniversary Month

The month that the customer’s eligibility begins becomes the customer’s anniversary month.

Choice Month

The first month of annual enrollment choice is the customer’s choice month. During this month the customer has the option to change to a different Health Plan.

Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (DSNP)

A type of Medicare Advantage (Part C) plan designed for persons who are eligible for both Medicare and Medicaid.

Enrollment Date

Enrollment with the health plan begins on the effective date of eligibility for AHCCCS Medical Assistance, but no earlier than the first day of the month of application.

Transition Month

The second month of annual enrollment choice is the Health Plans’ transition month. This period allows Health Plans to arrange for the transition in case management and providers.
