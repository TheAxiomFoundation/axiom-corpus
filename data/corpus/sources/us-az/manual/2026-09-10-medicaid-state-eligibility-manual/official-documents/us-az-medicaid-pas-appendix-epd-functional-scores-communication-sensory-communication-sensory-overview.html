Communication and Sensory Overview

 

Overview

The customer’s awareness of their environment, including people, place and time, and ability to communicate, hear, or see can impact their need for long-term care services and risk of institutionalization.

 

Things to Keep in Mind When Scoring Communication and Sensory Patterns

You may need to adjust how you interview when a customer has difficulty seeing, hearing, speaking, or a combination of these. Here are just few examples:

  • Ask multiple choice questions, where the customer can answer by nodding or holding up a certain number of fingers;

  • Show the customer the written question;

  • Have the customer write their answers; or

  • Use some other way to communicate.
