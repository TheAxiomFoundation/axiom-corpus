530 Receiving Social Security Title II

 

 

Revised 07/10/2020

Policy

To qualify for SSI-MAO in the Pickle coverage group, the customer must be receiving Social Security Title II benefits.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Social Security Title II benefits

Includes the following benefits:

  • Social Security Disability

  • Social Security Retirement

  • Social Security Survivors

 

Proof

Accept any of the following that show a person is receiving Social Security Title II benefits:

  • Written or electronic Social Security records;

  • Current award letter; or

  • Current check stub.
