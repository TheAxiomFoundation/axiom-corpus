OOO Unemployment Insurance

 

Policy

Unemployment Insurance payments are counted for all Medical Assistance programs as unearned income.

NOTE     A person may be working part-time and still receive a reduced unemployment payment. 

Unemployment Insurance payments received electronically are considered income on the date received. If mailed, payments are considered received as of the third day following the date of issue.

 

Definitions

Term

Definition

Unemployment Insurance Payments

Also known as unemployment compensation. A person receives these payments under a State or Federal unemployment law.

 

Proof

Income is first obtained through the Federal and State Data Services Hubs.

If needed, other proof includes:

  • Award letter from state agency providing the unemployment insurance; or

  • Collateral contact with the state agency providing the unemployment insurance.
