A QMB Begin Date Example

 

Programs

Manual Section

All programs

MA1309C

 

Example

The customer applies for QMB in January. Requested proof is received in February. The information provided shows the customer meets all conditions of eligibility in the month of January and ongoing. The approval is dispositioned on February 20th. The QMB eligibility begin date is March 1st.
