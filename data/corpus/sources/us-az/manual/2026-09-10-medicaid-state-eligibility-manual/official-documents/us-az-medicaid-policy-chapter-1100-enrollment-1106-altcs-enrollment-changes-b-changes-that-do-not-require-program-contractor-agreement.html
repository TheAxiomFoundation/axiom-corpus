B Changes That Do Not Require Health Plan Agreement

 

 

Revised 04/29/2025

Policy

The ALTCS local office may initiate some enrollment and fiscal county changes when information is received from the:

  • Customer;

  • Customer’s representative; or

  • Current ALTCS Health Plan.

 

The ALTCS local office may change the customer’s enrollment when:

  • A customer who is enrolled with DES/DDD loses developmentally disabled status;

  • A tribal contractor requests an enrollment change; or

  • The fiscal county changes.

NOTE     Customers enrolled in a Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (DSNP) with Medicare must have their EPD Health Plan aligned with their Medicare DSNP. To change the EPD health plan aligned with the DSNP, customers must first change their DSNP with Medicare. 
 

1) Loss of DD Status

Enrollment must be changed when a customer who is enrolled with DES/DDD loses developmentally disabled (DD) status:

  • If the customer is an American Indian, the enrollment is changed based on the policy in MA1104.D.

  • All other eligible customers are enrolled with an EPD health plan based on the customer’s county of fiscal responsibility.

 

2) Tribal Contractor Requests Enrollment Change

When an American Indian customer living within the bounds of the tribal nation, either in a medical institution or HCBS facility, moves to an HCBS facility outside the bounds of the tribal nation, the tribal contractor must determine if the customer should be enrolled with the health plan in the new location.

To request an enrollment change, the tribal contractor must call or write the Division of Managed Care Operations (DMCO). If the DMCO agrees with the recommendation, the tribal contractor sends an Electronic Member Change Report to the ALTCS local office instructing the Benefits and Eligibility Specialist to change the health plan to the county where the customer now resides. Agreement by the new health plan is not required.
 

3) Fiscal County Changes

If the fiscal county changes to a county that is not served by the current health plan, enrollment is changed to a health plan serving the county in which the customer’s home is located.
