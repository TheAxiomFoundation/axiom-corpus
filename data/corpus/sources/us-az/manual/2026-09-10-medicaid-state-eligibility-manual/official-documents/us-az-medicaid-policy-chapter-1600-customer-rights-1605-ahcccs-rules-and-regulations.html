1605 AHCCCS Rules and Regulations

 

Policy

Descriptions of the federal and state authorities governing the operation of the AHCCCS programs are provided in MA105 of this manual. Internet links to many of the authorities listed are also provided. Copies of these authorities may also be found in public libraries and law libraries.

Upon request, AHCCCS will provide copies of sections of the authorities that are cited on AHCCCS letters and sections this eligibility policy manual to the customer or the customer’s representative.
