D Income Received on Behalf of Another Person Example

 

Program

Manual Section

ALTCS Only

MA604.B

 

The customer is an ALTCS recipient with a Long-Term Care (LTC) flat rate insurance policy that would be making payments, but the customer has not submitted the insurance claim. The payments are considered constructively received for ALTCS purposes. The payments are considered income just as if the customer had received them.
