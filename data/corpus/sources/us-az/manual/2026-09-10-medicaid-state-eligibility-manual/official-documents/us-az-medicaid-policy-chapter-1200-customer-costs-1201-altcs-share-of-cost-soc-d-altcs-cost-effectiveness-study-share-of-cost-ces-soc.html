D ALTCS Cost Effectiveness Study Share of Cost (CES SOC)

 

 

Revised 03/30/2018

Policy

ALTCS Health Plans cannot by law pay more for a person’s HCBS than they would pay for that same person in a nursing facility, except for a very short amount of time.

The Cost Effectiveness Study Share of Cost (CES SOC) gives the ALTCS Health Plan a figure to use in determining if providing HCBS to a customer is cost effective.

The ALTCS Health Plan may advise the person to consider other alternatives or options if the cost of the HCBS needed is more than the ALTCS Health Plan would be allowed to pay if the customer were in a nursing facility.
