Developmental Disabilities

 

Overview

The Developmental Disabilities page tells whether the customer has or had a DD qualifying diagnosis and gives the DD history.  The fields on this page are automatically populated by the system.  

When the DD diagnosis question is answered “Yes”, the DD Case Status is also shown.  Click the Focus Response button to view the DD Case Status report.  Any changes to the DD status will be listed in the DD history.  The DD history includes the following information:

  • DD status

    1. Not DD – the customer does not have a DD status

    2. DD – the customer has or had a DD status

    3. Potential DD – the customer is potentially eligible for DDD.

    4. DD in NF – the customer has or had a DD status while in a skilled nursing facility.

  • Begin date – the date the DD Status began.

  • End date – when a customer has a change to DD Status, this is the date the previous DD Status ended.

NOTE     It is very important to make sure the DD status is correct before completing the PAS.
