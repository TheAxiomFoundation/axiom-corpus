C-1431 Whole Monthly Allotments by Household Size

Revision 25-4; Effective Oct. 1, 2025

SNAP Allotment Chart (PDF)

The shaded portions on the table in this section show monthly, not prorated allotments available to categorically eligible households. This can be $1 or more.

The minimum monthly, not prorated Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) allotment for a one-or two-person household is $24.
