TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MCManual Change #72

 

MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  April 1, 2025

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy and clarify existing policy.  

 

Highlights of these policy revisions are described below.  If you have any questions, contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

 

410(4)     Verification

 

  • Changed Child Support Services Division (CSSD) to Child Support Enforcement Division (CSED).
  • Revised section for clarity

 

410(5)E     Withdrawing an Application

 

  • Clarified that APAAdult Public Assistance application withdrawal requests can be done with a recorded signature through the VCCVirtual Contact Center.

 

423     Alaska Residency

 

  • Clarified when caseworkers should consider Alaska residency questionable.

 

433(4)8     Certain Employed ABLEAchieving a Better Life Experience Account Beneficiaries May Make Additional Contributions

 

  • Clarified the amount of additional contributions can be made for certain employed ABLEAchieving a Better Life Experience account beneficiaries.

 

460(6)A(2)     Support Payments          

 

  • Changed Child Support Services Division (CSSD) to Child Support Enforcement Division (CSED).

 

Addendum 2     The Leave and Earnings Statement

 

  • Changed Child Support Services Division (CSSD) to Child Support Enforcement Division (CSED).
