TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MCManual Change #71

 

MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  December 2, 2024

 

This year Social Security announced a 2.5% Cost of Living Allowance (COLACost of Living Allowance) increase effective January 2025. We have incorporated the 2025 standards into the examples and Addendum 1. The COLACost of Living Allowance adjustment also causes:

 

  • An increase in the income limit for Substantial Gainful Activity; and
  • An increase in the student earned income exclusion.

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy and clarify existing policy.  

 

Highlights of these policy revision are described below.  If you have any questions, contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.
