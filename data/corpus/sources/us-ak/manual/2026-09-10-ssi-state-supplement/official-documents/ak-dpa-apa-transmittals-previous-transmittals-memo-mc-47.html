MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  July1, 2016

 

We are making a couple of changes to clarify existing policy and to correct an oversight.  

 

Highlights to policy revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact Policy and Program Development Team at dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

441-2 C.           DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS PAYMENTS

 

  • Clarified what is needs based and what is not needs based income from Veteran's Administration.

 

452-2 A.           INELIGIBLE SPOUSE'S INCOME EQUAL OR LESS THAN THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN SSI STANDARDS

 

  • Fixed the example from $356 to $367 (the difference between the SSI payment standard for an eligible couple and an eligible individual); i.e. $1100 - $733 = $367.
