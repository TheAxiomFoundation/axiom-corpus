TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #49

 

MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  April 3, 2017

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy, clarify existing policy, and align policies within our program policy manuals. Other changes are either technical in nature or clarifications that are the result of policy questions or suggestions from the field.

 

Highlights of these policy revision are described below.  If you have any questions, contact the Policy and Program Development Team at dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

 

426-2 F.       CONTACTING THE MEDICAL REVIEWER

 

  • Added a note to write "terminal", when appropriate, on the Interim Assistance paperwork when sending to the Medical Reviewer

 

433-3         PERMANENT FUND DIVIDEND

 

  • Deleted a reference to the PFD Hold Harmless manual because it no longer exists

 

433-4         ABLE ACCOUNTS

 

  • Clarified that housing distribution from an ABLE account only is counted as a resource the month after the month of receipt
