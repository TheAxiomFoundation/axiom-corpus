STATE OF ALASKA

Department of Health and Social Services

Division of Public Assistance

 

 

TRANSMITTAL NUMBER: MC #64

 

MANUAL: Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE: April 1, 2022

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy, clarifying existing policy, and align policies within our policy manuals. Other changes are either technical in nature or clarifications that are the result of policy questions or suggestions from the field.

 

Highlights of these policy revisions are described below. If you have any questions, contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.

 

 

410-4            Verification

 

  • Clarified that SVESState Verification Exchange System must always be checked at application and recertification, and death match alerts that are generated during the certification period must be processed within 10 days.

  • Clarified that BENDEXBeneficiary and Earnings Data Exchange should be checked only if it appears the applicant or recipient could be eligible for Social Security benefits.

 

426-2F        Contacting the Medical Reviewer

 

  • Updated email address for IAInterim Assistance Reviewer from DPAInterim.Assistance@alaska.gov to hss.dpa.interim.assist@alaska.gov.

 

426-7(D)      Not Eligible for SSISupplemental Security Income Due to Not Meeting Disability Criteria

 

  • Clarified that we do not terminate IAInterim Assistance benefits if a recipient has their SSISupplemental Security Income claim denied for not being disabled.  As a recipient, we give them 30 days to provide verification that they are appealing their Social Security denial.  If they provide verification that they have appealed the SSISupplemental Security Income decision, IAInterim Assistance benefits continue during the appeal process.
  • Added a link to APA MS 426-5.
