TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MCManual Change #67

 

MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  April 3, 2023

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy and clarify existing policy.  

 

Highlights of these policy revision are described below.  If you have any questions, contact the Policy and Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

420-2(B)       Time Limit for Pursuit of SSISupplemental Security Income Benefits

 

  • Clarified that APAAdult Public Assistance applicants have 30 days from the interview date to be in pay status with SSISupplemental Security Income.
  • Clarified that for APAAdult Public Assistance recipients, benefits continue until the Social Security Administration makes a decision, if they provide proof of an SSISupplemental Security Income application.

 

433-4           ABLEAchieving a Better Life Experience Accounts 

 

  • Updated 2023 Max Allowable Additional Contributions for certain employed ABLEAchieving a Better Life Experience account beneficiaries.

 

441-2L        Support and Alimony Payments

 

  • Clarified that child support is only countable to an APAAdult Public Assistance recipient if the child no longer lives with the APAAdult Public Assistance recipient.

 

452-12      Retroactive APAAdult Public Assistance Benefit Calculations

 

  • Added information regarding APAAdult Public Assistance underpayments when IAInterim Assistance is converted to APAAdult Public Assistance.
