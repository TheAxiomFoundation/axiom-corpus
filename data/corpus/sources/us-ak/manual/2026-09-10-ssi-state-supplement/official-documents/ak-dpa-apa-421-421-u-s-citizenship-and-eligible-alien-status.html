421           U.S. CITIZENSHIP AND ELIGIBLE ALIEN STATUS

 

To receive APAAdult Public Assistance, a person must be:

 

  • A United States citizen or U.S. National;
     
  • A qualified alien, as described in section 421-2.  Qualified aliens may be subject to limited eligibility as explained in section 421-4;
     
  • A Native American born in Canada or Mexico who has treaty rights to cross the U.S./Canada and U.S./Mexico borders, as described in section 421-3.

 

Refer to MS 460-2B for policy related to sponsored aliens.
