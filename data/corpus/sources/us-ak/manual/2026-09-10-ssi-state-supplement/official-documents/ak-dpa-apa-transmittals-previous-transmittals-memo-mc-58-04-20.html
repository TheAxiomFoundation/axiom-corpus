STATE OF ALASKA

Department of Health and Social Services

Division of Public Assistance

 

 

TRANSMITTAL NUMBER: MC #58

 

MANUAL: Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE: April 1, 2020

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy, clarifying existing policy, and align policies within our policy manuals. Other changes are either technical in nature or clarifications that are the result of policy questions or suggestions from the field.

 

Highlights of these policy revisions are described below. If you have any questions, contact the Policy and Program Development Team at dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

410-2A      WHAT IS AN ACCEPTABLE APPLICATION FORM

 

  • Added that Senior Benefits will accept the GEN 72 to determine continued eligibility. This change is effective with the April 2020 form revision.

 

410-2C      WHEN IS AN APPLICATION NOT REQUIRED

 

  • Revised section to remove automatic APAAdult Public Assistance eligibility without application for an SSISupplemental Security Income recipient turning 18 years of age. This change is being done to match regulations.

  • Renumbered section

 

410-3A      WHEN IS AN INTERVIEW REQUIRED

 

  • Added that interviews are required during initial application and at reviews

 

423-3      APPLICANTS RECEIVING ASSISTANCE FROM OTHER STATES

 

  • Added note to clarify when an application must be pended due to an out of state address showing with the Social Security Administration

 

425-2A      TEMPORARY DISABILITY OR BLINDNESS OR DISABILITY DECISIONS

 

  • Updated section to match federal regulations allowing a licensed advanced practice registered nurse or licensed physician assistant within their licensed scope of practice to determine disability

 

425-2C      SSA BLINDNESS OR DISABILITY DECISIONS

 

  • Added note to clarity that APAAdult Public Assistance applications are not pended for proof of appeal if the SSASocial Security Administration denies due to no disability

 

426-2      ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS FOR INTERIM ASSISTANCE

 

  • Added note to clarify when to authorize an IAInterim Assistance case

 

426-2C(2)      BLIND APA APPLICANTS REQUIRED TO SUBMIT AN AB 3 FORM

 

  • Updated section to match federal regulations allowing a licensed advanced practice registered nurse or licensed physician assistant within their licensed scope of practice to determine disability

 

426-2C(3)      DISABLED APA APPLICANTS REQUIRED SUBMIT AN AD 2 FORM

 

  • Updated section to match federal regulations allowing a licensed advanced practice registered nurse or licensed physician assistant within their licensed scope of practice to determine disability

 

430-1      RESOURCES

 

  • Added note to clarify that client statement is allowed regarding resources unless questionable. Cases that involve a trust still require expenditure review.

 

432-2C      BURIAL SPACE EXCLUSION

 

  • Revised section for clarity

  • Renamed subsection to Burial Space Exclusion

  • Added an example showing what expenses shown on a burial contract would be considered burial space and which would be considered burial funds

 

432-2D      BURIAL FUNDS EXCLUSION

 

  • Revised section for clarity

  • Renamed subsection title to Burial Funds Exclusion

  • Renumbered list within the subsection

 

433-4(1)      ABLE ACCOUNTS

 

  • Added 2020 Max Allowable contribution amount of $15,000

 

433-4(8)      CERTAIN EMPLOYED ABLE ACCOUNT BENEFICIARIES MAY MAKE ADDITIONAL CONTRIBUTIONS

 

  • Added a table for yearly additional savings for certain employed account holders

 

442-2G      IMPAIRMENT RELATED WORK EXPENSES FOR DISABLED

 

  • Added note to clarify when IRWEImpairment Related Work Expensess can be excluded from earned income and when a disability decision is needed

 

442-2I      WORK EXPENSES FOR THE BLIND

 

  • Added note to clarify when blind work expenses can be excluded from earned income and when a blindness decision is needed

  • Updated mileage link

 

480-1D      INTERVIEW REQUIREMENTS

 

  • Clarified that an interview must be done for APAAdult Public Assistance and APAAdult Public Assistance related Medicaid reviews
