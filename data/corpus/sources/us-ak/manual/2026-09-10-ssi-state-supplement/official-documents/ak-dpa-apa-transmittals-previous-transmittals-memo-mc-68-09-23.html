MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  September 1, 2023

 

We are making several changes to incorporate new policy and clarify existing policy.  

 

Highlights of these policy revision are described below.  If you have any questions, contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

 

410-3(B)       Who Must Participate in the Interview

 

  • Clarified that if legally married, only one spouse needs to participate in the interview

 

426-7           Converting Interim Assistance

 

  • Added a link to EISEligibility Information System Procedure 2012-1

 

Addendum 3   Countable and Excluded Income for Adult Public Assistance   

 

  • Added Needs Based Income, such as Senior Benefits
  • Added a link to APAAdult Public Assistance MS 442-3(B)
  • Added VADepartment of Veterans Affairs Disability Payments
