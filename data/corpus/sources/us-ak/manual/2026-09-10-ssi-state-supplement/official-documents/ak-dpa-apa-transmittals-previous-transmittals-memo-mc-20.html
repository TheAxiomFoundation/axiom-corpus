TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #20

 

MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE:  December 1, 2008

 

 

Each year the Adult Public Assistance need and maximum payment standards are adjusted by the same percentage as the COLACost of Living Allowance increase in Social Security benefits.  This year the COLACost of Living Allowance increase is 5.8%.  We have incorporated the 2009 standards into the examples and Addendum 1.  These standards become effective with the benefit month of January 2009.  The COLACost of Living Allowance adjustment also causes:

 

  • An increase in the income limit for Substantial Gainful Activity; and

  • An increase in the student earned income exclusion.

 

We made some language changes to the interview section of the Application Process chapter in conjunction with the other program manuals.  We aligned policy about Native Restricted Lands with other programs to the extent possible.  We also clarified several policy issues requested by field staff.

 

Highlights of these policy revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at 465-3382 or email dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

MS 410-3E  Conducting Interviews

  • Revises language that incorrectly implied an applicant had to come into a DPA Office for an interview.

 

MS 410-3F  Changes Reported During the Interview

  • Revises language that incorrectly implied an applicant had to come into a DPA Office for an interview.

 

MS 425-2A  Temporary Disability or Blindness Decisions

  • Clarifies that chiropractors and osteopaths are considered to be proper medical providers for a temporary disability decision; however psychologists are not.

 

MS 426-2C  Interim Blindness or Disability Determination

  • Adds a cross reference in subsection 3. to new subsection 4.

  • Adds a new subsection 4. that provides instructions on how to complete the Authorization for Release of Protected Health Information (Gen 150) form.

 

MS 432-1E  Native Restricted Land

  • Revises the section to clarify policy and describe what is meant by "Native Restricted Deed."

  • Clarifies that any house or related structure that is permanently attached to excluded ANCSAAlaska Native Claims Settlement Act land is also excluded from resources.

  • Changes the ”Verification" note to more clearly describe the requirement to verify Native Restricted Deed status.

 

MS 432-4L  Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act ( ANCSA ) Assets

  • Clarifies that any house or related structure that is permanently attached to excluded ANCSAAlaska Native Claims Settlement Act land is also excluded from resources.

 

MS 433-1  Transfer of Resources

  • Updates examples to reflect the 2009 need and maximum payment standards.

 

MS 442-4  The Order In Which Income Exclusions Are Applied

  • Clarifies the IRWEImpairment Related Work Expenses section to inform Eligibility Technicians that they have to manually deduct an IRWEImpairment Related Work Expenses from the adjusted gross earned income on the APASAPA Authorization Screen screen in order to have the payment amount figured correctly.

 

MS 452  Payments

  • Updates examples to reflect the 2009 need and maximum payment standards.

 

MS 460-5  Income Allocations

  • Updates examples and standards to reflect the 2009 need and maximum payment standards.

 

Addendum 1  Program Standards

  • Updates program standards to reflect the COLACost of Living Allowance changes for 2009.
