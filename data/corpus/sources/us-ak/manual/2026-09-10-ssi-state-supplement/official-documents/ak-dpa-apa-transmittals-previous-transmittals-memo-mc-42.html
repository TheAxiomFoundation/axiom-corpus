TRANSMITTAL NUMBER: MCManual Change  #42

 

MANUAL: Adult Public Assistance

 

DATE: January 2, 2015

 

 

This year Social Security announced a Cost of Living Allowance (COLACost of Living Allowance) increase effective January 2015. The Adult Public Assistance need and maximum payment standards are adjusted by the same percentage as the COLACost of Living Allowance  increase in Social Security benefits. This year the COLACost of Living Allowance  increase is 1.7%. We have incorporated the 2015 standards into the examples and Addendum 1. These standards become effective with the benefit month of January 2015. The COLACost of Living Allowance  adjustment also causes:

 

    • An increase in the income limit for Substantial Gainful Activity; and

    • An increase in the student earned income exclusion.

 

Highlights of these policy revisions are described below. If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at 465-3382 or email dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

MS 433-1 Transfer of Resources

  • Updates examples to reflect the 2015 need and maximum payment standards.

 

MS 433-2 A Eligibility for Conditional Benefits

  • Updates the SSISupplemental Security Income  payment standard to the new 2015 amount in order to accurately calculate if an applicant is eligible for conditional benefits.

 

MS 452 Payments

  • Updates examples to reflect the 2015 need and maximum payment standards.

 

MS 460-5 Income Allocations

  • Updates examples and standards to reflect the 2015 need and maximum payment standards.

 

Addendum 1 Program Standards

  • Updates program standards to reflect the COLACost of Living Allowance  changes for 2015.
