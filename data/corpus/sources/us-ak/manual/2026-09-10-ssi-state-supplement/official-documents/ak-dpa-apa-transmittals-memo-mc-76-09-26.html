TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:  MCManual Change #76

 

MANUAL:  Adult Public Assistance

 

Date:  September 1, 2026

 

Beginning July 4, 2025 the 2025 Reconcilliation Bill (H.R. 1) was signed into law.  This law contains several provisions that affect the non citizen requirements of the Medicaid program that are effective October 1, 2026.  APAAdult Public Assistance and SSISupplemental Security Income recipients will no longer be automatically eligible for Medicaid due to the non citizen requirements that are effective October 1, 2026.

 

Highlights of these and other policy revisions are described below.  If you have questions, contact the Policy and Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

MS 400-1      AN OVERVIEW OF THE ADULT PUBLIC ASSISTANCE PROGRAM

 

  • Revised section to reflect APAAdult Public Assistance recipients must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements as an eligibility factor for Medicaid effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 410-4      VERIFICATION

 

  • Added a note regarding the new death match and prisoner match notices

 

MS 410-7 C      ELIGIBILITY FOR MEDICAID

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements as an eligibility factor for Medicaid effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 421-6 A      VERIFICATION OF U.S. CITIZENSHIP IS NOT REQUIRED FOR

 

  • Revised note to remove automatic Medicaid eligibility for APAAdult Public Assistance recipients due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 425-2 C      SSASocial Security Administration BLINDNESS OR DISABILITY DECISIONS

 

  • Revised section to remove automatic Medicaid eligibility for APAAdult Public Assistance recipients due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 425-3 C      BLIND OR DISABLED APPLICANTS WHO DO NOT APPEAR ELIGIBLE FOR SSISupplemental Security Income AND HAVE NOT BEEN DETERMINED ELIGIBLE FOR SSASocial Security Administration DISABILITY

 

  • Revised section to reflect APAAdult Public Assistance recipients must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements, due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 426-7 C      NOT ELIGIBLE FOR SSISupplemental Security Income SOLELY BECAUSE OF EXCESS INCOME

 

  • Revised section to reflect the individual meets all other factors of the Medicaid program. Due to Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 433-4 (3)      ABLEAchieving a Better Life Experience ACCOUNTS

 

  • Revised section to reflect Medicaid alien requirements as an eligibility factor for Medicaid effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 480-1 B      REVIEWS FOR SSISupplemental Security Income RECIPIENTS

 

  • Revised section to reflect Medicaid may continue if Medicaid eligibility requirements are still met due to Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026

 

MS 480-5 A      SUSPENSION

 

  • Revised section to reflect Medicaid may continue if Medicaid eligibility requirements are still met due to Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026
