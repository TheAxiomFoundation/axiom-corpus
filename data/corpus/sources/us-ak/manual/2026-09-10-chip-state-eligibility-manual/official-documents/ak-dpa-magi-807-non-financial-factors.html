807            NON-FINANCIAL FACTORS OF ELIGIBILITY

 

The following factors of eligibility generally apply to all Medicaid categories unless there is a specific exception provided.

 

General non-financial factors of eligibility include:

 

1.  U.S.United States Citizenship or Qualified Alien status (section 808);

 

2.  Alaska Residency (section 809)

 

3.  Social Security Enumeration (section 810)

 

4.  Assignment of Rights (section 811)

 

5.  Cooperation with Child Support (section 812)
