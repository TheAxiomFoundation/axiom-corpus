TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #73

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  September 1, 2026

 

Beginning July 4, 2025 the 2025 Reconciliation Bill (H.R. 1) was signed into law.  This law contains several provisions that affect the non citizen requirements of the Medicaid program that are effective October 1, 2026. 

 

Highlights of these and other policy revisions are described below.  If you have any questions, contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES 

 

500(A)      Applicable Eligibility Categories

 

  • Revised section to remove automatic Medicaid eligibility for APAAdult Public Assistance and SSISupplemental Security Income recipients due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.
  • Added note to reflect individuals must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

501(A)      Issuing Medicaid to Eligible Recipients

 

  • Revised section to reflect new process and new design of Medicaid cards. 

 

511 B(1)      Who is Not Required to Cooperate 

 

  • Revised postpartum period from 60 days to 12 months to align with postpartum period change.

 

520(C)      Proof of United States Citizenship and Qualified Alien Status

 

  • Removed reference to APAAdult Public Assistance MS 421, added reference to MAGI MS 808, due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

522      Medicaid Exceptions to APAAdult Public Assistance Eligibility Policy

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

522(A)      Special Non-Immigrant Groups

 

  • Removed reference to APAAdult Public Assistance MS 421, added reference to MAGI MS 808-2 and MAGI MS 808-4(D) due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

522(E)      Renewal Requirements

 

  • Revised section to remove automatic Medicaid eligibility for SSISupplemental Security Income recipients due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

530      SSISupplemental Security Income and APAAdult Public Assistance Related Categories

 

  • Revised section to remove automatic Medicaid eligibility for SSISupplemental Security Income and APAAdult Public Assistance recipients due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.
  • Added note to reflect SSISupplemental Security Income and APAAdult Public Assistance recipients and applicants must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

532      SSISupplemental Security Income or APAAdult Public Assistance Ineligible for Reasons that Do Not Apply to Medicaid.

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

533(A)      TEFRATax Equity and Fiscal Responsibility Act, P.L. 97-248 Eligibility

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

540      Grandfathered Eligibility Categories

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

550      Eligibility Categories for an Individual Living in an Institution

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

576(A)      Definitions

 

  • Removed reference to APAAdult Public Assistance MS 421-6, added reference to MAGI MS 808-5 and MAGI MS 808-4 due to the Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.

 

580      Medicare Savings Programs

 

  • Added note to reflect individuals must also meet Medicaid qualified alien requirements effective October 1st, 2026.
