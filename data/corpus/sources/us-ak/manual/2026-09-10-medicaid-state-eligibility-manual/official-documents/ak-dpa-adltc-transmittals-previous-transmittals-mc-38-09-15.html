OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

514 D        PENAL INSTITUTION

 

  • Added clarification that an incarcerated individual may be eligible for Medicaid for hospital stays over 24 hours.

 

520 E        PENAL INSTITUTION

 

  • Added clarification that a SSA disability determination trumps a DDS disability determination.

 

ADDENDUM 2  

 

  • Added Incarcerated subtypes and corrected QM and SL resource limits.
