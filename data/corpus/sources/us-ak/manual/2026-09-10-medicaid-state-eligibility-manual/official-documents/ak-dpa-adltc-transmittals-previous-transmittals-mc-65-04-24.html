TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #65

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  April 1, 2024

 

We have updated the 2024 Federal Poverty Level guidelines for income eligibility effective April 1, 2024. We have also updated other sections that use Federal Poverty Level guidelines for income eligibility.

 

This manual change also consists of several technical changes or clarifications as a result of suggestions from the field. Highlights of these revisions are described below. 

 

If you have any questions, please contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov.
