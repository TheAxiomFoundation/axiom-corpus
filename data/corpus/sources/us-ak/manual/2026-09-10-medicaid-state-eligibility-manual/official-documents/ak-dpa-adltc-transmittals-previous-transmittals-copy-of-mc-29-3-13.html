TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #29

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  March 8, 2013

 

We have updated the Program Standards and Pickle Amendment Chart for 2013.  We have also updated the chapter on TEFRA cases to more accurately reflect the current process.

 

Highlights of these revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at 465-3382 or e-mail dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

MS 506 O  SOCIAL SECURITY OFFICES

  • Corrected contact information for the Juneau Social Security Office

 

MS 533      DISABLED CHILDREN AT HOME (TEFRATax Equity and Fiscal Responsibility Act, P.L. 97-248)

  • Updated chapter to more accurately reflect the current TEFRATax Equity and Fiscal Responsibility Act, P.L. 97-248 process.

 

MS 534 WORKING DISABLED MEDICAID BUY-IN

  • Updated examples with 2013 FPGFederal Poverty Guidelines amounts and APAAdult Public Assistance Standards.

 

MS 560 E  HCB WAIVER REFERRALS AND INTERAGENCY COMMUNICATIONS

  • Updated section to clarify DSDS waiver denials are sent to the LTC Unit, not the LTC Coordinator.

 

MS 575  BREAST AND CERVICAL CANCER MEDICAID

  • Updated application address to remove Breast and Cervical Cancer Coordinator and replace with LTC Unit as there is no longer a coordinator for this program.

 

ADDENDUM 1  NEEDS STANDARDS

  • Updated Needs Standards tables for current FPGFederal Poverty Guidelines

  • Updated Pickle Amendment table.
