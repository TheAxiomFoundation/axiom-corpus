TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #58

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  December 1, 2021

 

This year Social Security announced a Cost of Living Allowance (COLACost of Living Allowance) increase effective January 2022.  We have updated Addendum 1 and the examples throughout the manual with the following items to reflect the 5.9% increase for 2022:  

 

  • An increase to the Long Term Care community spouse resource and income allowance and household member standards;
  • An increase to the Excess Home Equity resources maximum;
  • An increase to the Special Long Term Care Income Standard;
  • Changes in Medicare Part A and B premiums; and
  • Changes in Medicare Part D premium and coverage information, including changes to the Low-Income Subsidy program

 

This manual change also consists of several small technical changes or clarifications as a result of suggestions from the field. Highlights of these revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at e-mail dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

512      SOCIAL SECURITY ENUMERATION

 

  • Renamed section to align with MAGIModified Adjusted Gross Income Medicaid Manual
  • Added link to new Addendum 9 for the notice template when they must request a SSNSocial Security Number from the SSASocial Security Administration

 

520 A    APPLICATION AND REVIEW PROCESS

 

  • Revised section for clarity
  • Clarified that telephonically recorded application signatures are accepted through the Virtual Call Center (VCC)
  • Revised note to clarify that for paper applications, the signature must either be telephonically recorded by a caseworker through the VCCVirtual Call Center or a "wet signature"

 

522 A   SPECIAL NON-IMMIGRANT GROUPS

 

  • Clarified that although a COFACompact of Free Association migrant is exempt from the five-year waiting period, if their status changes to a qualified alien as outlined in APA MS 421-2, the five-year waiting period will apply unless they meet an exemption listed in APA MS 421-4(D).

 

554 D    LOOK BACK PERIOD

 

  • Updated example due to COLACost of Living Allowance change

 

570 C    INDIVIDUALS NOT SUBJECT TO COST-OF-CARE

 

  • Clarified that individuals receiving Medicaid in the Refused Cash category are not responsible for cost-of-care

 

570 E    INDIVIDUALS NOT SUBJECT TO COST-OF-CARE

 

  • Updated ATAPAlaska Temporary Assistance Program ANIAdult Not Included standard due to COLACost of Living Allowance change

 

570 H    NURSING HOME EXAMPLES

 

  • Updated example due to COLACost of Living Allowance change

 

570 J     HOME AND COMMUNITY BASED WAIVER CASE EXAMPLES

 

  • Updated example due to COLACost of Living Allowance change

 

580       MEDICARE SAVINGS PROGRAMS

 

  • Updated QMBQualified Medicare Beneficiaries and SLMBSpecified Low Income Medicare Beneficiaries resource limits

 

ADDENDUM 1

 

  • Updated standards due to COLACost of Living Allowance change
  • Updated Medicare Part A, Part B, and Part D premiums for 2022
  • Updated 2022 Spousal Impoverishment Standards

 

ADDENDUM 2

 

  • Changed terminology to "ineligible non-immigrants" for Medicaid Subtypes IL and II due to COFACompact of Free Association migrants

 

ADDENDUM 9

 

  • Added a new addendum for the social security enumeration request letter template
