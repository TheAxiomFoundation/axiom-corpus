TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #55

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  December 1, 2020

 

This year Social Security announced a Cost of Living Allowance (COLACost of Living Allowance) increase effective January 2021.  We have updated Addendum 1 and the examples throughout the manual with the following items to reflect the 1.3% increase for 2021:  

 

  • An increase to the Long Term Care community spouse resource and income allowance and household member standards;
  • An increase to the Excess Home Equity resources maximum;
  • An increase to the Special Long Term Care Income Standard;
  • Changes in Medicare Part A and B premiums; and
  • Changes in Medicare Part D premium and coverage information, including changes to the Low-Income Subsidy program

 

This manual change also consists of several small technical changes or clarifications as a result of suggestions from the field. Highlights of these revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at e-mail dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

517        Fair Hearings

 

  • Added a new chapter to match federal regulations that allow a verbal fair hearing request for Medicaid.

 

518        Alien Sponsor Income

 

  • Added a new chapter to clarify Alien Sponsor Income deeming.

 

520(F)   Verification and Documentation

 

  • Added a link to APA MS 410-4 for further clarification on verification.

 

520(I)    Reserved

 

  • Revised section as reserved due to fair hearing policy is no longer the same as APA.  See ADLTC 517.

 

523(B)   Reserved

 

  • Revised to reserved due to information moved Alien Sponsor Income to new chapter ADLTC 518.

 

524(A)   Reserved

 

  • Revised to reserved due to information moved Alien Sponsor Income to new chapter ADLTC 518.

 

532(C)   Alien Sponsor Deeming

 

  • Removed outdated section and moved Alien Sponsor Income to new chapter ADLTC 518.
  • Renumbered section.

 

554(D)     Look Back Period

 

  • Updated example due to COLACost of Living Allowance increase.

 

570(A)      Definitions

 

  • Added note clarifying that Native Dividend income over $2,000 is countable, even if deposited to a trust.

 

570(C)       Individuals Not Subject to Cost of Care

 

  • Clarified that SSI and APA recipients are not responsible for cost of care.

 

570(H)     Nursing Home Examples

 

  • Updated example due to COLACost of Living Allowance increase. 

 

570(J)      Home and Community Based Waiver Case Example

 

  • Updated example due to COLACost of Living Allowance increase.

 

580          Medicare Savings Programs

 

  • Added a note that incarcerated individuals are not eligible for Medicare Savings Programs.
  • Updated the QMBQualified Medicare Beneficiaries and SLMBSpecified Low Income Medicare Beneficiaries resource limits.
  • Updated LIS resource limits and coverage information.

 

Addendum 1

 

  • Updated standards due to COLACost of Living Allowance change
  • Updated Medicare Part A, Part B, and Part D premiums for 2021
  • Updated 2021 Spousal Impoverishment Standards
  • Updated QMBQualified Medicare Beneficiaries and SLMBSpecified Low Income Medicare Beneficiaries resource limits
