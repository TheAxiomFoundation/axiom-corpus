MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  January 7, 2013

 

 

The resource limit for the Low Income Subsidy Program typically increases each January when Social Security announces their COLA increase.  This year, we did not receive the new resource limits for LIS until after the manual was posted.  The ADLTC manual has now been updated to reflect the correct resource limits.

 

Highlights of these revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at 465-3382 or e-mail dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

MS 580 D  Low-Income Subsidy (LIS) Program

  • Updated with current resource limits for the Low-Income Subsidy Program

 

Addendum 6 Medicare Part D Low Income Subsidy Guide

  • Updated the guide with the 2013 resource limits.
