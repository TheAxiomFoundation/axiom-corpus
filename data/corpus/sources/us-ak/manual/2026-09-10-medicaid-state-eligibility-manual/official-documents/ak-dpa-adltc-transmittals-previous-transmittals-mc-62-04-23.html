TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #62

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  April 3, 2023

 

We have updated the 2023 Federal Poverty Level guidelines for income eligibility effective April 1, 2023. We have also updated other sections that use Federal Poverty Level guidelines for income eligibility.

 

This manual change also consists of several technical changes or clarifications as a result of suggestions from the field. Highlights of these revisions are described below. 

 

If you have any questions, please contact the Policy & Program Development Team at hss.dpa.policy@alaska.gov. 

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

500     Overview of Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid

 

  • Changed Alaska's fiscal agent from Conduent to Health Management Systems. 

 

501(A)(4)     Manual Benefit Issuance

 

  • Changed Alaska's fiscal agent from Conduent to Health Management Systems. 

 

513(B)     Temporary Absence and Residents Residing Out of State

 

  • Changed Alaska's fiscal agent from Conduent to Health Management Systems.

 

514(G)     Children in Treatment

 

  • Changed Alaska's fiscal agent from Conduent to Health Management Systems. 

 

520(A)     Application and Review Process

 

  • Removed reference to application processing time frame of 30 days.

 

522(B)     Actions taken on the Application 

 

  • Added new subsection regarding actions taken on the application.
  • Changed section title from Medicaid Exception to APAAdult Public Assistance citizenship Requirements to Medicaid Exceptions to APAAdult Public Assistance Eligibility Policy.

 

534(K)     Example of an Eligibility Determination

 

  • Updated examples with 2023 Federal Poverty Level Changes.

 

534(M)     Example of an Eligibility Determination With a Medicaid Qualifying Trust

 

  • Updated examples with 2023 Federal Poverty Level Changes. 

 

570   Post Eligibility Treatment of Income (Cost of Care)

 

  • Added new subsection regarding notice requirements.

 

580      Medicare Savings Program

 

  • Updated QMBQualified Medicare Beneficiaries and SLMBSpecified Low Income Medicare Beneficiaries resource limits.
