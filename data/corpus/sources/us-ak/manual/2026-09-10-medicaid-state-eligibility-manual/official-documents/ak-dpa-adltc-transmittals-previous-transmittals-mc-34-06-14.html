TRANSMITTAL NUMBER:   MC #34

 

MANUAL:  Aged, Disabled and Long Term Care Medicaid Eligibility Manual

 

DATE:  June 2, 2014

 

We have updated the ADLTC manual to include several small changes.  Highlights of these revisions are described below.  If you have any questions please contact the Policy and Program Development Team at 465-3382 or e-mail dpapolicy@alaska.gov.

 

OVERVIEW OF CHANGES

 

520-D         DEVELOPMENT OF INCOME

 

  • Adds a note to explain that if a client is potentially eligible for income from UIB, early retirement or retirement they must pursue it.

 

554-B        VERIFICATION AND DOCUMENTATION

 

  • Adds that we can accept client statement for bank accounts unless questionable.

 

562-A         PROCESSING HCB HOME AND COMMUNITY BASED WAIVER CASES FOR A CHILD RECEIVING ANY MAGI MEDICAID

 

  •  Deleted the reference to DKC, Denali Kidcare, Family Medicaid or Under 21 Medicaid.

 

562-B         PROCESSING HCB WAIVER CASES WHEN A DDS DETERMINATION IS PENDING

 

  • Adds a step to send notice N011 requesting a MED3 form.
