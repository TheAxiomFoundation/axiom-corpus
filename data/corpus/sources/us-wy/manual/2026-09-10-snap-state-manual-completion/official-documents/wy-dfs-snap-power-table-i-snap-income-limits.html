SNAP Income Allotments and Deductions

October 1, 2026 – September 30, 2027

The maximum allowable gross and net income standards for determining eligibility of assistance units and the maximum allotments authorized appear below.

  • The standard deduction is $217 for AU sizes of 1-3, AU size of 4 is $229, AU size of 5 is $268, and  AU size of 6 and above is $308.
  • The standard medical deduction is $175 (for deductions in excess of $35). 
  • The maximum excess shelter cost for nonelderly/nondisabled assistance units is $769.
  • The utilities only standard is $352. (two (2) utilities; not heating/cooling). 
  • Wyoming’s Standard Utility Allowance (SUA) is $528. 
  • The telephone only allowance is $59. 
  • The Homeless Shelter Deduction is $205.66.
  • The earned income deduction is 20%. 
  • The minimum monthly SNAP benefit for categorically eligible assistance units of one (1) or two (2) persons is $25.
  • Mileage reimbursement rate is $0.725 cents per mile. (implemented 2/12/2026).

October 1, 2025 – September 30, 2026

The maximum allowable gross and net income standards for determining eligibility of assistance units and the maximum allotments authorized appear below.

  • The standard deduction is $209 for AU sizes of 1-3, AU size of 4 is $223, AU size of 5 is  $261, and  AU size of 6 and above is $299.
  • The standard medical deduction is $175 (for deductions in excess of $35).
  • The maximum excess shelter cost for nonelderly/nondisabled assistance units is $744.
  • The utilities only standard is $340. (two (2) utilities; not heating/cooling).
  • Wyoming’s Standard Utility Allowance (SUA) is $510.
  • The telephone only allowance is $57.
  • The Homeless Shelter Deduction is $198.99
  • The earned income deduction is 20%.
  • The minimum monthly SNAP benefit for categorically eligible assistance units of one (1) or two (2) persons is $24
  • Mileage reimbursement rate is $0.725 cents per mile. (implemented 2/12/2026)
