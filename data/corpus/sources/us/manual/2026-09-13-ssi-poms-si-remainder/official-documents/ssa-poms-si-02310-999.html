2

Numeric

THS Dependency Code

2

Numeric

Standard Dependency Codes will be used except for the following: For Parents DIC-(91) Sole Surviving Parent (rate chart); (92) One of Two Parents with No Spouse (rate chart). For Apportionment Awards-(10) Spouse; (81) Children; and (50 or 60) Parents. Fields will always be populated with a value. Note: THS Dep Code=How Many Dependents the Beneficiary is Actually Being Paid For.
