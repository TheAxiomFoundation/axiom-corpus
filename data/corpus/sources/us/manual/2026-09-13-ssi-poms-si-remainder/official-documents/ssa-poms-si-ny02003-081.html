SI NY02003.081 New Jersey General Assistance Agency Codes (RTN 420 – 12/2009)

A. General Assistance

Beginning in mid 1997, the State of New Jersey began reorganizing their Interim Assistance Reimbursement (IAR) procedures. They started to change the IAR payment locations from the municipalities to the 21 counties; each county receives the grant reimbursement (GR) for many municipalities. Below is an updated list of the current New Jersey grant reimbursement codes (GRC).

NOTE: SSA is in the process of automating IAR. The implementation is currently scheduled for 2010. The implementation of the new electronic IAR process (eIAR) will allow state\county entities to receive IAR data via a secure website. State\county employees will review initial claims allowances or PE events on the GRCs they are associated with and post interim assistance data. SSI recipients will automatically receive notices.
