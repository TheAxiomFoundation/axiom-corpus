138.7 Hearing Aid Batteries and Repairs

A grant for the actual cost of hearing aid batteries, repairs or service is available to TANF or SBP recipients.  

 55 Pa. Code § 175.23

If the need is continuing, the CAO may include the actual minimum cost in computing the regular monthly assistance payment or the allowance may be provided as an OTI.
