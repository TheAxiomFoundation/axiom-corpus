123.4 Homeless Clients

Homeless individuals must verify that they are living in Pennsylvania if it is questionable. See Section 123.6, Verification. Homeless individuals are not required to have a permanent address or fixed residence. They are required to provide a mailing address where the budget group can receive benefits and CAO correspondence. The mailing address should be one where mail will not be lost or stolen. It can be any of the following:

  • ·A friend or relative’s address;

  • A post office box;

  • A cooperating social service agency's address;

  • The CAO's address if no other appropriate address is available.         

 55 Pa. Code § 147.23(a)
