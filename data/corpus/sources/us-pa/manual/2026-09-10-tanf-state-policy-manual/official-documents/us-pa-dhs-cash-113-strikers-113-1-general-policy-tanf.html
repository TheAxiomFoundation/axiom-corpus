113.1 General Policy

The entire budget group  is ineligible for the whole month if one of the following budget group members is on strike on the last calendar day of the month:

55 Pa. Code § 141.41(c)

      

  • The biological parent

  • The adoptive parent

  • The sole TANF dependent child

If another member of the budget group is on strike, only that individual is ineligible for the month.
