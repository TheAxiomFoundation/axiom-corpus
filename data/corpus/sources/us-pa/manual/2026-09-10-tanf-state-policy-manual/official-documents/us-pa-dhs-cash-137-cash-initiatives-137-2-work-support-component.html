137.2 Definitions

The following acronyms and terms are used in this chapter:

  • ELRC—Early Learning Resource Center

  • CDC—Career Development Component

  • CWDS—Commonwealth Workforce Development System

  • DRA—Deficit Reduction Act of 2005

  • EDBC—Eligibility Determination/Benefit Calculation

  • MEDA—Medical Eligibility Determination Automation

  • PSC—Program Status Code

  • TCA—Transitional Cash Assistance

  • WER—Work Expense Reimbursement

  • WED—Work Expense Deduction

  • WPR—Work Participation Rate
