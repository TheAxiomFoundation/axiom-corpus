123.1 General Policy

To be eligible for benefits, individual must verify that they are a current resident of Pennsylvania. The individual can have more than one residence address.          

Exception: Under some conditions, nonresidents may receive temporary Cash Assistance. See Section 123.9.      

 55 Pa. Code § 147.23(e)

NOTE: A victim of domestic violence may receive mail at an alternate address, such as the CAO address or at a legal substitute mailing address from the Address Confidentiality Program (ACP). See Chapter 108.1021.
