179.4 When a New Application is Required

A new application is required if TANF benefits were denied or closed for any of the following reasons:

  • The budget group’s income was over the income limit at the time of the review for eligibility unless the income was unverified and the budget group subsequently provides verification they are within income limits.

  • The budget group’s resources were over the resource limit at the time of the review for eligibility unless the resources were unverified and the budget group subsequently provides verification they are within resource limits.

NOTE: A new application is required if there is anyone new who wants to apply for Cash Assistance. See Section 104.15.
