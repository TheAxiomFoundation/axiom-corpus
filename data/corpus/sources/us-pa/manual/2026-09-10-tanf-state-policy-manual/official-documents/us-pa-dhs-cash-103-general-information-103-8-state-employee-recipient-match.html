103.8 State Employee/Recipient Match

The Office of Income Maintenance (OIM) headquarters compares the Central Management Information Center files with the eCIS  files. When a match is found, a case folder is prepared. The folder includes the following materials:

  • Copies of the State Employee Match Report

  • SNAP and Cash Assistance transcripts

  • Payroll history

The Area Manager must give the folder to the Executive Director.
