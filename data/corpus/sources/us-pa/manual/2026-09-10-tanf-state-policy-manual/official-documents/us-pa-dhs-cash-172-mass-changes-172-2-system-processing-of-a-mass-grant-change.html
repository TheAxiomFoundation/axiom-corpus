172.2 System Processing of a Mass Grant Change

The new FSA will be applied automatically to each open budget that is receiving a grant based on the maximum FSA.

This includes:

  • Zero cash grants;

  • Budgets not receiving a payment due to the $10 minimum payment provision;

  • Budgets receiving less than the full FSA due to the adjustment of income; and

  • Budgets receiving more than the full FSA due to receipt of a recurring special items allowance.

The system will increase the payment for the appropriate date and provide the person with notification of the change.
