138.6 Clothing and Personal Care Items

The CAO will provide an allowance for clothing and personal care items for a TANF recipient who is to be admitted to a rehabilitation center or residential school if the following conditions are met:  

  55 Pa. Code § 175.23

  • The recipient does not have the initial supply of specified clothing and personal care items; and

  • The items are required as a condition of admission.

If the above conditions are met, the CAO will authorize an OTI for the total cost of the required items as specified in Appendix D.

NOTE: If the facility requires more than one unit of a particular item, the CAO will multiply the listed price by the number of units required.
