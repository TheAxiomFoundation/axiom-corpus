168.6 Redetermination

The County Assistance Office (CAO) will complete a prospective determination of need if the redetermination indicates a change in budget group income or circumstances that may affect eligibility.

   55 Pa. Code § 183.12

Any income expected at redetermination will be used to determine eligibility and payment amount for the next semiannual (six month) or eligibility period. 

Example: At the redetermination interview in February, Ms. Plum reports a new job. The CAO verifies the employment and that the first pay is due March 6. Ms. Plum’s expected wages are used to determine eligibility and payment amount.
