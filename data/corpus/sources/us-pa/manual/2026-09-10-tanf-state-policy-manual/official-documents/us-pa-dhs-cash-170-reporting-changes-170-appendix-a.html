Appendix A: Cash Assistance Reporting Requirements

Changes must be reported by the 10th of the month following the month of the change.

Cash Budgets Not in SAR

Cash budgets not in SAR must report the following:

  • Receipt of a lump sum amount

  • An increase in current gross unearned income of more than $50 a month

  • Receipt of new unearned income

  • A job start

Once notified of a change in income, a plan to verify the income is made according to Section 170.5.

Cash Budgets in SAR

Cash budgets in SAR must report the following:

  • Receipt of new unearned income

  • Receipt of a lump sum amount

  • An increase in gross earned income of more than $100 a month for budgets with known earned income

  • An increase in gross unearned income of more than $50 a month

  • A job start for budgets with no earned income

  • A change in employment that results in an increase in income of more than $100 a month

Once notified of a change in income, a plan to verify the income is made according to Section 170.5.

All Cash Assistance Budgets

All Cash Assistance budgets must report the following:

  • A change in living arrangements

  • A change of address

  • A change in household composition

  • A change in marital status

  • A change in real property ownership

  • A change in resource ownership

  • A change in student status if category or income exemption is affected

  • A change in residence

  • Pregnancy

  • A change in medical insurance coverage

  • A change in the need for special items

  • A change of address or circumstances of absent parents that would affect support if the change is known to the recipient.
