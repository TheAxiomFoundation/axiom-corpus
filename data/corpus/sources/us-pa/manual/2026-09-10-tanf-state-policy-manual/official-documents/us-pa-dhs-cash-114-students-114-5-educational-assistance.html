114.5 Educational Assistance

Educational assistance received by TANF eligible students from loans, grants, scholarships, and work-study income does not count as income or as a resource.

NOTE: Income from an assistantship is not excluded for Cash Assistance. Assistantship income is countable unless the college or university confirms that the assistantship position is work-study.

The following are examples of proof of educational assistance:

  • An award letter

  • A statement from the person or organization giving the loan, grant, or scholarship

  • A statement from the school financial aid office

 

55 Pa. Code § 177.21(a)(13)
