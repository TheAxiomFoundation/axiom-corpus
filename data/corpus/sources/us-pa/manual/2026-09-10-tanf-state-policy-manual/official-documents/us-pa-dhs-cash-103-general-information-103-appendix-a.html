Appendix A: Nondiscrimination Notice

COMMONWEALTH OF PENNSYLVANIA
DEPARTMENT OF HUMAN SERVICES

THIS FACILITY IS OPERATED IN
ACCORDANCE WITH THE REGULATIONS OF
THE PENNSYLVANIA DEPARTMENT OF HUMAN SERVICES

The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services complies with and enforces the laws and regulations (Title VI and Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, the Age Discrimination Act of 1975, the Pennsylvania Human Relations Act of 1955, as amended, Executive Order 11246, Executive Order 1972-1, and to applicable agencies Governor's Affirmative Action Program as outlined in Executive Order 1984-1) which prohibit discrimination against persons because of their race, color, religious creed, life style, handicap, ancestry, national origin, union membership, age, or sex.

All facilities operated directly, regulated, approved, contracted for, or licensed by the Pennsylvania Department Of Human Services to provide services, must provide those services to all persons without discrimination.

Any complaints of discrimination should be directed to the Bureau of Equal Opportunity at 717-783-1130.
