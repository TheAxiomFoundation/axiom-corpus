171.3 Reviewing the Semiannual Reporting Form

The CAO must review each SAR form to make sure the client reported the following information for all persons in the budget group:

  • Changes in budget group composition.

  • Employment.

  • Earned income.

  • Dependent care costs.

  • Unearned income.

  • Address.

  • Changes in resources.

The client must attach verification of the following:

  • Gross earned income.

  • Dependent care payments.

NOTE: If there is no verification provided of child support paid for children outside the household or payments for child care or care of a sick or disabled person, there are no deductions given for those expenses.

  • Gross unearned income only if there was a change in the amount received.

  • New address.

  • Employment begin, change, or end.

The CAO must review each SAR form to determine whether it was received by the due date and is complete. The CAO must track each timely SAR form as either complete or incomplete in the CFSR system. If the SAR form is tracked incomplete, the CAO must data-enter a code so that  the missing information is identified on the L/IN when it is generated.

If the CAO tracks the SAR form as timely and complete, benefits are issued on the Regular Cash Issuance Date.

 

171.31 Timely Semiannual Reporting Form

Consider the SAR form or L/IN timely if the CAO receives it on or before the Extended Due Date.

NOTE: If the CAO receives the SAR form after the Extended Due Date but it is postmarked before the Extended Due Date, the CAO must approve Good Cause due to delay by the postal service. Keep the envelope in the case record to document the Good Cause decision.

 

171.32 Complete Semiannual Reporting Form

The SAR form is complete if all of the following requirements are met:

  • All required questions are answered.

  • The form is signed by the payment name.

  • Acceptable verification of gross earned income is attached.

  • Acceptable verification of a change in employment is attached.

  • Acceptable verification of a change in gross unearned income is attached.

NOTE: The CAO must scan or copy and save pay stubs and/or employer statements used to determine eligibility and grant amount at application, renewal, SAR review, or any time new earned income information is reported. Verification of actual hours worked is required to complete the Federal TANF Reports.

NOTE: If a client fails to answer only the question related to dependent care expenses and/or fails to verify those expenses, the CAO must track the SAR form or L/IN complete. Determine eligibility and payment amount without dependent care deductions.

 

171.33 Incomplete Semiannual Reporting Form

The SAR form is incomplete when:

  • Any question except dependent care or child support paid for children outside the household is not answered;

  • Verification is not attached; or

  • The SAR form is unsigned.

 

171.34 Incomplete vs. Incorrect Semiannual Reporting Form

The CAO may receive a SAR form that is timely and complete but includes information the CAO knows is incorrect.

Do not track an incorrect SAR form as incomplete. A SAR form is complete if all the questions are answered, it is signed by the payment name, and the necessary verification is attached. When a client submits a timely and complete SAR form, benefits are based on information given on the form.

A SAR form is incorrect if:

  • It is complete in all respects; and

  • It includes information that the CAO knows is wrong.

The client may have misunderstood a question and provided a wrong answer, making the information incorrect.

Examples:

  • The client submits four pay stubs incorrectly because he or she indicated the pay end date instead of the pay receipt date.

  • The client indicated no unearned income but the CAO has documentation or verification that the client receives regular Social Security benefits.

 

171.35 Processing Incorrect SAR Forms and L/INS

The CAO must track an incorrect SAR form as complete if it is timely and complete. The CAO must then contact the client to resolve the issue and record the contact in the case record. If the client cannot be contacted or information cannot be obtained, provide an Advance Notice before taking adverse action.

The L/IN has the same distinction between incomplete and incorrect and the same processing of incorrect forms as the SAR.

 

171.36 Good Cause

Good Cause must be established within 30 days of the Extended Due Date, regardless of whether the information is provided on the SAR form or the L/IN. See Section 171.5. If the system suspended benefits for failure to submit a SAR form and the client claims Good Cause, the CAO must reinstate benefits as soon as Good Cause is established.

If the case is closed and Good Cause is established within the required 30-day period, reopen benefits without an application.

The client may claim Good Cause verbally, on the SAR form, on the L/IN, or in writing. The CAO must review and determine Good Cause.

NOTE: Good Cause exists when the SAR form is postmarked before the Extended Due Date but the CAO received it after that date. Keep the postmarked envelope in the case record to provide documentation and to prevent Quality Control errors.

The CAO must evaluate the Good Cause claim. Good Cause includes, but is not limited to, the following:

  • Circumstances beyond the client’s control, such as serious illness, death, accident, or mail delays caused by the postal service.

  • Illiteracy, language problems, physical or mental handicap, or similar ongoing circumstances.

NOTE: The CAO must help a client complete and file the SAR form if he or she was granted Good Cause for these reasons. The CAO may refer the client to a community resource, relative, friend, neighbor, or agency for help in completing the form. Record the referral in the case record. The CAO must not review the claim’s validity again unless the community resource becomes unavailable.

The CAO must determine if Good Cause exists. Supervisory approval is required if Good Cause is found. The supervisor must either support or deny the claim of Good Cause. The decision must be recorded in the case record.

After termination, if a client does not meet Good Cause conditions within the required 30-day period and wants Cash Assistance benefits, he or she must reapply.

 

171.37 Replacement Forms

The CAO must provide a replacement SAR form to any client who asks for one.

The CAO must take the following actions:

  • On the CIS MAIN MENU, select CLIENT NOTICES (CQCNMN) from INQUIRY MENUS.

  • On Line 2 – CLIENT NOTICE SUMMARY (CQCNSM), enter the county number, record number, and appropriate category, if known (optional). Case notice summary will be displayed.

  • The SAR notice is identified by Notice number 746, Option 1 for all categories. Notate the appropriate notice ID number.

  • On the OIM HOME PAGE, go to CAO RESOURCES and select “CIS Client Notice Reprint.”

  • Click on “Notice Reprint.” Enter Notice ID number. Click on “Find Notice.” The SAR form will be displayed.

  • Print the SAR form and give it or mail it to the client with a postage-paid envelope.

NOTE: See Appendix D, SAR Critical-Dates Charts and Terminology.

 

171.38 Penalties

The assistance payment may be late when the client submits an untimely complete SAR form or submits a complete L/IN by the Extended Due Date. The CAO must determine eligibility based on the information on the SAR form. If the client is found eligible, the budget is reinstated.

NOTE: If the SAR form has pay stubs but no verification of dependent care expenses attached, the CAO must allow the earned income disregard and the incentive with no dependent care deductions.

 

171.39 Notification of the Changes in Benefits

The CAO must notify the client of eligibility or payment amount change using a Confirming Notice if the change is due to information reported on the SAR form or L/IN.
