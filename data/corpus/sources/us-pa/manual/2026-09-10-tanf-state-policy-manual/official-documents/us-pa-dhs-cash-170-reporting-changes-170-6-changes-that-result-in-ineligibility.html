170.6 Changes that Result in Ineligibility

The CAO will explore all Cash Assistance, Medical Assistance or SNAP benefits or services at application, at renewal or whenever any benefit is stopped. Determine eligibility or ineligibility for other available benefits before the effective date of any current benefit change or termination.

See the Medical Assistance Eligibility Handbook, Chapter 338, Medicaid Benefits for information about extended medical benefits after closing a cash budget.
