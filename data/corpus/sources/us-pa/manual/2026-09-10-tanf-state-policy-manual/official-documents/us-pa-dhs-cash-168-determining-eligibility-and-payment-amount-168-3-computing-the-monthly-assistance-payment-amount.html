168.3 Computing the Monthly Assistance Payment Amount

The Family Size Allowance (FSA) in Chapter 168, Appendix B, is the maximum monthly assistance payment that can be granted to a budget group to meet their basic needs.  

   55 Pa. Code § 175.21

A budget group with no countable income or no net income after allowable deductions will receive the maximum FSA.

If the budget group with countable income passes the net test, the County Assistance Office (CAO) will determine the monthly payment by deducting the total net income from the budget group's FSA.    

    55 Pa. Code § 183.101(d)

 

NOTE: Special allowances for supportive services related to work or education are not added to the FSA in determining eligibility or payment amount. The net income is budgeted prospectively. See Chapter 167, Prospective Budgeting.

In eCIS, a budget group is identified by the category and the grant group (GG) data field. The budget group is usually entered on the system as one grant group with one benefit issued as two semimonthly payments for that budget group. See Chapter 180, Issuing Benefits, Appendix A, for a schedule of benefit issuance dates.

The CAO will compute the payment amount using these steps: 

1.  Compute net income from the calendar month according to Section 168.211.

2.  Subtract the budget group’s net income from the maximum FSA for the budget group to determine the monthly assistance payment.

3.  Round the monthly payment amount down to the next whole dollar.     

 55 Pa. Code § 183.107

4.  Divide the rounded monthly assistance payment by two to determine the semimonthly benefit amount. 

 

 

168.31 Minimum Payment Amount

The budget group will not receive a payment if the monthly assistance payment amount is less than $10. This applies to ongoing eligibility as well as to the first month of eligibility when the monthly payment amount prorates to less than $10. The budget group is considered an open budget for all other purposes, such as issuance of the Pennsylvania EBT ACCESS card, Special Allowances for Supportive Services (SPAL), or semiannual reporting form. The CAO will data enter all information, but a payment will not be generated.    

  55 Pa. Code § 183.108 

 

Exception: If the monthly assistance payment is reduced to less than $10 by recoupment, the budget group will receive a payment even though it is less than the minimum payment amount.
