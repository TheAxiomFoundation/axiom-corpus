127.11 TANF

To be eligible for TANF, a child who meets TANF eligibility requirements must be living with a caretaker or relative who qualifies as a specified relative.

An eligible specified relative may also get TANF. See Section 127.2 for requirements.    

  55 Pa. Code § 151.41

NOTE: TANF eligibility includes the specified relative of an SSI child who meets age and deprivation requirements.
