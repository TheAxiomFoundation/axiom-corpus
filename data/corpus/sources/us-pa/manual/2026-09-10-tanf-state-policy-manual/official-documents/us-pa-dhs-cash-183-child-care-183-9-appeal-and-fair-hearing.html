183.9 Appeal and Fair Hearing

The cash assistance provisions in the Supplemental Handbook, Chapter 870, Appeal and Fair Hearing, apply to any action or proposed action to be taken by the CAO on determining child care eligibility or ineligibility of an applicant or recipient.                          

55 Pa. Code § 168.101
