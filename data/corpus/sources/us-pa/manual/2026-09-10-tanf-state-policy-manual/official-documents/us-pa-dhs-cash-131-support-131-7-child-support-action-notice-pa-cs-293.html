131.7 Child Support Action Notice, PA/CS 293

An automated interface provides information to the Pennsylvania Child Support Enforcement System (PACSES) for: 

  • Case closure

  • Transferring a case to another county

NOTE: The receiving CAO must authorize an Electronic Verification for Support Services PA/CS 643 to be generated when an inter-county transfer case is received.

  • Suspending a case for one month due to increased income that is expected to last for only one month

  • Removing a child for whom support is being paid and/or owed by the LRR from a budget group/case record, or transferred to another budget group/case recordTTT T.

The Child Support Action Notice PA/CS 293 is system generated when any of the above actions are completed.
