108.5 Assistance for Clients in Immediate Danger

If the individual is in immediate danger:     

  55 Pa. Code § 108.6

  • Provide a private space to allow the individual to call a domestic violence hotline if requested. Ask the CAO director for help if no private space is available.

  • The police should not be called unless the victim/survivor makes the call or requests the call be made or the abuser poses a danger to others at the CAO.

  • Offer the individual assistance in making any arrangements related to emergency shelter, medical care, transportation, childcare, and work.

Provide Universal Notifications if necessary and applicable; “Community Resources for Supportive Services, You have a right to be Safe”, (CM 305) brochure, and “Community Resources for Supportive Services, You have a right to be Safe”, (Pub 388) palm card, (see Appendix A).
