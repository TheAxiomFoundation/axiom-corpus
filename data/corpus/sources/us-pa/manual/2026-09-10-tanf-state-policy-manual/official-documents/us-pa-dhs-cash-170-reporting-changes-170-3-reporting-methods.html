170.3 Reporting Methods

An individual is responsible for reporting changes by phone, by mail, in person, through their COMPASS account, the MyCOMPASS PA mobile app, or according to a plan arranged by the CAO.

The CAO will give the Rights and Responsibilities page from the PA 600 to the individual at application and at renewal. The CAO may also give the individual a copy of Appendix A, showing what changes must be reported. The PA 600 and Appendix A explain the responsibility to report changes and list the types of changes that should be reported.        

55 Pa. Code § 125.24(d)(2)
