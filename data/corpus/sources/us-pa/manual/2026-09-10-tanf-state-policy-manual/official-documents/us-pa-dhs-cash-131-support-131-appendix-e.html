Appendix E: Bureau of Child Support Enforcement (BCSE) Area Offices

BCSE can assist the CAO with questions regarding the following topics:

  • General support information
  • Alert information
  • Support referrals
  • Good cause
  • Support pass-through
  • Client compliance or non-cooperation
  • Acknowledgement of Paternity and the Paternity Tracking System (PTS)
  • Assignment of support and payments to the Department

If the CAO needs assistance, they should contact the appropriate Area Office using the email addresses listed below.

NOTE: BCSE field offices are not secured facilities. To protect employees, most DRS do not take walk-in appointments.

 

For general questions, individuals can be referred to the PA Child Support website at https://www.humanservices.state.pa.us/csws/.

The CAO can access recent history through the Child Support History screen in eCIS under the History tab. Income Maintenance Caseworker Supervisors (IMCWS) can access older child support information via ePortal via WebTS.
