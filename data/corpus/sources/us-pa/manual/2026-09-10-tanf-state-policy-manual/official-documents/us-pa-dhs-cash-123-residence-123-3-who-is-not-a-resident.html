123.3 Who Is Not a Resident

123.31 TANF

An individual is not a resident if they live in Pennsylvania for a temporary purpose, such as a visit or to attend school, and plans to leave when the purpose ends. See Section 123.9, Temporary Assistance for Nonresidents.
