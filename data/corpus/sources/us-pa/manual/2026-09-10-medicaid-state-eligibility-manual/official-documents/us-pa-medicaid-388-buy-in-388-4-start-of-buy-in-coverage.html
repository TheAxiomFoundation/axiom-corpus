388.4 Buy-In Begins

The CAO sets the beginning date for Buy-In of Part A and Part B as follows:

 

  • QMBs eligible for Healthy Horizons cost-sharing benefits – Buy-In eligibility begins the first day of the month following the month in which MA was authorized. There is no retroactive coverage under this program.

 

Example: Client is authorized PG00 effective 3/1/18. Buy-In should be authorized effective 04/2018.

            

  • QMBs eligible for Healthy Horizons categorically needy benefits – If the individual is already eligible for Medicare, Buy-In eligibility begins in the month that Healthy Horizons eligibility begins. Retroactive Buy-In coverage may go back up to three months prior to the date of application.

 

NOTE: If an individual becomes eligible for Medicare after Healthy Horizons eligibility begins, Buy-In eligibility begins in the month the individual becomes eligible for Medicare.

 

Example: Client was authorized PH00 effective 10/1/17. Exchange 3 indicates Part A and/or Part B entitlement date 03/2018. Buy-In should begin 03/2018.  

 

  • SSI recipients – Buy-In eligibility begins in the month that Medicare eligibility begins. The SSA will auto-accrete SSI recipients when they become eligible for Medicare at age 65.

 

NOTE: If the SSA has not enrolled the individual in Buy-In after three months of Medicare eligibility, the CAO will process a manual accrete to enroll the individual. To confirm Buy-In enrollment, the CAO must set an alert to check Exchange 7. The alert should be set for three months after the date of Medicare eligibility.

 

  • Special SSI recipients—If NMP is approved for the month after the SSI closure, Buy-In eligibility begins immediately with no break in coverage. If NMP eligibility is approved later, Buy-In eligibility begins the month that NMP is authorized.

 

  • TANF recipients –  Buy in eligibility begins in the first month that the individual is eligible for both Medicare and TANF cash assistance and is a resident of Pennsylvania.

 

  • Transitional Medical Assistance recipients—An individual who loses eligibility for TANF cash assistance, but remains eligible for extended NMP benefits, also remains eligible for Buy-In. When the TMA budget is opened, Buy-In eligibility begins immediately, with no break in coverage. If TMA eligibility is approved later, Buy-In eligibility begins in the month that TMA is authorized. 

  

  • Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiaries (SLMBs)—Retroactive Part B Buy-In coverage may be authorized up to three months prior to the date of application. SLMBs are eligible for payment of the Medicare Part B premiums only. The CAO will use the manual accrete process to enroll SLMBs for past periods of Buy-In eligibility. (See Section 388.62, Manual Accrete.)

  • Qualifying Individuals (QIs)—Retroactive Part B Buy-In coverage may be authorized up to three months prior to the date of application. QIs are eligible for payment of the Medicare Part B premium only. The CAO will use the manual accrete process to enroll QIs for past periods of eligibility. (See Section 388.62, Manual Accrete.)

  • Qualified Disabled Working Individuals (QDWIs) – Buy-In eligibility begins in the first month that the individual is identified by the SSA as a QDWI. QDWIs are eligible for payment of the Medicare Part A premium only.
