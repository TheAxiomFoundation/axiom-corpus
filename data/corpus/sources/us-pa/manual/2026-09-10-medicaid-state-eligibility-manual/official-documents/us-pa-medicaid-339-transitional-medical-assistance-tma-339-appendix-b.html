Appendix B:Transitional Medical Assistance (TMA) Notice Reason/Options

Below are a list of Reason and Option Codes that can be issued for TMA:

 

  • 977 – Option A (Eligible): Eligible MG71 (12 Month Period)

  • 977 – Option B (Eligible): Eligible MG23 (4 Month Period)

  • 977 – Option C (Discontinue): End of 12 Month Period (MG71)

  • 977 – Option D (Discontinue): End of 4 Month Period (MG23)

  • 977 – Option E (Eligible): Eligible MG71 (Returning to MG71)

  • 977 – Option F (Discontinue): Dependent Child No Longer in Household (MG23 & MG71)

  • 977 – Option G (Discontinue): Household Not Maintained Employment (MG71 – Second 6 Months)

  • 977 – Option H (Discontinue): Household Income Exceeds 185% FPIG (MG71 – Second 6 Months)

 

Full text of the notices can be found in eCIS in the “Text Search” tab under “Correspondence” or “Corr”.
