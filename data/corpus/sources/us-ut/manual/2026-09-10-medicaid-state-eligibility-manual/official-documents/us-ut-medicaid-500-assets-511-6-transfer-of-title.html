511-6 Transfer of Title

No Previous Policy

 

Vehicles - including motor vehicles, trailers, etc.

 

Generally, consider a vehicle available to the applicant or recipient if the vehicle is registered with Motor Vehicles to the applicant or recipient.  However, in the state of Utah, the owner of a vehicle can transfer the title by signing the title over to the new owner.  The title does not have to be notarized.  The new owner does not have to have the vehicle registered with the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) for the transfer to be considered complete.

 

Ideally, the original owner should notify DMV that the vehicle is no longer owned by him or her and have the registration canceled.  However, this is not necessary since a legal transfer is completed when the original owner signs the title over to the new owner.

 

If the applicant or recipient disputes owning a vehicle registered to him or her, ask for verification that the vehicle has been sold or ownership transferred.  If the applicant or recipient provides verification that the vehicle has been sold or ownership has been transferred, do not count the vehicle as an asset.  Verification may include a copy of the signed title, a bill of sale, or other legal document as proof that the vehicle has been sold or transferred.  If transfer is complete, legal ownership is with the new owner.

 

If the applicant or recipient cannot provide verification that the vehicle has been sold or transferred, consider the vehicle available to the original owner.

 

 

All Other Property with a Title Document

 

 

When the individual states property has been sold, but the title document has not been transferred, contact the program specialist to determine the availability of the property.  Send all documents related to the property and the transfer.  Be sure to include any conditions attached to the transfer.

 

If the program specialist determines that the asset is not available even though the title has not been transferred, the asset has no countable value.  If the program specialist determines that the asset is available until the title has actually been transferred, count the equity value of the asset.
