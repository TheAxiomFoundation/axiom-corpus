Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

601 Health Plan Selection and Education

Effective Date: July 1, 2018

No Previous Policy (The following sections combined to create 601: 226, 226-1, 226-2, 226-3, 603-5 )

 

A.   Medicaid Education

1.    The Health Program Representative (HPR) provides Medicaid education and help the members to understand Medicaid benefits and how to appropriately use the Medicaid card.

2.    HPRs provide information on Medicaid-covered services, including primary care, specialist and mental health services.

3.    Any questions about Medicaid programs or covered benefits should be referred to the HPR at 1-866-608-9422. 

o   Additional information can be found online at https://medicaid.utah.gov/medicaid-education-materials.

B.   Health/Dental Plan Selection Requirement

Applicants who live in certain counties are required to enroll in a health and dental plan. This includes individuals eligible for a home and community-based waiver program:

1.    Medical and Dental Plans (Managed Care Organizations) are contracted with the Department of Health and Human Services.

2.    Members who are required to select a Medical and or Dental Plan, can do so by contacting a Health Program Representative with the Department of Health and Human Services.

3.    Members that are required to enroll in a health and dental plan will receive a plan specific member handbook from their selected plans.  The Member Handbook includes an explanation of covered benefits and a provider.

C.     Making Changes in the Health or Dental Plan Selection

Under certain circumstances, a member may change their health or dental plan outside of the annual open enrollment period.  Enrollees must contact the HPR with any specific requests at 1-866-608-9422.
