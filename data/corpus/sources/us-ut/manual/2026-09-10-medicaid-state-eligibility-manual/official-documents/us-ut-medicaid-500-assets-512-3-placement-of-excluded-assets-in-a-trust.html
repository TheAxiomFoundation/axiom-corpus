Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

512-3 Placement Of Excluded Assets In A Trust

Effective Date: April 1, 2004

No Previous Policy

 

Excluded assets that are placed in a trust remain excluded even if the trust is treated as available.

Home of an Institutionalized individual

Transfer of an institutionalized person’s home to a trust makes the home countable unless the home could be excluded under 521-1.
