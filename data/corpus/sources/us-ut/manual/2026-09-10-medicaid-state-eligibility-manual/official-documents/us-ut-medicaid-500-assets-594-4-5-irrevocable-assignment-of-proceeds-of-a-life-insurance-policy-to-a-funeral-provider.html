594-4.5 Irrevocable Assignment of Proceeds of a Life Insurance Policy to a Funeral Provider

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

Insurance companies usually do not permit the irrevocable assignment of proceeds without an irrevocable assignment of ownership.

 

If you receive a copy of a policy that has an irrevocable assignment of the proceeds but the individual retains ownership, ask the individual to provide verification from the insurance company of the assignment of proceeds and the ownership.

If the company confirms that ownership has been revocably assigned, follow the policy in 594-4.3.

If the company confirms that the proceeds have been revocably assigned follow the policy in 594-4.3.

If the company confirms that the individual irrevocably assigned ownership, follow the policy in 594-4.4.

If the company confirms that the individual irrevocably assigned proceeds and the individual retains ownership of the policy, follow the rules below:

Burial Spaces

Do not apply the burial space exclusion to the cash value of the life insurance policy.  The provider has not received any payment; therefore, the individual has not purchased any burial spaces.  The provider is not obligated to provide any burial spaces until the individual dies.

Burial Funds

The asset value of the pre-need funeral contract is equal to the cash value of the life insurance policy.

 

If the face value of all life insurance policies on the individual’s life is $1,500 or less, exclude the cash value under the life insurance exclusion (521-16 D.1, ABD Medicaid).  Reduce the burial fund exclusion by the face value of the policy.

If the face value of all life insurance policies exceeds $1,500, apply the policy for burial fund exclusions (592) to the cash value of the policy.
