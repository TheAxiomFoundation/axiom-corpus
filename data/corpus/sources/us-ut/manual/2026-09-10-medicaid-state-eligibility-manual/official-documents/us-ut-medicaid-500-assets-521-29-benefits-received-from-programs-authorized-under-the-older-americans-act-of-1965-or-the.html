Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-29 Benefits Received from Programs Authorized under the Older American’s Act of 1965 or the Domestic Volunteers Services Act

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

A.   Do not count as a resource, payments that are not wages received under programs such as Senior Community Service, Retired Senior Volunteers program, Foster Grandparent Program, or payments or reimbursements made to AmeriCorps*VISTA volunteers, Senior Health Aides, SCORE, Senior Companions and ACE, if such payments are retained beyond the month of receipt. Interest earned on retained funds from these programs is excluded from income, but not from resources.

B.   Aged, Blind, Disabled and Long-term Care Medicaid.  As of 9/1/2008, do not count as a resource any retained payments received from AmeriCorps State and National or AmeriCorps National Civilian Community Corps (NCCC). See sec. 419-1 for income policy.)

C.    Medically Needy Family, Child and Pregnant Woman Medicaid.  Retained funds received from AmeriCorps State and National or AmeriCorps National Civilian Community Corps (NCCC) are not excluded from resources.
