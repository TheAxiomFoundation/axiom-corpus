402-6 Targeted Adult Medicaid (TAM) Household Composition

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

No Previous Policy

 

 

TAM Household Composition

The TAM program follows the MAGI methodology to determine the adult’s household composition (401-1). Section 346 explains the other program requirements. The household size is used to determine if the countable income falls within the program income limit for the individual (Table VII). 

 

The following adults qualify for the TAM program if they meet the program requirements stated in 346 and 440-1 through 440-5.

 

·       Adults:  A single adult or two spouses in the home can be included in the household size.

 

o    TAM households may not include a child. If the household contains a child under 19, consider the adult for PCR (345) or Adult Expansion (348) coverage.
