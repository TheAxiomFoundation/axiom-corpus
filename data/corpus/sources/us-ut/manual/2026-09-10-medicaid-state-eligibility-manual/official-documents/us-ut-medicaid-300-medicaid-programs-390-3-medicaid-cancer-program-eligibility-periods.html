Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

390-3 Medicaid Cancer Program Eligibility Periods

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

 Retroactive Coverage

Eligibility for retroactive coverage can begin the third month before the application month, but no earlier than the month in which the individual is screened for breast and/or cervical cancer.  CDC must either do the screening and diagnosis or review the screening and diagnosis, and confirm the need for treatment of breast and/or cervical cancer.  (See 390-1)

 Eligibility Period

1.   For an individual diagnosed with a pre-cancerous condition, the eligibility period can be approved for up to 3 months starting with the month the screening is completed.

2.   For an individual diagnosed with breast or cervical cancer the eligibility period can be up to 12 months.

3.   An individual is not limited to one period of eligibility.  Eligibility can be renewed at the annual  or 3 month review.  (See 390-4 for review requirements.)

4.   If an individual’s Cancer program closes, they may reapply and receive a new period of eligibility and coverage if they have been screened and determined by the CDC program to need treatment of breast or cervical cancer, or a pre-cancerous condition and meets all other eligibility criteria.
