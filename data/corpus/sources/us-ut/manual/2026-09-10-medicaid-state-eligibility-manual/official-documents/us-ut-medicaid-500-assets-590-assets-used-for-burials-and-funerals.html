Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

590 Assets Used for Burials and Funerals

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

This section explains the Medicaid eligibility rules for counting or excluding assets that individuals own or set aside to pay for burial spaces and burial services.   These assets include:

• Burial spaces (591)

• Burial funds (592)

• Pre-need funeral contracts (594)

• Burial trusts (596)
