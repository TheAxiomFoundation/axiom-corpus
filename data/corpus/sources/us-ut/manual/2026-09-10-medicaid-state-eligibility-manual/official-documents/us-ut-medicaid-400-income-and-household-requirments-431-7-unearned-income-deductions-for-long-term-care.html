Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

431-7 Unearned Income Deductions for Long-term Care

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (431-7 was previously found in section 409-8 until November 1, 2021)

 

1. Do not deduct anything from unearned income of individuals who are residents of medical institutions. Institutionalized members receive deductions for personal needs (431-8), health insurance premiums (431-1), and medical bills (460).  If an institutionalized person is married and his or her spouse is not institutionalized or receiving services under a Home and Community Based Care waiver, the institutionalized person may be able to contribute some income to the spouse (431-9) and dependent family members (431-10).

 

2.  For HCB Waiver members see the appropriate waiver sections for policy on deductions.

 

3. Non-institutionalized and non-waiver members receive a $20 deduction from unearned income.  (See Section 431-5.)
