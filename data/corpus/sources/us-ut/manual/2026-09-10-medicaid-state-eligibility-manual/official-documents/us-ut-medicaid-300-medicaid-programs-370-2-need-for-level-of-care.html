Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

370-2 Need for Level of Care

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

All nursing home residents must meet a nursing home level of care or Medicaid will not pay for that care.  The nursing home is responsible for submitting the necessary forms to the Department of Health and Human Services for approval.
