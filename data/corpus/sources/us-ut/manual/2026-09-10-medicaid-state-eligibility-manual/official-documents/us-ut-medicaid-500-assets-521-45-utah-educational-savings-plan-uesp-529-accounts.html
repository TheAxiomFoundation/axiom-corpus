Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-45 Utah Educational Savings Plan (UESP) 529 Accounts

Effective Date: July 1, 2017

 

No Previous Policy

 

 Definition

UESP 529 account is a way to save money to use for educational expenses. The account must have an owner and a designated beneficiary. Funds withdrawn from such accounts are not counted as income if they are used for the beneficiary's educational expenses. Individuals set up the account through the Utah Educational Savings Program, which then manages the accounts.

 

 Resource Disregard for UESP 529 Accounts

Disregard resources held in a UESP 529 account for the following Medically Needy programs:

Pregnant Woman;

Child; and

Parent Caretaker Relative.

Count withdrawals from a UESP 529 account as resources if the withdrawal was not used for qualified higher education costs.  Qualified educational costs include:

tuition and fees, books, supplies and required equipment;

computers and related software, peripheral equipment and internet access; and

room and board for students enrolled at least half-time.
