825-5.3 Assets - First Moment of the Month Rule and 10-10-Notice Rule for ABD

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

1.    When an aged, blind, or disabled member has an increase in the value of the countable resources, use the first moment of the month rule and the 10-10-notice provisions to determine the month that the overpayment started:

A.    The first moment of the month rule for the ABD  programs requires the eligibility agency to use assets held as of the first moment of the month to determine resource eligibility for that month (503-2).

B.    The 10-10-notice provisions determine the first month that the agency can make adverse changes in the member’s eligibility.

2.    Establish when the value of the resources increased.

3.    Determine whether the member continued to hold the excess resources on the first of the following month.

4.    Then, apply the 10-10-notice provisions as explained in 825-5.2, #3 to determine the first month that the agency could have stopped the medical assistance.   If the member still had the excess resources on the first of that month, that is the month the overpayment started.  

5.    The overpayment includes that month and continues for each following month that the member had the excess resources. If the member doesn’t have the excess resources at the beginning of the next month, decide whether the transfer of assets rules apply (573).
