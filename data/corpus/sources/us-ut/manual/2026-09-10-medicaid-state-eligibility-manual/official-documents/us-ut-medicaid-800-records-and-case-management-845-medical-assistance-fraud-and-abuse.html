Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

845 Medical Assistance Fraud and Abuse

Effective Date: January 1, 2012

Previous Policy

 

Individual Fraud or Abuse

Fraud is a conviction made by the District Court on Intentional Program Violations of specified severity which are referred for criminal prosecution.

The Department and the eligibility agency reserve the right to pursue prosecution of fraud based upon cost effectiveness and contracts agreed upon with the AG’s office.

Individuals are required to disclose information for medical assistance eligibility.  The requirement is in Utah law at 76-8-1203.    

The definition of fraud, for purposes of medical assistance eligibility and public assistance, is in Utah law at 76-8-1205.

The penalties for medical assistance and public assistance fraud are in Utah law at 76-8-1206

Fraud is established by the district court under state law and legal precedent.

Individual abuse includes recipient practices that result in unnecessary cost to the medical assistance program.   If you suspect that a individual is abusing a medical assistance program, refer the situation to the restriction program at 1-800-955-2210

Provider Fraud or Abuse

Provider fraud is “an intentional deception or misrepresentation made by a person with the knowledge that the deception could result in some unauthorized benefit to himself or some other person. It includes any act that constitutes fraud under applicable Federal or State law.

Provider abuse of the medical assistance program includes provider practices that are inconsistent with sound fiscal, business, or medical practices, and result in an unnecessary cost to the Medicaid program, or in reimbursement for services that are not medically necessary or that fail to meet professionally recognized standards for health care.

If you suspect that a provider has submitted an improper claim, call 1 (855) 403-7283.
