Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

409 Whose Income to Count for Aged, Blind and Disabled Medicaid and Medicare Cost Sharing Programs

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (409 was previously found in 485 until November 1, 2021)

 

General Income Counting Concepts

 

Count income of the eligible individual to determine eligibility for Medicaid.

·       If the individual has a spouse, the spouse's income is deemed available to the individual; however, the process of counting a spouse's income depends on a number of factors based on the program.

·       If the eligible individual is a child under age 18, count the child's income as well as the income deemed from the parents.

·     Refer to section 410-2 and 410-3 for policy on how to deem income from a spouse or a parent.

 

For Medicare Cost Sharing programs, count the Medicare recipient's income.

·       If both spouses receive Medicare and both want coverage, include the spouse's income.

·     Deem income from an ineligible spouse, or ineligible parent of an eligible, dependent child following policy in 410-2 and 410-3.

 

The Medicaid Work Incentive program applies different rules on how to count income from a spouse or a parent. Refer to section 314 for this policy.

 

The sections listed below have detailed information about how to count income to determine an individual's eligibility.

 

·       Medicaid for SSI Recipients and SSI Protected Groups (409-1)

·       Aged and Disabled Poverty-Related Medicaid, QMB, SLMB, and QI (409-2)

·       Aged, Blind and Disabled Medically Needy Medicaid (409-3)
