Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

100 General Provisions

No Previous Policy

 

Title XIX of the Social Security Act (Act) authorizes medical assistance for certain low income individuals through a variety of programs.  Each program has its own eligibility requirements, but the term "Medicaid" is generally used to refer to all of the medical assistance programs under Title XIX of the Act.  Although each program has its own eligibility requirements, some general provisions apply across all of the programs.  Section 100 discusses these general provisions as follows:  authority of Office director; DHHS (Department of Health and Human Services) and DWS (Department of Workforce Services) employee requirements; rights and responsibilities of applicants and recipients; voter registration requirements; protection of member information; member complaints; and fair hearings.
