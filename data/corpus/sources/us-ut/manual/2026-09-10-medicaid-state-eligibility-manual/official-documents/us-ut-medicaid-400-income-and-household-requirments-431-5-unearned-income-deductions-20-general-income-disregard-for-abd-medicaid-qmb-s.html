Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

431-5 Unearned Income Deductions - $20 General Income Disregard for ABD Medicaid, QMB, SLMB and QI

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (431-5 was previously found in section 409-6 until November 1, 2021)

 

1.        Deduct a $20 General Disregard for each ABD Medicaid, QMB, SLMB, or Qualifying Individual (QI) case.

·        Do not deduct $20 from a needs-based VA payment.

·        The $20 can only be deducted from other unearned income the individual has, if any, or from earned income.  

·       A married couple only receives one $20 deduction.

2.    When both spouses are eligible, combine their unearned income before deducting the $20.  

3.    If one spouse is eligible and there is deemable unearned income from the ineligible spouse, add the deemed unearned income to the eligible spouse's unearned income before deducting the $20 disregard.  

4.    Members on the NCW, CTW, LSW whose eligibility is determined under community group rules also receive a $20 general disregard to determine eligibility.

      ·    Single members on the NCW, CTW, LSW or the Physical Disabilities waiver who are eligible under the Spenddown Waiver group receive the $20 general disregard before deducting the 100% of poverty for one person to determine the member's spenddown.  (See sec.  384-2, 386-2, 387, or 388-2)

 

5.    If no one on the case receives at least $20 of unearned income that is not a VA needs-based payment, you will not be able to allow the full $20 disregard.

·      Any portion of the $20 that cannot be deducted from unearned income can be deducted from earned income, if the person or couple has earned income.
