825-2.2 Member's Responsibility to Repay Overpayments and Understated Liability Errors

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

  1. The member is responsible to repay overpayments and understated liability when those errors are caused by Inadvertent Household Errors or Intentional Program Violation errors (107-5). The agency does not attempt to recover overpayments or understatements of liability caused by agency error.

  2. According to Utah Law at 35A-3-603 (4), each person who knowingly assists a member, provider, or other person in obtaining an overpayment is jointly and severally liable for the overpayment.
