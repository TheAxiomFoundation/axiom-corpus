Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-30 Dedicated Bank Account Containing Lump Sum Payments of Retroactive SSI Paid for a Child

No Previous Policy

 

Exempt as a resource the balance of funds deposited in a dedicated bank account containing payments of retroactive SSI benefits for a minor child as long the account is used in accordance with the SSI requirements for such accounts.  The exclusion continues after the child turns 18 if he or she still receives SSI.  Refer to section 531-2 for more complete information about these accounts.  See Section 417-16 for exclusion of interest earned on these accounts.
