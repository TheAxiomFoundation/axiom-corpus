Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

591 Burial Spaces

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

Burial spaces are items defined in 591-1 that the individual holds for the burial of the individual, the individual’s spouse, or any other member of his or her immediate family.  The exclusion for burial spaces is defined in 591-2.
