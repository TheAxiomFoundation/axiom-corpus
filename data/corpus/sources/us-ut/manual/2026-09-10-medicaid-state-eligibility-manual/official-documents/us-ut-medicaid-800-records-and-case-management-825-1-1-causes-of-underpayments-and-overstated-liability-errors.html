Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-1.1 Causes of Underpayments and Overstated Liability Errors

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

 

Agency Error: a mistake made by DHHS or eligibility agency staff in determining eligibility or the amount of the member’s liability. Agency errors include:

Untimely action on a change the household reported.

Incorrect decisions about the household’s eligibility.

Policy incorrectly applied.

Information entered incorrectly in eREP.

Inadvertent Household error: Inadvertent errors are unintended mistakes the member or member’s representative make.  Examples are:

The member or member’s representative did not understand or forgot the requirements.

The member or member’s representative unintentionally failed to report changes in the member’s circumstances.

The member or member’s representative unintentionally provided incorrect or incomplete information.

The member or member’s representative did not understand that the information would result in a decrease in member liability or in eligibility for an eligibility coverage group with a broader scope of services.

The member or member’s representative mistakenly thought that, if reported, the information would result in an increase in liability but, instead, the information would have resulted in the member’s eligibility for an eligibility coverage group with a broader scope of medical services or a decrease in the member’s liability.

The member or member’s representative chose not to receive a conditional payment and the agency decision was overturned by a fair hearing or SSA overturned a denial of disability or the agency or fair hearing decided the member qualified for a hardship waiver while taking all reasonable actions to make a transferred resource available (575-9) or to make the equity value of home worth more than $500,000 available (575-3, 575-9).
