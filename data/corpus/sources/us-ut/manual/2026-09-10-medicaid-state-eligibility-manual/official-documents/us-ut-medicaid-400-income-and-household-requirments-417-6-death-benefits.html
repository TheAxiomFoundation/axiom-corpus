Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

417-6 Death Benefits

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (417-6 was previously found in 403-4.29 until November 1, 2021)

 

Death Benefits

 

1.       Do not count the portion of death benefits spent on the deceased person's burial or last illness.  

2.    This includes life insurance, cash gifts, and inheritances received as a result of the death of another person.

3.     See Sec. 531-4 for resource exclusion.
