Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

417-19 Reparation Payments

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (417-19 was previously found in multiple sections 403-4 until November 1, 2021)

 

Reparations Payments

 

Do not count the following types of reparations payments as income (with some exceptions listed below).

 

A.   Japanese-American, Japanese-Canadian, and Aleutian  Restitution Payments

1.    Definition

·   Restitution payments made by the U.S. Government or the Canadian Government to individual Japanese-Americans, Japanese- Canadians, or their survivors and Aleuts who were interned or relocated during World War II.  

2.    Do not count interest earned on retained restitution payment funds.

3.    See Sec. 521-11 for resource exclusion.

 

B.   German Reparation Payments/Payments to Victims of Nazi Persecution

1.    Definition

                                     a.            The German government has several provisions in place to make payments to various groups of individuals who were subjected to Nazi persecution.

                                     b.            The most recent provision was passed in July 2000.

                                                         i.             Under this law, the German government in conjunction with a number of German companies set up a foundation to make payments to people who were slave or forced laborers under the Nazis. 

                                                       ii.            The foundation is called "Remembrance, Responsibility and the Future." 

                                     c.            Payments may be monthly or just a one-time lump sum. 

2.       In the absence of supporting evidence or conflicting information, accept the individual's signed statement of the source of such income. 

3.       Do not count interest earned on retained funds as income.

4.       See Sec. 521-22 for resource exclusion.

 

C.   Netherland WUV Payments to Victims of Persecution

1.    Definition

·       These are payments made by the Dutch government to persons who were victims of persecution during the German and Japanese occupation of the Netherlands and the Netherlands East Indies (now the Republic of Indonesia).

2.    In absence of supporting evidence or conflicting information, accept a person's signed statement as to the source of these funds. 

3.    Do not count interest earned on retained funds as income

4.    See 521-35 for resource exclusion.

 

D.  Payments to Certain Persons Captured and Interned by Northern Vietnam

1.    Definition

a. These are payments made by the Department of Defense to an individual who was captured and interned by North Vietnam when that payment is made under Section 657 of the National Defense Authorization Act (P.L. 104-201). 

b. If the individual is deceased, the payment can be made to a surviving spouse, or to surviving children.  

c. The payment is a one-time payment of $40,000. 

d. $2,000 can be added for each year that the person's confinement exceeded 20 years up to a total payment of $50,000.  

2.    Do not count interest earned on retained funds as income.

3.    See Sec. 521-38 for resource exclusion.  

 

E.   Payments to Filipino Veterans of World War II

Definition

a.    On February 17, 2009, the President signed the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (ARRA).

b.    Section 1002 of the ARRA established the FVECF to issue one-time payments to eligible Filipino veterans who aided American troops during World War II.

c.    To receive the one-time payment, an eligible veteran must file an application with the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) within one-year of enactment (02/17/09 through 02/16/10) of the ARRA.

d.    A surviving spouse may receive the FVECF payment if the eligible veteran filed for it but did not receive the payment prior to death.

e.    Filipino veterans who are citizens of the United States (U.S.) are eligible for a one-time payment of $15,000, and veterans who are non-citizens are eligible for a one-time payment of $9,000.

 

F.   Austrian Social Insurance Payments

1.    Definition

·   These are Austrian Social Insurance payments received by individuals because they were a victim of Nazi persecution (i.e.: were imprisoned, unemployed, or forced to flee Austria) during the period of March 1933 - May 1945.

2.    If a person states he or she receives Austrian Social Insurance Payments based on having been a victim of Nazi persecution, accept the signed statement from the person and do not count the payment as income.  

3.    Do not count interest earned on retained funds as income.

4.    See Section 521-28 for resource exclusion.
