Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-38 Payments to Certain Persons Captured and Interned by North Vietnam

Effective Date: September 1, 2004

No Previous Policy

 

Do not count as a resource, payments made by the Department of Defense to an individual who was captured and interned by North Vietnam when that payment is made under Section 657 of the National Defense Authorization Act (P.L. 104-201).  If the individual is deceased, the payment can be made to a surviving spouse, or to surviving children.  The payment is a one-time payment of $40,000.  $2,000 can be added for each year that the person's confinement exceeded 20 years up to a total payment of $50,000.  See Sec. 417-19 for income exclusion.  Interest earned on retained funds is excluded from income, but not as a resource.
