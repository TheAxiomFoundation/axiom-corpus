405-3 Minor Parents Between Age 18 and 19 Who Live with Their Parents or Stepparents

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (405-3 was previously found in 465-2 until November 1, 2021)

 

 

When a minor parent between age 18 and 19 does not qualify under a MAGI-based coverage group, determine eligibility for medically needy and whose income and assets to count based on the minor parent's school attendance, living situation and deprivation. If the child could qualify for CHIP and a medically needy coverage group, the child has the choice of programs.

A.   Medically Needy Family or Child

 

1.    Determine eligibility for a minor parent who meets the age criteria as defined in 345-1 as follows:

a.   Determine eligibility for Medically Needy Family when the minor parent is deprived of support (345-2).

b.    Determine eligibility for Medically Needy Child when the minor parent is not deprived of support.

c.   Include the following individuals in the household size of the minor parent (405-2):

·       the minor parent

·       a spouse

·       parents

·       siblings

·       children of the minor parent

·       unborn children of anyone in the household size

d.    Count the income and assets of the minor parent and spouse (if living in the home). Count the income of the parents.  Do not deem income from their siblings or child.

2.    When determining eligibility for Medically Needy Family for a minor parent who does not meet the age criteria in 345-1, include only the minor parent, spouse and any children in the household. Only count the income and assets of the minor parent and spouse.

·       Determine eligibility for the minor parent's child for MAGI-based Child Medicaid (347). If the child fails MAGI-based coverage, the child may be covered under the Medically Needy Family program (405-1).

3.    The minor parent may be eligible for Medically Needy or CHIP. Allow the member to choose.

4.    If the spouse lives with the minor parent, determine the spouse's eligibility first using the MAGI-based methodology (401-2). If the spouse is not eligible, determine eligibility under Medically Needy Family (405-1).

Example:  Sara and Jim are both 18 and married. They are parents of Cody. They live with Sara's mother. Sara is deprived of support and meets the student criteria. She is not eligible under a MAGI-based group and wants medically needy Medicaid. Count her mother, herself, her spouse and their child in the household size. Deem income and assets from Sara's mother and Sara's spouse. If Jim wants Medicaid, determine his eligibility first under a MAGI-based coverage group. If he fails, he can be considered for family or child medically needy considering only his and Sara's income and assets.

 

B.   Medically Needy Pregnant Woman Medicaid for the 18-year-old

If the 18-year-old is pregnant and fails the MAGI-based coverage groups, determine eligibility for medically needy Pregnant Woman Medicaid. Student status does not matter (349).

·       Count her income and assets, and her spouse's, if he is living with her.

·       Do not deem income and assets from her parents.

·       Do not deem income and assets from the father of the baby if he is not married to the mother, even if he has established paternity.

·       Count in the household, the number of unborn children of her or her spouse.

·      See 503-2 for the asset limit.
