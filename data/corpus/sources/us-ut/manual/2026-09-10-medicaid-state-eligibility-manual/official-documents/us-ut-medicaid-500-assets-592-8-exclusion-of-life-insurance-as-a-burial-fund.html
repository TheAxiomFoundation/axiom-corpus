592-8 Exclusion of Life Insurance as a Burial Fund

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

  1. If an individual designates a countable life insurance policy as a burial fund, apply the exclusion amount to the cash value of the policy.  The cash value is the countable resource value of the policy; therefore, the burial fund exclusion applies to the cash value of the policy.

  1. If the individual has a dividend accumulations account with the life insurance policy, the value of the account is a countable resource separate from the cash value of the policy.  Apply the burial fund exclusion to the dividend accumulations only if the individual has designated them as a burial fund.  The dividend accumulations count as a resource if the individual does not designate them as burial funds.  The value of the accumulations designated for burial is also used to reduce the burial fund exclusion.  

  1. If the individual irrevocably assigns ownership of a life insurance policy to fund a pre-need funeral contract, see 594-4.
