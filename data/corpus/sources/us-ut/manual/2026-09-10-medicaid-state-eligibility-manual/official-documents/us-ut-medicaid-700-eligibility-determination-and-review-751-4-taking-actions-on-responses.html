Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

751-4 Taking Actions on Responses

No Previous Policy

 

Any contact from the individual about the Income Match is a response.  Document the response in the case.  If the individual says he is NOT ABLE to obtain verification on his own, help him obtain the verification.  You may contact the source of the income on his behalf.

 

  1. If you receive complete verification, adjust the current case as needed.  Send written ten-day notice of changes in eligibility or benefits. 

  2. If the response is incomplete decide what further action is needed.  Take that action and set a task for the return of additional information as needed. Document your actions on the case. 

  3. If the information indicates that additional actions will not be successful, document the reason on the case.  An example of this might be that the employer where the income was coming from is no longer in business.  This would not affect current eligibility in this case.

  4. If information from the income match or the response from the individual indicates eligibility or liability errors in past months, see section 825, Eligibility Payment Errors.
