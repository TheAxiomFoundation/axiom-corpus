Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

435-1 Definitions

Effective Date: June 1, 2020

Previous Policy

 

Definitions

The following definitions apply to policy on budgeting income.

1.    Benefit Month: The calendar month, or partial month, for which the medical eligibility is approved.

2.    Retroactive Period - The 3 calendar months immediately preceding the application month for Medicaid, SLMB, QI or QDWI.  (See Sec. 705-1 to determine the retroactive period, and 711 for exceptions to beginning coverage on the first day of a month.)  The QMB and UPP programs do not have retroactive coverage.  

3.     Prospective Budgeting: A process of determining countable income for the benefit month based on anticipated income expected to be received during that month or during the upcoming certification period.

4.    Best Estimate:  A calculation of the household's anticipated income, deductions and size during the certification period in all benefit months based on the most current information available.  Use this estimate to figure the eligibility and spenddown amounts for each month in the certification period.   Determine a new best estimate when changes are reported that may affect eligibility and were not already factored into the estimate and eligibility decision. Do not determine a new best estimate for programs with a continuous period of eligibility until that period is about to end (such as MAGI-based Pregnant Woman program, UPP, or 12-month Transitional Medicaid.)

5.    Anticipating Income:  Determining monthly income through a history of past or current knowledge of circumstances and applying those circumstances to future periods.  Income that has no history can be anticipated if verification shows the individual will receive this income, such as a signed job offer or employment contract, showing employment dates and expected amount of earnings.

6.    Averaging Income:  Determining monthly income from an average of income received or expected to be received over a pre-determined period of time.  This method is particularly useful for hourly workers whose weekly hours fluctuate.  Factor income paid on a weekly or bi-weekly basis for Parent/Caretaker Relative, Pregnant Woman, Child, and medically needy Family, Child and Pregnant Woman Medicaid programs (see 435-2 on factoring).

7.    Annualized Income:  Determining income for an amount of time by using income from a like season, year, or contract period that is representative of future income.  Income can be annualized over 12 months or less, depending on the circumstances of the individual.

8.    Stable Income:  Income that does not vary in either amount or frequency of receipt such as a set monthly salary. 

9.    Fluctuating Income:  Income that varies in amount or frequency of receipt.  This income must be averaged or anticipated.

10. Contract Income:  Income received for services or work under a contractual agreement. This income may be received in installments over a specified period, or it may be paid at the end of the period covered by the contractual agreement.

11. Recurring Income Received Less Often than Monthly:  Income that is paid quarterly, semi-annually, or annually.  For example, military summer camp pay, and bonus and incentive income.
