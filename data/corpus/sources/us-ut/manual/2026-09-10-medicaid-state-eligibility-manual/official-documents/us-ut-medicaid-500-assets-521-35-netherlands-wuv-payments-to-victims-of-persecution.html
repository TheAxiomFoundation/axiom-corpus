Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-35 Netherlands WUV Payments to Victims of Persecution

Effective Date: September 1, 2004

No Previous Policy

 

Excluded from resources any unspent WUV payments made by the Dutch government to persons who were victims of persecution during the German and Japanese occupation of the Netherlands and the Netherlands East Indies (now the Republic of Indonesia).  Accept the signed statement from the person as to the source of these funds and do not count those amounts as resources. Interest earned on retained amount of WUV payments is excluded from income, but not from resources.
