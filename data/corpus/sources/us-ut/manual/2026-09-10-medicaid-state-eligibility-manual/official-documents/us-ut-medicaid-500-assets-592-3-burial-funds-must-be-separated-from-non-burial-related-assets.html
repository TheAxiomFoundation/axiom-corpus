Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

592-3 Burial Funds must be Separated from Non-Burial Related Assets

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

  1. To be excluded from resources, burial funds must be kept separate from non-burial related resources.  The individual may commingle burial funds with other burial related resources.  If the individual does not separate the burial funds from non-burial related resources, the entire amount is countable.

  1. Burial related resources that may be commingled are burial funds (excluded and non-excluded) and burial spaces, such as contracts for the purchase of a burial space and/or contracts for purchase of both burial spaces and burial services.
