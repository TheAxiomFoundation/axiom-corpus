Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-34 Treatment of VA Payments

Effective Date: October 1, 2015

Previous Policy

 

VA Payments to Veterans' Children Born with Spina Bifida or Other Birth Defects

 

Do not count payments made by the Dept. of Veteran’s Affairs to veterans, or to the veteran's child, because the veteran's child was born with Spina Bifida or certain other birth defects.  Disabled children born to veterans of the Vietnam and Korean Wars may be eligible for these payments, if there is a causal link between the child's birth defects and the veteran's exposure to Agent Orange or other chemical herbicides. Interest earned on retained amounts of these payments is excluded as income, but not from resources.  

 

VA Educational Income

Any portion of a VA educational benefit that is a withdrawal of the veteran’s own contribution is a conversion of an asset and is not income.  However, any portion of the withdrawal that is retained into the month following the month of receipt is a countable asset.

Any portion of VA educational benefits under a vocational rehabilitation program does not count as an asset.

Any portion of other VA educational benefits are subject to the exclusion period for educational grants, scholarships, fellowships or gifts. (See sec. 521-10.)
