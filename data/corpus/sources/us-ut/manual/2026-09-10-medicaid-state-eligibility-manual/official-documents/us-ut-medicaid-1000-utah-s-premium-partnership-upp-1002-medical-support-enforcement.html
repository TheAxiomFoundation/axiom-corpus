Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

1002 Medical Support Enforcement

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

Previous Policy

 

UPP applicants/recipients are not required to cooperate with Medical Support Enforcement requirements. 

 

A person who would otherwise qualify for Medicaid without paying a spenddown or MWI premium, except he or she chooses not to cooperate or has been sanctioned for non-cooperation with Medical Support Enforcement requirements is not eligible for UPP.
