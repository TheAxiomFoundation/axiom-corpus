Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-22 German Reparation Payments, Payments to Victims of Nazi Persecution

Effective Date: October 1, 2005

No Previous Policy

 

The German government makes payments to individuals who were subjected to Nazi persecution.  In July 2000, the German government set up a foundation to make payments to people who were slave or forced laborers under the Nazis.  The foundation is called "Remembrance, Responsibility and the Future."  Do not count any payments from the German government or this foundation made to victims of Nazi persecution as income. Payments may be monthly or just a one-time lump sum.  In absence of supporting evidence or conflicting information, accept the individual’s signed statement of the source of such monies.  Interest earned on retained funds is excluded from income, but not from resources.
