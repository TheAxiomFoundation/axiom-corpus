Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

215 Residents of a Household or an institution

Effective Date: January 1, 2008

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

Medicaid policy defines all dwellings as either a household or an institution.  Residents of households are eligible for Medicaid if all other factors of eligibility are met. Residents of certain institutions are not eligible for Medicaid.  Residents of other institutions may be eligible for Medicaid if all other factors of eligibility are met.
