Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

592-12 Burial Funds That Were Excluded Before August 1, 1990

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

Before August 1, 1990, individuals could have burial funds in any form of asset.  Burial funds could be commingled with non-burial related assets but had to be separately identifiable.

 

After August 1, 1990, individuals had a brief time to change the form of the burial funds to meet the requirements of the new burial fund rules.  However, if a circumstance beyond the individual’s control made the change impossible or impracticable, the burial funds remain excluded IF:

 

• The circumstance continues, and

• The individual has remained and still remains continuously eligible.  A break in continuous eligibility occurs when there are one or more calendar months during which the individual is not covered by Medicaid.

 

If you have a individual in this situation, document at each review that the condition preventing the funds from being converted to acceptable burial funds continues.
