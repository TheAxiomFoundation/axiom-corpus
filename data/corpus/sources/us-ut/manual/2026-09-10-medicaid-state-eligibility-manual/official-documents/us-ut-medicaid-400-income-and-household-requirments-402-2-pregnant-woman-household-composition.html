No Previous Policy

 

 

Pregnant Woman Household Composition

The Pregnant Woman program follows the MAGI methodology to determine the adult’s household composition (401-1). Section 349 explains the other program requirements. The household size is used to determine if the countable income falls within the program income limit for the individual (Table VII). 

 

1.   Pregnant Woman Medicaid follows the PCR household criteria found in 402-1.

 

2.    Only the pregnant woman will receive Medicaid coverage through this program.

 

3.    The Pregnant Woman household composition will not change once initial eligibility has been determined.
