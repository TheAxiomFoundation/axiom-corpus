Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

107-5 Medical Assistance Overpayments and Understatements of Liability

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

Medical assistance members are responsible to pay to the state eligibility agency the amount of a medical assistance overpayment or the amount of the understatement of liability as required by the sections 825, 825-1, 825-2, 825-3, 825-4, 825-5, 825-6, and 825-7.
