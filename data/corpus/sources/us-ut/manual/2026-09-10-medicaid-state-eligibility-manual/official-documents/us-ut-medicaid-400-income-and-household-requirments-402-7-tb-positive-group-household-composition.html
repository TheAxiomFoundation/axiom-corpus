TB Positive Group Household Composition

The TB Positive Group follows the MAGI methodology to determine the individual’s household composition (401-1). Section 392 explains the other program requirements. The household size is used to determine if the countable income falls within the program income limit for the individual. 

Include all adults or children who are part the TB positive individual’s MAGI household in the household size.
