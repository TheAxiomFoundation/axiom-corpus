Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

603-3 Benefits for the QDWI - Qualified Disabled Working Individuals

Effective Date: April 1, 2004

No Previous Policy

 

The QDWI are not eligible for Medicaid benefits.  They are entitled to ONLY one benefit:  payment of their Medicare Part A premium.  These individuals do not receive a medical card.  Enrollment is done manually and the payment of the Part A premium is done manually.  Contact the Medicaid Program Specialist if you believe you have someone who is eligible for this benefit (320-5.)

 

This program should not be confused with the Medicaid Work Incentive Program, which is a Medicaid program for working people with disabilities.  It may be possible for QDWI eligible individuals to qualify for the Medicaid Work Incentive program; however, they would no longer receive payment of their Part A Medicare premium (314).
