Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-3.2 Ineligible Member or Ineligible Cases

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

Decide whether the member or all members of the case are currently ineligible for medical assistance programs.

If the member is currently receiving medical assistance but is not currently eligible for any medical assistance program, close the program for that member. Issue notice of the closure as required by 811.

If the members of the case are currently receiving medical assistance and none of them are eligible for any medical assistance program, close the case. Issue notice of the closure as required by 811.

Ask the member to return the medical card to prevent continued use of it.  However, do not tell the member that returning the card will prevent additional payment errors.

The medical assistance program may still make payments, including an amount to cover administrative costs, to a health plan or mental health agency.  The member may be responsible to pay back those costs.
