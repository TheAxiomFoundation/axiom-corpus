825-3.1 Eligibility for Other Medical Assistance Programs

Effective Date: April 1, 2019

Previous Policy

 

Determine if anyone on the case was eligible for another program during the period of the overpayment error or under stated liability error (827).  

1.    If the member was eligible for another program with the same scope of services during the same period, there is no overpayment.

2.    If the member qualified for a program with a more limited scope of services such as nontraditional Medicaid or QMB, the overpayment could be reduced.

3.    If the payment error was an understated liability, decide if any case member was eligible for a medical assistance program without a member liability, such as a poverty level program.

4.    If a member received medical assistance from a MAGI based program but was not eligible for that program, decide if the member was eligible for a program with a member liability.

If the member was eligible for QMB assistance, or was eligible under a different Medical assistance program, or was eligible on a spenddown program, describe on the referral which household members, if any, were eligible for the different medical assistance program or coverage group.
