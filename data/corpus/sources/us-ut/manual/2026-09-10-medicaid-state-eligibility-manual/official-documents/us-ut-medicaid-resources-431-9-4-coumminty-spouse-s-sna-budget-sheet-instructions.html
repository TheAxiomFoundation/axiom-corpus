431-9.4 Community Spouse's SNA Budget Sheet Instructions

 

Community Spouse Budget Sheet

(To prepare for a hearing)

 

Explanation:

The Spousal Needs Allowance (SNA) is the amount of income your spouse is able to give to you (the community spouse) while your spouse is in a nursing home or on a Medicaid waiver for long-term care. It reduces the amount your spouse has to pay for his or her medical care under the Medicaid program each month.

 

You have asked for a fair hearing to increase this amount, which would reduce what your spouse pays for medical care.  This worksheet is to help you document your monthly expenses to show why you need a higher Spousal Needs Allowance.

 

Carefully consider all of your monthly expenses as outlined on this worksheet. For annual expenses (say property tax), divide the total by 12 to get to a monthly amount.

 

At the hearing, you can discuss your expenses with the hearing officer. Some receipts may be needed. Enter expenses in the Annual or Monthly columns.

 

Send a copy of the completed budget to the hearings office to help document your expenses. Keep a copy for yourself.

Instructions:

·         Enter your monthly (or in some cases annual) expenses on this worksheet. Be thorough.

·         Some expenses are not monthly. Try to estimate the amount spent in a year.

o    For things like property taxes, enter the annual amount.

o    For occasional expenses, such as car maintenance, try to estimate what you spend each year. Example, estimate the cost for oil changes, tires, repairs, seasonal fluid checks, etc.

o    If you own a home, try to estimate costs of upkeep services like furnace checks, plumbing needs. If you pay for lawn care or snow removal, include that cost.

o    For medical care, include premiums. Estimate what you pay in deductibles and copayments, dental care, prescriptions, etc.

o    If you are paying off past medical bills, include that monthly cost.

o    If your spouse has past medical bills that are owed to the provider, send proof of those amounts to your eligibility worker. The amount owed may be deducted from your spouse's payment for care.

o    Credit card or loan payments: If you have borrowed money or used your credit card to pay off medical bills, or to pay off things like home or car expenses, or charged such amounts to your credit cards, identify these and the amount that you are paying off each month.

·         If you have special diet needs, enter those amounts in that row.

·         Estimate what you spend for non-food and personal care items, hair care, clothing, shoes. The hearing officer will allow at least a minimum amount for these.

·         Include costs for internet, cable TV, or other entertainment, fitness activities (like a gym membership) and hobbies.

·         We can allow payments you make for you and your spouse's funeral/burial arrangements.

·         Enter your monthly income in the last row. This is your income without counting any income from your spouse.
