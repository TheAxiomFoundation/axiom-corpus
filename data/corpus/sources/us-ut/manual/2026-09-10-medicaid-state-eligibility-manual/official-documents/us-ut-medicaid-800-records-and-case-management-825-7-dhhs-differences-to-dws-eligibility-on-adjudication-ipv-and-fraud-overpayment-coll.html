Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-7 DHHS differences to DWS eligibility on: Adjudication, IPV and Fraud, Overpayment Collections, Enforcement, Bankruptcy, and Overpayment

Effective Date: July 1, 2024

Previous Policy

 

Follow the rules in sections 820, 825, 830, 840, and 850 of the DWS eligibility manual with the following differences:

  • 820-3 Statute of limitations – there is a 3-year limit on the time from occurrence of a payment error to initiate adjudication and recovery of a medical assistance overpayment or understatement of liability.  Overpayments will not be adjudicated and collected from periods older than 3 years prior to the date the Notice of Agency Action (NAA) is mailed to the member (825-5.1).
  • 820-9 Judgment Liens – Adjudication –Section 1917(a)(1)(A) states: “No lien may be imposed against the property of any individual prior to his death on account of medical assistance paid or to be paid on his behalf under the State plan, except—

(A) pursuant to the judgment of a court on account of benefits incorrectly paid on behalf of such individual . . .”

  • 825 Intentional Program Violation and Fraud - There are no provisions in medical assistance statute or federal regulations for disqualification of currently eligible members from medical assistance because of intentional program violation or fraud.
  • 830-1A Assistance Reduction – medical assistance correctly paid cannot be adjusted or reduced to recover previously incurred overpayments or understatements of liability.
