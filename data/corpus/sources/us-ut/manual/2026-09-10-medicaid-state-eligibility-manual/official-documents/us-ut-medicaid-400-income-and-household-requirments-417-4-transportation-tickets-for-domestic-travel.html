Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

417-4 Transportation Tickets for Domestic Travel

Effective Date: December 1, 2022

Previous Policy

 

 ABD Unearned Income Exclusion

 

Do not count the value of transportation tickets for domestic travel if the tickets are not exchanged for cash. Domestic travel includes travel that both begins and ends in any of the following: the 50 states, Washington D.C., Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands.
