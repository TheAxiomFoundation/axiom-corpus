404-3 Foster Care Non-IV-E Medically Needy Medicaid Household Composition

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

No Previous Policy

 

 

Foster Care Non-IV-E Medically Needy Household Composition

 

1.   Children who do not meet IV-E foster care criteria when being taken into state custody, but have income in excess of the income limit for the MAGI Based Non-IV-E foster care programs, may qualify for a medically needy program. Section 354 provides the Foster Care Medically Needy program requirements.

 

2.    Do not include a foster child, placed in a foster home, in the foster family’s household size. 

 

3.    A child who is in foster care must have their own Medicaid case.

 

4.    If siblings are placed together in a foster home, they can be in the same Medicaid household. See section 404-6 for sibling deeming rules.

 

Note: This does not refer to a foster care child who has been placed with their own parent or other non-parent caretaker relative under a "Preliminary Placement" by DCFS (354).
