Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

751-2 Special Rules for Income Matches Required When Individuals Apply

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

No Previous Policy

 

1.    Check IEVS electronic data sources for all new applicants and household members whose income must be considered to determine eligibility.  DO NOT delay Medicaid to wait for Income Match reports.

 

2.    When electronic IEVS match information or printed match reports RETURN BEFORE the case has been approved, resolve any conflicting information that affects current eligibility BEFORE approving the application.  Ask the individual to explain the discrepancy. Request hard copy verification if needed. Extend the application period if needed to resolve the conflicting information.  (See Sec. 703-5.)
