405-1 Parents Under Age 18, or Parents Between Age 18 and 19 for Medically Needy Coverage

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (405-1 was previously found in 465 A until November 1, 2021)

 

A.   MAGI-Based Groups and Minor Parents

1.    Determine eligibility for Child Medicaid for the minor parent under age 19 living with their parents (347).

2.   If a woman is under age 19, pregnant, living with a parent and is not eligible for Child Medicaid, consider eligibility for Pregnant Woman Medicaid (349).

B.   Medically Needy Family, Child or Pregnant Woman

A parent under age 19 who does not qualify for a MAGI-based coverage group, may qualify under a medically needy coverage group.  

1.   A minor parent is defined as a parent who is under age 18, or who is between the ages of 18 and 19.  

2.  Eligibility is determined based upon the minor parent's age, student status, deprivation and living arrangements (See 345-1, 345-2 and 401-4).
