Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

751-6 What To Do When There Is An Overpayment

No Previous Policy

 

If the information received indicates that an eligibility or liability error occurred in past months, refer to sections 825 and 825-4.1.  

Special Rule for the IRS Match.  The IRS match reports must be verified before you base overpayment referrals on them.  DO NOT refer any overpayments that are based only on an unverified IRS match report.
