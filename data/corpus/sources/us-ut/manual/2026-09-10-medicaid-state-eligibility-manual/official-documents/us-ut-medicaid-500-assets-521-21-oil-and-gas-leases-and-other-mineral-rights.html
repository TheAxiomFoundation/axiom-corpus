Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-21 Oil and Gas Leases and Other Mineral Rights

No Previous Policy

 

The Individual Receives No Royalty from the Mineral Rights.

 

The value of mineral rights is a countable resource unless the mineral rights are on land which is being excluded because it is the individual's home.  In this case, the mineral rights can be excluded as a resource along with the exempt home and contiguous property.  In all other cases, determine the value of the mineral rights in one of the following ways and count them as a resource.

 

Ownership of land and mineral rights.  If the individual owns the land to which the mineral rights pertain, current market value of the land can be assumed to include the value of the mineral rights.

Ownership of mineral rights only.  If the individual does not own the land to which the mineral rights pertain, obtain a current market value estimate from a knowledgeable source.  Such sources include:

the Bureau of Land Management

the U.S.Geological Survey

real estate brokers

the local office of the Farm Home Administration (for rural land)

banks, savings and loan associations, mortgage companies, and similar lending institutions

an official of the local property tax jurisdiction (be sure to obtain the individuals estimate rather than the office assessment); and

the county Agricultural Extension Service.

 

Individual Receives Royalty from the Mineral Rights

 

When a individual is receiving royalties from mineral rights determine the value by verifying the net total of the last 12 months of royalty checks and multiplying that amount by 3.3.

 

If the individual disagrees, have him provide verification from a legitimate source stating the value of the mineral rights.
