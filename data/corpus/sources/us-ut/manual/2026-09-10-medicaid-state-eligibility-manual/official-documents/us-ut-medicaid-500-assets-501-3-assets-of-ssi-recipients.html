Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

501-3 Assets of SSI Recipients

Effective Date: April 1, 2011

Previous Policy

 

The receipt of SSI does not necessarily mean that the individual meets the asset criteria.  You should verify all questionable assets.  Any individual with countable assets over the asset limit is not eligible for Medicaid.  Inform Social Security when you deny Medicaid for an SSI recipient because of excess assets.

 

1.   Exception For SSI Recipients

 

If an individual receives SSI based on excess real property or excess personal property that has been exempted by SSI due to a good faith effort to sell the property, you must also exclude that property as a resource for the Medicaid eligibility decision.  Make sure that all other criteria of eligibility are met.  This rule applies only for SSI recipients.  Continue Medicaid for as long as SSI continues the conditional or extended SSI payments.

 

Do not exempt excess real property or excess personal property where the individual is showing a good faith effort to sell the property for any other individual.
