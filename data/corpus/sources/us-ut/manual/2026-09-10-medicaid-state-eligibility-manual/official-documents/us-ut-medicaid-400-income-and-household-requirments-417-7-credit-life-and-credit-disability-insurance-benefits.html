Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

417-7 Credit Life and Credit Disability Insurance Benefits

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (417-7 was previously found in 403-4.30 until November 1, 2021)

 

Credit Life and Credit Disability Insurance Benefits

 

Do not count Credit Life and Credit Disability Insurance which cover the payments on loans (such as credit cards), mortgages, etc., if the insured person dies or becomes disabled.
