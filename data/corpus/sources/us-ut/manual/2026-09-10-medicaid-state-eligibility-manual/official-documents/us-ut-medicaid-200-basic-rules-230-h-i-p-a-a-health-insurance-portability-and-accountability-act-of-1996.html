Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

230 H.I.P.A.A. (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996)

Effective Date: January 1, 2005

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

This section of the policy manual describes the action required of eligibility staff to assure Medicaid compliance with H.I.P.A.A. 

 

Effective April 14, 2003, all Medicaid recipients must be informed in writing of the privacy practices of the Utah Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Integrated Healthcare (DIH).

Eligibility staff are required to furnish all Medicaid applicants a copy of the NOTICE OF PRIVACY PRACTICES.

If Medicaid recipients have additional questions or concerns regarding their privacy rights, eligibility staff should refer them to the DIH's Privacy Officer at (801) 538-9925.
