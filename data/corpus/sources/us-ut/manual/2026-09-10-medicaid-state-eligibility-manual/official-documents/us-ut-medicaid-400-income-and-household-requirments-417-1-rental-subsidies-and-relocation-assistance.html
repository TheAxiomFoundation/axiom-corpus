417-1 Rental Subsidies and Relocation Assistance

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (417-1 was previously found in 403-4.6 until November 1, 2021)

 

Rental Subsidies and Relocation Assistance  

 

Do not count rental subsidies and relocation assistance as income. This includes:

 

1.    Payments received under the Uniform Relocation Assistance and Real Property Acquisition Policies Act of 1970.

2.    Federal Relocation Allowances paid because of missile testing.

3.    Rental and utility subsidies resulting when a public housing authority applies the 30% of income to rent ratio authorized by the Brooke Amendment to the Housing Act of 1937.

4.    Relocation Assistance from a State or Local Government

5.    The National Housing Act

6.    Section 101 of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1965

7.    Title V of the Housing Act of 1949

8.    Section 202h of the Housing Act of 1959
