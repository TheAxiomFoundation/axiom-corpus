Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-41 Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program (EEOICP)

Effective Date: July 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

 

Definition

The EEOICP makes lump sum payments and reimbursement of medical expenses to employees of the Department of Energy (DOE) or of private companies under contract with DOE, who suffer from specific diseases because of their work in the nuclear weapons industry, and designated beryllium workers.  Survivors of these employees may also receive payments.  

There are two parts to this law, Part B and Part E.  Each one has different effective dates, illness criteria and medical/compensation allowances.  (See 417-20 for income exclusion.)

 

Resource Rules

Exclude from resources, lump sum payments made under the EEOICP, including reimbursement for medical expenses that the recipient retains.   To receive this resource exclusion, these funds must be separated from the individual's other funds.

Some individuals who receive a payment under EEOICP may also receive a payment under the Radiation Compensation Act.  See 521-24 for rules about the Radiation Exposure Compensation Trust Fund.

Include in countable resources, interest earned on retained funds that the individual still has in any month after the month of receipt.

Verify the EEOICP Payment

Review DOL documents the individual has, or

Contact the local DOL district office.  A list of the DOL district offices and telephone numbers is included on the DOL Internet website at: https://www.dol.gov/owcp/energy/regs/compliance/contactphones.htm
