Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

417 Unearned Income Exclusions

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (403-4 was previously found in 417 until November 1, 2021)

 

A.   MAGI-Based Programs

See section 440-4 to see how to count unearned income.

 

B.    All Other Programs

This volume describes unearned income that is not counted to determine eligibility. A couple of the following topics include information about some related income that must be counted. Applicants and recipients must provide enough information and verification for the Medicaid worker to decide if income is to be counted or if it is an excluded income. Some types of excluded income may also be excluded as a resource. See Sec. 521 for resource exclusions. (See Sec. 413-6 for things that are not income.)
