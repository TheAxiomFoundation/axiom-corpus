Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

409-1 Medicaid for SSI Recipients and SSI-Protected Groups

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (409-1 was previously found in 485-1 until November 1, 2021)

 

A.   Individuals who Receive SSI or a State Supplemental Payment (SSP)

 

Do not count any income for individuals who receive SSI or a State Supplemental Payment (SSP).  

 

 

B.   Individuals on Disabled Adult Child Medicaid

 

Do not count any income to decide an individual’s eligibility if:

·       They meet the criteria of the Disabled Adult Child protected group, and

·       They pass the income test described in 330-1,

 

C.   Individuals on Pickle Amendment and Disabled Widows/Widowers Medicaid

 

Disabled Widows and people eligible because of the Pickle Amendment require the following income calculation. 

1.    Deem income from the individual’s spouse or parent. 

2.    If the person passes the income calculation for the protected group they qualify for Medicaid without a spenddown.

 

 

D.  Individuals on 1619(a) or 1619(b) Medicaid

 

Do not count any income for individuals who are in 1619(a) or 1619(b) status. They are "income eligible" for Medicaid.
