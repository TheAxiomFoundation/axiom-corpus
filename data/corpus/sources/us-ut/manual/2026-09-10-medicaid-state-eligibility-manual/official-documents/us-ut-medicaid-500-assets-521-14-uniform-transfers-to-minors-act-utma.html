521-14 Uniform Transfers to Minors Act (UTMA)

No Previous Policy

 

Do not count any asset, or the interest from the asset, which is held within the rules of the Uniform Transfers to Minor's Act (UTMA).  This is an irrevocable gift of money or property to a child under the age of 21.  The gift can be made to only one child with only one custodian holding the asset for the child under the Utah UTMA rules. 

 

The law includes different ways to make transfers to minors.  Depending on the way the transfer was done, the funds shall be paid to the minor either when the minor turns 18 or when the minor turns 21.  Check with the custodian holding the asset to find out when the funds become available to the minor (at 18 or 21).  (U.C. 75-5a-101 through 123)

 

Count any money the child receives from the asset as unearned income in the month received.  Count the funds as an asset to the child when the funds become available to the child (when the child turns 18 or 21 depending on the type of transfer.)
