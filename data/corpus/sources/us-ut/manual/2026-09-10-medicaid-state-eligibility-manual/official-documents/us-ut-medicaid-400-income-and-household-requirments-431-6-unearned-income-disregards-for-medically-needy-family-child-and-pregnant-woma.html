Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

431-6 Unearned Income Disregards for Medically Needy Family, Child and Pregnant Woman Medicaid

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (431-6 was previously found in section 409-7 until November 1, 2021)

 

 

There are no unearned income disregards for medically needy family, child or pregnant woman Medicaid programs.
