Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

801-2 Removing Case Records From the Office

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

No Previous Policy

 

Information in member case records can be shared with other state agencies that need the information for the purpose of doing disability determinations, fair hearings, Quality Control and PERM reviews, eligibility for other public assistance such as Food Stamps and TANF, or by request of the Director of the Division of Integrated Health Care, the Regional Director or the Department of Workforce Services.

Case records will be transmitted to the Federally Facilitated Marketplace under an agreement to share information for the purpose of determining eligibility for Insurance Affordability Programs.  This transfer of information will be done via secure electronic transmissions.   

If information must be mailed, use sealed envelopes for mailing.

If you receive a subpoena to release case information, see Sec. 111-1.

Workers who must transport case records because of their job description are required to follow the established procedures of their agency's security policies.
