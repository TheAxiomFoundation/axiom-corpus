410-4 Deeming Rules Chart

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (410-4 were previously found in 427-4 until November 1, 2021)

 

 

This chart is a guide to help eligibility staff decide when to deem income from an ineligible parent or ineligible spouse for aged, blind or disabled Medicaid, or for a Medicare Cost-Sharing program. It does not provide all the information needed to properly deem income. Refer to the policy in sections 410 through 410-3, and to the worksheets in 410-5 and 410-6 for complete deeming instructions.

When making a decision about whether an eligible person qualifies for a home and community based waiver (HCB waiver), refer to the specific waiver policies to determine eligibility. (380)
