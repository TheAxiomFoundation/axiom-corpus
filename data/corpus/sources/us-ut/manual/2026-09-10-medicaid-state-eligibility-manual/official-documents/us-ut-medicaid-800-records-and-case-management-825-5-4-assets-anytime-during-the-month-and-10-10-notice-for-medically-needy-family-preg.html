825-5.4 Assets – Anytime During the Month Rule and 10-10- Notice Rule for Medically Needy Family, Pregnant Woman and Child Programs

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

When a member or family in a Medically Needy Family, Pregnant Woman or Child  program has an increase in countable resources, use the anytime during the month rule to determine the month the value of the resources exceeded the resource limit for the entire month.

According to 503-2, a member is resource eligible for an entire month if the value of the resources is less than the resource limit at anytime during the month.

The member is ineligible for a month when the value of the resources exceeds the resource limit for the entire month.  

Use 825-5.2 to determine the first month the overpayment started.

Retroactive month or month of application:  The first month of the overpayment period is a retroactive month or month of application if the value of the resources exceeded the resource limit for the entire retroactive month or month of application (825-5.2, #1).

Conditional Payments: The first month of the overpayment period is the first month conditional payments were issued if a hearing decision supported the agency’s decision that the value of resources exceeded the resource limit (825-5.2, #2).

Month after the month of application: The first month of the overpayment period is the first month following the end of the 10-10-notice period if the value of the resources increased above the resource limit for the entire month following the end of the 10-10-notice period.   (825-5.2, #3).

The overpayment period includes each month that the value of the resources exceeded the resource limit for the entire month.
