Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

408-3 Aged, Blind and Disabled Medically Needy Medicaid

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (408-3 was previously found in 475-2 until November 1, 2021)

 

A.   The Medically Needy Household

 

Individuals eligible for the Medically Needy (spenddown) program for aged, blind and disabled persons will spenddown to 100% of the federal poverty guideline for the applicable household size.   The following policies determine whether to use the poverty rate for one or two persons.  

 

B.   Who to include in the household size

 

The household size for medically needy Medicaid must include the following individuals:

1.    The aged, blind or disabled person.

2.    Spouse living in the same home. 

 

Note: A spouse is living in the same home if either the individual or the spouse is temporarily absent for schooling, vacation, work, or medical care as defined in 408-2  

a.    Aged, blind or disabled spouse.

                    i.       Combine the income of both spouses to compare to 100% of poverty for a two-person household size to determine the spenddown amount when both spouses want  Medicaid coverage.

                   ii.    Deem income of the spouse per policy in Sec. 410-6 when only one spouse wants coverage and has deemable income.  

·       Count the spouse in the household size and use the income limit for two if the spouse has deemable income.

·       Do not count the spouse in the household size and use the income limit for one if the spouse does not have deemable income.

b.    Spouse receives SSI.

                    i.       Include an SSI recipient spouse in the household size.

                   ii.        Do not count the SSI spouse's income

                  iii.        Compare countable income to 100% of poverty for one person to determine eligibility for the non-SSI spouse.

c.    Spouse who is not aged, blind or disabled.

·       Deem income of a spouse who is not aged, blind or disabled if that spouse's deemable income is more than the Spousal Allocation on Table II.  (See Sec. 410-2.) The spouse is included in the household size only if the spouse has deemable income.

d.    Non-qualified non-citizen spouse.

·       Deem the income of a non-qualified non-citizen spouse with deemable income following deeming rules in Sec. 410-2. Also count assets of the non-qualified non-citizen spouse.

·       If there is deemable income, include the non-qualified non-citizen in the household size (BMS), but not in the Medicaid coverage.    

 

C.   Who Not  to Include in the Household Size

 

1.    Do not include the sponsor of a non-citizen in the household size.

2.    Do not include a spouse who is a resident of a medical institution or is eligible under a HCB waiver.

3.   Do not include a spouse who is aged, blind or disabled and does not want coverage if that spouse's deemable income is less than, or equal to, the Spousal Allocation on Table II.  (See Sec. 410-2)

4.   Do not include a spouse who is living in the same home who is not aged, blind, or disabled if that spouse's deemable income is less than, or equal to, the Spousal Allocation on Table II.  (See Sec. 410-2)

5.    Do not include parents or siblings of a disabled child.
