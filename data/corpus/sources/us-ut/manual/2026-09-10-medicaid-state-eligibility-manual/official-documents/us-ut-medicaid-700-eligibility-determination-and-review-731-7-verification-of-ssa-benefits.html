Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

731-7 Verification of SSA Benefits

Effective Date: April 1, 2018

 

Previous Policy

 

Computer interfaces are appropriate verification of benefits received from the Social Security Administration. The interface with the Social Security systems can verify the amount and source of any OASDI (Title II Social Security Benefit) or SSI (Title XVI Supplemental Security Income Benefits), and eligibility for Medicare benefits.  Verify the source and amount of such income at application, and when a recipient becomes eligible for Social Security Benefits.
