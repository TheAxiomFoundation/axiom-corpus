Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

431-9.4 Fair hearing order for more than the Minimum Spousal Needs Standard

Effective Date: July 1, 2022

Previous Policy

 

Requesting a Fair Hearing

 

If the member or the community spouse believes the Spousal Needs Allowance is less than what the community spouse needs to meet expenses, they have the right to ask for a higher allowance through the fair hearing process.

1.       Either spouse may ask for a Fair Hearing to increase the amount of the Spousal Needs Allowance to meet the living expenses of the community spouse.

2.      The DWS ALJ will take into consideration the current expenses of the community spouse and increase the SNA if needed.

·         If the request is denied by the DWS ALJ, the member has the right to request an Agency Review.

·        O EP staff may also request an Agency Review if they dispute the DWS hearing decision.

3.       The following forms are only to be used during the fair hearing process to determine if an SNA increase is needed.

SNA Budget Sheet

Community Spouse's SNA Budget Sheet Instructions

Hearing Officer's SNA Budget Sheet Instructions
