Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

573-3 Deciding Asset Eligibility for the Institutionalized Spouse

Effective Date: October 1, 2014

Previous Policy (373-3 renumbered to 573-3 as of November 1, 2017)

 

Determine Countable Assets for Institutionalized Spouse

 

Determine the value of countable assets held by the couple, or by either one of the spouses, in the month of application (or in the retroactive month if determining eligibility for long-term care benefits for such month.)  Assets that either spouse has an ownership interest in are counted in the assessment.  It does not matter if the couple has an agreement to keep assets separate.

For aged, blind and disabled Medicaid, use the assets owned by the couple as of the first moment of the month for which eligibility is being determined.

For Family-related Medicaid, use the assets owned by the couple at the beginning of the month for which eligibility is being determined, unless the value of the couple's assets are lower on a different day during the month.  In which case, use the lower value of the assets. (503-2)

Subtract the community spouse's protected share of resources determined according to section 573-2 from the couple’s total assets. The remaining amount is the amount of assets attributable to the institutionalized spouse.

If the institutionalized spouse has received benefits under a long-term care partnership insurance policy, subtract from the assets attributed to the institutionalized spouse an amount equal to the amount of benefits paid to or on behalf of the institutionalized spouse for long term care services by the LTC insurance partnership policy.  (see 513)  This is a dollar-for-dollar disregard of assets.

The remainder is the value of the countable assets used to determine eligibility for the institutionalized spouse. 

Asset Test

If the institutionalized spouse's countable assets determine in A.4. are less than or equal to $2,000, the institutionalized spouse is asset eligible.

Do not consider the community spouse's protected share of resources as available to the institutionalized spouse beginning the month after the institutionalized spouse’s share is less than or equal to $2,000.

When assets that are part of the community spouse's protected share of resources are owned by the institutionalized spouse or are jointly held, the couple has until the next review (721) to use assets for their own benefit, or to transfer all but $2,000 of the couple's combined assets into the community spouse’s name (573-6).  If this is not done, assets still held in the institutionalized spouse's name are counted as available to the institutionalized spouse back to the date eligibility was established.

If the institutionalized spouse's countable assets determined in A.4. is more than $2000, the institutionalized spouse is not asset eligible. If this happens, the community spouse's protected share is not yet set.
