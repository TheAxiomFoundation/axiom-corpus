Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

390-4 Cancer Program Eligibility Review

Effective Date: April 1, 2015

Previous Policy

 

Do not require a review more than once every 12 months; except for pre-cancerous cases which will be reviewed every 3 months.

The agency will complete an ex parte review for the Medicaid Cancer program. (section 721)

Assume other eligibility factors have not changed unless the member reports a change.

Request verification of on-going treatment post-eligibility.

Do not ask for income verification unless information indicates the individual could qualify under a different Medicaid program that does not require a spenddown, or MWI premium.
