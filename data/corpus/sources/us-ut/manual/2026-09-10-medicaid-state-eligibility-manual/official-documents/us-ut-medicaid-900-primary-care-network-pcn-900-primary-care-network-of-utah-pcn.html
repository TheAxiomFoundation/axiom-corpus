Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

900 Primary Care Network of Utah (PCN)

Effective Date: September 1, 2020

Previous Policy

 

The Primary Care Network (PCN) program closed on March 31, 2019. Members previously enrolled in PCN were automatically enrolled in Adult Expansion Medicaid (348).
