Office of Eligibility Policy Mission Statement

         

We commit to serving the people of Utah by:

  • Making Utahans the healthiest people by providing access to quality, cost effective health care;

  • Creating clear and accurate eligibility policies; and

  • Ensuring quality eligibility and disability decisions.
