Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

602 Medicaid Member Card

Effective Date: July 1, 2018

 

Previous Policy

 

A.   Medicaid Member Card – All Programs

1.    The first time an individual is determined eligible for Medicaid, the agency will send a wallet size plastic Medicaid card. The cards will not have eligibility information listed on them. Instead, they will have the members name, Medicaid ID number, and date of birth.  Providers will use the member information on the medical card to verify eligibility and benefit information.  The back of the card has helpful contact information and websites for both provider and members.

2.    Each Medicaid member in a household will receive their own card.  The new Medicaid member card will be used whenever the member is eligible for Medicaid.  A new card will not be mailed each month. 

o    Replacement cards will be issued by DWS if the card is reported lost or damaged.

3.    Members can access additional information at https://medicaid.utah.gov/using-my-medicaid-member-card

B.   Form 695 – Verification of Interim Eligibility

If an eligible member has stated they have an urgent medical need that requires proof of Medicaid eligibility before a card can be mailed to the member, complete the form 695, Verification of Interim Eligibility.   

1.    The individual must meet a spenddown or MWI premium (if applicable) before receiving the 695.  

2.    If the member uses medical bills to meet the spenddown, the agency will provide a notice listing the incurred expenses to the member.  The notice will list the provider, the medical expense and the amount the member is responsible to pay.  A copy of this notice must be attached to the 695.  

3.    Do Not give a Form 695 to individuals eligible for the following programs:

o    Retroactive Medicaid;

o    Emergency Medicaid;

o    QMB;

o    QDWI;

o    SLMB; or

o    QI
