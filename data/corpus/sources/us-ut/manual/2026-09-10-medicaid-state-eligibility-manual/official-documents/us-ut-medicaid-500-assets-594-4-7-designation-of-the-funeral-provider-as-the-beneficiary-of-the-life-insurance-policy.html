594-4.7 Designation of the Funeral Provider as the Beneficiary of the Life Insurance Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

The individual can designate a funeral provider as the beneficiary of a life insurance policy without an irrevocable assignment of ownership.  This designation does not fund the pre-need funeral contract.

 

The beneficiary does not have a right to the cash value or dividend accumulations of the policy.  The owner retains the rights of ownership even if the owner designates the beneficiary irrevocably.

 

If the individual revocably or irrevocably designates the funeral provider as the beneficiary of the policy, but the individual retains ownership of the policy, then follow the rules below:

 

Burial Spaces

Do not apply the burial space exclusion to the cash value of the life insurance policy. The funeral provider has not received any payment and the individual has not                 purchased burial spaces. The provider is not obligated to provide any burial spaces until the individual dies.

Burial Funds

The asset value of the pre-need funeral contract is equal to the cash value of the life insurance policy.

If the face value of all life insurance policies on the individual’s life is $1,500 or less, exclude the cash value under the life insurance exclusion (521-16).  Reduce the               burial fund exclusion by the face value of the policy.

If the face value of all policies exceeds $1,500, apply the policy for burial fund exclusions (592) to the cash value of the policy.  As explained in 592-8, the burial fund             exclusion applies to dividend accumulations only if they are designated as burial funds.  If the dividend accumulations are not designated for burial, or if they cannot all         be excluded as burial funds, they are a countable resource.
