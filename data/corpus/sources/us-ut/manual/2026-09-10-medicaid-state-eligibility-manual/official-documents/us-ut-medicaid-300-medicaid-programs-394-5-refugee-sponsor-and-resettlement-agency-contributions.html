Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

394-5 Refugee Sponsor and Resettlement Agency Contributions

Effective Date: July 1, 2009

Contact a DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

Use the Refugee Sponsor Contribution Form 1043 to contact the sponsor and verify sponsor contributions to the refugee.  Send a copy of the completed form to the resettlement agency.

 

 Refugee Sponsor

 

Count any cash contributions from a sponsor.

Do not count the sponsor's income or assets.  

Do not count in-kind services or shelter provided by the sponsor to the refugee.

 Resettlement Agencies

Do not count any cash or in-kind benefits received from the resettlement agencies or under a matching grant program as noted on the form 1043.

One-time reception placement  in-kind benefits  from local resettlement agencies under the Department of State or Department of Justice Reception and Placement programs are excluded for all medical assistance programs.

One-time reception placement payments received in cash are excluded as income for  Refugee Medicaid.  Cash payments for one-time reception placement are countable for Medicaid and CHIP programs.
