Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-1.4 Refunds of Overstated Liability and Estate Recovery

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

 

The state is required to recover, from a deceased member’s estate, the medical costs that Medicaid paid for the member after age 55 (835).    Refunds of overstated liability issued after a member’s death are part of the member’s estate.

Family members or guardians who have paid from their own funds for burial/funeral related items can receive all or part of the refund, depending on the amount they paid.  If the member died, explain this on the Form 79R.

Include the name and address of the person who paid for the burial/funeral items.  The Office of Financial Services will send a letter to this person explaining how to receive all or a portion of the refund.
