521-20 Resources Set Aside as Part of a Plan for Achieving Self Support for SSI Recipients

No Previous Policy

 

1.   Background

 

The Plans for Achieving Self-Support (PASS) policy is an intent by Congress to put into law a provision for SSI recipients "to provide every opportunity and encouragement to the blind and disabled to return to gainful employment."

 

PASS grants income and resource exclusions that allow SSI recipients to set aside income and/or resources for a work goal such as education, vocational training, or starting a business.  SSI recipients can even set aside funds to purchase work related equipment.

 

The SSI recipient must have a feasible occupational objective, a specific saving/spending plan, and must provide for clear identification of the funds that are set aside.  These SSI recipients are identified by Social Security as PASS participants.  A PASS plan must be in writing.  A PASS plan can never exceed 48 months.

 

2.   Procedures

 

Obtain a copy of the PASS plan from the SSI recipient.  A PASS plan can only be written by SSA or a state rehabilitation agency.  It must be approved by the Social Security Administration.  It will show what money and other resources the SSI recipient has and will receive that will be used to obtain the occupational objective.  It will also show how the money set aside under the plan as resources will be kept identifiable from other funds.  The plan should identify the beginning date of the plan, the ending date of the plan, and whether an extension of the plan is expected.

 

Do not count resources owned by the SSI recipient when they are set aside under an approved PASS plan.
