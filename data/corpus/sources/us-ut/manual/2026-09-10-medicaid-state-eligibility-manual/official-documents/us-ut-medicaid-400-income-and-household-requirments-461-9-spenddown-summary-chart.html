This chart is a quick guide to help you decide how to use medical expenses to meet spenddown. Use this chart for all programs that allow spenddown. Do not use this for nursing home and waiver programs.

  • All medical expenses must meet the criteria for medical bills found in 461-5.

  • This chart does not include all factors medical bills must meet to use for spenddown. It does not take the place of the policy. Always follow policy to decide what bills to use for spenddown.

How Does Insurance Coverage Affect Medical Bills?

 

1.    Qualified Medicare Beneficiaries (QMB) program covers all Medicare copayments and deductibles. QMB does not allow retroactive coverage, so member may still owe amounts from past months before QMB started.

2.    Third party insurance (TPL).  Allow only the portion the member must pay (deductibles and copayments). If insurance has not paid yet, estimate the member's obligation based on what the coverage is. (Example: some plans cover 80% of allowable charges.)

3.    Medicaid and Affordable Care Organizations (ACO). Most Medicaid members must enroll in an ACO. Do not use incurred bills after the member is enrolled in an ACO.

4.    Mental Health capitated plans. Medicaid contracts with a mental health provider in all but one county. Do not allow incurred mental health service expenses to meet spenddown.
