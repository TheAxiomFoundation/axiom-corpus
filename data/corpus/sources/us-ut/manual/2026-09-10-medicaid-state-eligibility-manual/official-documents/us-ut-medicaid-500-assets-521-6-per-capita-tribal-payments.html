Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

 

521-6 Per Capita Tribal Payments

Effective Date: October 1, 2016

No Previous Policy

  1. Exclude from resources per capita payments tribal members retain if the Secretary of the Interior held the funds in trust before distribution.  This includes Golden Age and Senior Retirement payments made by the Ute Indian Tribe. (417-14)

  1. Exclude from resources interest or investment income earned on the per capita payments tribal members retain.

  1. Exclude from resources any initial purchases a tribal member makes with per capita payments.

  1. Do not exclude from resources per capita payments that tribes distribute from funds not held in trust by the Secretary.   Examples are revenue from tribal businesses including:

  • tribally managed gambling

  • sales of items not held in trust, or

  • storage of nuclear waste on tribal lands.
