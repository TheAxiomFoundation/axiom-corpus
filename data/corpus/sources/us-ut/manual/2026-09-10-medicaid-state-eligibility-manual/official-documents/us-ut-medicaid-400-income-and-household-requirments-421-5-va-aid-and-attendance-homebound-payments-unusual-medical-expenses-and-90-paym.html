Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

421-5 VA Aid and Attendance, Homebound Payments, Unusual Medical Expenses, and $90 Payments to Nursing Home Residents

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (421-5 was previously found in section 407-5 until November 1, 2021)

 

A.  MAGI-based eligibility groups

VA benefits, including these special payments, do not count as income for MAGI-based eligibility groups.

 

B.  Non-MAGI based eligibility groups 

1.    VA payments for aid and attendance (A&A), homebound payments, unusual medical expenses (UME), and the $90 Improved Pension payments because they are considered aid and attendance payments, are not counted as income for institutionalized and waiver recipients.

2.   There will be no TPL collection on (A&A), (UME) or the $90 payments.    See Sec. 415-2.

3.    Other VA income, including pensions based on need or compensation payments, is countable income for non-MAGI based eligibility groups.

·       The lump sum payment must be counted as income in the month of receipt. See Sec. 421-3.
