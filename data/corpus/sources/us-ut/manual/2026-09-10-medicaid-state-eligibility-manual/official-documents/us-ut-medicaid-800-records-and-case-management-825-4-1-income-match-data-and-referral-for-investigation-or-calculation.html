Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-4.1 Income Match Data and Referral for Investigation or Calculation

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

 

If you receive information from the income match that indicates that an overpayment occurred:

Use the rules in section 751.

Document all attempts to obtain proof of income.  If all efforts to obtain proof fail, do not refer the case for an overpayment calculation solely because the member does not respond to requests for verification.

If the information received indicates that an overpayment occurred and you do not have enough evidence to support an overpayment or understatement of liability calculation, refer the case for investigation.

If you cannot verify information on the Income Match report, prorate the income over the period of time the report indicates the income was received to determine if it affects eligibility.  If this indicates an overpayment or understatement of liability occurred, refer the case for investigation.
