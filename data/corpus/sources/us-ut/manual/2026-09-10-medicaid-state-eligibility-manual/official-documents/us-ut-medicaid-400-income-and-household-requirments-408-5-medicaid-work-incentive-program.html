Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

408-5 Medicaid Work Incentive Program

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (408-5 was previously found in 475-4 until November 1, 2021)

 

A.   The Medicaid Work Incentive Program

 

The following policies determine who to include in the household size when comparing income to the 250% poverty rate.

 

B.   Who Must be Included in the Household Size?

 

The household size for medically needy Medicaid must include the following individuals:

1.    The disabled person

2.    A spouse living in the home:

a.    If the spouse is disabled and also qualifies for the program include the spouse in the MWI coverage.

b.    If the spouse does not qualify for the Work Incentive program, their income must be deemed.

c.    If the spouse receives SSI, their income does not count.

3.    The parent of a minor individual living in the home:

a.    Parents’ income is deemed.

b.    If one parent receives SSI, both parents’ income is excluded.

4.    Minor children of a disabled individual and siblings of a disabled minor individual.

·       Also include children and siblings up to age 21 who are full-time students.  (When a child turns 18, and is no longer a full-time student, change the child's status in the household so they are no longer included.)

 

C.   Who Not to Include in the Household Size

 

1.    Do not include the sponsor of a non-citizen.

2.    Do not include a spouse, child or sibling who is a resident of a medical institution, or who is eligible under a HCB waiver.
