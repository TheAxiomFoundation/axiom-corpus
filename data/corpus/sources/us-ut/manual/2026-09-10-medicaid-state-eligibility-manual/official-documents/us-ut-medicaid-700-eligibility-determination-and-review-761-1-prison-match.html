Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

761-1 Prison Match

No Previous Policy

 

If SSA has information about an incarcerated individual who is on an assistance case on the State's member information computer system, the system will retrieve the information, display it, and generate an alert for the eligibility worker.

The information displayed will be:

That the individual is incarcerated but no further information is provided.

That the individual is incarcerated and all known information is provided.

If there is no match, the system will generate a “no information found” message.  In this instance, no further action is required.
