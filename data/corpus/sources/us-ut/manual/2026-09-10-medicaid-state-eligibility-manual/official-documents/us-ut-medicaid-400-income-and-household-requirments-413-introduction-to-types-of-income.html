Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

413 Introduction to Types of Income

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

No Previous Policy

 

A.   Introduction

The policies in 413 apply to all non-MAGI-based Medicaid groups and the Medicare Cost Sharing programs. Some specific sections may include information for MAGI-based groups for clarity, or may reference 440-4 for treatment of income for MAGI-based groups. The budgeting section in 435 has been modified to apply to both the non-MAGI-based and the MAGI-based coverage groups. 

See 440 for policy about how to determine income for MAGI based programs.  

B.   General Content

An individual's income must be considered to determine eligibility for medical assistance. The income from a spouse, a parent of a minor child, or a sponsor of a non-citizen may also have to count as income to the individual for non-MAGI-based programs. 

This section includes the following information.

1.    The rules for deciding what amount of a individual's income (or income of a individual's spouse, parents, or sponsor) must be counted to decide eligibility for the individual for non-MAGI based programs. 

2.    Explanations about what income is countable, what is exempt, how to count income from a parent, a spouse, or a non-citizen's sponsor, and what income deductions are allowed.

3.    The rules for budgeting income and change reporting.

4.    The rules for calculating how much the individual must pay to receive Medicaid, if the individual does not qualify for Medicaid coverage without cost. Some programs do not allow a person to pay to receive coverage
