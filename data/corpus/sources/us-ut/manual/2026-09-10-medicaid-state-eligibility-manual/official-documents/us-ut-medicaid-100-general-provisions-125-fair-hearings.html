Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

125 Fair Hearings

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

 

Previous Policy

 

Applicants and recipients have a legal right to ask for a fair hearing any time they disagree with an agency action taken on their case.  

Applicants and recipients should file the fair hearing with the agency that took the action on their case that they disagree with.
