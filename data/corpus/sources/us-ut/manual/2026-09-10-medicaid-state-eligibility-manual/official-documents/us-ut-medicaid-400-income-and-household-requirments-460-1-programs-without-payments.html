Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

460-1 Programs without Payments

Effective Date: June 1, 2020

No previous policy

(Sections in 415 have been rewritten into 460 sections as of June 1, 2020)

Individuals who meet the requirements for the following programs do not make payments to receive coverage.

A.  Eligible under MAGI-related programs

Medical programs that use MAGI-based income do not allow a person to spenddown to that income limit.  Review an individual's eligibility for one of the MAGI-based programs before considering a medically needy spenddown program. The following MAGI-based programs include:

1.  Child Medicaid (347)

2.  Parent Caretaker Relative (345)

·        The 4-month and 12-month transitional programs also do not have a spenddown

3.  Pregnant Woman (349)

4.  Adult Expansion (348)

5.  Utah Premium Partnership (1000)

6.  Targeted Adult Medicaid (346)

Sometimes, because medically needy coverage is determined differently than the MAGI eligibility groups, you may find a pregnant woman, or a parent/caretaker relative who fails the MAGI group, but is eligible for medically needy coverage without a spenddown.

 

B.  Eligible under SSI-related groups

SSI related groups are mandatory coverage groups. Review eligibility for an SSI related group before considering a medically needy spenddown group. These include the following individuals.

1.    SSI recipients (aged, blind or disabled). (See sections 329)

2.    Recipients of State Supplemental Payments. (See section 329)

3.    Recipients eligible as an SSI Protected Group person as defined in section 330.

 

C.  Other programs without payments

Several other Medicaid programs do have payments to be eligible. These include the following programs.

1.    Medicare Cost-Sharing programs such as the QMB, SLMB, QI and QDWI programs. (These programs do not provide full Medicaid benefits, so some individuals may want to be on Medicaid, too.)  (320)

2.    The 100% poverty-related Aged and Disabled Medicaid coverage groups.  

3.    Blind individuals whose income does not exceed 100% of poverty are eligible without a spenddown. (They are under a medically needy group, but do not owe a spenddown.)  (303)

4.    TB Positive Medicaid. (392) 

5.    Medicaid Cancer program. (390)

6.    Foster Care Independent Living and Former Foster Care programs. (354)

7.    Subsidized Adoption. (355)

When an individual does not qualify for a program without cost, consider medically needy programs, or the Medicaid Work Incentive (if disabled and working).

If institutionalized or on a waiver, refer to policy on long-term care programs.
