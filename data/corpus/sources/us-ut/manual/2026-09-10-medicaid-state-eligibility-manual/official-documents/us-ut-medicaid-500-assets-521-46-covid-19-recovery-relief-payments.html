Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-46 COVID 19 Recovery Relief Payments

Effective Date: April 16, 2020

 

Previous Policy

 

A.   General Information

Beginning in April 2020, individuals may receive an advanced tax credit called the Recovery Rebate. This payment is a response to the national COVID-19 health emergency. The Federal government authorized these payments under the COVID-19 CARES Act of 2020.

 

These Recovery Rebates payments do not count as resources for a period of 12 months after the month of receipt to determine eligibility for Medicaid benefits.  (Sec. 417-21 for income exclusion.)

 

Individuals may receive up to $1200, joint filers up to $2400, plus an additional $500 per qualifying child. While payments are scheduled to begin in April 2020, some individuals may not receive a payment for several weeks or months.

 

B.   Federal Pandemic Unemployment Compensation

The COVID 19 Unemployment extra benefit checks of $600 per week will not be countable as an asset for Non-MAGI programs.
