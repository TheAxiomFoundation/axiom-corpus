591-1 Definition of a Burial Space

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

A burial space is a:

• burial plot

• gravesite

• crypt

• mausoleum

• casket

• urn

• niche

• Other repository customarily and traditionally used for the deceased’s bodily remains.

• Other necessary and reasonable improvements or additions to the burial space, including:

  • vaults

  • headstones, markers, or plaques

  • burial containers

  • arrangements for the opening and closing of the gravesite, or

  • a contract for care and maintenance of the gravesite sometimes referred to as endowment or perpetual care.
