Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-1.3 Correction of Overstated Liability Errors

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

 

The refund of the overpaid amount of the member’s liability is not subject to 12 month time limits.  

The eligibility worker, not the Benefit Accuracy Analyst, corrects overstatements of liability.

Use the medical refund request procedure and form 79R for the refund of an overstated liability amount.  

If there is an outstanding overpayment or understatement of liability caused by inadvertent household error or IPV, use the refund to offset the overpayment.

If there is an outstanding overpayment or understatement of liability caused by agency error, do not use the refund to offset the overpayment or understatement of liability because we do not recover overpayments or understatements of liability caused by agency errors (825-6, #1).

If the eligibility agency correctly calculated the member’s liability but the member thinks the amount the program paid for the member’s services was less than the liability amount, the refund may take up to one year to process. Providers have up to one year from the date of service to bill Medicaid.  No refund will be authorized until the Medicaid agency has received all billings for which it is liable.

If the refund is for an incorrectly calculated liability amount, there will not be a 12 month wait for the refund.
