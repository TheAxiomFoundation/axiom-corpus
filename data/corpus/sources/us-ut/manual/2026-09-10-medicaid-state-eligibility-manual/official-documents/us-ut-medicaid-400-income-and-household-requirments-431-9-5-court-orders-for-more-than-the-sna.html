Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

431-9.5 Court Orders for More Than The SNA

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (431-9.5 was previously found in section 409-11.5 until November 1, 2021)

 

Court Ordered Spousal Needs Allowances

 

If there is a court order against the member for monthly support of the spouse, the SNA for the spouse must be at least the amount of court ordered support. The SNA maybe more than the court ordered support if the calculated SNA under current policy is more.
