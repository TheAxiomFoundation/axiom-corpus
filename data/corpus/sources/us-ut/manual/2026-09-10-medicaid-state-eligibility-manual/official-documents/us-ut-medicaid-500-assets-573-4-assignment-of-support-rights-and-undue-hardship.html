Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

573-4 Assignment of Support Rights and Undue Hardship

No previous policy (373-4 renumbered to 573-4 as of November 1, 2017)

 

If the institutionalized spouse claims that excess assets can’t be used for the cost of care, have the institutionalized spouse complete the Assignment of Support Rights Form 48 when the community spouse:

  • Cannot be located, or

  • Will not cooperate with the assessment, or

  • Has retained assets exceeding the spousal asset allowance, and

  • Counting the community spouse’s assets causes the individual to exceed the asset limit.

If the institutionalized spouse assigns rights to the state the spouse is asset eligible even when assets exceed the asset limit.

 

Undue Hardship

Excess assets are unavailable to the institutionalized spouse if:

  1. The individual is not able to complete the ORS Form 48; AND

  2. Counting the spouse's assets as available to the institutionalized individual causes the individual to exceed the asset limit; AND

  3. The community spouse refuses to make the assets available; AND

  4. Without Medicaid coverage for institutional care, the individual will not be able to get the medical care needed; AND

  5. The individual is at risk of death or permanent disability without institutional care.  This must be verified by a physician's statement.
