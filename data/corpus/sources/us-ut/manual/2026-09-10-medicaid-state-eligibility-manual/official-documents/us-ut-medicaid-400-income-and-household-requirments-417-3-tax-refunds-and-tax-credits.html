Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

417-3 Tax Refunds and Tax Credits

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (417-3 was previously found in 403-4.13 until November 1, 2021)

 

Tax Refunds and Tax Credits

 

Do not count any state or Federal tax refunds and tax credits as income. See section 521-32 for the asset rules for these types of funds.
