Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

229-4 Common-Law Marriage

Effective Date: October 1, 2011

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

In Utah, common-law marriage is legal and valid only when it has been established by Court or Administrative Order.  

  • Living arrangements by themselves are not enough to establish a common-law marriage.

  • Even the fact that a child is born of the couple does not prove common-law marriage.
