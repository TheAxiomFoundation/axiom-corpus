Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

1003 Health Insurance

Effective Date: June 1, 2016

Previous Policy

 

Applicants must meet the requirements defined in the following sections regarding enrollment in or access to a health insurance plan or COBRA. If the application lists any type of health insurance, the agency must determine the type of insurance before making an eligibility decision.
