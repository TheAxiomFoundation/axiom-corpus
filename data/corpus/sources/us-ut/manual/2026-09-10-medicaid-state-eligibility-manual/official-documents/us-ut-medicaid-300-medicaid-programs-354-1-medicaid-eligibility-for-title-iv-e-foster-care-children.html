354-1 Medicaid Eligibility for Title IV-E Foster Care Children

Effective Date: November 1, 2018

Previous Policy

 

Any foster care child who meets Title IV-E criteria is eligible for Medicaid. Once DHHS determines a child  meets the criteria for Title IV-E foster care,  DHHS authorizes the IV-E Foster Care Medicaid coverage.  This includes those children who are living with a parent who is receiving in-patient substance abuse treatment.

 

These children do not have to file a separate application, and Medicaid reviews are not required.
