Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

592-10 Redesignation or Replenishment of Burial Funds After Use for Another Purpose

Effective Date: August 1, 2007

No Previous Policy

 

When the use of burial funds for another purpose causes the remaining burial funds (not including interest or appreciation) to fall below the amount originally designated, the individual must re-designate or replenish the funds.  Failure to correct the designation or replenish the funds may confuse the individual about how much is excluded as a burial fund and may cause incorrect asset eligibility decisions.

 

Re-designation

Re-designation does not mean that the burial fund exclusion is lost and reapplied.  Re-designation means the individual states a new amount of funds set aside for burial.  This new designation is the amount of the originally designated funds that remain after some funds were used for another purpose, not including excluded interest.

If the individual re-designates the burial fund instead of replenishing it, calculate the new ratio of excluded funds to total burial funds so that you can calculate future amounts of excluded interest.  Use the ratio of excluded funds to total funds in the new designation instead of the ratio in the prior designation.  Document the new ratio.

Replenishment

The individual may replenish the burial funds by adding money to the burial fund to bring the total up to the originally designated amount.  The amount of funds that can be excluded may be less than the amount the individual originally designated if the individual has purchased excluded life insurance or an irrevocable burial arrangement.

The individual cannot replenish excluded funds in excess of $1500, or the available burial fund exclusion, because the replenishment of the burial funds applies only to the amount originally designated.  Interest and appreciation are not part of the original designation and cannot be replenished.

Re-designation not required after use for another purpose

If the use of the burial funds does not reduce the amount of designated burial funds (not including interest or appreciation) to less than the amount originally designated, then re-designation will not be necessary.

The burial funds will be the amount originally designated plus any remaining accumulated interest or appreciation.  Document any change in the ratio of excluded to countable funds.
