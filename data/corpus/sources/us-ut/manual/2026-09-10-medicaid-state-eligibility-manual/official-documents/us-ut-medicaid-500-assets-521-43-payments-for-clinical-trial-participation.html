Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

521-43 Payments for Clinical Trial Participation

Effective Date: October 1, 2012

No Previous Policy

 

Aged, Blind or Disabled (Community or Long-term Care)

Exclude from countable resources the first $2,000 of compensation received by an individual per calendar year  for participating in a clinical trials for a rare disease or condition that meets the income exclusion criteria listed in 417-9, which is that each clinical trial:.

• must be reviewed and approved by an Institutional Review Board (IRB);

• must involve research and testing of medical treatments; and

• must target a rare disease or condition

This exclusion is valid until October 5, 2015.  After this date, any unspent compensation under this exclusion will count as a resource.  Excluded clinical trial compensation payments do not need to be separately identifiable.

This exclusion does not apply to Family Related programs.
