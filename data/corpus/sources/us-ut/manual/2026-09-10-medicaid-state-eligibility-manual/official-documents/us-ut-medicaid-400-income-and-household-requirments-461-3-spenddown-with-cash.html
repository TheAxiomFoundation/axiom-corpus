Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

461-3 Spenddown with Cash

Effective Date: June 1, 2020

No previous policy 

(Sections in 415 have been rewritten into 461 sections as of June 1, 2020)

 

A.   Cash Payments

Members may choose to meet their spenddown with a cash payment. They may also use cash after the agency has applied any health insurance premiums, or medical deductions to reduce the spenddown.

  1. To spenddown with cash, members must pay the Department of Workforce Services (DWS) the exact amount of the spenddown.  Do not require members to pay a spenddown that is less than $1.00.
  2. Cash payments include checks, money orders, credit or debit card payments.  members can mail payments to DWS, called in a payment using credit or debit cards, or pay online.  members should not mail cash to DWS.
  3. The premium paid by a Medicaid Work Incentive recipient will reduce the spenddown owed by another family member if the MWI premium is paid first. (462)
  4. Cash should only be used to meet a spenddown if the member does not have sufficient medical expenses to meet the spenddown, or upon the member’s specific request.
  5. Before having a member meet a spenddown with cash, deduct any allowable health insurance premiums, or Medicare premiums (when applicable) from countable income. (See 431-1.1)

a.    Apply medical bills that meet the criteria of a "medical deduction" to reduce the spenddown. These are expenses that do not meet the definition of an "incurred medical bill." Deductions include, but are not limited to:

§  Unpaid medical bills from months when the person did not have Medicaid.

§  Bills for medically necessary services not covered by the Medicaid service plan for which the member qualifies.

§  Medical bills for other family members (spouse, children) when those expenses will not be covered by Medicaid, CHIP or a third party.

b.    The member may meet any remaining amount owed with cash, but only when none of the medical bills used to reduce the spenddown meet the criteria of "incurred expenses". 

 

For changes that occur after making an eligibility decision, and possible refunds, see 460-4.
