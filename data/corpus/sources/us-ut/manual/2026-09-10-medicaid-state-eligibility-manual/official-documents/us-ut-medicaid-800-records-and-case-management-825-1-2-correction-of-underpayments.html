Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

825-1.2 Correction of Underpayments

Effective Date: October 1, 2010

No Previous Policy

  1. Utah’s medical assistance programs do not make payments directly to medical assistance members for medical services.  Consequently, do not use the Medical refund request procedure to correct underpayments.   

  2. When you approve eligibility for past months, but within 12 months from the date of the medical service, to correct an underpayment, the member must ask the provider to bill the medical assistance program for payment.  However, providers are not required to accept medical assistance payments after they have already provided the service.  

  3. When a fair hearing decision or court order overturns the agency’s decision, you must approve eligibility for the period of time covered by the hearing decision or court order.  The member must take the copy of the hearing decision or court order to the medical provider.  Medicaid can pay for a claim submitted after the 12 month period if the provider has documentation of the hearing decision or court order.
