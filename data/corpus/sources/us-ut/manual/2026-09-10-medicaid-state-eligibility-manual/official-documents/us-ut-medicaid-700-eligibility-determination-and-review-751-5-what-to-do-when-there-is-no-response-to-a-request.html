Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

751-5 What to Do When There is NO Response to a Request

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

No Previous Policy

 

IEVS income match reports may prompt a need to verify both current and past eligibility.

 

When the Request is about Current Eligibility

To continue benefits, you must resolve all questions about current eligibility.  If the individual does not respond and questions are not resolved, the individual is no longer eligible.  Close the case.  Give proper written ten-day notice.  Keep copies of all written requests and document on the case all other attempts to verify the income.  Be sure these comply with Safeguard Rules in Section 115.

 

When the Request Concerns ONLY Past Eligibility

Leave Medicaid benefits as they are when IEVS data concerns only past eligibility.

Ask the individual to provide verification to resolve the questions about past eligibility.

You may also request information from the source of income.  

Keep copies of letters and document on the case all attempts to obtain proof of the income.  Be sure these comply with Safeguard Rules in Section 111 and 115.

See section 825 If the information you receive indicates eligibility or liability errors in past months.
