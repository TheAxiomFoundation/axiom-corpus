Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

400 Income and Household Requirements

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

No Previous Policy

 

A.   Introduction

 

The organization of section 400 covers the following topics:

·       the steps needed to determine the household size and composition, including temporary absence, for the various programs;

·       policy about whose income must be counted to determine eligibility;

·       deeming income from a spouse, parent, or a non-citizen's sponsor;

·       the income calculation methodologies for each program;

·       the types of income, including unearned income and earned income;

·       income exclusions;

·       income deductions;

·       budgeting concepts and methods; and

·       policy about how to meet a payment requirement.
