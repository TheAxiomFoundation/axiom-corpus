Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

1009-7 When Married Couples Separate During the Certification

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

No Previous Policy

 

When a married couple separates and they are both UPP recipients and remain eligible for the employer-sponsored health insurance, remove one spouse from the original case and open a new case for that person for the remainder of the certification period.  At the end of the original certification period, both individuals will be required to complete an eligibility review.
