431-9.2 When to Deduct the SNA

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (431-9.2 was previously found in section 409-11.2 until November 1, 2021)

 

A.   When to Deduct the SNA

 

Deduct the monthly SNA beginning:

1.    the month the institutionalized spouse is determined eligible, or

2.    the month that the waiver spouse becomes eligible for Home and Community Based Waiver services, based on the date approved on the form 927.

 

B.   When to Stop deducting the SNA

 

Stop deducting the monthly SNA the month after:

1.    the institutionalized spouse leaves the medical institution, or

2.    the waiver spouse stops receiving waiver services, or

3.    the community spouse enters a medical institution, starts receiving HCB Waiver services, or dies.
