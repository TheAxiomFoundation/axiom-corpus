Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

460-2 Payment Notification

Effective Date: June 1, 2020

No Previous policy 

(Sections in 415 have been rewritten into 460 sections as of June 1, 2020)

 

Introduction

The agency must inform members when they must make a payment to receive Medicaid.

 

A.   Eligibility Agency Communication to the Member

 

1.    Send a notice to the member with the following information:

a.    Who qualifies for assistance

b.    What they must pay to receive coverage,

c.     Which months a payment is owed,

d.    When the payment is due,

e.    The different methods for meeting the payment, and

f.     Where they make the payment.

g.    For members who must meet a spenddown, include a copy of the 1049 form “Statement of Medical Need”.

2.           Notify members when we use incurred medical expenses to meet the spenddown or cost of care contribution. The notice must list the medical provider, the medical expense, which month we are using it for, and the amount the member is responsible to pay on each bill used.

 

B.   Member Communication to DWS

 

DWS must know which months a member wants to meet their payment. DWS may need to call members if they are not clear which month or months a member is trying to meet the payment.

 

1.    The member may complete the form 1049 and return it with their payment and/or their medical bills to DWS. They must indicate on the form which month, or months, they want to meet the payment. If it is not clear, contact the member to clarify.

a.    If a 1049 form is not included, the member must indicate in some fashion which month, or months, they want to meet the payment.

b.    Narrate the call and choice in the case notes and notify the eligibility worker or Business Office as applicable.

2.   If a member sends a payment and/or medical expenses without clearly indicating what months they want to meet their payment, the eligibility agency must contact the member to find out what months they want to receive coverage. The most expeditious option is to try calling the member.  (See 460-3 for due dates and returning payments)
