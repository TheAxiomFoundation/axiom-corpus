592-11 Burial Fund Exclusion and Unreported Assets

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

No Previous Policy

 

When you discover previously unreported assets, the individual may designate up to the $1500 maximum exclusion amount as a burial fund.  If the individual has other burial related assets, the available amount of the burial fund exclusion must be reduced by the other assets.  See sec. 592-5.  

 

The funds must meet all the requirements in subsection 592 before you can exclude them, including:

The funds must be designated as burial funds, and

The funds must be separated from non burial related assets, and

The assets must be burial funds, and

There must be no evidence that the funds were used for another purpose and replaced during the time the assets were unreported.  If the funds were used for other purposes, that is clear evidence that they were commingled with non-burial related assets or were not designated as burial funds.

The effective date of the exclusion would be:

For Aged, Blind, Disabled, MWI, and Medicare cost sharing, the first of the month following the month the individual :

said was the month the funds were designated for burial, and

separated the burial funds from non-burial related assets.

For Medically Needy Family, Child, and Pregnant Woman the day of the month the individual:

said was the day of the month the funds were designated for burial, and

separated the burial funds from non-burial related assets

Exclude interest that has been earned on excluded burial funds and left to accumulate in the account from the date the funds were designated for burial.
