Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

761 Prisoner Verification Match

Effective Date: September 1, 2016

Previous Policy

 

The Prisoner Verification System (PVS) is an automated match with the Social Security Administration’s (SSA) records on individuals who are incarcerated in correctional facilities around the United States.  The PVS interfaces with the SSA database using the same automated system that verifies Social Security numbers (SSN).  

 

The automated SSN query occurs on a daily basis when there is an application registration, an individual is added to a case, or a Social Security number is entered or added.  The match against the SSA prisoner records will occur as part of that same match.  In addition, the PVS match will run once a month on all active cases for household members who are 16 years of age and older.
