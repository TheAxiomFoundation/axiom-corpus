Medicaid Policy                                                                 

 

390 Medicaid Cancer Program

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

 

Contact DHHS Policy Specialist for Previous Policy

 

 The Medicaid Cancer program is for women and men who:

Are under age 65.

Have been determined by a Center for Disease Control (CDC) provider to need treatment for breast or cervical cancer or precancerous conditions of the breast or cervix.  

Do not have health coverage.

There are no income or asset limits for this program.

The Utah Cancer Control Program (UCCP) is the CDC provider that does cancer screenings in Utah.
