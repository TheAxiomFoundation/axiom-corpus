220 Health Insurance

Effective: June 1, 2016

Previous Policy

 

A child must meet the requirements defined in the following sections regarding enrollment in or access to a health insurance plan.  If the application lists any type of health insurance, the agency must verify the type of insurance before making an eligibility decision.
