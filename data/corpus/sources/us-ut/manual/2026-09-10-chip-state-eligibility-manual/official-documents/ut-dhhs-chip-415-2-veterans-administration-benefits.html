415-2 Veterans Administration Benefits

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (415-2 was previously found in 402-2 as of November 1, 2021)

VA income is not counted as income for CHIP eligibility using the MAGI-based methodology. This includes pensions, compensations, Aid and Attendance, Unusual Medical payments, Homebound Assistance, and educational income which may include an allowance for housing or subsistence.
