110 Safeguarding Information

Effective Date: January 1, 2014

No Previous Policy

 

The eligibility agency must safeguard or restrict access to any information on specific enrollees.  It does not matter whether this information is kept in the case record, in the computer system or somewhere else.  It can be shared only as described in Sections 111-1 and 111-3.  Any employee who fails to safeguard information is subject to both civil and criminal penalties.

 

There are strict rules for treatment of data from Income Match. (This is the Income Eligibility Verification System (IEVS) of computerized income matches among agencies.  See sections 110-1, 110-2, 111-3 #4 & 706 for details.)
