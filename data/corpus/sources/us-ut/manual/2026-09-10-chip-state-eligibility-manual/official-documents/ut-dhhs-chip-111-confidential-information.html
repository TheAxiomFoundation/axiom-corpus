111 Confidential Information

No Previous Policy

 

Confidential information cannot be shared with the enrollee.  This information is not shared with other people or agencies, unless it is necessary for the administration of assistance programs or to provide other social services.

 

The name of a person who has disclosed information about a household without the household's knowledge is confidential.  However, if the person disclosing the information states in writing that his name may be used, it is no longer considered confidential.

 

Any data regarding the nature or status of a pending criminal prosecution is confidential.
