701-6 Incarcerated Individuals in Jail or Prison

Effective Date: June 1, 2025

 

No Previous Policy

Applications received for an incarcerated individual (in a public institution) who are within 30 days of release and who meet all factors of eligibility, will be approved and placed in a suspended status. Deny all application that do not meet all factors of eligibility.
