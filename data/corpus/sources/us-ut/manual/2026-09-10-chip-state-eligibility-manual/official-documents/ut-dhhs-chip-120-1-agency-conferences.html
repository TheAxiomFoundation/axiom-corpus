120-1 Agency Conferences

Effective Date: July 1, 2010

Previous Policy

 

An agency conference is a less formal procedure than a fair hearing.  Its purpose is to quickly resolve problems that are simple and straightforward.

1.     The agency conference is not mandatory.   

2.      The enrollee has the right to proceed with a formal fair hearing regardless of the outcome of the agency conference, as long as the enrollee requests a hearing in a timely manner.

3.      The enrollee may request a fair hearing if the agency takes any new adverse case action as a result of the agency conference.

4.     The enrollee and agency staff, including a supervisor, participate in the agency conference.

5.      Document the results of the agency conference in the case record.  Take any appropriate case action.
