804-4 Removing Children From CHIP

Effective Date: September 1, 2022

 

Previous Policy

 

Children can be removed from CHIP without changing the renewal period as long as there is one eligible child still open on the case.

1.   When an eligible child becomes ineligible for CHIP, remove the child from the CHIP case giving 10 day notice.

2.   If an eligible child moves from one CHIP household to another household, the child may remain on the previous household’s case until the end of the 12 month renewal period.

3.   If the member was an eligible child while on CHIP and ages off, see 210.
