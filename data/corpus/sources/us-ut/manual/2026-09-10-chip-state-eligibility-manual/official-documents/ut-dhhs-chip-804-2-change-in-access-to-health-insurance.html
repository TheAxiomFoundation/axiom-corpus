804-2 Change in Access to Health Insurance

Effective Date: February 1, 2012

 

A CHIP enrollee who becomes covered by a health insurance plan or gains access to an employer sponsored health insurance plan must report this change within 10 days of the date of the change.  

If a CHIP enrollee gains access to or becomes covered by health insurance under an employer-sponsored plan or COBRA coverage, the CHIP enrollee may qualify for UPP.

  • The enrollee must report the health insurance within 10 calendar days of enrolling, or within 10 calendars days of when coverage begins, whichever is later.

  • The employer-sponsored plan must meet UPP criteria.

Failure to make a timely report may result in an overpayment.
