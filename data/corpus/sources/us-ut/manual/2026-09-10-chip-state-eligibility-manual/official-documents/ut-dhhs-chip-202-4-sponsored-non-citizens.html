202-4 Sponsored Non-Citizens

Effective date: January 1, 2014

Previous Policy

 

Do not deem sponsor's income when determining eligibility for CHIP, unless the sponsor is someone whose MAGI-based income must be counted according to the MAGI rules.  Do count actual cash contributions the sponsor gives to the non-citizen.  See sec.  412 for MAGI-based income rules.

 

 

Iraqi and Afghan Special Immigrants are not required to have sponsors, so sponsor deeming does not apply at any time.
