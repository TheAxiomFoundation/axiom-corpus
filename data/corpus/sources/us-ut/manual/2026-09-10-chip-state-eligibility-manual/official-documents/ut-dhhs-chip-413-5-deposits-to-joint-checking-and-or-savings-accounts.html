413-5 Deposits to Joint Checking and/ or Savings Accounts

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

No Previous Policy

Deposits to Joint Checking and/ or Savings Accounts

1.    Do not count deposits to joint checking or savings accounts made by a non-household member.

2.    The only deposits to joint accounts that are countable are those that are income of the applicant or enrollee, or household member, when that person's income and the type of income received must count under the MAGI-based income methodology.
