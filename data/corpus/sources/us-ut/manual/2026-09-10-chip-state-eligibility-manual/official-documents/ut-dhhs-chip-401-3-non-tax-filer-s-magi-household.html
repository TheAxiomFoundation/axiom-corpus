401-3 Non-Tax Filer’s MAGI Household

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

Previous Policy (401-3 was previously found in 230-3 as of November 1, 2021)

 

A.   Non-Tax Filer Household 

1.    A non-tax filer is someone who states they do not intend to file a tax return and do not expect to be claimed as a dependent by someone else for the tax year in which the eligibility agency is making an eligibility determination. Accept the client's statement that they do not intend to file a tax return and does not expect to be claimed as a dependent by someone else.

2.    The MAGI household for a non-tax filer is based on who lives together and the relationships of those individuals. These rules apply to the non-tax-filer, the non-filer's family, and to certain individuals claimed as a dependent. 

The non-tax filer rules apply to:

a.    A child applicant who meets the age criteria not living parents and who does not expect to file a tax return nor be claimed as a dependent by any other tax filer;

b.    A child who meets the age criteria in section 210 and who lives with:

·       Parent(s) who do not expect to file a tax return, whether or not the child will file taxes.

·       One parent but is claimed as a tax dependent by a non-custodial parent.

·       Both parents (biological or adoptive), and is claimed by one parent, but the parents do not file a joint return.

·       One parent that plans to file a tax return with their estranged spouse and the couple is permanently separated.  The estranged spouse will not be included in the MAGI household.

·          A child who is placed with a tax filer for adoption.

o      Whose adoption has not yet been finalized before the court; and

o      Whose adopting parents are claiming them as a tax dependent.

3.    A child who is claimed as a tax dependent by someone other than a spouse or legal parent, regardless of whether the person will file a tax return of their own.  

B.   MAGI Household for Children who must use Non-Filer Rules

The MAGI household size for a child living in a situation described in A.2., will follow non-tax filer rules to determine the MAGI household size.

1.    To determine the MAGI household size, include the following individuals living with the child:

·        The child;

·       All parents of the child;

·       All siblings of the child who are

o      Under age 19; or

o      Age 19 or 20 and enrolled in school full-time.

·       The number of expected unborn children of anyone included in the household who is pregnant;

·       If the child is married, the spouse of the child; and

·        Any children of the child or child's spouse who meet the age criteria in c.

2.    Temporary absence rules apply (402-1).
