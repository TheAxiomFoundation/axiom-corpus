417-8 Payments under the National Flood Insurance Program

Effective Date: November 1, 2021

No Previous Policy

 

Payments under the National Flood Insurance Program

 

1.    Do not count as income any assistance provided under a program for flood mitigation activities with respect to a property the individual owns.

2.    Interest earned on any retained funds is also excluded from income.
