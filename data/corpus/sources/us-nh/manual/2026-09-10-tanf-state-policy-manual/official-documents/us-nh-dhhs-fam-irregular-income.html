IRREGULAR INCOME (FAM)

SR 25-07 Dated 02/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Any income received in the certification period too infrequently to be reasonably anticipated. Such income may not be in excess of $30 per quarter of earned income or $60 per quarter of unearned income.

Treatment: Excluded Income

 

References: He-W 601.03(t); RSA 167:4 (I)(a); RSA 167:80; 45 CFR 233.20(a)(3)(iv)(F); 42 U.S. Code § 1382a (b)(3)
