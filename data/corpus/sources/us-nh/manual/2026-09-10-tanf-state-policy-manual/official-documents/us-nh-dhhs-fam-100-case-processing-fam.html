100 CASE PROCESSING (FAM)

 

Chapter 100 contains information regarding the processing of Financial Assistance to Needy Families (FANF) cases, including:

•       individual’s rights and responsibilities;

•       the initial application process;

•       benefit delivery time frames;

•       hardship extensions;

•       notices;

•       changes;

•       redeterminations; and

•       termination of assistance.
