315.07 Informing the Household about Voluntary Quit (FAM)

SR 11-30 Dated 07/11

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

When households apply for *FANF assistance, advise them of voluntary quit policy at the interactive interview.

Provide NHEP-eligible *FANF applicants with Form 221, New Hampshire Employment Program (NHEP), an informational pamphlet that includes information about voluntary quit.
