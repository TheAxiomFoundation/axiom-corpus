919.01 Required Verification (FAM)

SR 20-23 Dated 08/20

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

See Section 941.01, Required Verification for a Child Experiencing a Disability or Significant Special Need.

For chronic, non-changing special needs, verification is required only once. For all others, verification is required annually.

References: He-C 6910.09(j), 45 CFR 98.20
