The assistance group’s countable personal and real property resources cannot exceed the limits below:

 

FANF Financial *Assistance

 

Group Size

Resource Limit

Any

Applicant $1,000

Recipient $5,000

 

The term recipient includes individuals who currently receive FANF financial *assistance or have received FANF financial *assistance in at least 1 of the last 6 months.

 

*
