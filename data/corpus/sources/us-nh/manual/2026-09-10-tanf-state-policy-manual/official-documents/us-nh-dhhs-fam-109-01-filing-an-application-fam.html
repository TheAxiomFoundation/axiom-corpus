109.01 Filing an Application (FAM)

SR 14-08 Dated 01/14

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Individuals have the right to file an application on the same day they contact the District Office. They are directed to NH EASY, DHHS websites, or the individual will be given BFA Form 800, Application for Assistance to apply.

 

To submit an application for FANF assistance the document must at minimum contain the individual’s:

•       name;

•       address;

•       signature, or the signature of an authorized representative (AR) acting on behalf of the applicant; and

•       Indicate which program(s) of assistance they are requesting.

 Applications can be submitted:

•    online via the NH EASY website at www.nheasy.nh.gov;

•    Face-to-face at the District Office;

•      via mail-in; or

•      through a voice recorded signature captured by the department.

 

The official Filing Date for applications for Financial Assistance to Needy Families (FANF) is the date when the application is completed and received by the department, during business hours on a day the office is open.  If received after hours or on a day when the office is closed for business, the official filing date of the application is the next day the department is open for business.

 

Exception: When a resident of an institution applies for assistance prior to leaving the institution, the filing date of the application is the date the applicant is released from the institution.

 

References: He-W 601.01; He-W 601.03; RSA 167:8; RSA 167:78,II; 7 CFR 273.2(b)(3); 45 CFR 206.10(a)(1) & (b)(1)-(3)
