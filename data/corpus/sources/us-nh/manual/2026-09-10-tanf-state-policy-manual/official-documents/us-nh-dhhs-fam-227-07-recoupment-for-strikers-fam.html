227.07 Recoupment for Strikers (FAM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Recoup any financial assistance paid to individuals who were on strike on the last day of the month:

·         If the entire assistance group was ineligible, recoup all financial assistance paid; and

·         If an individual other than the parent/caretaker relative included was ineligible due to a strike, recoup the amount by which the grant would decrease if the individual were removed from the financial assistance group.

 

References: He-W 634.01; RSA 167:79, III(l)
