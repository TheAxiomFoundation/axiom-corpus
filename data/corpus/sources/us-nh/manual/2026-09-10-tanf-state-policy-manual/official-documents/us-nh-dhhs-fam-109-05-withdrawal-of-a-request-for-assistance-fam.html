109.05 Withdrawal of a Request for Assistance (FAM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

An applicant may voluntarily withdraw a request for assistance for any reason, at any time before an eligibility determination is made.

 

If the applicant chooses to withdraw their application:

•       They are notified that all rights to an administrative appeal are forfeited in the withdrawal process;

•       The withdrawal is documented in the applicants case record, along with the reason, if given; and

•       They have the right to re-apply at any time by submitting a new application for assistance.

 

References: RSA 161:4-a, II-III; RSA 167:78,II; 45 CFR 206.10(a)
