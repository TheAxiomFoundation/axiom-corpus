Tuition assistance is only available to NHEP participants when all other sources of funding have been exhausted, as current funding allows.

 

Tuition assistance must be authorized on the EP and be consistent with the specified employment goals.

Tuition assistance may be provided if the vocational education and training program or course of study preparatory to employment meets the following requirements:

•       prepares the individual for employment; and

•       leads to a certificate in an area of specialization.

Tuition assistance may be provided for a single training course or course(s) of study provided the course(s):

•       is vocationally specific; and

•       improves the individual’s immediate employability.

 

Tuition Assistance Payment Limits

The limit for tuition assistance is $2,500 per NHEP participant, per State Fiscal year beginning on July 1 and ending on June 30.

 

Tuition Assistance Time Limits

Basic Education: Tuition assistance shall be authorized for a maximum period of 2 years, for programs leading to a high school diploma or equivalent or leading to basic literacy classes that meet for a minimum of 30 hours per week.

Exception: The time limits given above do not apply to individuals with a documented learning disability as verified by a state certified educational professional or licensed psychologist. To extend participation time, the documentation must justify the need and specify the length of time required.

 

Participant Requirements

Tuition assistance is authorized for NHEP participants when the individual:

•       has agreed upon a vocational education and training goal with the NHEP representative;

•       participated in an assessment interview and developed an employability plan (EP) that has been approved by the NHEP representative; and

•       been enrolled in an approved activity and is complying with the program and its required hours indicated in the EP or making a good faith effort to participate, if just beginning an approved activity.

 

Provider Requirements

The provider must:

•       be an enrolled provider with the department in order to receive direct payment;

•       complete and sign the provider section of Form NHEP257; and

•       forward the completed form to the Data Management Unit

 

Verification Requirements

Individuals will provide signed and dated statements from the school or training site coordinator stating:

•       individual is enrolled;

•       class schedule; and

•       length of program.
