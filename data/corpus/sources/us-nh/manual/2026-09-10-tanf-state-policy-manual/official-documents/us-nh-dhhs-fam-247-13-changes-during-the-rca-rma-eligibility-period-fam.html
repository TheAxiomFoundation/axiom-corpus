247.13 Changes During the RCA Eligibility Period (FAM)

SR 25-24 Dated 07/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA) must be terminated if changes in the refugee’s circumstances cause the refugee to become ineligible for assistance prior to the end of the eligibility period. For more information see section 247.11, RCA Length of Eligibility.

For information on Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA), see Medical Assistance Manual PART  263, REFUGEE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE (RMA).

 

References: 45 CFR 400.1-45 CFR 400.107
