905 LENGTH OF ELIGIBILITY (FAM)

SR 25-10 Dated 02/25

___________________________________________________________

 

Child Care Scholarship eligibility is determined for a 12-month period of time, regardless of eligibility for other DHHS programs of assistance. This is called a certification period. Families are notified when it is time to redetermine their eligibility. If the family fails to complete the redetermination process by the end of the certification period, child care assistance will close with no additional notification. The individual must reapply and be determined eligible for NH Child Care Scholarship again.

 

The month of application is the first month of the eligibility period, regardless of what day of the month the individual applies. The eligibility period begins on the day the application is received or the child care provider link date, whichever is later.

 

Example: If an application for child care is received on May 26 and the child care provider link date is June 3rd, the eligibility period begins June 3rd and will end June 30 of the following year.

 

If a household adds an eligible child during their 12-month certification period, the redetermination date shall advance to provide the full 12 months of eligibility for the additional child.

 

Example: A child is receiving child care scholarship in an open case and their initial eligibility period started in December. On June 28th, the family wants to apply for child care for their newborn child, the new eligible child is added to the existing child care case with a link date of July 3rd. The case is confirmed on July 3rd. The child care redetermination period will extend to July of the following year due to the addition of the new eligible child in order to ensure a full 12 months of eligibility for the new child. The existing child’s redetermination date for child care will match the new child’s redetermination date.

 

Cases eligible for the online or mail-in redetermination process are not required to participate in a face-to-face/phone interview (see Section 161.01, Timely Notice).
