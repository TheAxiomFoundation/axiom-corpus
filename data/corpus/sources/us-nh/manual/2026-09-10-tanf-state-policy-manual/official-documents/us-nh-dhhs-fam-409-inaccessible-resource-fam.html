INACCESSIBLE RESOURCE (FAM)

SR 14-04 Dated 01/14

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Resources that are not legally available to the assistance group.

Examples of inaccessible resources include:

• jointly owned property (see Jointly Owned Personal Property and Real Property),

• security deposits held by another,

• property currently in probate, and

• irrevocable trust funds*

• irrevocable burial funds.

Treatment: Excluded Resource
