247.11 RCA Length of Eligibility (FAM)

SR 26-25 Dated 09/26

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Most recipients of Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA) are eligible for services for a limited timeframe from the date of entry into the United States, or date asylum was granted.

 

If the date of entry into the U.S. or, for asylees, the date asylum was granted is January 1, 2026, or later, eligibility is up to 8 months, starting from the date of entry or, for asylees, the date asylum was granted. 

 

For information on Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA), see Medical Assistance Manual PART  263, REFUGEE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE (RMA).
