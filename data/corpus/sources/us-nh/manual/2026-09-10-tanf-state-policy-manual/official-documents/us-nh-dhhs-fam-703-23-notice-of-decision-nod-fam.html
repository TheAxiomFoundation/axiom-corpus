703.23 Notice of Decision (NOD) (FAM)

SR 05-07 Dated 12/05

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

New HEIGHTS issues automated notices for all EA permanent housing services other than multiple security deposits.

For each subsequent security deposit request authorized during the 12-month period, send a manual * Notice of Decision (NOD) within 15 working days from the date the application was filed in the District Office.

Include all the following on the NOD:

• the eligibility decision;

• the type and amount of any assistance approved;

• an explanation of how an approved amount was computed, if it is less than the amount requested;

• the reason for a denial; and

• Form 810, Rights and Responsibilities.
