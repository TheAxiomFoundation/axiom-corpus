119 ADDING A PROGRAM (FAM)

SR 04-04 Dated 05/04

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

If an applicant adds another program during their initial interview or if a current recipient adds a new program during a scheduled redetermination/recertification interview:

•     A new application and a separate interview are not needed;

•     The applicant is given the EIS to review and is allowed 10 days to make changes; and

•     The application date for the add-a-program is the date the interview is conducted.

Example: An individual submits an application to apply for SNAP with an application date of May 13th , and is scheduled for an interview on May 17th.  During the SNAP interview, they chose to add Financial Assistance to Needy Families (FANF). The FANF application date would be May 17th , and no separate interview or new application is needed.

 

Example: SNAP recipient with a recertification interview scheduled on September 20th  adds FANF assistance during the recertification - The FANF application date is September 20th , and no separate interview or new application is needed.

 

 

Note: If an individual adds a program to an online or paper (mail-in) redetermination/application, an interview may be required to be scheduled.

 

Example: SNAP recipient with an online/paper (mail-in) recertification adds FANF financial assistance on their 800R, Application for Continued Eligibility for Financial, Medical, Child Care and SNAP Benefits - The individual will need to be scheduled for an interview.

 

 

When the same eligibility factors are involved for each program, verification(s) provided to meet the requirements for one program  may also be used to meet the verification requirements for all other programs requested.

 

Example: If each requested program of assistance requires verification of citizenship and the applicant provides a Birth Certificate, the Birth Certificate satisfies citizenship verification requirements for all programs requested.

 

 

 

Note: The eligibility criteria for each program of assistance may require different verifications that may not be needed for other programs.

 

Example: The SNAP program uses shelter deductions when determining SNAP eligibility, but the FANF program does not. Therefore, shelter and utility costs may be requested for SNAP eligibility, but are not needed for FANF eligibility.

 

 

The eligibility decision is not delayed for one program when all the required verification(s) for that specific program are provided, but not for the other(s).

 

Application processing timeframes must be met for each program of assistance for which the individual applied. If applicants are denied for one program while a decision is pending for other programs, they are not required to file a new application. The original application is used to determine eligibility for the remaining programs.

 

References: He-W 601.01(p); He-W 602.03; RSA 167:8
