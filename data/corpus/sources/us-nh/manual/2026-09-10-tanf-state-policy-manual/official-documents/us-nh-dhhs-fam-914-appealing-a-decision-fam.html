914 APPEALING A DECISION (FAM)

SR 20-23 Dated 08/20

Previous Policy

________________________________________________________

A parent has 30 calendar days from the date on the notification of an eligibility decision regarding NH Child Care Scholarship to request an appeal.

 

In order for NH Child Care Scholarship to continue at the previously determined level during the Administrative Appeal process, a parent must request continuation of the NH Child Care Scholarship by filing an appeal within 15 calendar days from the date on the notification.

 

If the original decision is upheld by the Administrative Appeals Officer, the NH Child Care Scholarship will be denied, decreased or terminated effective the date indicated on the original notification. The parent must repay in full the extra assistance they received as a result of the continued benefit.

 

References: He-C 6910.19, RSA 167:83,II(a), RSA 541-A:31,I and II, 45 CFR 98:20
