SR 23-40 Dated 12/23

Previous Policy

Mandatory and voluntary participants may be approved for the following NHEP work program activities:

• Alternative Work Experience Program (AWEP)

• Basic Education

• Community Work Experience Program (CWEP)

• Employment

• English as a Second Language (ESL)

• Interim Activities

• CWEP

• Job Readiness

• Job Search

• Job Skills Training Directly Related to Employment

• On The Job Training (OJT)

• Post-Secondary Education

• Self-Employment

• Single Course

• Vocational Educational Training
