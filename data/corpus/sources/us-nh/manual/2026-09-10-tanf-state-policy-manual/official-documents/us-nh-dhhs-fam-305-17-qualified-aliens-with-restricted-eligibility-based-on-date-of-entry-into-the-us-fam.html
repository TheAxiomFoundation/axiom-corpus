305.17 Qualified Aliens with Restricted Eligibility Based on Date of Entry into the U.S. (FAM)

SR 00-08 Dated 02/00

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

The following qualified aliens are EITHER:

•       eligible - if residing in the U.S. before 8/22/96; OR

•       ineligible for 5 years from date of entry into the U.S. -  when such entry occurred on or after 8/22/96:

-    lawfully admitted permanent residents regardless of work history;

-    conditional entrants;

-    parolees; and

-    victims of family/domestic violence.
