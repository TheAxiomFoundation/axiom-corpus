808.39 English as a Second Language (ESL) (FAM)

SR 09-22 Dated 07/09

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

English as a Second Language (ESL) is a secondary activity that may be included on an NHEP participant’s employability plan.

•       This activity must be supervised daily.

 

The countable hours must be verified and documented no less than once a month. Participants must submit Form NHEP266, NHEP Education/Training Activities Verification Form, or other verifications approved by the NHEP Representative and signed by the education or training organization and returned to NHEP on a weekly basis.

 

References: He-W 637’s; RSA 167:77; RSA 167:79; RSA 167:85; RSA 167:88; RSA 167:91
