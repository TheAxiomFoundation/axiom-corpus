503 AVAILABLE AND ANTICIPATED INCOME (FAM)

SR 25-07 Dated 02/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Income (earned or unearned) is considered available when an assistance group member receives it on a regular and recurring basis. Regular and recurring means an income amount that the assistance group can reasonably anticipate for use each month. However, some income is not received by the assistance group yet is considered available to the assistance group anyway. See Deemed Income. For income that is infrequent see Irregular Income.

 

Anticipated income is considered available when its receipt is reasonably certain, and the income is intended to continue in the future. These cases are handled on a case-by-case basis.

 

Income that represents contributions, panhandling, or compensation for a period of more than 1 month, or varies steadily from month to month, is averaged to obtain a monthly figure. The monthly average is then considered to be the available income expected for the household’s use each month.

 

Example: During an initial interview for an application dated 4/25/2024, Tanya stated she is meeting her phone bill expense by panhandling once a week. She states she started panhandling in March 2024 and intends to continue through her certification period. Tanya provides the following verifications of her panhandling income for March:

 

-3/7/2024: $55

-3/14/2024: $32

-3/21/24: $74

-3/28/2024: $91

 

Because this income is considered available and anticipated to continue, this would be countable income. The monthly average unearned income would be $272.79. ($55+$32+$74+91=$252/4 (weeks)=$63 weekly average X4.33= $272.79).
