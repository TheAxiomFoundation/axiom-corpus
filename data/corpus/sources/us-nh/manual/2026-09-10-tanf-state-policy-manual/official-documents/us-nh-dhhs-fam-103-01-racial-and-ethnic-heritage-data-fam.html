103.01 Identifying and Demographic Data Collection (FAM)

SR 24-10 Dated 04/24

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Certain aspects of an individual’s personal identifying information are captured and recorded in New HEIGHTS for required federal reporting. Such as:

•       Individual's social security number;

•       Amount of assistance received;

•       Educational level;

•       Employment status;

•       Work participation activities;

•       Citizenship status; and

•       Earned and unearned income.

 

The above data must be provided for both adults and children.

Racial, ethnic heritage and primary language data is also recorded if it is provided.

 

When race and ethnicity is not provided either verbally or in writing, the options provided are left unchecked. This will then translate as ‘unknown’ for Federal reporting.

 

Language is not a mandatory field for data collection, but is a required field in New HEIGHTS. If the individual’s spoken language is not known to the department, the information is retained upon asking the client the type of language translation service they require.

 

References: 45 CFR 265.3(b); 42 USC 611(a)(1)(vii) & (viii)
