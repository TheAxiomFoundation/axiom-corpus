709.09 Time Frame for Electronic Stolen Benefits (FAM)

SR 23-36 Dated 09/23

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Approved replacement for benefits stolen by electronic means must be issued within 10 calendar days of receiving the individual’s signed statement or BFA Form 473. See Section 709.07, Replacing Electronic Stolen Benefits, for more information on this topic.
