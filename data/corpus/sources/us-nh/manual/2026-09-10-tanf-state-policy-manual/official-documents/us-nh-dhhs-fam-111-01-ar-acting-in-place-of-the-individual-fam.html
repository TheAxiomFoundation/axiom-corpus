111.01 Authorized Representative Acting on Behalf of the Individual (FAM)

SR 17-07 Dated 05/17

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

To act on behalf of the individual, the Authorized Representative (AR) must: 

•       Be an adult (age 18 or older) or an agency who has:

-      expressed concern for the individual's well-being,

-    sufficient knowledge about the individual's circumstances to assist in the application for and/or the continuation of assistance, and

-    the capability of obtaining information about the individual's circumstances.

An individual who chooses an AR to help with some or all the responsibilities of applying for or receiving FANF must:

•       Complete BFA Form 778, Authorized Representative Declaration; or

•       Provide all the following in writing:

-       The name, address, and telephone number of the AR;

 

-       The duties that the AR will carry out;

 

-       The individual's relationship to the AR;

 

-       A statement signed and dated by the individual acknowledging:

 

o    His or her responsibility for any errors, omissions, failures to report information to DHHS, or inaccurate information reported to DHHS by the AR;

 

o    That if the AR uses the individual’s benefits without permission, these benefits will not be replaced by DHHS;

 

o    That the person the individual names as the AR will continue to act for the individual until the individual or the AR tells DHHS of a change; and

 

o    Comprehension of the individual’s choice of AR and the duties assigned to that AR; and

 

-       A statement signed and dated by the AR:

 

o    Agreeing to accept the responsibilities designated by the individual;

 

o    Acknowledging that the AR understands that:

 

Ø  Proof of the AR’s identity is required;

 

Ø  If disqualified for a program violation, the person identified as the AR can no longer act as an AR unless there is no one else suitable to represent the individual; and

 

Ø  The AR will continue to act as an AR for the individual until the AR or the individual tells DHHS of a change.

 

References: He-W 601.01; He-W 603.01; RSA 21-B:1; 45 CFR 206.10(a)(1)(iii)
