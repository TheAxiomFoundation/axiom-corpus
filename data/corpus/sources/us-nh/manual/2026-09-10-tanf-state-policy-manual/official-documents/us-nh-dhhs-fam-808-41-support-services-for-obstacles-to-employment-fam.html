808.41 Support Services for Obstacles to Employment (FAM)

SR 26-14 Dated 04/26

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________________

Employment and training support (ETS) services are available for participants of the New Hampshire Employment Program (NHEP) to assist in aiding the removal of employment related obstacles.

 

Removal of these employment obstacles must be essential and necessary for the individual to participate or remain in an approved NHEP activity, including employment.

 

A total statewide budget is established each fiscal year and is set aside for ETS services. Once this funding is exhausted, support services will be denied for the remainder of the fiscal year.

 

 

ETS Services Available for Obstacles to Employment

The following ETS Services may be available to NHEP participants as funding allows:

•       Auto insurance - for only the usual and customary rates, not for higher rates due to accidents, violations, etc.;

•       Auto registration fees;

•       Driver’s license fee - for both operator and commercial licenses, or replacement fee;

•       Adult cosmetic dental care for services not covered under Medicaid;

•       Office clothing (non-uniform attire) needed for job interviews or to begin employment; and

•       Additional funding for obstacles to employment (including personal care items) subject to Bureau of Employment Supports (BES) approval.

-    Additional obstacles may include basic driver’s education courses but does not include alcohol education courses or any courses required due to violations such as driving while intoxicated (DWI), etc.

NOTE:Approval for a driver’s education course will only be considered if the individual has a vehicle or immediate access to one.

 

 

Payment for ETS services is requested using Form NHEP255, Employment and Training Support Services Payment Request Invoice.

 

 

Required Prior Approval for ETS Services

If the support service being requested is for Other ETS Services, prior to approval of the service, the NHEP representative will write a justification memo demonstrating the need for the support service to  the administrator of BES, State Office. The memo will include:

•       the individual’s name;

•       the type of obstacle;

•       the cost of the service;

•       other resources that have been explored;

•       the team member requesting the approval of the service; and

•       any other pertinent information.

Written notification of approval/disapproval will be sent to the NHEP Representative within two weeks from receipt of the request.

 

 

Participant Requirements

ETS Services are authorized for NHEP participants when the individual has:

•       agreed upon a vocational education and training goal with the with the NHEP Representative;

•       participated in an assessment interview and developed an employability plan that has been approved by the NHEP representative; and

•       been enrolled in an approved activity and is complying with the program and its required hours indicated in the EP or making a good faith effort to participate, if just beginning an approved activity.

 

 

NHEP Requirements

The NHEP Representative may authorize reimbursement for more than one type of employment obstacle, if resolution of those obstacles will lead to employment, enable the individual to keep employment, or to participate or remain in an approved NHEP activity specified on the EP.

Approval for payment to remove obstacles to employment must be done on the EP in advance of the actual service and documented in the case.

The NHEP Representative will follow up with the participant and verifying that the funds provided to the participant were used for the purpose intended and that the service(s) enabled the participant to maintain current employment or obtain immediate employment.

If the participant cannot provide verification that the funds were spent on an authorized service or services, The NHEP representative will advise that the participant that they must provide a reimbursement check made payable to, "Treasurer, State of New Hampshire," for the amount that was provided and not verified.

If the participant is unable or unwilling to provide reimbursement:

•       request by memo that the Data Management Unit (DMS) put a balance due on the case for the amount of reimbursement owed back to the department; or

•       if fraud is suspected, make a referral to the Special Investigations Unit.

 

 

Service & Payment Limits for Obstacles to Employment

There are 3 separate payment limits for Other ETS Services:

•       $1,500 per state fiscal year per individual for adult cosmetic dental care. Receipt of adult cosmetic dental care does not affect payment for other services available to remove obstacles to employment;

•       $1,500 per state fiscal year per individual for additional ETS services if needed; and

•       $1,500 per state fiscal year per individual for other ETS services for obstacles to employment combined (auto insurance, auto registration, driver’s license fee, office clothing).  

 

All other potential sources of funding must be explored before any payment under this policy is authorized.

 

Certain other ETS services have payment limits and conditions; others have no specific limit on the amount of times the specific support service can be authorized. As long as the overall total of all services does not exceed the state fiscal limits specified.  

 

The NHEP team may also authorize any amount up to the maximum payment limit for a specific ETS service.

•       An individual may receive payment for more than one Other ETS service mentioned above, provided that the total amount for all services combined (excluding adult cosmetic dental care and additional supports, as they have a separate limit) does not exceed $1,500 in a state fiscal year; or

•       An individual may receive payment for one particular service that equals the maximum amount for all Other ETS services of $1,500, only if no additional Other ETS services excluding adult cosmetic dental care and additional supports are authorized in the same state fiscal year.

 

If any ETS service request exceed the payment limits stated for that particular support service, or maximum payment limit for the service, the NHEP participant must demonstrate the following, prior to the NHEP representative authorizing funds from the additional support service:

•       How the remainder of the request will be secured;

•       How the payment authorized by NHEP will remove the employment related barrier; and

•       Verification that a payment plan has been arranged for the remainder of the bill.

 

Verification Requirements

Auto insurance, auto registration, and driver’s license fees may be billed prior to the services actually being provided. An estimate or statement of cost must be received with Form NHEP255.

 

For all Other ETS services, an itemized bill must be attached to Form NHEP255. In instances where an estimate or statement of cost is used for the initial billing of the service, after the payment is made to the provider or the participant, the participant is than required to send in the following verifications to the NHEP representative:

•       Auto insurance - a statement of coverage and charges from the insurance carrier;

•       Auto registration - a copy of the new registration; and

•       Driver’s license - a photocopy of the new driver’s license.
