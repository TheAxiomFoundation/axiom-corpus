109.11 Eligibility for Multiple Types of Financial Assistance (FAM)

SR 24-10 Dated 04/24

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

An individual has the right to apply for any category of financial assistance administered by the Department. They may not receive financial assistance under more than one program of assistance being administered, at the same time.

 

Individuals receiving Financial Assistance to Needy Families (FANF) assistance who subsequently become eligible for financial assistance under a State Supplement Program (SSP) such as, Old Age Assistance (OAA), Aid to the Permanently and Totally Disabled (APTD), or Aid to the Needy Blind (ANB), may not receive financial assistance under that program for any months in which FANF assistance has already been received. Even if the recipient voluntarily repays DHHS for FANF assistance received in the past. See FAM 211.01, Who Must be Members.

 

FANF recipients applying for financial assistance under SSP assistance are notified of this restriction at the time of their application for SSP.

 

An individual may be eligible for and receive one category of financial assistance, while other members of the same assistance group are eligible for and receive financial assistance under another category.

 

Example: HH consist of Michael (dad), and Gregory (Michael’s son) and Sophia (Michael’s niece). Michael and Gregory are receiving New Hampshire Employment Program (NHEP) and Sophia is receiving Financial Assistance Program (FAP).
