909.01 Changes in NH Child Care Scholarship Due to Birthdays (FAM)

SR 20-23 Dated 08/20

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

The NH Child Care Scholarship Weekly Standard Rate is based on the child’s age in months, child care provider type, and the authorized level of service, and is paid on a weekly basis.

 

At initial application, deny child care if the child turns age 13 within 30 days of the application date.

Children cannot be found initially eligible for NH Child Care Scholarship at age 13.

NH Child Care Scholarship terminates for children turning 13 years old *at the end of their 12-month eligibility period.

 

A child under the age of 72 months, regardless of the family’s Step level, cannot receive NH Child Care Scholarship if the child attends a License-Exempt Child Care Center.

 

Exception: A child may continue to be eligible for NH Child Care Scholarship up to age 18 if the child is experiencing a disability or significant special need whose condition limits the child’s ability to care  for him  or herself or would cause harm to him or  herself or others without supervision. See Section 941.01, Required Verification for a Child Experiencing a Disability or Significant Special Needs.
