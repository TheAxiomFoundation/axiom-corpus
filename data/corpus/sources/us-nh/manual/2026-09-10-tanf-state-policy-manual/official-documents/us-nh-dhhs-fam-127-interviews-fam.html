127 INTERVIEWS (FAM)

SR 23-04 Dated 02/23

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Interviews are official and confidential discussions of household circumstances between the applicant, recipient or Authorized Representative (AR) and the department staff member, in order to:

• Discuss all circumstances which have a bearing on eligibility;

• Resolve any unclear or incomplete information;

• Advise the applicant, recipient or AR of the eligibility requirements and the applicant’s rights and responsibilities; and

• Afford applicants or recipients an opportunity to ask any questions about the department’s assistance programs.

 

Interviews are conducted prior to each initial determination and each redetermination of eligibility.

Exceptions:

• Cases eligible to be redetermined via the mail-in or an online redetermination process are not required to have an interview, however one must be granted if it is requested. See PART 161, REDETERMINATION and Section 161.01, Timely Notice.

• If the information provided for an initial determination of eligibility establishes that the individual is    ineligible, the department may deny the case without an interview.

 

The department may also require an interview as a result of reported changes or the discovery of conflicting information regarding eligibility criteria.

 

 

References: He-W 601.06; He-W 636.01; He-W 684.01-02; RSA 167:83(II)(c)-(d); RSA 167:79,III(j)
