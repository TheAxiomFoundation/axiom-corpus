BURIAL FUND (FAM)

SR 06-06 Dated 04/06

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

A burial fund is any separately identifiable fund that has been designated for burial expenses, such as a burial contract, burial trust, or burial arrangement.

A burial contract is a written agreement between an individual and a funeral home to pay for the individual’s funeral and burial expenses.

Consider only those burial funds that are accessible to the household. Irrevocable burial contracts or trusts, for example, are considered inaccessible resources. * See also Inaccessible Resources and Life Insurance.

Treatment: Exclude one burial contract for each assistance group member, up to $1,500. Count the portion of the equity (cash surrender) value which exceeds $1,500 as a resource unless the value of the resource is otherwise excluded by policy.
