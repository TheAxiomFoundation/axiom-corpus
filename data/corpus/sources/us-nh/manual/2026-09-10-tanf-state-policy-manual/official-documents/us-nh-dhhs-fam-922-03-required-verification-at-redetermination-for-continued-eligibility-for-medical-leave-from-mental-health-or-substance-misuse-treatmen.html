922.03 Required Verification at Redetermination for Continued Eligibility for Medical Leave from Mental Health or Substance Misuse Treatment Program for a Recipient of NHEP or FAP (FAM)

SR 20-23 Dated 08/20

  

_____________________________________________________________

If at redetermination a parent is experiencing a medical leave due to their own health or is caring for the other parent of a common child living in the household or another child living in the household, the parent must provide a completed, signed and dated Form 2691, Verification for Participation in a Mental Health or Substance Misuse Treatment Program, to verify the parent is participating in an approved mental health or substance misuse treatment program.

 

If the parent fails to provide the required verification above, the NH Child Care Scholarship eligibility will end and child care will close.

 

  References: He-C 6910.09(p)(1), RSA 167:83,II(c) and (o), 45 CFR 98.20
