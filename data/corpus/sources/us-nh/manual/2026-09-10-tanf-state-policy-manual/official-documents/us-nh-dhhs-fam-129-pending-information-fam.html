When information or verification is needed to complete the eligibility determination, the individual is allowed 10 calendar days to provide it.

 

AE0026, Verification Checklist, explains what information or verification is needed and the date the information or verification is due back to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS).  The checklist is automatically generated anytime something is entered into New HEIGHTS and marked "Not Yet Verified," and eligibility is run.

•     If New HEIGHTS is unavailable, the paper backup Form 808, Proof Needed to Determine Your Assistance, is used.

•     If the individual does not provide the information or verification by the due date, the application is denied by the 30th day of application.

 

Exceptions:

•     When there are split assistance groups (AG) with the same casehead.

Example: An individual and their daughter applies for NHEP Cash and their nephew who resides in the same household applies for FAP Cash, specific eligibility verification(s) that are not received, will not affect the eligibility of the entire case but only for the AG who failed to provide.

•     When the specific eligibility factor in question does not affect the eligibility of the entire AG, disqualify the ineligible member(s) and open the remaining members. See PART 211, COMPOSITION OF ASSISTANCE GROUP.

•     If the AG was denied due to lack of verification but then provides all the required verification within 30 days of the date of the denial notice, follow the Simplified Reapplication Process. See Section 109.07, Reuse of an Application After Denial or Closing.

 

References: He-W 606.01; RSA 161:4-a, IV; RSA 167:3-c; 45 CFR 206.10; 42 USC 608
