209.09 Incapacity (FAM)

SR 00-06 Dated 02/00

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

A child is deprived of parental support or care when a parent is physically or mentally incapacitated and such incapacity is expected to last for at least 30 continuous days. Incapacity is defined as a "physical or mental defect, illness, or impairment which substantially reduces or eliminates the parent’s ability to support or care for the child." See also Section 209.37 , Verification of Deprivation.
