247.19 Termination of RCA (FAM)

SR 25-24 Dated 07/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Mass change will terminate Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA) at the end of the eligibility period. It will verify eligibility for all BFA cash assistance programs for recipients of RCA who reach the end of their eligibility period.

 

Only terminate RCA prior to the end of the eligibility period if the refugee becomes ineligible for assistance or eligible for another BFA cash program.

 

For information on Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA), see Medical Assistance Manual PART  263, REFUGEE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE (RMA).

 

References: 45 CFR 400.1-45 CFR 400.107
