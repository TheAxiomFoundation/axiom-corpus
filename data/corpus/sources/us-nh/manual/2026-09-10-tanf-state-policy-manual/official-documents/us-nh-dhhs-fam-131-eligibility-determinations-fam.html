131 ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATIONS (FAM)

SR 15-07 Dated 01/14

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

The Department determines eligibility for family assistance financial programs.

Eligibility determinations are based on the following policy:

•       Chapter 200, Program Criteria;

•       Chapter 300, Nonfinancial Criteria;

•       Chapter 400, Resources;

•       Chapter 500, Income; and

•       Chapter 600, Standards and Budgets.

 

References: RSA 161:2,I & XII; RSA 161:4-a; RSA 167:3-c; RSA 167:4
