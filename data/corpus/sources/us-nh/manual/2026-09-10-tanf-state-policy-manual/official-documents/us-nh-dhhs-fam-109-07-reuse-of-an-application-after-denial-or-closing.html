109.07 Reuse of an Application After Denial or Closing in Error/Simplified Reapplication Process (FAM)

SR 23-17 Dated 04/23

Application Denied in Error

If an application was denied due to agency error, a new application is not required, the denied application is reused. If the information on the application has changed, verification must be provided and the changes must be documented by the department. Another interview will be conducted if necessary.


Example: Original Application date 7/5 was denied for NHEP Cash on 7/25 due to agency error. The applicant called inquiring why the cash was denied. Upon review it was discovered cash was denied by no fault of the applicants. Cash is opened reusing the denied application date of 7/5.

 

 

Case Closed in Error

If a case closed due to agency error, the case must be reopened.

The application date on Program of Assistance (POA) is the date of closure.  If any information has changed, the individual must provide verification if required, and the changes must be documented by the department.  An interview will be conducted if necessary.

 

 

Simplified Reapplication Process

If an application was denied or a case was closed due to failure to provide verifications, the information from the original application is reused and the Simplified Reapplication process followed. The Simplified Reapplication process can be used when:

·        an applicant provides required verification within 30 days of the denial notice; or

·        a former recipient provides all required verification within 10 days of the last day of the advance notice period.

If verification is provided within those timeframes, the Simplified Reapplication Process is used:

·        The applicant/former recipient must fill out the following information on a new BFA Form 800, Application for Assistance, with their name, address, program of assistance requested, and signature.

·        The Family Services Specialist must briefly meet with the individual (in person or over the phone) to ensure the household meets all program eligibility requirements, all missing verifications have been received, no additional programs have been requested, and no additional changes were reported.

·        The date the new/simplified BFA Form 800 is filed is the new application date.

 

Example: Original Application date 11/10 is denied on 11/22 for failure to provide verifications. On 12/14 the application provides all missing verifications. This is within 30 days of the denial notice so the Simplified Reapplication process can be used.

 

 

Important: If any changes are reported from the original application or if any new programs are being requested, the Simplified Reapplication process cannot be used. A new complete application must be provided and the individual must participate in another interview.

 

References: He-W 601.01; He-W 601.03; He-W 606.01; RSA167:8,I; 45 CFR 206.10(a)(1) & (b)(1)-(3)
