311.05 Determining the Amount to Be Contributed (FAM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

The NH court systems and the Bureau of Child Support Services (BCSS) determine the amount to be contributed by the liable relative based on the contributor’s gross annual income. Resources are not considered in determining the amount.
