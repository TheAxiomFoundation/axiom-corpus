707.05 Penalties for an EBT Card Violation (FAM)

SR 15-27 Dated 09/15

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

An EBT card violation (see Family Assistance Manual (FAM) Section 707.03, Using Cash Benefits), results in cash penalties applied to the EBT cardholder for the financial assistance group (AG). Penalties differ based on whether the EBT cardholder is or is not a member of the financial AG.

When the EBT cardholder is a member of the financial AG, apply the following penalties:

  • First offense: suspend the EBT cardholder’s portion of the monthly cash grant for 2 pay periods;

  • Second offense: suspend the EBT cardholder’s portion of the monthly cash grant for 4 pay periods; and

  • Third or additional offenses: suspend the EBT cardholder’s portion of the monthly cash grant for 6 pay periods.

The Special Investigations Unit (SIU), in conjunction with the EBT Administrator, monitors EBT usage, determines when an EBT card violation has occurred, and applies penalties. Penalties for EBT cardholders who are members of the financial AG are applied as follows:

  • The penalty is applied beginning the first semi-monthly payment period following the date that the EBT card violation has been discovered;

  • Financial assistance benefits for the AG during the suspension period are determined by the following steps:

1.     Recalculating eligibility based on removing the EBT cardholder as an eligible member of the AG, while counting that individual’s income, resources, and expenses for the AG, to determine the sanction amount;

2.     Deducting any other sanctions currently being applied to the AG from the amount derived in Step 1 above;

3.     Dividing the amount obtained in Step 2 above by two, to determine the two semi-monthly payments that will be issued; and

4.     Removing the sanction amount as determined in Step 3 above from the AG’s EBT card for the number of payment periods required.

 

When an EBT cardholder is not a member of the financial AG, such as when the EBT cardholder is a payee relative not included (PRNI) or authorized representative (AR):

  •    The financial AG must assign a new EBT cardholder within 15 days of the date on the notice of  the EBT card violation.

  •    The EBT cardholder must, within 15 days of the date on the notice of the EBT card violation, repay all of the funds used at the prohibited business, or must contact SIU to develop repayment terms. In the case of a repayment plan:

 -    The EBT cardholder must provide their date of birth, mailing address, and case number (if the individual is a member in another AG).

 

-       Within 15 days of the date on the repayment plan agreement that the SIU will send to the individual, the individual must return the repayment plan agreement with the EBT cardholder’s dated signature attesting to the amount, frequency, and dates of the partial payments that the EBT cardholder will make until the amount used at the restricted location is paid in full.

Failure to reimburse the state or set up a payment plan within the timeframes above shall result in a fraud referral to the NH Attorney General’s office. Failure to designate a new EBT cardholder within 15 days of the notice of the EBT card violation shall result in termination of financial assistance for the entire AG.

 

References: He-W 608.01; He-W 608.02; RSA 167:7-b; 42 USC 602(a)(1)(A)(vii); 42 USC 608(a)(12)
