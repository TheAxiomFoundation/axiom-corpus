923.01 Required Verification for Job Search at Initial Eligibility and Redetermination (FAM)

SR 20-23 Dated 08/20

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

At initial eligibility and at redetermination, require proof of one of the following to verify that the individual is actively seeking employment:

●

The parent’s personal summary page printed from the NH Works Job Match System; or

●

The parent’s receipt of unemployment compensation benefits.

 

References: He-C 6910.09(q)(r), RSA 167:83,II(c) and (o), 45 CFR 98.20
