105.01 Failure to Cooperate (FAM)

SR 24-10 Dated 04/24

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

If the Department has fulfilled its responsibilities, but the individual fails to cooperate or comply with completing any eligibility requirement, the Department will deny, reduce, or terminate assistance using adverse action procedures.

 

Once denied or terminated for failure to cooperate, individuals may reapply for assistance at any time however, eligibility is only determined after the applicant cooperates with all requirements.

 

References: He-W 601.01; He-W 601.03; He-W 606.01; RSA167:8,I; 45 CFR 206.10(a)(1)&(7); 45 CFR 206.10(b)(1)
