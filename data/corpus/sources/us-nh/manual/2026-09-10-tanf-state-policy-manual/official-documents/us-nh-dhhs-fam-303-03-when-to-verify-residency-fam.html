303.03 When to Verify Residency (FAM)

SR 16-06 Dated 01/16

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Verify New Hampshire residency during the application process for initial determination of eligibility, at redeterminations, upon learning the individual has moved, and if residency is questionable.
