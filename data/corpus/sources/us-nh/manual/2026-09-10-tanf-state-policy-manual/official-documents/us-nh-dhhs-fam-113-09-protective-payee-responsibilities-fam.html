113.09 Protective Payee Responsibilities (FAM)

SR 97-27 Dated 08/97

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

The protective payee must do all of the following:

•         Work closely with the individual;

•         Help the individual learn how to manage household budgeting;

•         Encourage active participation by the individual in the management of the individual’s

income, resources, and expenses;

•         Receive the entire assistance payment, and pay bills for the well-being of the individual

according to a budget planned with the individual;

•         Maintain accountability in management of the individual’s assistance payment;

•         Cooperate with the Department in helping the individual; and

•         Comply with the Department’s standards of confidentiality.

 

References: He-W 686.01; RSA 167:5; RSA 167:26; RSA 167:83,II(g-h); 45 CFR 234.60
