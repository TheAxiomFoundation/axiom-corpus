Transportation assistance is available to individuals participating in approved NHEP activities when authorized on the Employment Plan (EP), which includes:

•       direct payment to enrolled public/private carriers or

•       mileage reimbursement to participants.

The transportation assistance maximum authorization limit is $250 per month. This limit applies to transportation services for public/private carriers and mileage reimbursement.

 

Payment For Public/Private Carriers

A public/private carrier is an enrolled transportation services provider that transports the general public and charges for transportation either by a fixed number of rides (such as a single ride bus pass or a 10 ride bus pass), or a weekly or monthly pass, depending upon the individual’s needs and transportation arrangements with the carrier. Direct payment to enrolled public/private carriers is available for individuals participating in approved NHEP activities when authorized on the EP.

 

Participant Requirements

In order for the carrier to receive direct payment on behalf of an NHEP participant or for the participant to be reimbursed, the individual must:

•       have agreed upon a vocational education and training goal with the NHEP representative;

•       participated in an assessment interview and developed an EP that has been approved by the NHEP representative;

•       have NHEP prior authorization for the service on Form NHEP256A, Public/Private Transportation Carrier Provider Invoice;

•       submit Form NHEP256A to the provider for completion and forwarding to the Data Management Unit (DMU);

•       been enrolled in an approved activity and is complying with the program and its required hours indicated in the EP or making a good faith effort to participate, if just beginning an approved activity.

 

NHEP Requirements

The NHEP representative will complete the following steps when authorizing provider payments to public/private carriers :

•       determine the individual’s need for the service and indicate the service on the EP;

•       prior to authorizing provider payments to a public/private carrier, check the participant’s file for other transportation authorization(s) for mileage reimbursement or other public/private carrier services within the same month to ensure that payment limits will not be exceeded;

•       if payment limits will not be exceeded, authorize the service by completing and signing the NHEP section of Form NHEP256A;

instruct the participant to take Form NHEP256A to the provider for completion and further processing

 

Provider Requirements

The provider must:

•       be an enrolled provider with the department in order to receive direct payment;

•       complete and sign the provider section of Form NHEP256A; and

•       forward the completed form to the Data Management Unit.

 

 

Mileage Reimbursement

In order for the participant to receive reimbursement, the following shall apply:

•       Mileage reimbursement may be approved for NHEP participants in approved NHEP activities and for appointments with an NHEP Representative;

•       Mileage reimbursement is allowable for round-trip travel between the participant’s home, child care provider, and the activity site;

•       Mileage reimbursement is not allowable for travel costs to and from unsupervised study sites, such as a library;

•       Mileage reimbursement is available for the actual cost of travel, up to a maximum of $250 per month, including monthly or multiple ride transportation passes as authorized by an NHEP representative.
