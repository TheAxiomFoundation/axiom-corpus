Redetermination for Eligibility

Financial Assistance to Needy Families (FANF) cases do not have certification periods. Financial assistance is determined for a specific period of time called the eligibility period. At the end of the eligibility period, benefits will continue to be issued automatically until action is taken to terminate assistance. A Redetermination for eligibility will occur for all households on regularly scheduled intervals. The longest eligibility period for FANF financial assistance is 12 months.

60-Month Lifetime Limit on Receipt of FANF

All the programs under FANF have a 60-month lifetime limit on receipt of financial assistance, although FAP cases are exempt from the lifetime limit.

 

Individuals who receive FANF as an adult, minor casehead, or minor spouse of the casehead are restricted to a 60-month (120 payroll period) lifetime limit on receipt of financial assistance.

 

Any payroll periods in which financial assistance is received while a dependent child are not counted toward the lifetime limit.

The 60 months do not have to be consecutive. All adult individuals in the assistance group have both a federal and state timeclock. The clocks advance as follows:

  • The federal clock advances for every month TANF financial assistance (NHEP and FAP) is received; and

  • The state clock advances for every month FANF financial assistance (NHEP, FAP, IDP and FWOC) is received.

  • Months in which an adult individual receives TANF financial assistance count towards both the federal and state clock, however, months spent receiving IDP or FWOC financial assistance only count towards the state clock.

Other states inquiring about the number of months an individual received TANF financial assistance in NH must only be provided with the count on the federal clock.

 

Certain assistance groups may have eligibility extended beyond the 60-month state clock. See PART 134, HARDSHIP EXTENSIONS TO THE FANF 60-MONTH LIFETIME LIMIT.

 

Eligibility for other assistance programs, including Medicaid and SNAP, is not connected to the 60-Month Lifetime limit on receipt of FANF. Families whose eligibility for financial assistance ends due to the FANF lifetime limit must have eligibility for other assistance programs determined independently of their previous category of eligibility.
