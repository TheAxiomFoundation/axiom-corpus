* The individual must provide verification according to the following table:

 

Approved Activity

Required Verification

Employment

See Section 933.03

Actively seeking employment (job search)

See Section 923.01

Mental Health or Substance Misuse Treatment Program

See section 922.01

Training

See Section 925.07

Attending class, which includes internet classes

See Section 925.07

Required internship

Current class schedule showing enrollment in the internship and statement or other documentation from the school indicating that the internship is required to complete the degree program.

Work Study (earned income)

See Section 933.03 and require a statement from the employer indicating that this is a work study activity.

AmeriCorps VISTA duties

AmeriCorps VISTA documentation indicating the weekly hours of participation.

Resting

A signed and dated statement from the individual's employer verifying that the individual regularly works any 4 hours between 10 PM and 6 AM.

Participating in NHEP

Employability Plan or a service level authorized by NHEP.

Seeking housing when a parent is experiencing homelessness

Acceptable verification shall be a self-declaration stating that the parent is experiencing homelessness and seeking housing while employed, seeking employment,,in an employment related training,educational activity,or mental health or substance misuse treatment program.
