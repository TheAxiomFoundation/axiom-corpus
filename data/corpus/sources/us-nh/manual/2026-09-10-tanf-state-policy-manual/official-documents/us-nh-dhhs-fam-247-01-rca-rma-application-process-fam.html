247.01 RCA Application Process (FAM)

SR 14-04 Dated 01/14

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

An individual may request an official application for Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA) *by contacting any District Office. The application may also be accessed online. RCA* applicants must be determined ineligible for all regular DFA financial *assistance programs before eligibility for RCA* is determined.

Individuals have the right to:

• apply for assistance;

• file an application on the same day the District Office has been contacted; and

• have an eligibility decision made on their application for assistance.

For information on Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA), see Medical Assistance Manual PART 263, REFUGEE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE (RMA).

References: 45 CFR 400.1-45 CFR 400.107
