PLAN FOR ACHIEVING SELF-SUPPORT (PASS) (INCOME) (FAM)

SR 14-19 Dated 08/14

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Money set aside for a work goal under an SSA approved PASS by a blind, disabled, or elderly SSI recipient. Any type of income may be set aside under an approved plan.

 

Treatment: Excluded Income

Exclude income set aside under a current SSA-approved PASS for the duration of the plan.

 

References: He-W 601.06; He-W 606.65; RSA 167:80,IV(i); 20 CFR 416.1180-.1182
