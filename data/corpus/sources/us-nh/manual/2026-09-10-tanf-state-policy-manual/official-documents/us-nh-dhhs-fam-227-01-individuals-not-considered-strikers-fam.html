227.01 Individuals Not Considered Strikers (FAM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

An individual is not considered a striker if the individual is:

·         not participating in the strike, but cannot work because of the strike (such as flight attendants who cannot work because of an airline pilot strike),

·         afraid to cross the picket line because of threatened harm, or

·         locked out of the job by the employer, including individuals who were considered strikers before the lockout.

 

References: He-W 601.07; He-W 634.01; RSA 167:79, III(l)
