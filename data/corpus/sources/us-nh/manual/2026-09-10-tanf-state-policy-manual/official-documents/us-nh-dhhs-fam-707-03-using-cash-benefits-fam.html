707.03 Using Cash Benefits (FAM)

SR 15-27 Dated 09/15

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

The best use of the cash grant issued to a financial assistance recipient is to pay for costs to support the recipient’s family such as:

·         rent or mortgage payments

·         utility bills

·         child care costs so the recipient can work

·         laundromats to clean the family’s clothes

·         gas to get back and forth from work or medical appointments

 

State and federal law forbids EBT cards, or cash obtained from EBT cards, from use at or in the following prohibited places:

·        Gaming Establishments, such as commercial and charitable bingo halls, card halls, table games, or race tracks (including off track betting). Do not include establishments such as, but not limited to, grocery stores or convenience stores whose only gaming activity is in the sale of lottery tickets, and the sale of these tickets is secondary to the establishment’s sales unrelated to gaming.

·        Businesses that provide adult-oriented entertainment in which performers disrobe or perform in an unclothed state for entertainment – whether live or video. Stores where greater than 50% of visible inventory being sold or rented is printed matter, visual representation, live performance or sound recording including, but not limited to, books, magazines, motion picture films, pamphlets, pornographic records, pictures, photographs, figures, statues, plays, or dances, in which performers disrobe or perform in unclothed states for entertainment. Also, providers of on or off-site adult-oriented entertainment for bachelor parties or peep shows, in which performers disrobe or perform in an unclothed state for entertainment.

·        Places that exclusively or primarily sell alcoholic drinks. Establishments in which greater than 50% of visible inventory is beer, wine, or other alcoholic drinks.

·        Business establishments primarily engaged in the practice of body piercing, branding, or tattooing.

·        Cigar stores and stands, pipe stores, smoke shops, and tobacco shops.

·        Marijuana dispensaries, excluding alternative treatment centers as defined in RSA 126-X:1, I.

 

An EBT card which is used at or in one of these prohibited places, as determined by the EBT record of transaction indicating a successful EBT card transaction at a point-of-sale (POS) device or ATM machine that is located within the walls of the place of business, and in which the EBT card or cash obtained with the EBT card is exchanged for goods or services, is considered to be an EBT card violation and results in cash penalties applied to the individual designated as the EBT cardholder for the cash AG.

 

References: He-W 608.01; He-W 608.02; RSA 126-X:1,I; RSA 167:7-b; RSA 650:1,III; 42 USC 602(a)(1)(A)(vii); 42 USC 608(a)(12)
