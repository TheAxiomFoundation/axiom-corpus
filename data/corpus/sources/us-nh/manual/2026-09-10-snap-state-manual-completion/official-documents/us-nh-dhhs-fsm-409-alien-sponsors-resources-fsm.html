ALIEN SPONSOR'S RESOURCES (FSM)

SR 03-27 Dated 10/03

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________________________

Liquid resources of an alien’s sponsor or sponsor’s spouse, including bank accounts, stocks, bonds, and savings certificates, are considered available to the alien applying for benefits.

Exception: When determining Food Stamp eligibility and benefit amount for sponsored non-citizen individuals under age 18, do not count the resources of the child’s sponsor (or the sponsor’s spouse).

• Count or exclude the sponsor’s resources and that of their spouse as if they were applying for the same category of assistance for which the alien applies.

• Deem liquid resources of an alien’s sponsor or sponsor’s spouse to the sponsored alien until:

- the sponsored alien becomes a naturalized citizen;

- the sponsored alien can be credited with 40 qualifying quarters of work; or

- the sponsor dies.

• Compute the total countable resources of the sponsor and the sponsor’s spouse.

• Subtract $1,500.

• Count the remaining balance as available to the alien.

Treatment: Countable Resource

 

Resources: RSA 161:2, XIII; RSA 161:4-a, IV; RSA 167:4, I(a); RSA 167:7, IV; 7 CFR 273.4(c)(2); 7 CFR 273.8(c)(3); 7 USC 2014(i)
