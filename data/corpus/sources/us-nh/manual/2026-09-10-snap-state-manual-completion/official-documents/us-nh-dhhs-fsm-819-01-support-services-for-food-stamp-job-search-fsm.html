819.01 Support Services for the SNAP Employment &Training Program (FSM)

SR 25-16 Dated 05/25

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________

Individuals participating in the SNAP Employment & Training (SNAP E&T) program may receive reimbursements for support services in the following areas:

•      Licensing and Training fees

•      Computer and Technology needs

•      Tools and Equipment

•      Clothing and Uniforms

•      Books and Supplies

•      Misc/Other expenses for directly related training or education needs (determined case by case)

•      Child care. Child care reimbursements are limited to registration fees for individuals enrolled in the New Hampshire Child Care scholarship program; and

•   Mileage and transportation reimbursement (see 819.03 Transportation Reimbursement).

 

Child care expenses that are reimbursed may not be used as a SNAP dependent care deduction.

 

A maximum of $575 per federal fiscal year (October 1 to September 30) is allowed for reimbursement for expenses that are reasonably necessary and directly related to participation in SNAP E&T activities, as long as the following criteria are met:

•      the documented costs are for SNAP E&T activities;

•      reimbursement documentation includes the date the costs were incurred, vendor/participant name, signature, and mailing address, and the participant’s RID number; and

•      documentation is received no later than 90 days from the date of the expense.

 

Transportation reimbursement is not included in the $575 annual reimbursement cap.

Allow 8 business days for the reimbursement check to arrive at the individual’s residence before instituting a query or stop payment process.
