135 PROVIDING NOTICES TO INDIVIDUALS (FSM)

SR 25-28 Dated 12/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Individuals will be provided with notices timely regarding the following actions:

• the outcome of all eligibility determinations,

• changes which affect the benefit amount or eligibility level,

• approval or denial of good cause claims,

• change in SNAP work requirement or Able-Bodied Adults Without Dependents (ABAWD) work requirement status,

• the beginning and ending dates of the certification period,

• upcoming expiration of the certification period, and

• shortened certification periods.

 

References: He-W 704.04, RSA 161:4-a,IV, 7 CFR 273.7(c)(1)(ii), 7 CFR 273.7(c)(1)(iii), 7 CFR 273.10(f)(4), 7 CFR 273.13, 7 USC 2020(e)(4)
