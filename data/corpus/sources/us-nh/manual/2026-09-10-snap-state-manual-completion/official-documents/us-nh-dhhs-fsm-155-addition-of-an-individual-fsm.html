155 ADDITION OF AN INDIVIDUAL (FSM)

SR 25-23 Dated 06/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Eligibility must be determined when a new individual is added to a case.

If an individual reports a new household member and provides required verification within 10 calendar days, the addition of the new member will be effective the first allotment issued after the change is reported.

If the individual delays providing verification beyond 10 calendar days, the new member will be added effective the first allotment issued after the verification is provided.

For a new public assistance household, add the new member only to the SNAP case if all information is provided for SNAP but not for public assistance.

If a disqualified member becomes eligible, the individual shall be added effective the first allotment issued after the disqualification ends.

If an individual reports a child as a new household member, and that child is currently included in a separate SNAP household due to a shared custody situation, the child must not be added as a new member unless the individual reporting the child as a new household member verifies:

  • the child’s circumstances have changed and the child now primarily resides in the household of the individual reporting the child as a new household member; or

  • shared custody of the child is an exact 50/50 split, and the individual reporting the child as a new household member provided more meals to the child over the past 6 months.

See Section 211.13, Shared Child Custody Situations, for more information.
