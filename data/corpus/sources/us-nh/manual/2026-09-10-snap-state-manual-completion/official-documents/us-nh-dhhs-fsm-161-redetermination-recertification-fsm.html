SR 25-22 Dated 07/25

Previous Policy

SNAP eligibility is determined for a specific period of time called the certification period. At the end of the certification period, entitlement to SNAP benefits ends. The individual must apply and be recertified in order to continue to receive SNAP benefits.

 

Eligibility for all individuals shall be recertified at regularly scheduled interval periods of either 6 or 36 months. See section 133.09, Certification Periods.
