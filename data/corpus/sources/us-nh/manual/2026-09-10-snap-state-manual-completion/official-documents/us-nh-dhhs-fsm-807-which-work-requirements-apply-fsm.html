807 WHICH WORK REQUIREMENTS APPLY? (FSM)

SR 25-28 Dated 12/25

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________

All SNAP recipients must comply with the general SNAP general work requirements and SNAP ABAWD work requirements, unless they are exempt.

 

Any SNAP recipient who is not receiving NHEP financial assistance may also participate in the SNAP Employment and Training (SNAP E&T) program, if they choose to. SNAP E&T is voluntary; do not sanction if the individual chooses to not participate.

 

The Consolidated Work Notice explains the differences between SNAP general work requirements and SNAP ABAWD work requirements and outlines exemptions, exceptions and good cause. There is also an overview of the SNAP Employment & Training program (SNAP E&T).
