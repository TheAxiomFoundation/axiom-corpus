145.04 Periodic Reports (FSM)

SR 26-05 Dated 02/26

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Periodic reporting is required for households that no longer meet the ESAP Criteria. Households that no longer meet the ESAP criteria must maintain their 36-month certification period and will be required to complete a periodic report every six months. 

These households will be sent BFA form 478, Periodic Report, in the first week of the 5th month from the date they reported the change in their circumstances (and each six months thereafter until their recertification). On this form, the household must report the following changes:

Changes in Unearned Income:

·        source; and

·        amount of gross monthly unearned household income from a private source if the amount changes by more than $125 per month.

Changes in Earned Income:

·        source; and

·        hourly wage rate or salary if the change causes gross monthly earned income to change by more than $125 per month.

Other Changes That Must be Reported:

·        household composition;

·        residence and any resulting changes in shelter costs;

·        the amount of legally obligated child support; and

·        when an ABAWD’s work hours fall to less than 80 hours per month.

Households that are not categorically eligible (regular or expanded) for SNAP must also report changes in resources that put their household over the resource limit, including vehicles.

Changes reported are processed like any other change that has been reported in a SNAP case during the certification period. See PART 147, ACTIONS ON CHANGES, PART 151 , CHANGES THAT INCREASE BENEFITS, and PART 153, CHANGES THAT DECREASE BENEFITS.

All available databases (NECSES, BENDEX, VCI, SDX, Equifax) must be used to verify reported changes before verification is requested. Income or deductions that were not reported on the periodic report form do not need verification unless questionable.

The household must return the completed form by the 5th day of the following month. If the Periodic Report is filed by this date, benefits must be issued by the 5th of the following month.

If the household has not returned their Periodic Report by the due date, a reminder notice will be sent advising the household that it has 10 days from the date of the notice to file a complete report. If an eligible household files a complete periodic report during this 10-day period, and the case closed, the case must be reopened no later than the 15th of the following month. If the household does not respond to the reminder notice, the household's SNAP case will be closed, and the household will be sent a notice of decision.

If an eligible household that has been closed for failure to file a complete report files a complete report after the notice of decision was sent, but before the end of the following month, benefits will be reinstated at the appropriate level without proration.

If the household does not return their periodic report by the end of the month after it was due, the household must reapply.

If the household files a complete report resulting in reduction or termination of benefits, a notice of decision must be received by the household no later than the 5th of the following month. If the household fails to provide requested information or verification regarding a deductible expense, the household's benefits will be determined without regard to the deduction.
