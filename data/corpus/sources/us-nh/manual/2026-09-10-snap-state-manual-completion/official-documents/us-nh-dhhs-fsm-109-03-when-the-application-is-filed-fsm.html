109.03 Processing a Filed Application (FSM)

SR 25-28 Dated 12/25

Previous Policy

 

The timeframes for processing the application begin on the date the completed application is received or filed by the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). If the application is received outside of regular business hours, the next business day will be considered the application date. The online application date is the date the application is submitted, or the next business day if the application was submitted outside of business hours. Once the application is filed, applicants are scheduled for an initial eligibility interview.

 

Potential Ineligibility for All Programs of Assistance Requested

If the applicant is ineligible for all programs of assistance requested that require an interview, the Department will advise the individual of the reason(s) prior to starting the interview. No application shall be denied without the applicant having the option to participate in a full interview.

If the individual does not want a full interview:

• the application will be denied before the start of the interview;

• the application can be withdrawn, for more information see FSM 109.05; and

• they will be referred to a community, state, or federal agency that may be able to assist    them.

 

      Potential Eligibility for All Programs of Assistance Requested

If potential eligibility exists, the interview will be conducted for all programs of assistance the applicant has applied for.

Upon completion of the interview, the applicant:

• agrees to review the Eligibility Interview Summary (EIS), which summarizes the information provided during the application process and will notify the department within 10 days if any of the information is incorrect or has changed;

• will review the Your Rights and Responsibilities;

• will complete the Statements of Understanding;

• if the applicant:

      - has internet access, will receive BFA Form 800W, Website Forms; or

      - does not have internet access, will be provided with the required program specific paper forms listed on the BFA Form 800W for review.

 

Prior to Ending the Interview, the Applicant is Advised of:

• the need to provide all required verifications;

• what changes need to be reported and how to report them timely;

• SNAP work requirements and ABAWD work requirements, if applicable, exemptions, exceptions, good cause, and the SNAP Employment & Training (SNAP E&T) program; and

• the right to appeal a decision. If the individual does not agree with BFA’s decision, then they have the option to file an appeal with the Administrative Appeals Unit or through DHHS.
