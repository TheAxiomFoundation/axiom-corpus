303.01 Residence for Eligibility (FSM)

SR 97-45 Dated 12/97

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________________________

Individuals must live in New Hampshire to be eligible for benefits. They need not have lived - or intend to live - here for any length of time.

Exception: Individuals in New Hampshire solely for vacation purposes are not eligible for food stamps.

Individuals may not simultaneously participate in 2 or more households. See Section 321 Ineligibility For Fraudulent Receipt of Multiple Benefits.

 

References: 7 CFR 273.3(a)
