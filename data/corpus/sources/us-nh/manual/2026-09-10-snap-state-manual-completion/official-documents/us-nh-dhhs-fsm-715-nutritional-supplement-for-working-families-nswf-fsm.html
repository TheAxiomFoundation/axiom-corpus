715 NUTRITIONAL SUPPLEMENT FOR WORKING FAMILIES (NSWF) (FSM)

SR 11-08 Dated 10/11

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Nutritional Supplement for Working Families (NSWF) is a fixed monthly amount of assistance automatically offered to working, single-parent Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) families that can only be used to buy SNAP-approved items. See Family Assistance Manual PART 715, NUTRITIONAL SUPPLEMENT FOR WORKING FAMILIES (NSWF), for more information.
