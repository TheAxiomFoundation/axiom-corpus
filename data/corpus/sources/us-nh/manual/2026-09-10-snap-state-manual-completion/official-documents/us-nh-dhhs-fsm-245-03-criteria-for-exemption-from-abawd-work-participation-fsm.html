245.03 Exceptions from SNAP ABAWD Work Requirements (FSM)

SR 26-24 Dated 09/26

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

If an individual does not meet an exemption from SNAP general work requirements, they are subject to SNAP ABAWD work requirements. Individuals will be screened for all exceptions at initial application, recertification, and if there is a change in status from exempt to mandatory. The ABAWD work requirements do not apply if the individual meets one of the below exceptions:

·       A member of a SNAP household that includes a child under age 14 who lives in the home, or who is temporarily absent from the home and intends to return, regardless of the ABAWD individual’s relationship to or responsibility for the minor child, and regardless of the minor child’s eligibility for SNAP benefits.

o    The child under age 14 must be considered a member of the ABAWD individual’s SNAP household or would be considered a member of the ABAWD individual’s household if not excluded or disqualified from being a member in that household.

o    If the child is excluded from receiving SNAP in one household because the parents have 50/50 custody and the child is already receiving SNAP in the other parent’s household, both parents are exempt from ABAWD work requirements.

·         Exempt from the SNAP general work requirements;

·        Aged 65 or older;

·        Under the age of 18;

·        Pregnant;

·        Residing in certain areas with a high unemployment rate;

·        An Indian, Urban Indian, California Indian, or other Indigenous individual that is eligible for Indian Health Services; or

·        Medically certified as physically or mentally unfit for employment. An individual is medically certified as physically or mentally unfit for employment if they have limitations that prevent them from obtaining or retaining employment, whether temporary or permanent. This includes, but is not limited to:

o    physical illness;

o    injury;

o    disability;

o    mental health issues that negatively impacts ability to work;

o    facing significant barriers; or

o    are obviously mentally or physically unfit for employment. If unfitness is not obvious (questionable), a statement is needed from a medical professional that verifies the individual is physically or mentally unfit for employment.

An individual who meets a SNAP general work requirement exemption or SNAP ABAWD work requirement exception at any time during the month is considered exempt for the entire month. The exception that will be in effect the longest will be applied. SNAP ABAWD exceptions are self-attested unless questionable.

 

An individual who is exempt from ABAWD work requirements is not automatically eligible for SNAP benefits. The individual must also be eligible for SNAP benefits in all other respects.

 

Loss of Exemption Status

If an individual’s status changes from exempt to mandatory the Consolidated Work Notice will be sent to the household. Reevaluate the individual’s eligibility and take appropriate case action.

 

Exemption After Loss of Eligibility for Failure to Meet ABAWD Work Requirements

An individual who loses eligibility for SNAP benefits due to failure to meet ABAWD work requirements, but who later becomes exempt from those work requirements, is no longer subject to SNAP ABAWD work requirements until the exemption no longer exists.

 

References: 7 CFR 273.7(b); 7 CFR 273.7(c)(1)(ii); 7 CFR 273.7(c)(1)(iii); 7 CFR 273.24(c); 7 CFR 273.24(k); 7 USC 2015(o)(3)
