305.15 Qualified Aliens Eligible With Restrictions (FSM)

SR 26-06 Dated 03/26

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________________________

The following categories of qualified aliens are eligible for Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits with restrictions. Individuals are eligible after being in the U.S. five years after becoming a Legal Permanent Residents( LPR) if all other SNAP financial and non-financial eligibility criteria are met. 

·         Lawful Permanent Residents (LPR)

Note: Some Lawful Permanent Residents that have a former non-citizen status or meet a certain criterion may be eligible without restrictions. See FSM 305.13 for more information.

 

References: 7 CFR 273.4(a)(6)(ii) & (iii)
