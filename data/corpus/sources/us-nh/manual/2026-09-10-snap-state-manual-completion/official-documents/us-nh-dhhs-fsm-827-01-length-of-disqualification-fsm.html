827.01 Length of Disqualification (FSM)

SR 23-29 Dated 08/23

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________

An individual who has failed or refused to comply with SNAP work requirements, without good cause, is ineligible to receive SNAP as follows:

 

·         For the first violation: 1 month;

·         For the second violation: 3 months; and

·         For the third and all subsequent violations: 6 months.  

                 

 

References: He-W 734.01; 7 CFR 273.7(f)(2)
