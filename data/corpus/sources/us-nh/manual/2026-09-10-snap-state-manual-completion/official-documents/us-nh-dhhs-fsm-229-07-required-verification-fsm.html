229.07 Required Verification (FSM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

The individual must provide verification of factors affecting target population status if questionable. Assist the individual in obtaining required verification if the individual is incapable of providing it.

If the individual is claiming a disability which is not apparent, request a signed statement from a physician or licensed or certified psychologist which substantiates the individual’s claim.

If the individual is claiming a permanent disability based on approval for disability benefits, request a statement from the agency approving the benefits if questionable.

If needed, obtain verification of Railroad Retirement disability benefits and Medicare eligibility from the Railroad Retirement Board, P.O. Box 2448, Boston, MA 02208, or telephone (617) 424-5790.

 

References: RSA 161:4-a,IV, 7 CFR 271.2, 7 CFR 273.2(f)(1)(viii), 7 USC 2012(j)
