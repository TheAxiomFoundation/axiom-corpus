161.03 Required Verification for Recertification (FSM)

SR 25-22 Dated 07/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

The household must be notified in writing of any required information or verification needed to complete the recertification, and the due date for returning the information or verification.

New HEIGHTS will generate a verification checklist that includes the information/verification that is needed and the due date by which it must be provided.

If New HEIGHTS is unavailable, a paper backup BFA Form 808, Proof Needed to Determine Your Assistance shall be used

 

Deductions (including medical deductions) incurred by the household DO NOT need to be re-verified when:

•       there have been no changes in the deductions; and

•       the deductions have been previously verified by a third party.

Households do not need to re-verify previously reported ongoing medical expenses that exceeded $35 at their last certification.

 

The department shall assist households in obtaining missing verification when:

•       the household is unable to provide verification due to circumstances beyond their control; or

•       a request for information was sent or given to a third party and that party fails or refuses to cooperate.

If the attempt to assist the household is unsuccessful:

•       the case record must document that the attempt was made, and that the household and the department were unable to obtain verification; and

•       the household shall be allowed to self-attest in writing to verify the missing information.

 

The household must provide verification of all of the following:

•       changes in income, including termination of employment, if the source changed, or if the amount changed by more than $50 per month;

•       new medical expenses that exceed the standard medical deduction ($135), or changes in total recurring medical expenses of more than $35 per month;

•       work hours, if ABAWD and not exempt from ABAWD work requirements; and

•       other information which has changed. Unchanged information shall not be verified unless the information is incomplete, inaccurate, inconsistent or outdated.

 

References: RSA 161:4-a,IV; He-W 766.01; He-W 766.02; 7 CFR 273.2(f)(8); 7 CFR 273.14(b)(4)
