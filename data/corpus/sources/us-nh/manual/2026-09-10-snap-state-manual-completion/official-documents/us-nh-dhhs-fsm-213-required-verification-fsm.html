SR 14-15 Dated 12/14

Previous Policy

The individual must provide verification of factors affecting household composition if questionable.

Assist the individual in obtaining verification if the individual is incapable of providing it because of a mental or physical disability.

Questionable Factor

Acceptable Verification

Household size

•       A signed statement by an individual residing outside of the household who knows the individuals in the household.

-       The statement must include a listing of the individuals in the household and their address.

Marital Status

•       Original or certified copies of legal documents such as marriage licenses or divorce decrees.

•       Signed statements from relatives or individuals residing in the community.

•       Other similar verification.

Purchasing and preparing food separately

•       A statement signed by all adults residing within the household.

•       A signed statement by an individual residing outside of the household who knows the individuals in the household.

•       A completed BFA Form 768, Shared Shelter Arrangements.

•       Other similar verification.

In a situation in which it is an adopted child > 18 and < 22 who is now living with his or her birthparents, a signed statement, under penalty of unsworn falsification, that he or she purchases food and prepares meals separately from his or her birthparent.

Disability

If not apparent, a physician’s statement that the disability prevents the individual from purchasing and preparing food.

Income less than 165% of net monthly limit

See Chapter 500 , Income.
