133.01 ESAP Households (FSM)

SR 26-23 Dated 08/26

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

 

A certification period of 36 months shall be assigned to households that meet all of the criteria for the Elderly Simplified Application Project (ESAP):

• All adults in the household are disabled (see section 229.03, Definition of Disabled)  or aged 65 or older.

• The adults in the household have no earned income.

References: He-W 746.02(a), RSA 161:4-a,IV, 7 CFR 273.10(f), 7 USC 2012(f), FNS wavier 00011190
