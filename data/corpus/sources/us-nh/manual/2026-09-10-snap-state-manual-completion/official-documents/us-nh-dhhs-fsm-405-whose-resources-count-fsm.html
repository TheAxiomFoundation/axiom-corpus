405 WHOSE RESOURCES COUNT? (FSM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Consider all resources available to all members of the assistance group (AG). AG composition differs based on the type of assistance requested. See Part 211, Composition of Assistance Groups, to determine who is included in the AG.

Resources may be counted from people not in the AG, such as alien sponsors and excluded parents.

 

References: He-W 744.02(b); RSA 161:2, XIII; RSA 161:4-a, IV; RSA 167:7, IV; 7 CFR 273.4(c)(2); 7 CFR 273.8; 7 CFR 273.11(c)
