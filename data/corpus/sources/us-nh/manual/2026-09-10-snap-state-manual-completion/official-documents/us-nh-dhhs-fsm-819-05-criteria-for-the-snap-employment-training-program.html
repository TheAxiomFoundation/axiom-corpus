819.05 Criteria for the SNAP Employment &Training Program (FSM)

SR 24-27 Dated 12/24

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________________________

To be eligible for the SNAP Employment and Training program (SNAP E&T), an individual must:

·         be 18 years of age or older.

·         receive SNAP benefits

·         not receive FANF financial assistance (NHEP, FAP, FWOC, IDP)

·         be able to work upon program completion

 

Individuals who contact SNAP E&T and wish to enroll in the program will be assessed whether they are a suitable referral to SNAP E&T.

Individuals found suitable will be referred to SNAP E&T and enrolled in the program. Individuals may be placed on a waiting list should the program be at capacity. SNAP E&T staff will contact wait-listed individuals as openings become available in the order they were wait-listed. 

Individuals found not suitable for SNAP E&T will not be referred to the program. These individuals can request SNAP E&T staff review their pre-referral determination for suitability in the future without the need to be rescreened.

An individual’s suitability for SNAP E&T is determined based on assessments conducted by SNAP E&T Staff and the individual’s goals, needs, and ability or willingness to work. Not every individual eligible for SNAP E&T will be a suitable referral for the program.
