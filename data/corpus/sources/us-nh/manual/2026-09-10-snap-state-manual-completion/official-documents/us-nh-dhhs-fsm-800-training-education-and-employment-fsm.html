SNAP General Work Requirements:

All SNAP recipients who are aged 16 but are younger than age 60 must comply with the SNAP general work requirements, unless exempt. See PART 801, SNAP GENERAL WORK REQUIREMENTS, for more information.

SNAP ABAWD Work Requirements: All SNAP recipients who are aged 18 but are younger than age 65, who do not have a child under age 14 in their SNAP household, and who are fit for work must comply with  SNAP ABAWD work requirements, unless exempt. See PART 245, ABLE-BODIED ADULTS WITHOUT DEPENDENTS, for more information.

SNAP Employment & Training (SNAP E&T) Program:

SNAP E&T is a voluntary program available to SNAP recipients with the goal of helping individuals support their families by accepting and retaining employment. See PART 819, SNAP EMPLOYMENT & TRAINING PURPOSE & ACTIVITIES, for more information.
