145.01 Reporting Requirements (FSM)

SR 25-23 Dated 06/25

All SNAP households, regardless of certification period, are ONLY required to report:

•       lottery or gambling winnings, won in a single game before taxes or other withholdings, equal to or greater than $4,500

•       changes that result in an ABAWD’s work hours falling below the requirement of 80 hours per month;

•       changes that cause gross household income to exceed 130% of the current federal poverty income guideline for the household size

Exception:Consider households whose income exceeds the 130% threshold at the time of certification, to have met the special 130% income reporting requirements, and do not need to report any increase in income until their next recertification.

These changes must be reported by the 10th day of the month after the month in which the change occurred. 

Households that have been certified for 36 months and no longer meet the ESAP criteria will be placed on an ESAP hold. Individuals in ESAP hold are considered regular SNAP households but must maintain their 36-month certification period. While on ESAP hold, they are required to report any changes in their household circumstances that may affect their benefits and eligibility to the department every six months on their periodic report.

Households receiving SNAP in addition to receiving financial assistance, medical assistance, or NH Child Care Scholarship must comply with the reporting and verification requirements of those programs. Workers must act on all reported changes for all programs, including SNAP
