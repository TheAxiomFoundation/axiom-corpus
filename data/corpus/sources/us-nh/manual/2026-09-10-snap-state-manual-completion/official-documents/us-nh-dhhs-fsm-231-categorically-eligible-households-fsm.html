231 CATEGORICALLY AND EXPANDED CATEGORICALLY ELIGIBLE HOUSEHOLDS (FSM)

SR 26-12 Dated 03/26

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Certain SNAP households may be considered Categorically Eligible or Expanded Categorically Eligible based on meeting certain criteria. These households are subject to fewer eligibility requirements than regular SNAP households.

See Section  231.01 Categorical Eligibility Criteria and Section 231.05 Expanded Categorical Eligibility Criteria for more information.

 

A household cannot be considered Categorically Eligible or Expanded Categorically Eligible when:

·        The household includes a member who has been suspended from receiving SSI due to noncompliance with drug and/or alcohol abuse treatment requirements (categorical eligibility does not apply until the suspension ends and the member begins receiving SSI again);

·        The household includes someone disqualified due to an intentional program violation; 

·        The household includes someone disqualified because of noncompliance with SNAP work requirements due to Voluntary Quit;

·        The household includes a fleeing felon, certain convicted felons, or probation or parole violator;

·        A household closed for substantial lottery or gambling winnings, until the household has certified under regular SNAP rules.

 

References: RSA 161:4-a,IV; RSA 167:4,I(a); 7 CFR 273.2(j)(2) & (j)(4); 7 CFR 273.9(a); 7 CFR 273.11(r)
