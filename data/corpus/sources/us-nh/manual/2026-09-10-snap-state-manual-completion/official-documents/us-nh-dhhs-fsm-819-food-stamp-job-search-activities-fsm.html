819 SNAP EMPLOYMENT & TRAINING (SNAP E&T) PROGRAM  (FSM)

SR 24-27 Dated 12/24

The mission of the SNAP Employment & Training (SNAP E&T) Program is to improve the economic mobility of every SNAP E&T Participant. SNAP E&T provides the training and education needed for participants to gain employment in high-wage and/or high-demand occupations or to assume positions of higher pay/responsibility within those occupations. Participation in the SNAP Employment & Training (SNAP E&T) program is not required and is completely voluntary.

 

The SNAP E&T program offers assistance towards increasing economic mobility through all of the following activities:

·          Comprehensive case management;

·          Job search coaching;

·          Career and technical education;

·          Referrals to training;

·          Referrals to supportive services;

·          Participant reimbursements; and

·          Referrals to workforce development partners.

 

SNAP E&T participants must receive case management and required participant reimbursements and be enrolled in at least one SNAP E&T component. Participants can be enrolled in multiple components either concurrently or sequentially.

·        Supervised Job Search (SJS): Assessment, case management, case monitoring, reimbursement authorizations, and resource referrals are provided to job seekers.

·        Job Search Training (JST): Review of participant’s resume, cover letter, or sample application and recommendations for improvement. Participants are guided through career exploration, and encouraged to pursue informational interviews within their field of interest.

·        Job Retention (JR): For individuals that received SNAP in the month of or the month prior to beginning new employment, JR services include case management, job coaching, and participant reimbursements. JR services are available for a minimum of 30 days to a maximum of 90 days.

·        Career/Technical Education Programs or other Vocational Training (EPC):

Credentialing and certification programs in high wage and/or high demand occupations.
