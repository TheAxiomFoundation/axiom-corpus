819.03 Transportation Reimbursement for SNAP Employment &Training  (FSM)

SR 24-27 Dated 12/24

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

 

Individuals participating in the SNAP Employment & Training (SNAP E&T) program may receive reimbursements for transportation according to the following criteria:

•       Mileage reimbursement is available at a rate of $0.30 per mile for private transportation.

•       Reimbursement is available at actual costs for public transportation including ride shares, bus passes, taxi rides, et cetera.

 

A maximum of $100 per month is allowed for transportation reimbursement (private or public transportation) that is directly related to participation in SNAP E&T activities, as long as the following criteria are met:

•       the documented costs of mileage is for SNAP E&T activities;

•       mileage reimbursement documentation includes the date the mileage costs were incurred, the location/address of the SNAP E&T activity, the specific actions taken during the SNAP E&T activity, contact name and phone number, the miles traveled, the participant’s printed name and dated signature certifying that the information provided is true and accurate, and the participant’s RID number; and

•       documentation is received no later than 90 days from the date of travel.

 

To remain eligible for mileage reimbursement, SNAP E&T participants who have received mileage reimbursement for 12 or more months over the participant’s lifetime receipt of SNAP benefits must participate in a reassessment every 6 months to determine the SNAP E&T participant’s abilities, skills, and barriers to employment while continuing to search for employment.

 

Allow 8 business days for the reimbursement check to arrive at the individual’s residence before instituting a query or stop payment process.
