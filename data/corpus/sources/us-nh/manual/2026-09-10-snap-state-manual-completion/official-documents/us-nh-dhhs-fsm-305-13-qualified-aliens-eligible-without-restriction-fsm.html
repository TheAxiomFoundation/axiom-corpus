305.13 Qualified Aliens Eligible Without Restriction (FSM)

SR 26-06 Dated 03/26

Previous Policy

__________________________________________________________________________

The following categories of qualified aliens are eligible for Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits without restriction (eligible immediately, there is no 5-year waiting period) if all other SNAP financial and non-financial eligibility criteria are met:

·       Cuban/Haitian Entrants;

·       Non-citizen U.S. nationals;

·       COFA Migrants- individuals who lawfully reside in the United States who have citizenship from the Federated States of Micronesia, the Republic of the Marshall Islands, or the Republic of Palau in accordance with the Compact of Free Association (COFA); and

·       Lawful Permanent Residents (LPR) only if LPR also is meeting one or more of the following conditions;

o    are under 18 years old;

o    have 40 qualifying work quarters;

o    are blind or disabled;

o    were lawfully residing in the U.S. and 65 or older on August 22, 1996;

o    have U.S. military connection;

o are admitted to the U.S. as an Amerasian immigrant;

o    are a certain American Indian born abroad;

o    are a certain Hmong or Highland Laotian tribal member;

o    are a former Refugee;

o    are a former Asylee;

o    Individuals who are LPR whose deportation was withheld;

o    Iraqi and Afghan Special Immigrants (SIV) Certain Afghan Nationals Granted Parole Between February 24, 2022-September 30, 2024 (Humanitarian Parolees) who have obtained LPR;

o    Certain Ukrainian Nationals Granted Parole Between February 24, 2022-September 30, 2024 (Humanitarian Parolees)who have obtained LPR; or

o    Victims of Severe trafficking who have obtained LPR.

 

 

References: 7 CFR 273.4(a)(2)-(6);, 8 USC 1611-1613, 1621, & 1641; 62 Federal Register (FR) 61348-61349 (Nov. 17, 1997)
