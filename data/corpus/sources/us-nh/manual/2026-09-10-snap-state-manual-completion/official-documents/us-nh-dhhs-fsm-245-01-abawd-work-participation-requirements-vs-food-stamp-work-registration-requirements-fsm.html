245.01 ABAWD Work Requirements vs SNAP Work Requirements (FSM)

SR 25-28 Dated 12/25

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

• Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Able-Bodied Adults Without Dependents (ABAWDs) work requirements are distinct and separate from SNAP general work requirements. For SNAP ABAWD work requirements see FSM 245.05 and for SNAP general  work requirements see FSM 801.

• All individuals who are currently required to comply with SNAP work requirements must continue to do so as noted in FSM PART 800.

• An ABAWD is exempt from ABAWD work requirements if the ABAWD meets one of the  exemptions from SNAP work requirements.

• Satisfying ABAWD work requirements may not satisfy SNAP work requirements.

 

Individuals will be notified of their work requirement status with a Consolidated Work Notice that will explain the differences between SNAP ABAWD work requirements and SNAP general work requirements and outlines exemptions, exceptions, and good cause. Additional forms are also available for further clarification.

• BFA Form 213, SNAP Work Requirements; and

• BFA Form 216, Are You an ABAWD?

 

References: RSA 161:4-a, IV; 7 CFR 273.7(c)(1)(ii); 7 CFR 273.7(c)(1)(iii); 7 CFR 273.24; 7 USC 2015(d)(1) & (o)
