235.05 When Residents Leave the Group Living Arrangement (FSM)

SR 25-23 Dated 06/25

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

When a resident leaves the group living arrangement, the facility must do the following:

1.  Notify DHHS of the change.

2.  Advise the individual to report changes in circumstances.

Group living arrangements acting as AR must also do all the following:

1.  Give the household its SNAP EBT card; and

2.  Give the household its full monthly allotment if no benefits have been spent on its behalf, regardless of when in the month the household leaves the facility; or

Give the household one-half of its monthly allotment if benefits have already been spent on its behalf and the household leaves the facility on or before the 15th of the month; or

Give the household any remaining portion of its allotment if benefits have already been spent on its behalf and the household leaves the facility after the 15th of the month; or

Give the departing members their pro rata share, if several residents have been certified together as one household but only some household members are leaving.

3.  If a resident leaves the facility unannounced, return the individual’s SNAP EBT card and any unused SNAP benefits to DHHS.

 

References: RSA 161:4-a,IV; 7 CFR 271.2; 7 CFR 273.2(n)(1)(ii); 7 CFR 273.11(f)(4)&(5); 7 CFR 274.7(g)(3)(ii); 7 CFR 278.2(g)
