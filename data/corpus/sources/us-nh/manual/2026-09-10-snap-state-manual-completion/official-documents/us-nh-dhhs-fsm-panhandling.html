PANHANDLING  (FSM)

SR 25-07 Dated 02/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Panhandling - The sum of money that is received by a household member as a result of soliciting funds through words, signs, or other gestures usually in a public place.

 

Treatment: Unearned Income

 

Exception: Excluded if considered Irregular Income.

 

Example: During an interview, a client reports that he panhandles weekly to be able to pay for his cell phone bill. He reports that he earns $35 a week in panhandling income. The monthly average used would be $35 X 4.33 (monthly average) = $151.55 monthly average unearned income.
