231.03 Required Verification for Categorical Eligibility (FSM)

SR 22-37 Dated 01/23

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Consider categorically eligible households to have already verified the following eligibility factors:

• resources;

• social security enumeration requirements;

• sponsored alien information; and

• residency.

 

If questionable, require verification of each of the following:

• the household is composed solely of individuals receiving public assistance or SSI;

• the household meets the SNAP household composition criteria; and

• the household does not include someone disqualified due to an intentional program violation or noncompliance with work registration requirements.

 

References: RSA 161:4-a,IV; RSA 167:4,I(a); 7 CFR 273.2(f), (j)(2) & (j)(4); 7 CFR 273.9(a)
