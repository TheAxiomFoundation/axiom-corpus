603.11 Verification of Deductions (FSM)

SR 25-26 Dated 10/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

It is the SNAP household’s responsibility to report and provide proof of any income deductions that they are claiming, and to do so within timeframes. If verification is unclear, the department shall contact the SNAP household to request additional information. The Department will not contact SNAP households about providing proof of deductions the household has requested.

 

At application, individuals must verify any claimed expenses that would result in a deduction.

If the individual does not report the expense or fails to provide verification of a claimed expense within timeframes, the case will be processed with a deduction amount of zero.

 

At recertification, unless questionable, deductions incurred by a recipient household do not need to be verified when:

·        the household has not changed residences;

·        the household is requesting the same deductions; and

·        the deductions have been previously verified by a third party, and the verifications continue to meet current verification requirements.

 

If any of the above criteria is not met, e.g., the household has changed residences, the household is requesting new deductions or has not requested a deduction at all, or the verification in the case file does not meet current verification requirements, the household must verify the deduction and residence, if applicable. In these circumstances where verification is required, if the recipient household fails or refuses to provide verification of a claimed expense within timeframes, the case shall be processed with zero as the deduction amount.

 

When a change in a deduction is reported or discovered during the certification period that is expected to:

-   Reduce benefits, see PART 153, CHANGES THAT DECREASE BENEFITS

-   Increase benefits, see PART 151, CHANGES THAT INCREASE BENEFITS.

 

Note:

·        If at recertification the household is no longer requesting a deduction that had been requested before, the deduction shall not be applied.

·        Estimates of anticipated medical expenses based on verified past expenses shall be accepted.

·        For target households who are recertifying, have not moved since the last recertification, have been receiving the heating/cooling (A/C) SUA due to the receipt of LIHEAP, another state funded fuel assistance program, or weatherization payments, and have not provided updated proof of receipt at recertification, requirements have been met if:

-   current proof is in the case file that a member of the target household has received payment of more than $20 annually in LIHEAP, other state funded fuel assistance, or weatherization within the last 12 months, then no further proof is required from the household to continue to receive that SUA deduction. The deduction shall be considered verified and applied.

-   the case file does not contain proof that a member of the target household has received more than $20 annually in LIHEAP, other state funded fuel assistance, or weatherization, it is the household’s responsibility to provide proof within timeframes. The deduction shall not be considered verified, and if the verification is not provided within timeframes, the deduction shall not be applied.

 

Acceptable verification must document all of the following:

·        type of expense;

·        amount of expense;

·        date and frequency of expense;

·        amount of any reimbursement for a claimed expense; and

·        proof that the expense meets the specific requirements for the deduction.
