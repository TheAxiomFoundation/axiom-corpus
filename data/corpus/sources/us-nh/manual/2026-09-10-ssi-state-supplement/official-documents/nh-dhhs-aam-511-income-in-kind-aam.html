Goods, commodities, or services provided as compensation or contribution in lieu of cash.

 

Unearned income-in-kind is a contribution which does not represent compensation for a job performed.

Treatment: Excluded Income

 

Exception: In-kind payments from trusts or similar legal devices may be countable unearned income. All trusts will be forwarded to the State Office trust department for review. They will determine if any disbursements are countable as income.

 

 

Earned income-in-kind represents compensation received for work performed (employed by another or self-employed) in place of, or in addition to, wages, profit, or payment in cash.

Treatment: Earned Income

 

Assign a dollar value to earned income-in-kind received on a regular basis. Determine the dollar value as follows:

·        Employer statement indicating the frequency and dollar value of the earned income-in-kind.

·        If unable to obtain a statement from the employer, multiply the number of hours worked times the approximate hourly wage, as provided by the employer.

·       If self- employed, or the employer is unable to provide an approximate hourly wage, multiply the number of hours worked times the current minimum wage.

 

References: He-W 654.07; 42 USC 1382a(a)(2)(A)
