155.03 Termination at Redetermination (AAM)

SR 24-31 Dated 12/24

Previous Policy

 

Financial assistance will be terminated when any of the following apply:

·       The individual is participating in the mail-in redetermination process and fails to return a completed BFA Form 800R, Application for Continued Eligibility for Financial, Medical, Child Care and SNAP benefits;

·       Complete and return BFA Form 771A, Nursing Facility Care Renewal;

·       The individual is participating in the online redetermination process and fails to complete the online Redetermination Application without good cause;

·       The individual no longer meets eligibility criteria;

·    The individual is participating in the in-person or phone redetermination process, and has not completed an application (BFA Form 800, Application for Assistance or BFA Form 800HR) or voice recorder signature and fails to:

o    attend the scheduled interview appointment; or

o    contact DHHS to reschedule an appointment.

·    The individual fails or refuses to appear for a redetermination interview, if required (see PART 155, REDETERMINATION).

 

References: He-W 601.08; He-W 606.01; He-W 606.92; He-W 636.01; He-W 684.01-684.02; 45 CFR 206.10
