When an individual reports a change in earned income:

·         first attempt collateral contact. If collateral contact is successful, consider the reported information as verified and no further action is necessary.

·         if collateral contact is unsuccessful, verify the reported change by using:

-  information provided by the client, if the change is expected to decrease benefits. See PART 145, CHANGES THAT DECREASE BENEFITS; or

-  standard verification procedures, if the change is expected to increase benefits. See PART 143, CHANGES THAT INCREASE BENEFITS.

For other reported changes that have occurred, take all of the following actions:

·         Record the reported change and the date;

·         Redetermine eligibility by either:

-  a desk review (see PART 153, DESK REVIEWS); or

-  a full redetermination (see PART 155, REDETERMINATION);

·         Take action on the change within 10 working days;

·         Notify the individual of any needed verifications because of the reported change, using New HEIGHTS-generated AE0055, Proof Needed to Determine Your Assistance. If New HEIGHTS is unavailable, use paper backup BFA Form 808, Proof Needed to Determine Your Assistance;

·         Allow the individual 10 calendar days from the date the change occurs to provide verification of the change; and

·         Determine the effect of the change on the individual’s eligibility and benefits.

 

For other reported changes that are expected to occur, take all of the following actions:

·         Record the reported change and the date the change is expected to occur; and

·         Within 10 working days of the date the change was expected to occur, contact the individual to request verification of the change.
