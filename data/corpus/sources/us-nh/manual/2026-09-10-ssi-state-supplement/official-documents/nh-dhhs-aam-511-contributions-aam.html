CONTRIBUTIONS (AAM)

SR 25-07 Dated 02/25

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Sum of money that is given to an individual in order to help pay for personal or business expenses, by someone who is not in the assistance group.

 

Treatment: Unearned Income

 

Exception: Excluded if considered Irregular Income or Loans.

 

Example: An individual receives $150 each month from their parent to help cover bills, and they will not have to pay their parent back for the income received each month. The $150 per month will be counted as unearned income.

 

Example: An individual receives $150 each month from their parent to help cover bills, and they will have to pay their parent back for the income received each month. The $150 per month will be excluded income.

 

References: He-W 601.08(k); He-W 652.02(b); RSA 167:3-c,I, RSA 167:7,IV; RSA 167:80(l), Section 1612(a)(2) of the SSA [42 USC 1382a(a)(2)]
