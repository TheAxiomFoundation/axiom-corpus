619.03 Supplemental Payments: Nursing Facility (AAM)

SR 25-40 Dated 01/26

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Nursing facility residents are allowed a minimum $93 personal needs allowance each month.

Issue semimonthly supplemental payments to individuals with available income less than the $93 personal needs allowance. The difference between the available income and $93 is the monthly amount of the supplemental payment.

Supplemental payments are not subject to the $2.00 minimum payment requirement. See PART 617, GRANT DETERMINATION.

Example

A nursing facility resident has $30 in available income. The supplemental payment is $63 ($93 – $30 = $63). $31.50 will be issued on both the 15th and 30th.

 

References: He-W 854.17(b); RSA 167:27-a
