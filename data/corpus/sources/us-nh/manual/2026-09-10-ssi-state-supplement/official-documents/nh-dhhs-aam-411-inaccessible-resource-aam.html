INACCESSIBLE RESOURCE (AAM)

SR 96-02 Dated 01/96

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Resources that are legally not available to the assistance group. Examples include:

. irrevocable burial funds,

. irrevocable trust funds,

- Exception: See Medicaid Qualifying Trusts .

. some types of jointly owned property (see Jointly Owned Personal Property and Real Property ),

. property currently in probate

. security deposits, and

Treatment: Excluded Resource
