215 RESIDENTIAL CARE FACILITY (AAM)

SR 00-07 Dated 02/00

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Residential care facilities (RCF) provide personal and social care to elderly or physically disabled adults 18 years of age and older who cannot live alone but do not need to be institutionalized.

Exception: Residents of RCF who meet the medical requirements for Home and Community-Based Care for the Elderly and Chronically Ill (HCBC-ECI - See Part 221 , HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED CARE FOR THE ELDERLY AND CHRONICALLY ILL) may be approved for HCBC-ECI services while residing in an RCF.

Determine eligibility for financial assistance or categorically needy medical assistance using the RCF income limits if all of the following are met:

. the individual meets the categorical requirements for either OAA, ANB, or APTD;

. the individual resides in an RCF;

. the RCF is currently licensed by the State of New Hampshire as a sheltered care facility; and

. the individual is appropriate for placement in an RCF.

Determine eligibility using the appropriate independent living income limits if any of the above conditions are not met.

*

See Part 601, Table A , Income Limits, and 611 , Fluctuating Income for information regarding income limits for RCF and independent living arrangements.

For residents of RCF who are approved for HCBC-ECI services, see Part 601, Table B , Income Limits for HCBC Individuals, for information regarding income eligibility for HCBC-ECI services as categorically or medically needy.
