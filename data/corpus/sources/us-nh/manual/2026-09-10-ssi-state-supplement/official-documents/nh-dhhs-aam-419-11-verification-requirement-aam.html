419.11 Verification Requirement (AAM)

SR 94-04 Dated 04/94

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

The individual must provide third-party (collateral) documentation to:

. complete the resource assessment (see Part417, Resource Assessment),

. verify available resources at the time of application, and

. verify any changes in the institutionalized individual's resources.

Deny or terminate assistance if the individual fails or refuses to verify information.
