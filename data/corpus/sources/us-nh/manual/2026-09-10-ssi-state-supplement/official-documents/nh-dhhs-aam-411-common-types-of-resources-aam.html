This section lists the most common types of resources alphabetically. Each type of resource contains a description and the treatment. The treatment section explains whether the resource is excluded or countable and what adjustments must be made prior to counting the resource or any special instructions. See Part 413, Less Common Types of Resources, for less common types of resources.
