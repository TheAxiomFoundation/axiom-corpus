143 CHANGES THAT INCREASE BENEFITS (AAM)

SR 03-33 Dated 09/03

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

When an individual reports a change in earned income that will increase benefits:

. first attempt collateral contact. If collateral contact is successful, consider the reported information as verified and no further action is necessary; or

. if collateral contact is unsuccessful, use standard verification procedures.

Take action to increase benefits effective for the next available payroll, if verification of the change is provided within time frames.

If verification is not provided within time frames, terminate the case.

Exception: If the individual provides verification within the advance notice period, make the change, and issue corrective payments.

Restore lost benefits if the individual reported the change and provided verification on time, but the Division of Family Assistance (DFA) failed to make the change within the time frames specified above.
