215.01 Residential Care Facility Payment (AAM)

SR 25-40 Dated 01/26

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

If the individual is determined eligible for assistance, financial assistance is paid directly to the recipient. The recipient is responsible for paying the Residential Care Facility (RCF).

The individual and the RCF administrator agree on the rate of payment to the RCF.

The recipient is legally entitled to keep a $93 personal needs allowance from monthly income.

 

References: He-W 601.02; He-W 648.02; He-W 854.17(b); RSA 167:27-a; RSA 167:27-b; Section 1930 of the SSA [42 USC 1396u]
