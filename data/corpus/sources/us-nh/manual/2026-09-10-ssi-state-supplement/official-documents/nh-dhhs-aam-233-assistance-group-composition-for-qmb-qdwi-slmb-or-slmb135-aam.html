233 ASSISTANCE GROUP COMPOSITION FOR QMB, QDWI, SLMB OR SLMB135 (AAM)

SR 03-28 Dated 05/03

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

The following individuals must be included in the assistance group if they reside together:

. individual requesting assistance;

. individual's spouse; and

. any child requesting assistance.

If the individual requesting assistance resides in a special living arrangement, refer to the following sections for assistance group composition:

. RCF, community residence, nursing facility, HCBC-DD, HCBC-ABD, or HCBC-IHS. See PARTS 229, ASSISTANCE GROUP COMPOSITION FOR GROUP LIVING, and 230, ASSISTANCE GROUP COMPOSITION FOR HCBC-DD/ABD/IHS.

. HCBC-ECI. See PART 231, ASSISTANCE GROUP COMPOSITION FOR HCBC-ECI.
