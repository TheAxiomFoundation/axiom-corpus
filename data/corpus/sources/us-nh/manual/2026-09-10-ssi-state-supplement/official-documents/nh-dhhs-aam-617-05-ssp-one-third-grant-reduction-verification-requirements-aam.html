617.05 SSP One-Third Grant Reduction Verification Requirements (AAM)

SR 22-24 Dated 08/22

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

SSP applicants and recipients who are living with others in an independent living arrangement and not receiving SNAP, but who are claiming residential and/or food costs would need to provide proof of at least one of the following:

·       residential costs:

o   lease, rent receipt, mortgage payment, property tax bill in individual’s name;

o   utility bill in the individual's name (electricity, cooking fuel, heating fuel, water/sewage or trash removal).;

o   signed statement from people the individual lives with stating whether or not the individual helps pay for rent or utilities, etc; or

·       food costs: signed client statement which includes the individual's name, address, and whether or not the individual pays toward the food the individual eats. 

 

Failure or refusal to provide the verifications would cause the SSP cash grant to be reduced by one-third.

 

References:  He-W 670.03; He-W 670.04; RSA 167:3-c,I; RSA 167:7; RSA 167:83,I(a); 20 CFR 416.1131; Section 1612(a)(2)(A) Social Security Act
