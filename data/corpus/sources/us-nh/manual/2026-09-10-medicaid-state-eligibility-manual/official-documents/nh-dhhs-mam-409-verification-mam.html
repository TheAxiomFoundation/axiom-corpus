Verification of Resources

 

The Department accepts self-attestation of resources at initial application. Resources held in financial institutions are verified primarily through the Asset Verification System (AVS) based upon self-attestation and/or what the individual has provided for resources during the eligibility determination process. If the AVS returns all account information, no further documentation is required.

 

Proof of resources is only required to maintain eligibility if:

·        there is a discrepancy between what the individual reported and the AVS (see 410 REASONABLE COMPATIBILITY FOR RESOURCES MAM); or

·       there is a reported resource that cannot be verified with the AVS.

 

Document the countable or excluded status of all the assistance group's resources. Document and list the value of countable resources. Show computations for determining equity or values for those resources with allowable deductions. Total the value of countable resources to prove the assistance group (AG) is within the appropriate resource limit.

 

Proof of resources may also be required to maintain eligibility for all countable and excluded resources, that cannot be verified electronically, at the initial eligibility determination and subsequent redetermination when:

·         a change is reported or discovered; or

·         conflicting information is discovered.

 

Reasonable explanation may be an acceptable verification if the attestation and information received from the data source are inconsistent.

 

Deny or terminate assistance for the entire assistance group if the individual refuses or fails to verify resources.

 

Asset Verification System (AVS) Requests

 

For non-MAGI categories of medical assistance, the AVS is used to verify the self-attested resources and to conduct a random search for undisclosed resources owned by the assistance group (AG). The AVS will detect and verify most common checking and savings accounts held in financial institutions.

 

AVS requests will be automatically sent out at:

·         initial eligibility determination; and

·         subsequent redeterminations.

If the AVS response indicates the individuals’ resources (disclosed and/or undisclosed) are within the appropriate resource limit, and are reasonably compatible, the AVS responses are adequate verification of the AG resources, and no further documentation is required.

 

Manually request AVS verification or reasonable explanation of an individual’s resources when:

·         a change is reported or discovered;

·         conflicting information is discovered;

·         the individual did not disclose all the resources contained in the AVS response;

·         the individual has more resources than the appropriate resource limit; or

·         the information is not reasonably compatible at initial application.

 

The request for additional information or documentation must be limited to the specific resource(s) with conflicting information.

 

The AVS responses of electronic information verifying an individual’s resources will be available within 15 days of the AVS request.

 

Transfer of Assets

 

LTC Workers will use AVS responses to examine whether an applicant for NF or HCBS made an impermissible transfer. An impermissible transfer occurs when both of the following factors are present:

·         An asset is transferred for less than fair market value; and

·         The asset is transferred for the purpose of gaining LTC Medicaid eligibility.

 

The AVS provides monthly balances on all accounts as of the first of each month for the 60 months prior to the application. LTC workers will review the account balances and determine if there are any impermissible transfers based on the balances each month. For any transfers that appear potentially impermissible, staff may, if appropriate, accept a reasonable explanation and self-attestation from the applicant before requesting paper documentation or third-party verification.

 

If the AVS does not return an account, request paper documentation as follows:

·         Monthly statements for the 12 months immediately preceding the month the application was submitted; and

·         One monthly statement for each of the 4 years preceding the 12 months.

 

NOTE: Statements must be from the same month each year and be the same month preceding the date of application.

 

Note: AVS will not do a lookback for trusts if the trust has a separate Employer Identification Number (EIN).
