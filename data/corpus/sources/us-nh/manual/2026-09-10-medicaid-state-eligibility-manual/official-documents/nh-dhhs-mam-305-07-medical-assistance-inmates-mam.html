305.07 Medical Assistance: Inmates (MAM)

Inmates in carceral settings, including NH Department of Corrections (DOC), county facilities, and Sununu Youth Services with an approaching release date may apply for medical assistance  while still incarcerated. Additionally, inmates in carceral settings may be retroactively eligible for certain types of medical assistance during any period of time the inmate was incarcerated and an inpatient of a medical facility. See Section 109.09, Application for Retroactive Medical Assistance, for additional information.

 

Inmates with an Approaching Release Date

DOC, SYSC or county facility staff may initiate the application process for medical assistance by using the automated process in NH EASY or by completing a paper application and all necessary forms. DHHS determines eligibility. If the paper process is used, or the individual is currently in a suspended status, the staff in the carceral setting  must send all information directly to

DHHS.CommunityReentry@dhhs.nh.gov for DHHS to determine eligibility.

 

If an inmate is not eligible for a MAGI category of assistance, determine eligibility for a non-MAGI category, as appropriate, requesting additional verification as needed.

 

Determine eligibility within the timeframes described in policy.

 

Exception: If an inmate, including an eligible juvenile, with an approaching release date intends to return to the residence of a specified relative, the carceral  staff have the option of completing the application process for the inmate and their family or instructing the inmate to apply for benefits upon release.

 

Inmates in a Carceral Setting Who Become Inpatients of a Medical Facility

Once the inmate in a carceral setting has been released from the medical institution, the carceral setting provides DFA Form 786 and initiates the application for retroactive medical assistance if applicable by using the automated process in NH EASY or by completing a paper application and all necessary forms. DHHS determines retroactive eligibility. If the paper process is used, the carceral setting must send all information, accompanied by DFA Form 786,Inmate Inpatient Stay Cover Sheet, directly to DHHS.CommunityReentry@dhhs.nh.gov to determine retroactive eligibility.

 

See DFA SR 08-03 for additional information about medical assistance for inmates.
