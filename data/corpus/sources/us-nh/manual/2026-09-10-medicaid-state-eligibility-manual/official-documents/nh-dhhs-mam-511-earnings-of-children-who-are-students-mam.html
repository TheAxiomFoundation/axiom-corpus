Earned income received by a dependent child who is included in an assistance group and is classified as a student.

Treatment: Excluded Income for the non-MAGI-related medical assistance groups. Could be counted for the MAGI-related medical assistance groups, depending upon the tax filing status of the applicant or recipient.

References: 42 CFR 435.603
