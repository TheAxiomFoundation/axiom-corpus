417.07 Resource Assessment Procedures (MAM)

SR 13-36 Dated 11/13

Complete a resource assessment for the couple as of the first day of the first continuous 30-day period of institutionalization.

1. Complete DFA Form 798, Resource Assessment Verification Notice. This notifies the individual of the required verifications.

2. Individual provides required verifications.

3. Total all countable resources of both spouses that were available on the date of institutionalization and divide the total by 2. The result is call the spousal share.

 

Example

 

$5,000

equity value of second vehicle owned by community spouse (first vehicle is excluded)

2,000

equity value of life insurance policy owned by community spouse

10,000

savings account of institutionalized individual

+ 20,000

checking account jointly owned together

$37,000

divided by 2 = $18,500 (spousal share)

 

4. Complete DFA Form 798A, Resource Assessment of Institutionalized Individuals With Community Spouses.
