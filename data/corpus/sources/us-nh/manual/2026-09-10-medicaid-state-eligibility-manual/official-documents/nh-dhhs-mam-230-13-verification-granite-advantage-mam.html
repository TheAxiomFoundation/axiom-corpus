230.13 Verification: Granite Advantage (MAM)

SR 19-06 Dated 01/19

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Granite Advantage uses modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) rules in the eligibility determination process. This means that MAGI rules about verification are also used. See Chapters 300 and 400 for verification rules on such topics as residency, citizenship, SSN, identity, and income.
