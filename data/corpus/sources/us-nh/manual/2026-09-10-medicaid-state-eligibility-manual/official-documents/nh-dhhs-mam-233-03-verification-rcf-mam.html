233.03 Verification: RCF (MAM)

SR 13-35 Dated 10/13

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Verify placement in an RCF at the initial eligibility determination and at subsequent redeterminations.

Acceptable verification documents all of the following:

. the individual meets the categorical requirements for either OAA, ANB, or APTD;

. the individual lives in a licensed RCF; and

. the RCF administrator determines the individual is appropriate for placement in an RCF.

References: He-W 648.02, RSA 151:2,I(e), RSA 167:3-c,II-b, RSA 167:7,I-a, RSA 167:27-c, 42 CFR 435.914
