175.03 Inmates Who Become Inpatients in a Medical Facility While Incarcerated (MAM)

SR 26-07 Dated 01/25

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Once the Bureau of Program Integrity approves the inmate’s inpatient stay in a medical institution, the inmate’s eligibility will be open for the period of time the inmate was an inpatient, as long as the inmate is otherwise eligible for medical assistance.

 

If the inmate is open in a suspended status,

If the inmate’s medical assistance is suspended, the inmate has already applied for medical assistance and has been found eligible.  Medical assistance  for the inpatient stay will be confirmed once the Bureau of Program Integrity  notifies BFA  that the inpatient stay meets the inpatient criteria. If the inpatient stay is approved by Program Integrity, unsuspend the inmate’s Medicaid for the approved duration only.

 

If the inmate is not currently open and in a suspended status,

If the inmate is not currently open for Medicaid, the inmate will be required to apply for Medicaid. The correctional setting may initiate the application for retroactive medical assistance in this circumstance using NH EASY or by completing the necessary form. DHHS determines retroactive eligibility. See Section 109.09, Application for Retroactive Medical Assistance.  If the inpatient stay is approved by Program Integrity, unsuspend the inmate’s Medicaid for the approved duration only.

If the inmate’s Medicaid eligibility ended due to failing to complete a redetermination, the inmate must provide all the necessary information within 90 calendar days of closure for DHHS to reconsider eligibility.  If the information is received outside the 90 calendar day period, the inmate must reapply. 

 

The correctional setting must submit BFA Form 786, Inmate Inpatient Stay Cover Sheet, directly to DHHS.CommunityReentry@dhhs.nh.gov or by other normal means for DHHS to determine inpatient eligibility.

 

 

References: He-W 824.01(a)(8); 42 CFR 435.1009; 42 CFR 435.1010
