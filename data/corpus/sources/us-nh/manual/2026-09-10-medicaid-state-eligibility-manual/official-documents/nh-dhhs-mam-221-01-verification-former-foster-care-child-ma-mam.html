221.01 Verification: Former Foster Care Child MA (MAM)

SR 23-32 Dated 09/23

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Verify eligibility for Former Foster Care Child medical assistance (MA) at initial eligibility only.

·         The Department of Children, Youth, and Families (DCYF) will verify that the child was in foster care and enrolled in NH Medicaid as of the age of 18; or

 

·         Self-attestation of enrollment in Medicaid in any other state at the age of 18 on or after January 1, 2023, is acceptable verification without any other proof required.

 

NOTE: Individuals can be eligible for, but must not be enrolled in, any other category of medical assistance.

 

References: 42 C.F.R. 435.945
