Parent - A biological, adoptive, or stepparent, or an individual acting in the place of a parent, such as a foster parent or legal guardian.

Parental Support or Care - Financial support, guidance, physical care, and supervision of a dependent child.

Parolee - An alien admitted to the U.S. at the discretion of the U.S. Attorney General for emergency reasons in the public interest.

Penalty Date - The first date upon which a penalty for transfer of assets for less than fair market value can be assessed.

Penalty Period - The length of time during which payment for institutionalized medical services is denied.

Personal Interview - A conference between the individual or an authorized representative and the Family Services Specialist (FSS).

Personal Property - A form of cash or an item which can readily be converted to cash, i.e., liquid asset.

Private Institution - A facility which provides shelter, custody, or care to two or more persons and is managed entirely or partially by private funds.

Probation and Parole Violators - Individuals who are violating a condition of probation or parole under Federal or State law.

Proof - Documentation or information that establishes the accuracy of statements made by the individual.

Processing Month - The month in which computer entries are made and confirmed.

Protected Income Level (PIL, medically needy medical assistance) - The income limit that net income is compared to when determining eligibility for an assistance group that is not categorically eligible, such as In and Out Medical Assistance. The PIL is based on assistance group size.

Provided Shelter - Shelter that is provided at no charge to the assistance group.

Pure SSI Household - A household in which all members are SSI recipients.

Public Institution - A facility which affords shelter, custody, or care to two or more persons and is managed entirely or partially by any public instrumentality, official, or employee acting in an official capacity.
