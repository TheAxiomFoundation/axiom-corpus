263 REFUGEE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE (RMA) (MAM)

SR 26-25 Dated 09/26

___________________________________________________________

Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA) provides medical assistance, including In and Out RMA to eligible refugees who are not categorically eligible for other BFA medical assistance programs.

 

RMA is provided to help eligible refugees become self-sufficient as quickly as possible after settling in New Hampshire. The program is administered by the NH Refugee Coordinator, in coordination with the Bureau of Family Assistance.

 

Eligibility Timeframes

If the date of entry into the U.S. or, for asylees, the date asylum was granted is January 1, 2026, or later, eligibility is up to 8 months, starting from the date of entry or, for asylees, the date asylum was granted.

 

For information on Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA), see Family Assistance Manual PART  247, REFUGEE CASH ASSISTANCE (RCA).
