263.13 Changes During the RMA Eligibility Period (MAM)

SR 25-24 Dated 07/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA) must be terminated if changes in the refugee's circumstances cause the refugee to become ineligible for assistance prior to the end of the eligibility period. For more information see section 263.11, RMA Length of Eligibility.

 

Exception: RMA-only may continue for the full eligibility period if the change causing the ineligibility is due to increased earnings. Once an individual is eligible for RMA, continuing eligibility for RMA for the maximum period is unaffected by increases in earned income, even if Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA) is terminated due to the increase in earned income. Discontinue RMA prior to the end of the AG's eligibility period if the individual becomes ineligible for any reason other than an increase in earned income.

For information on RCA, see Family Assistance Manual PART  247, REFUGEE CASH ASSISTANCE (RCA).
