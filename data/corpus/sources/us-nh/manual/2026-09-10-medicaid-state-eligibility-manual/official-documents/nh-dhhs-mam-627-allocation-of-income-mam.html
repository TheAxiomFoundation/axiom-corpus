627 ALLOCATION OF INCOME (MAM)

SR 26-18 Dated 05/26

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Allow recipients of medical assistance to allocate a portion of their income. Allocated income is considered an allowable income deduction when determining eligibility.

Exception: Allocated income is not considered an allowable deduction when determining eligibility for nursing facility cases. Allocated income is only recognized as a deduction when determining cost of care.

Refer to the following table to determine allocation requirements and the correct section to use to allocate income.
