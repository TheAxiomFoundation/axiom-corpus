421.03 Procedures for Applying the LTC Insurance Resource Disregard (MAM)

Fax all partnership policies to State Office ATTN: Trust Unit for review, prior to applying any resource disregards. The fax number for the Trust Unit is (603) 271-4637.

The LTC Insurance resource disregard:

. is applied to the individual's countable resources at the time of application. After the LTC Insurance resource disregard is applied, the individual's remaining countable resources must be less than the medically needy medical resource limit for the applicant to be resource eligible for LTC services;

. can be used to reduce the value of any of the individual's assets, except the equity value of a home which is above the Excess Home Equity Limit. Individuals who own property above the Excess Home Equity Limit will continue to be resource ineligible for LTC services until the proper disposal of that property;

. is not applied during a resource assessment. The spouse's protected resource amount must be subtracted from the couple's total resources and the resource assessment must be completed, prior to the LTC Insurance resource disregard being applied to the LTC applicant's available resource amount; and

. that is applied at application is used at all subsequent redeterminations of eligibility unless there is a break in eligibility, and the figure used is not adjusted at subsequent redeterminations. If the individual's resources increase in value, any additional benefits paid out by the partnership policy after the initial eligibility determination cannot be used to offset this increase. If the increased value of the individual's resources causes the individual to be over the medically needy medical resource limit after the LTC Insurance resource disregard is applied, the individual will no longer be resource eligible for LTC services until the increased value is spent down. All Transfer of Asset policies still apply. (See PART 415, TRANSFER OF ASSETS)

Benefits paid out by the LTC partnership policy:

. on behalf of the individual's spouse are not applicable to the individual applying for LTC services. Meaning, only the LTC partnership policy benefits paid directly to, or on behalf of, the applicant for LTC services may be used in determining the LTC Insurance resource disregard. LTC partnership benefits paid to the spouse of the applicant do not entitle the applicant to a LTC Insurance resource disregard; and

. do not need to be exhausted prior to the LTC Insurance resource disregard being determined and applied. If the LTC partnership policy benefits are not exhausted at the time the individual applies for LTC services, future LTC partnership policy benefits that are paid out are not used to change the LTC Insurance resource disregard that has already been determined. Benefits paid out by the LTC partnership policy after the last day of the month in which the individual applied for LTC services cannot be used in determining any future LTC Insurance resource disregard, unless the application is denied and the individual reapplies for LTC services in a future month.

Until the LTC Insurance resource disregard process can be automated in New HEIGHTS, do the following:

. determine the amount that the approved LTC partnership policy has paid out each month for nursing home or home care services directly to, or on behalf of, the individual applying for LTC services. Fax this verified figure with appropriate documentation to the Trust Unit for approval;

. after receipt of the Trust Unit's approval, enter all the individual's resources on the appropriate screens in New HEIGHTS, adding in all account numbers and policy numbers so that each resource can be easily identified;

. zero out as many resources as necessary to reduce the individual's resources by the amount of the LTC Insurance resource disregard;

. document in case comments the value of each resource at the time of application, and the amount each resource was reduced in order to apply the LTC Insurance resource disregard;

. during every subsequent redetermination, verify the current value of each of the individual's resources, and apply the same LTC Insurance resource disregard that was verified during the individual's initial eligibility determination;

. if the individual is over the medically needy medical resource limit during a redetermination after the LTC Insurance resource disregard is applied, notify the individual that a spend down is needed to remain eligible for LTC services;

. request that the individual provide verification that resources have been spent down appropriately by the end of the redetermination month; and

. if the individual fails to provide verification that the individual's resources have been spent down appropriately, terminate LTC services until the requested verification is provided, or the individual reapplies for LTC services.

The LTC Insurance resource disregard is only used when determining resource eligibility for LTC services. Do not use this disregard in the eligibility process for any other program.
