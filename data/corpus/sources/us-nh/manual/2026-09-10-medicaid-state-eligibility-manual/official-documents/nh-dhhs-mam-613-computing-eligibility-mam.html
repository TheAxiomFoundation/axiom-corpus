. Chapter 600, Standards and Budgets - Refer to the following sections:

- 601, Income Limits;

- 603.01, Earned Income Disregard (EID);

- 603.03, Employment Expense Disregard (EED);

- 603.05, Adult Standard Disregard; and

- 605, Deductions.
