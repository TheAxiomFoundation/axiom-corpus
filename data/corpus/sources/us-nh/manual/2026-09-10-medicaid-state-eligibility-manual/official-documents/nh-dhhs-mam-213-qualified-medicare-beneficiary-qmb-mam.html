213 QUALIFIED MEDICARE BENEFICIARY (QMB) (MAM)

SR 25-13 Dated 04/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

The Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) Program provides limited medical assistance coverage to certain individuals.

To be eligible for assistance under QMB an individual must:

·        Be entitled to Medicare Part A insurance as determined by the Social Security Administration (SSA) or enrolled in Medicare Part B for coverage of immunosuppressive drugs (Part B-ID);

·        Have income that does not exceed 100 percent of the Federal poverty guidelines (FPG); and

·      Have resources equal to or below the limit in Section 407.07 QMB, SLMB and SLMB 135 Resource Limits.

QMB medical assistance is determined without regard to eligibility for any other public assistance program.

For individuals entitled to Medicare Part A, QMB includes coverage for Medicare Part A (hospital insurance) and Part B (supplemental insurance) premiums, premium penalties for late enrollment, Medicare deductibles, and Medicare coinsurance expenses.

For individuals enrolled in Medicare Part B-ID, QMB includes only coverage of premiums and cost sharing related to enrollment in Medicare Part B-ID.

 

References: 42 CFR 406; 42 CFR 435.123; Section 1905(p)(1) & (p)(2)(C) of the Social Security Act (SSA); 42 USC 1395o(b); 42 USC 1396d(p)(1) & (p)(2)(C)
