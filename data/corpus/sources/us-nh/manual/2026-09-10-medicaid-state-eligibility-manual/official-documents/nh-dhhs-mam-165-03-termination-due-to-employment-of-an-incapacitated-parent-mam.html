165.03 Termination Due to Employment of an Incapacitated Parent (MAM)

SR 13-35 Dated 10/13

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

If the assistance group became ineligible for FANF financial or categorically needy MAGI medical assistance because of new or increased earnings of an incapacitated parent, the individual is eligible for up to 12 months of extended medical assistance.

 

References: He-W 882.05; RSA 167:82, VI; 42 USC 1396r-6(b); 42 USC 1396u-1(c)(2)
