175.01 Inmates With an Approaching Release Date (MAM)

SR 26-07 Dated 01/25

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Inmates of NH Department of Corrections (DOC),  county jails and Sununu Youth Services Center (SYSC) may apply for any category of medical assistance while still incarcerated.

Staff at DOC, county jails, or DCYF may initiate the application process using NH EASY or by completing BFA Form 800, Application for Assistance or BFA Form 800MA, Application for Health Coverage & Help Paying Costs.

 

 

If eligibility cannot be determined in real time through NH EASY or the paper process is used, the DOC, county jail or DCYF staff person must send all information needed to DHHS.CommunityReentry@dhhs.nh.gov for DHHS to determine eligibility.

 

 

Determine eligibility for a MAGI category of assistance. If an inmate is not eligible for a MAGI category of assistance, determine eligibility for the appropriate non-MAGI category of assistance, requesting additional verification as needed.

 

 

Determine eligibility within the time frames. If the individual meets all other eligibility requirements for the requested medical assistance group except for being incarcerated, suspend Medicaid eligibility during the period of time the individual is incarcerated. While in suspension status, the inmate or the inmate’s authorized representative must continue to respond to all DHHS requests and continue to complete redeterminations. If medical assistance remains in a suspended status the inmate will not have to reapply for assistance if he or she becomes an inpatient in a medical facility, meets the requirements for community reentry or  is released from incarceration. Failure to respond to DHHS requests or to complete renewals while incarcerated will result in closure or termination of medical assistance.  If the inmate subsequently submits a renewal form or missing verifications within 90 days of termination, DHHS will reconsider the inmate’s eligibility without requiring a new application.  If the  inmate remains incarcerated and fails to complete a redetermination and is outside the reconsideration period he or she will need to reapply for medical assistance upon release from incarceration or if he or she requires inpatient services at a medical institution.

 

 

To facilitate access to medical assistance:

·         upon the individual’s release from incarceration, DOC, county jails, DCYF staff or the inmate must notify the District Office or send an email to DHHS.CommunityReentry@dhhs.nh.gov and self-attest to their release date to ensure that the suspension is lifted and Medicaid opened upon the inmate’s release.

·         for community reentry and pre-release services, DOC, county jails, or DCYF staff must notify the Department by sending an email to DHHS.CommunityReentry@dhhs.nh.gov of the anticipated release date to ensure the suspension is lifted and medical assistance is provided for pre-release services.
