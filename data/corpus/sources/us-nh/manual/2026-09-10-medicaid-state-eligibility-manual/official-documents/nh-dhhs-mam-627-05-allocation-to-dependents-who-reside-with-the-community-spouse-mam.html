627.05 Allocation to Dependents Who Reside with the Community Spouse (MAM)

SR 26-16 Dated 07/26

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Individual’s may allocate income to dependents living with their  community spouse if, the institutionalized individual has a community spouse and they meet all of the criteria in Section 627.03, Allocation to a Community Spouse.

A individual is considered a dependent if all the following apply:

·           Lives with the community spouse; and

·           Can be claimed as a legal dependent on IRS tax returns as specified in IRS Form 501, Exemptions, Standard Deduction and Filing Information; and

·           Is one of the following relatives:

-    A natural or adoptive parent of either spouse;

-    A natural or adoptive child, either minor or otherwise dependent, of either spouse; or

-    A sibling of either spouse (including half-siblings, step siblings, or adopted siblings).

Steps for Allocating Income to a Dependent who Resides with a Community Spouse

The allocation process uses BFA Form 799, Spousal Income Protection, and BFA Form 799A, Income Computation Worksheet for Allocation of Income for Institutionalized Individuals to determine the amount of income to be allocated

·            Follow the step-by-step procedures.

1.     Determine the dependent's available gross countable income.

2.     Subtract the dependent's gross countable income from the maximum income standard (MIS) $2,705.
(If the dependent's income exceeds $2,705, no allocation is allowed).

3.     Divide the remaining amount by 3.

4.     The result is the dependent allowance.

5.     Repeat Steps 1 through 4 for each dependent to calculate each individual allowance.

6.     Add all dependent allowances together. This total is the family allowance.

7.    Subtract the total family allowance from the institutionalized individual's available income that is subject to allocation.
(the available income subject to allocation was determined in Section 627.03, Allocation to a Community Spouse, Step 7).

8.     The result is the maximum amount of income that may be allocated to the dependents.

9.     Allocate the institutionalized individual's income accordingly. When there is income available, the institutionalized individual must allocate it to dependents living with the community spouse. They cannot refuse to allocate.
