421.01 Entitlement to an LTC Insurance Resource Disregard (MAM)

SR 14-35 Dated 10/14

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

An individual applying for Long Term Care (LTC) services qualifies for a LTC Insurance resource disregard when:

•          the individual purchased a certified LTC partnership policy prior to applying for LTC services;

•             the partnership policy was certified on or after April 1, 2007;

•          the partnership policy was issued in a state that is included in the nation-wide reciprocity agreement (the only states that are not included in the reciprocity agreement are Alaska, California, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Illinois, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, New Mexico, Utah, and Vermont); and

•          the partnership policy is approved by the NH Insurance Department. All approved partnership policies include an Appendix A, Partnership Status Disclosure Notice for Long-Term Care Partnership Policies/Certificates.

Individuals who qualify for the LTC Insurance resource disregard are able to retain a dollar’s worth of resources over the medically needy medical resource limit for each dollar in benefits paid out by the partnership policy:

•             for LTC services only;

•          when the partnership policy benefits are paid directly to, or on behalf of, the individual applying for LTC services (partnership policy benefits paid to any individual other than the LTC applicant cannot be used toward that LTC applicant’s LTC Insurance resource disregard); and

•          up to, and including, the last day of the month in which the individual applies for LTC services.

Example: An individual enters a Nursing Facility on April 1, 2012. The individual applies for LTC services on December 15, 2014. His approved LTC partnership policy has been paying $8,000 per month from April of 2012 through December of 2014, which is a total of 33 months (includes the whole month of December). This individual qualifies for a $264,000 LTC Insurance resource disregard (33 months X $8,000 per month payment). This individual is resource eligible for LTC services if the individual’s total amount of assets, minus the $264,000 LTC Insurance resource disregard, is less than the medically needy medical resource limit of $2,500.

The LTC Insurance resource disregard is only used when determining resource eligibility for LTC services. Do not use this disregard in the eligibility process for any other program.

References: He-W 656.06(e), RSA 167:4,IV(d), 42 USC 1396p(b)(1)(C)
