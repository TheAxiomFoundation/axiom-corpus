Verification of Income

 

The Department accepts self-attestation of income at initial application. Income for medical assistance groups is verified through electronic data sources based upon self-attestation and/or what the individual has provided about income during the eligibility process. Proof of income is only required to maintain eligibility if there is a discrepancy between what the individual reported and the electronic data source (see 510 REASONABLE COMPATIBILITY STANDARD FOR INCOME MAM).

 

Proof of income may also be required to maintain eligibility for special categories of assistance, like Extended MA. In these cases, applicants may need to provide more detailed income information and for a longer period than what was needed during the initial application. This is especially true if the income can’t be verified electronically.

Exception for Both MAGI and Non-MAGI Categories of Medical Assistance

 

If a recipient is reporting a change in earned income during the period prior to a regularly scheduled redetermination, verification requirements are different. See:

 

• PART 145, CHANGES THAT INCREASE BENEFITS; or

• PART 147, CHANGES THAT DECREASE BENEFITS.
