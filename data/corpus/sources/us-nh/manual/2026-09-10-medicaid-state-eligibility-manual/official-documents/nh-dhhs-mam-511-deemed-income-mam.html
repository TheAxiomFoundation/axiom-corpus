Income which is considered to be available for use by an individual regardless of actual receipt, also called available deemed income (ADI).

Treatment: Unearned Income

ANB Parental Deeming

Income and resources are deemed only from parent(s) to an Aid to the Needy Blind (ANB) applicant or recipient during the ANB cash eligibility and benefit allotment determination process and only under certain circumstances. ANB parental deeming does not apply when determining the child's eligibility for ANB medical assistance. See the Adult Assistance Manual (AAM) 615.01, ANB Parental Deeming Principles, for more information on ANB parental deeming for cash assistance.
