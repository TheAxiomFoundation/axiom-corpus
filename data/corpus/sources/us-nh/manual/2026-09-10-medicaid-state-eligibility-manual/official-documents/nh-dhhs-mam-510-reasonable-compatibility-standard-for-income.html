Reasonable Compatibility

 

Reasonable Compatibility refers to the standard used to compare the self-attested income and income reported by an electronic data source. Reasonable Compatibility standard for income applies to all Medical Assistance (MA) recipients at initial application.

 

Reasonable Compatibility Standard for Income, at Initial Application.

 

v  Reasonable compatibility of income DOES NOT apply at Redetermination.

 

At initial application the self-attested income is compared to the electronic data source (e.g. VCI/NHES), and is managed as follows:

 

Income (MAGI and non-MAGI cases)

 

·        When the combined value of the self-attested income is at/below the income limit and the electronic data source (VCI) reported income, and any other countable income are under the income limit, the information is reasonably compatible, and the income test is met. No verifications are required.

 

·        When the combined value of the self-attested income is at/below the income limit and the electronic data source (VCI) reported income, and any other countable income are over the income limit, if the difference between the two is within 10%, the information is reasonably compatible, and the income test is met. No verification of income is required.

 

·        When the combined value of the self-attested income is at/below the income limit and the electronic data source (VCI) reported income, and any other countable income are over the income limit, if the difference between the two is greater than 10%, the information is not reasonably compatible, and the income test is not met. The self-attested income is used to open the medical and verification of income is required.

 

·        When the combined value of the self-attested income is at/below the income limit and the electronic data source (VCI) reported current employment but $0 income results, the information is not reasonably compatible, and the income test is not met. The self-attested income is used to open the medical and verification of income is required.

 

·        When the combined value of the self-attested income is at/below the income limit and the electronic data source (VCI) reported no results, The system will check NHES as a second data source, and if there is still no response the information is not reasonably compatible, and the income test is not met. The self-attested income is used to open the medical and verification of income is required.

v  Children and pregnant women who have been determined eligible for medical assistance based on attested information at initial determination, are entitled to a 12-month Continuous Eligibility (CE) period. The department must not terminate coverage for individuals during a CE period if financial information obtained indicated the individual is not eligible. They must remain open for coverage through the end of the 12-month period following the effective date of eligibility based on the initial determination.
