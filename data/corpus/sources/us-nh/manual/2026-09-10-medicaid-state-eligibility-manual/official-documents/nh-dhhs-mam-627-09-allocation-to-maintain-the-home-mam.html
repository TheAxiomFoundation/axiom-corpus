627.09 Allocation to Maintain the Home (MAM)

SR 26-18 Dated 05/26

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

Allow an individual residing in a nursing facility to allocate a portion of their income to maintain their home if the individual is expected to return to the home.

If the stay is expected to be 3 months or less, allocate $342 or actual costs, whichever is lower.

If the stay is expected to be more than 3 months but not more than 6 months, allocate the amount of the verified expenses for the first month and for the month the individual leaves the facility to return home.
