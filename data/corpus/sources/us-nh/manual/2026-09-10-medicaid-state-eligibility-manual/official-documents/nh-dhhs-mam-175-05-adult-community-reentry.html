175.05 Adult Community Reentry  (MAM)

SR 26-07 Dated 01/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

Incarcerated individuals ages 18 and older who have a Serious Mental Illness (SMI) or Substance Use Disorder (SUD) as determined by the carceral setting will receive limited Medicaid benefits 45 days prior to their release which includes:

 

  • Case management including coordinating appointments with community-based providers, reentry planning, and resource navigation.
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) services including a 30-day supply of all prescription medications immediately upon release.
  • Peer Support
  • Prerelease screenings with the SUD/SMI community based provider.

 

If the inmate’s release date changes to a later date, the 45 day clock will be paused and the individual’s Medicaid will be placed back into a suspended status if applicable. 

 

Individuals ages 18 through 20 or up to age 26 who meet former foster care eligibility may be eligible for both the limited benefits under the youth reentry and the adult reentry.
