707.03 Mailing Addresses (MAM)

SR 25-08 Dated 04/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

A household may receive mail at their home address or a separate mailing address such as a post office box, a friend/family’s address, or general delivery.

If an individual or household is homeless and does not have an available mailing address, they may pick up mail from the Department at the nearest district office.
