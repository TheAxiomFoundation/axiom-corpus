Reasonable Compatibility

 

Reasonable Compatibility refers to the standard used to compare the self-attested resources and the resources reported by an electronic data source. Reasonable Compatibility applies to all non-MAGI Categories of Medical Assistance (MA) recipients at initial application.

 

Note: resources are not countable for MAGI-Categories of medical assistance.

 

 

Reasonable Compatibility standard for Resources at Initial Application:

 

v  Reasonable compatibility of resources DOES NOT apply at Redetermination.

 

v  Applicable for non-MAGI cases only.

 

At initial application, the self-attested resources are compared to the electronic data source (Asset Verification System - AVS), and are managed as follows:

 

v  When the combined value of the self-attested resources is at/below the resource limit and the electronic data source (AVS) reported resources, and any other countable resources reported are under the resource limit, the information is reasonably compatible, and the resource test is met. No verifications are required.

 

 

v  When the combined value of the self-attested resources is at/below the resource limit and the electronic data source (AVS) reported resources, and any other countable resources reported are over the resource limit, if the difference is within 10%, the information is reasonably compatible, and the resource test is met. No verifications are required.

 

v  When the combined value of the self-attested resources is at/below the resource limit and the electronic data source (AVS) reported resources, and any other countable resources reported are over the resource limit, if the difference is greater than 10%, the information is not reasonably compatible, and the resource test is not met. The self-attested resources are used to open the medical and verification of resources are required.

 

v  When the combined value of the self-attested resources is at/below the resource limit and the electronic data source (AVS) reported no resource results, the information is not reasonably compatible, and the resource test is not met. The self-attested resources are used to open the medical and verification of resources are required.
