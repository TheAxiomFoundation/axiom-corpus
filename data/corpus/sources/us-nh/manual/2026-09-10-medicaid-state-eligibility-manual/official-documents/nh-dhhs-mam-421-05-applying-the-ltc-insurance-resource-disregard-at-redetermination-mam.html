421.05 Applying the LTC Insurance Resource Disregard at Redetermination (MAM)

SR 14-35 Dated 10/14

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

The Long Term Care (LTC) Insurance resource disregard that is applied at application is:

• used at all subsequent redeterminations of eligibility unless there is a break in eligibility; and

• is not adjusted at subsequent redeterminations.

If the individual’s resources increase in value, any additional benefits paid out by the partnership policy after the initial eligibility determination cannot be used to offset this increase. If the increased value of the individual’s resources causes the individual to be over the medically needy medical resource limit after the LTC Insurance resource disregard is applied, the individual will no longer be resource eligible for LTC services until the increased value is spent down. All Transfer of Asset policies still apply. (See PART 415, TRANSFER OF ASSETS)

The LTC Insurance resource disregard is only used when determining resource eligibility for LTC services. Do not use this disregard in the eligibility process for any other program.

References: He-W 656.06(e), RSA 167:4,IV(d), 42 USC 1396p(b)(1)(C)
