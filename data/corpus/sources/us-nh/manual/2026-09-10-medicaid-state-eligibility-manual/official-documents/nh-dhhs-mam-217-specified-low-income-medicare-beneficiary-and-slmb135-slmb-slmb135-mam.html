217 SPECIFIED LOW-INCOME MEDICARE BENEFICIARY AND SLMB135 (SLMB/SLMB135) (MAM)

SR 25-13 Dated 04/25

Previous Policy

_____________________________________________________________

The Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiary programs (SLMB and SLMB135) provide limited medical assistance coverage to help certain individuals pay their Medicare Part B (supplemental insurance) premium and premium penalties for late enrollment. Individuals enrolled in Medicare Part B-ID for coverage of immunosuppressive drugs may also be eligible for SLMB or SLMB135 for payment of the Part B-ID premium.

SLMB and SLMB135 provide full payment of the Medicare Part B or Part B-ID premium to eligible individuals.

To be eligible for assistance under SLMB an individual must:

·        be entitled to Medicare Part B or Part B-ID insurance as determined by the Social Security Administration (SSA);

·        have income that is greater than 100% of the federal poverty guidelines (FPG) but less than or equal to 120% FPG; and

·     have resources less than or equal to the limits in Section 407.07 QMB, SLMB and SLMB 135 Resource Limits.

To be eligible for assistance under SLMB135, an individual must:

·        be entitled to Medicare Part B or Part B-ID insurance as determined by the Social Security Administration (SSA);

·        have income that is greater than 120% of the FPG but less than or equal to 135% FPG;

·     have resources less than or equal to the limits in Section 407.07 QMB, SLMB and SLMB 135 Resource Limits; and

·        not be eligible for any other category of medical assistance.

o    Exception: If an individual entitled to Medicare Part B is eligible for SLMB135 and Medicaid In & Out (a spenddown), they may choose which program to open.

SLMB medical assistance is determined without regard to eligibility for any other public assistance program.

 

 

References: 42 CFR 407; 42 CFR 435.124-125; Section 1902(a)(10)(E)(iii)-(iv) of the Social Security Act (SSA); 42 USC 1396a(a)(10)(E)(iii)-(iv)
