715.17 When is Part B Buy-In Effective? (MAM)

SR 25-13 Dated 04/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Compute the eligibility date for Part B or Part B-ID Buy-In according to the most appropriate formula below:

Buy-In due to receipt of financial assistance:

·        If an individual is already eligible for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID, they are eligible in the first month they become eligible for financial assistance.

·        If an individual is already receiving financial assistance and then becomes eligible for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID at a later date, begin buy-in the first month they become eligible for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID.

·        If eligibility for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID is discovered late, compute buy-in to restore benefits up to 1 calendar year prior to the date of discovery. For any restoration period in excess of 1 calendar year, contact the Bureau of Data Management (BDM).

 

Buy-In due to receipt of categorically needy medical assistance:

·        Medical assistance applicants already eligible for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID are eligible in the first month they become eligible for medical assistance.

·        If an individual is already receiving medical assistance and then becomes eligible for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID at a later date, begin buy-in the first month they become eligible for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID.

·        If eligibility for Medicare Part B or Part B-ID is discovered late, compute buy-in to restore benefits up to 1 calendar year prior to the date of discovery. For any restoration period in excess of 1 calendar year, contact BDM.

 

Buy-In due to receipt of QMB:

·        Begin buy-in the month after the month in which QMB eligibility is determined.

·        If an individual is eligible for QMB and is eligible for buy-in due to receipt of financial or categorically needy medical assistance, the buy-in date is the earliest date they become buy-in eligible.

 

Buy-In due to receipt of SLMB or SLMB135:

·        Begin buy-in the first day of the month of application.

·        Retroactive assistance is allowed.

 Exception: Individuals may not be eligible for SLMB135 and Medicaid in the same month.
