INDIVIDUAL DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT (IDA) (MAM)

SR 17-06 Dated 02/17

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

An optional service for low-income working individuals and families in which eligible individuals can deposit earned income into dedicated savings accounts set up and managed by a community agency. Funds deposited into an Individual Development Account (IDA) are matched or enhanced by the managing agency and the accumulated funds may only be used towards purchasing a first home or financing post-secondary education *.

Treatment: Excluded Resource, including any matching funds and any interest accruing to the monies accumulating in the account. Also see PART 513, LESS COMMON INCOME TYPES: IDA WITHDRAWALS, for treatment of withdrawals from the account.

 

References: He-W 656.02(a); RSA 161:2, VI; RSA 161:4-a, III; RSA 167:4, I(a); 42 USC 604(h)(2)(B),(D) & (4)
