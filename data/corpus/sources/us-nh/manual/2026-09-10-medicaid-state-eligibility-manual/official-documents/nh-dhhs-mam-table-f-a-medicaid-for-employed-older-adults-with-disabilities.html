Important Note: The Medicaid for Employed Older Adults with Disabilities (MOAD) policy on this page differs from the Medicaid for Employed Adults with Disabilities (MEAD) policy.

All MOAD-eligible individuals are considered categorically needy.

Because MEAD and MOAD follow different federal laws, they have separate net income limits. MOAD uses an individualized disregard, which effectively raises its net income limit to 450% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines (FPG), aligning it with MEAD’s 450% limit.

Summary

•       Below 250% FPG: No disregard needed.

•       250% - 450% FPG: Disregard applied to reduce income below 250%

•       Above 450% FPG:Ineligible for MOAD
