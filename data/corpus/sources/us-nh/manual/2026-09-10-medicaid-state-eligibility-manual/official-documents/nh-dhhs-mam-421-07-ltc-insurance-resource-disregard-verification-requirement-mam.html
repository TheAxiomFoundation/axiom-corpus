421.07 LTC Insurance Resource Disregard Verification Requirement (MAM)

SR 14-35 Dated 10/14

Previous Policy

____________________________________________________________

To be eligible for the Long Term Care (LTC) Insurance resource disregard, the applicant must provide the following verification:

. proof that the certified LTC partnership policy was purchased prior to applying for LTC services;

. proof that the partnership policy was certified on or after April 1, 2007;

. proof that the partnership policy was issued in a state that is included in the nation-wide reciprocity agreement (the only states that are not included in the reciprocity agreement are Alaska, California, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Illinois, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, New Mexico, Utah, and Vermont);

. proof that the partnership policy is approved by the NH Insurance Department. All approved partnership policies include an Appendix A, Partnership Status Disclosure Notice for Long-Term Care Partnership Policies/Certificates. DHHS staff will be able to distinguish a NH Insurance Department approved partnership policy from other LTC insurance policies by the inclusion of this appendix; and

. proof of the monthly amount that the approved partnership policy has paid out for nursing home or home care services. This amount must be verified directly from the insurance company that issued the policy. Statements from Nursing Facilities are not acceptable verification, as this statement may not include benefits paid out prior to being admitted into the current Nursing Facility, or benefits paid out directly to the individual.

References: He-W 656.06(e), RSA 167:4,IV(d), 42 USC 1396p(b)(1)(C)
