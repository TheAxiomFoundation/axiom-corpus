263.19 Termination of RMA (MAM)

SR 25-24 Dated 07/25

Previous Policy

___________________________________________________________

Mass change will terminate Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA) at the end of the eligibility period. It will verify eligibility for all BFA medical assistance programs for recipients of RMA who reach the end of their eligibility period.

Only terminate RMA prior to the end of the eligibility period if the refugee becomes ineligible for assistance.

Exception: RMA-only continues for the full eligibility period if the ineligibility reason is due to increased earnings.

 

For information on Refugee Cash Assistance (RCA),  see Family Assistance Manual PART  247, REFUGEE CASH ASSISTANCE (RCA).

 

References: 45 CFR 400.1-45 CFR 400.107
