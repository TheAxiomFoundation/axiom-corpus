Children's Medicaid (CM) and Expanded Children's Medicaid are both Medicaid eligibility categories that use Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) in the eligibility determination process.

 

For eligible children, provide CM and Expanded CM through the day of the month before the day the child turns 19 years of age.

 

To be eligible for CM or Expanded CM, a child must be a:

•       U.S. citizen or eligible qualified alien;

•       New Hampshire resident; and

•     Meet the income requirements at PART 601, Table G, PIL, Granite Advantage, EMA, CM, Pregnant Women MA, FPEC MA, and Expanded CM.

There is no requirement that a child live with a parent or specified relative.
