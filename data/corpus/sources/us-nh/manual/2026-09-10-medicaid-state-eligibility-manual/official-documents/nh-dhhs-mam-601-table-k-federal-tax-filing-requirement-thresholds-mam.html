The Federal Tax Filing Requirement Thresholds, set annually by the IRS are used in the MAGI Medical Assistance eligibility determination process to decide who must file taxes. These thresholds dictate:

·       Which MAGI MA household an individual is counted in;

·       What types of income are included for the household; and

·       How income is counted within the household.
