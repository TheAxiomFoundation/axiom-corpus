SSI-E  Exceptional Expense SupplementAn additional amount of monthly cash benefit provided to certain SSI state supplement recipients. Eligibility is based on assessment and certification by county human or social services agencies. Eligible persons need ongoing supportive services or care.

SSI Caretaker SupplementAn additional amount of monthly cash benefit provided to certain SSI recipients in Wisconsin who reside with their dependent children. Wisconsin funds this benefit with a combination of state dollars and its federal Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) grant.
Effective July 1, 2023 **$275 for the first eligible child and $165 for each additional eligible child


*The SSI-E state payment is available to SSI recipients who reside in certain substitute care facilities or who require additional support services in order to live independently. Only County Social Services/Human Services agencies may certify eligible SSI recipients for the SSI-E supplement.

**The Caretaker Supplement state payment is available to SSI recipients whose dependent children are eligible for certain types of Medicaid.
