13.1.1 W-2 Agency Program Integrity Responsibilities

Each W-2W-2Wisconsin Works agency is required to establish a W-2 program integrity program and plan. This requirement includes:

  • The submission of standard operating procedures for agency program integrity management;
  • Follow-up on case findings;
  • BRITSBRITSBenefit Recovery and Investigation Tracking System case tracking and payment recovery; and
  • Administrative tracking and reporting.
