Wisconsin Works (W-2) Manual

14.3.1 Participation Requirements for Second Parent in a Household Receiving Wisconsin Shares

If the W-2W-2Wisconsin Works two-parent household is receiving Wisconsin Shares, the second parent in a W-2 two-parent household is required to participate in W-2 activities.

The assigned hours for both parents must add up to a minimum of 55 total hours.

To determine how many hours must be assigned to the second parent, the FEP must subtract the hours assigned to the placed parent (Total Placed Parent Hours) from 55 total hours.

The second parent must be assigned hours based on the following formula:

55 hours – Total Placed Parent Hours = Minimum Hours Assigned to Second Parent

Based on the FEPFEPFinancial and Employment Planner’s determination, in consultation with both parents, the FEP may assign additional activities to the second parent if those additional hours will help prepare the second parent for unsubsidized employment.

EXAMPLE: Ahmad, Sitara, and their two children are part of a W-2 two-parent household and are receiving Wisconsin Shares. Sitara is assigned to a CSJCSJCommunity Service Job placement. She is required to participate in Work Experience for 35 hours per week and GED classes for five hours per week for a total of 40 hours of assigned activities. To meet the combined total of 55 hours, Ahmad must be assigned at least 15 hours of allowable activities. Ahmad wants to improve his English language skills and meet with a job developer to talk about employment options. The FEP assigns Ahmad 10 hours of English as a Second Language class and 10 hours of Employment Search for a total of 20 hours per week. Ahmad and Sitara’s combined 60 hours per week meet the minimum of 55 hours required for their W-2 two-parent household receiving Wisconsin Shares.
