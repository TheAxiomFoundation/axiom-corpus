7.3.6 TEMP Time Limits

W-2W-2Wisconsin Works participation in TEMPTEMPTrial Employment Match Program is limited to 24 cumulative months. (See 2.10.3) TMPTMPCustodial Parent Trial Employment Match Program and TNPTNPNoncustodial Parent Trial Employment Match Program placements count toward one 24-month TEMP eligibility time limit.

Time spent in TEMP counts toward the state 48-month lifetime limit. (See 2.10.2)

Months accumulated in TEMP follow the individual regardless of changes in W-2 Group status.
