13.3.2 Review of Existing Comments in CWW and WWP

CWWCWWCARES Worker Web case comments are shared between:

  • W-2W-2Wisconsin Works;
  • Health Care (Badger Care Plus);
  • Child Care (Wisconsin Shares); and
  • Foodshare.

At application, FEPFEPFinancial and Employment Planners must review recent case comments in CWW to look for any comments from other programs that may indicate potential fraud or error on the case.

FEPs may also review case comments at any time and should review case comments if there is questionable information on the case.

FEPs should also review WWPWWPWisconsin Work Programs PIN comments if there is questionable information on the case.
