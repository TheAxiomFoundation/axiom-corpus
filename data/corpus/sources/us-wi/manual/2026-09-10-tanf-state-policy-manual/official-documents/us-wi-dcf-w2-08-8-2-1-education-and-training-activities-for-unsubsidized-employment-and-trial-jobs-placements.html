8.2.1 Education and Training Activities for Unsubsidized Employment Placements

If a participant is placed in CMFCMFCase Management Follow-Up, CMF+CMF+Case Management Follow-Up Plus, or CMUCMUCase Management Underemployed, the education and training case management services may include:

  • Employment skills training;
  • English as a second language classes;
  • A course of study meeting the high school graduation equivalency standards established under Wis. Stat. s. 115.29(4); or
  • Other remedial education courses (e.g., adult basic education, literacy services).

The W-2W-2Wisconsin Works agency must include an education and training activity in the participant’s EPEPEmployability Plan and pay for the education services if:

  1. The W-2 agency determines that the appropriate placement for an individual is CMF, CMF+, or CMU; and
  2. The individual needs and wishes to pursue basic education.
History: Release 24-11; Release 13-03.
