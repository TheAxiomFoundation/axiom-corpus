7.3.1 TEMP Placement Types and Participant Circumstances

There are two TEMPTEMPTrial Employment Match Program placement types, depending on an individual’s parental status:

  1. Custodial Parent TEMP (TMPTMPCustodial Parent TEMP); or
  2. Noncustodial Parent TEMP (TNPTNPNoncustodial Parent TEMP ).

Individuals appropriate for a TEMP placement may:

  • Require a flexible schedule or other reasonable accommodations;
  • Have basic skills and/or education;
  • Not have skills that are marketable in the current labor market;
  • Be nearing a time limit; or
  • Have barriers preventing them from entering the labor market, such as;
    • Little or no employment history;
    • No recent employment history; or
    • A history of failed employment.

The W-2W-2Wisconsin Works agency must utilize informal assessment results to identify W-2 participants who are capable of working and may be appropriate for TEMP. (See 5.2 for more information on informal assessments)
