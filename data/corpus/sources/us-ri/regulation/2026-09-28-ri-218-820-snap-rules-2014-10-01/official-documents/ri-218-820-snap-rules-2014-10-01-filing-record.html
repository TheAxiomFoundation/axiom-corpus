Part 820 Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program Sections 1000 through 1083
Type of Filing Amendment
Regulation Status Inactive
Effective 10/01/2014 to 01/29/2015
Additional Information

Regulation Authority:

RIGL 40-6-8

Purpose and Reason:

Effective October 1, 2014, this rule is being amended to reflect the following: the Standard Utility Allowance (SUA) has increased from $601 to $627 due to an increase in the cost of fuel and utilities as calculated by the annual Consumer Price Index (CPI); the gross and net income eligibility standards have been increased as a result of the annual cost of living adjustment; the maximum excess shelter deduction has increased from $478 to $490; the maximum standard deduction for household sizes 1 through 3 has increased from $152 per month to $155 per month and the maximum standard deduction for household sizes 4-6 has also increased; the minimum monthly allotment has increased from $15 to $16; the maximum SNAP allotments from which the basis of issuance tables are calculated have been increased and the resource limit for SNAP households has increased from $2,000 to $2,250.

Brief statement of Reason for Finding Imminent Peril:

To avoid wrongful denial/discontinuance of benefits to applicants/beneficiaries.
