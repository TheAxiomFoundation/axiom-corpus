Revenue and Taxation Code - RTC

DIVISION 2. OTHER TAXES [6001 - 61050]

  ( Heading of Division 2 amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 279. )
  

PART 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX [17001 - 18181]

  ( Part 10 added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 659. )
  

CHAPTER 7. Exempt Trusts and Common Trust Funds [17631 - 17677]

  ( Chapter 7 repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 939. )
  

ARTICLE 3. Common Trust Funds [17671 - 17677]
  ( Article 3 repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 48. )

  
17671.  

Section 584 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to common trust funds, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 45. Effective October 10, 1999.)
