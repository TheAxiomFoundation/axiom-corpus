Budgeting Procedures for Medically Needy under Medicaid 510-03-90-50

(Revised 1/1/2024 ML #3776)

View Archives

 

 

Parents and caretaker relatives of deprived children, Pregnant Women and children under age 21, who fail under the ACA Medicaid Categorically Needy Coverage Group, may be eligible under the Medicaid Medically Needy Coverage Group, provided they have a Medical Need as defined in 510-03-35-35, Need. See Non-ACA Medicaid policy income considerations, income deductions and income disregards.
