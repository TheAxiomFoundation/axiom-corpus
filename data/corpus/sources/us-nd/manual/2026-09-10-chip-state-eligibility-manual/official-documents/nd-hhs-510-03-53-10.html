Individuals Covered 510-03-53-10

(New 7/1/2014 ML #3404)

View Archives

 

  1. An individual may be continuously eligible for Medicaid if he or she:
    1. Is under age 19 (including the month the individual turns age 19); and
    2. Is not eligible as medically needy.
  1. Individuals under age 19 include children, caretaker relatives, and pregnant women.
  1. Individuals eligible for Refugee Medical Assistance (RMA) or Emergency Services are NOT entitled to continuous eligibility.
