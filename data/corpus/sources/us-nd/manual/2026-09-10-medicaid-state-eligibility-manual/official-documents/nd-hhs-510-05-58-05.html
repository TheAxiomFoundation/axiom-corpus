Children with Disabilities 510-05-58

 

General Information 510-05-58-05

(Revised 4/1/12 ML #3321)

View Archives

 

 

The Children with Disabilities coverage allows options to families of children with disabilities under age 19, who are not eligible for Medicaid or who are eligible for Medicaid and have a client share (recipient liability), to buy into Medicaid. This coverage became effective in North Dakota on April 1, 2008.

 

All medically needy technical and financial eligibility factors apply to the Children with Disabilities coverage except as identified in sections 05-58-10 through 05-58-35.
