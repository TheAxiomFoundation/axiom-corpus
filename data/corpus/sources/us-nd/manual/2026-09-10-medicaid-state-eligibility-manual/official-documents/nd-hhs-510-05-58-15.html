Employer Based Insurance 510-05-58-15

(Revised 3/1/08 ML #3127)

View Archives

 

 

As a condition of eligibility for Children with Disabilities coverage, a child must be enrolled in a health insurance policy if:

  1. The family has an employer based health insurance available to them; and

 

  1. The employer pays at least 50% of the premium (excluding separate dental and vision coverage).
