Reciprocity 510-05-66-25

(Revised 7/1/09 ML #3183)

View Archives

 

 

The North Dakota Long Term Care Partnership Program will provide reciprocity with respect to long-term care insurance policies covered under other state long-term care insurance partnerships.  This will allow individuals who purchase a Partnership Policy in one state to move to another state with a Long Term Care Partnership program without losing the asset protection.  This protection is only available between states that offer reciprocity.  A federal database is being developed to identify states that agree to reciprocity, and to identify Partnership Policies from those states.
