Income Levels for the Medicare Premium Assistance Program 510-05-60-35

(Revised 12/1/19 ML #3569)

View Archives

 

Refer to Section 05-85-40 for the SSI Buy-In under the Categorically Needy income level and the Medicare Savings Programs income levels.

 

Note: Annual Title II cost of living allowances effective in January shall be disregarded when determining eligibility for Medicare Savings Programs for January, February, and March. This disregard prevents applicants and recipients from becoming ineligible pending issuance of the new poverty levels which are effective April 1 of each year.
