Continuous Eligibility for Children 510-05-53

 

General Statement 510-05-53-05

(Revised 6/1/08 ML #3146)

View Archives

 

 

Continuous eligibility for children allows recipients under age 19 who have been determined eligible for Medicaid, other than Medically Needy, to be deemed eligible for a total of up to 12 months regardless of changes in circumstances other than attainment of age 19. This has an effective date of June 1, 2008.
