Assets 510-05-70

 

General Information 510-05-70-05

(Revised 7/1/14 ML #3406)

View Archives

 

 

These medically needy asset provisions apply to all aged, blind, and disabled applicants and recipients of Medicaid unless otherwise specified in this chapter.

 

There is no asset test for applicants and recipients who are applying, or are eligible under the Children with Disabilities coverage.
