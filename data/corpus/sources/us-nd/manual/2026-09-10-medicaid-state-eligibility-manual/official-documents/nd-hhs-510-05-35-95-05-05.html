Institutions 510-05-35-95-05

 

General Statement (Coverage for Inmates Receiving Inpatient Care in Certain Medical Institutions) 510-05-35-95-05-05

(Revised 1/1/2025 ML #3916)

View Archives

 

An individual's  Medicaid coverage is suspended when he or she is incarcerated and is an inmate with the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation (DOCR) or a county or tribal jail.
