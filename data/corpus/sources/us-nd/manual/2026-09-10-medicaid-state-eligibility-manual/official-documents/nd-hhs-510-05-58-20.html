Income and Asset Considerations for Children with Disabilities 510-05-58-20

(Revised 6/1/2024 ML #3839)

View Archives

 

 

Income calculations must consider income in the manner provided for in the Medically Needy income considerations; ownership of income; unearned income; earned income; disregarded income; and income deductions; except:

  1. Income disregards in 05-85-30 and income deductions in 05-85-35 are allowed regardless of the individuals living arrangement; and

 

  1. The cost of any Children with Disabilities premium is not allowed as a deduction in the Children with Disabilities budget calculation.

 

There is no asset test for applicants and recipients who are applying for, or are eligible under the Children with Disabilities coverage.
