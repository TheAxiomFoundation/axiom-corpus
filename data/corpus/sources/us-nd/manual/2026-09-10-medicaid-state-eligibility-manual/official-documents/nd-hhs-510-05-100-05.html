Forms Appendix 510-05-100

 

SFN 560, "Assignment of Benefits" 510-05-100-05

(Revised 6/01 ML #2590)

View Archives

 

 

This form is used to obtain the signature of someone legally able to assign benefits when the individuals who signs the application do not have the right to do so.

 

This form is available through the Department of Human Services and may also be obtained electronically via E-Forms. (42 kb pdf)
