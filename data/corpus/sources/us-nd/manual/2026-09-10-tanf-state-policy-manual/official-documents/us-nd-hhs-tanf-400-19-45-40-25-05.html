Qualified Aliens Lawfully Admitted for Permanent Residence Before August 22, 1996 400-19-45-40-25-05

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-28)

 

Qualified aliens who lawfully entered the United States for permanent residence before August 22, 1996, and who meet all other TANF eligibility criteria may be eligible for TANF. These individuals are not banned from receiving benefits for a 5 year period.

 

This 5-year period is also known as a ‘5-year ban on benefits’ and is effective on the date the individual entered the country as a qualified alien or the date the individual becomes a qualified alien, whichever is later.
