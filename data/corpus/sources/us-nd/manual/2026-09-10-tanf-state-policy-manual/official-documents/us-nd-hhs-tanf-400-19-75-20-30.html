Good Cause for Non-Participation - Denied - Resulting in a Modified Employability Plan 400-19-75-20-30

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

When a request for good cause for non-participation in the JOBS program is denied, the JOBS Employment Contractor may determine that the individual can only participate at a reduced level. A Modified Employability Plan is developed based on the medical or psychological information provided. (See Section 400-19-75-40-40-05, Modified Employability Plan.)
