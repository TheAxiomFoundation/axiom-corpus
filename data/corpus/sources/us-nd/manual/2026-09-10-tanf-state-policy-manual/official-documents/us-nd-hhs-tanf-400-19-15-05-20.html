Child Protection 400-19-15-05-20

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

A TANF objective is to protect the health and welfare of children known or suspected of being victims of child abuse or neglect. eligibility workers may know of situations where it is suspected that children in TANF households are being abused or neglected, either by other household members or in settings outside of the home. As these situations occur, a eligibility worker may make reports without violating the confidentiality of the household. The eligibility worker may consult with the Child Protection Supervisor at the human service zone office if there are any questions on a potential child abuse or neglect situation.

 

A eligibility worker who is a social worker or, by profession, a mandated reporter must report suspected or known cases of child abuse or neglect.
