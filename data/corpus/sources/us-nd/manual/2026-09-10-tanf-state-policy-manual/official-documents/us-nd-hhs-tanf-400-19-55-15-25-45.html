Non-Allowable Expenses 400-19-55-15-25-45

(New 2/1/2015 ML #3426)

View Archives

 

 

The worker must determine if an expense is non-allowable based on whether the expense is part of producing income. The following expenses are not allowable deductions from self-employment income:

 

  • Expenses and net operating losses (NOL) from previous periods.
  • Depreciation/depletion - to allow these costs would result in exclusion for amounts that are not actual costs.
  • Other expenses that are not incurred as a result of self-employment income, such as:
    • Charitable contributions
    • Penalties and fines
