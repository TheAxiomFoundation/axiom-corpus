Purpose of JOBS 400-19-15-30-10

(Revised 4/1/2026 Release 26.1)

View Archives

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-83)

 

The purpose of the Job Opportunities and Basic skills (JOBS) Program is to combine education, training, and employment components to enable participants to become self-sufficient.

 

The North Dakota Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) is the administrative agency for the state’s JOBS Program. To the extent resources permit, all non-exempt individuals and all children age sixteen or older who are not attending secondary school full time and who are required to attend shall participate in the JOBS program.
