TANF Lifetime Limit Exemption Appeal Process 400-19-45-35-25

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

When an exemption from the 60 month lifetime limit is denied, an applicant or recipient may request a fair hearing in writing within 30 days from the date of the adverse action.  However, assistance is not continued pending the fair hearing.  (See Section 400-19-125, Appeals and Fair Hearings.)
