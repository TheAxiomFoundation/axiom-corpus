Documentation/Verification of Earned Income 400-19-55-15-20

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

The primary verification for declared earnings is the employee’s pay record (pay stubs, etc.) of wages received from his employer.

 

The information from Job Service and the Social Security Administration is not always current, but will serve as lead information for follow-up investigation.

 

Document or other record generally available from the client

  • Pay stubs
  • Employer’s wage records
  • Statement from client’s employer
  • Job Service
  • Copy of contract if income is received on a contractual basis.
