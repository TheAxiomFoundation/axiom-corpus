(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-20)

 

The date of the application is the date the signed application is received in the human service zone, provided the application was submitted during normal business hours. When an application is submitted after normal business hours, on a weekend, or on a holiday, the application is considered received the next business day.

Note: The eligibility worker must document the date an application is filed by recording the date received on the application.

 

The benefit start date is either the date the signed application is received in the county agency or the date the household becomes eligible, whichever is later.  

Note: When a family entering North Dakota has received a current month's TANF benefit from a state that issues grants twice per month, the benefit start date is the day following the date eligibility in the other state ended or the date the household becomes eligible, whichever is later.
