Eligibility for Minor Parents Attaining Age 18 or Parents Age 18 or Older Residing With Parents 400-19-45-25-30

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

Once a minor parent turns 18 years of age, the parent may be eligible for TANF benefits in their own right and minor parent living arrangement criteria no longer applies. An individual is considered age 18 on the first day of the month of their 18th birthday.

 

The income and assets of the minor parent’s parents are disregarded effective the first day of the month in which the minor parent attains age 18. However, the assets, income and expenses of the minor parent attaining age 18 must be considered in determining TANF eligibility and the benefit amount.

 

Regular contributions in cash by the minor parent’s parents must be considered.
