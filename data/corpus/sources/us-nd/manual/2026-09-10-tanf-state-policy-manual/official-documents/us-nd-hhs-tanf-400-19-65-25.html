JOBS Supportive Services - Proof of Performance 400-19-65-25

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

Transportation assistance may be authorized during a Proof of Performance (POP) in order to ensure the individual will be able to complete the POP. All other JOBS supportive services are not to be provided to a sanctioned individual.

 

Once the POP has been completed and the TANF case has been authorized as eligible, the JOBS Employment Contractor or Tribal NEW Coordinator is responsible to determine the participant’s anticipated transportation needs and to document the anticipated need on the Employability Plan.
