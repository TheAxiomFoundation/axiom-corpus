Definition of Income 400-19-55-10-05

(Revised 1/1/17 ML #3482)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-44)

 

Income is the gain or benefit, earned or unearned, derived from labor, business, capital, or property which is received or is available to the household. It is considered when actually available and when the applicant or recipient has a legal interest in a liquidated sum and has the legal ability to make such sum available for support or maintenance.
