Minor Dependent Child and Minor Parent JOBS and Tribal NEW Sanction Policy 400-19-85-40

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

A minor dependent child age 16 and older or a minor parent is subject to sanction under the JOBS or Tribal New Program if it is determined that the individual failed without good cause to attend high school, GED, a secondary school, a vocational or technical school that is equivalent to secondary school, or participate in an approved activity.   

 

All JOBS or Tribal NEW Sanction policies apply to these individuals.
