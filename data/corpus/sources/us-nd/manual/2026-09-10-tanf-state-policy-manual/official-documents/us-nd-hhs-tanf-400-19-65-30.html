JOBS Supportive Services - Sanctions 400-19-65-30

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

JOBS supportive services for allowable activities may continue to be paid until such time as the sanction becomes effective.

 

JOBS supportive services are not to be provided to a sanctioned individual during the Sanction Penalty Month unless the eligibility worker or JOBS Employment Contractor authorizes transportation assistance to help ensure the individual will be able to complete a Proof of Performance (POP).

 

If a child support sanction is imposed against a JOBS participant, the individual shall continue to receive authorized supportive services until such time as the child support sanction progresses to closure of the TANF case.
