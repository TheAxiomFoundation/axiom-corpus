Notification of Availability of Family Planning Services 400-19-15-25

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

Public Law 92-603 requires that applicants and recipients be advised of the availability of family planning services. This can be accomplished by routinely making the pamphlet, "North Dakota Family Planning Program," available at the time of application. Human service zone offices should order supplies of this pamphlet directly from the North Dakota Family Planning Program, State Health Department, Second Floor Judicial Wing, 600 East Boulevard Avenue, Bismarck, North Dakota 58505.
