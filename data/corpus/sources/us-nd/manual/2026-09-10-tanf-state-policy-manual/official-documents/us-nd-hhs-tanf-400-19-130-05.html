Overpayments and Underpayments 400-19-130

 

Overview 400-19-130-05

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-71)

 

When a payment is determined to be incorrect, each benefit month that is incorrect must be reworked, using the policies and procedures that were in effect for the payment month. Reworking an incorrect benefit month may result in an overpayment or underpayment.

 

The overpayment or underpayment is the difference between the benefit amount the client actually received and the benefit amount the client should have received.
