SSI Recipient who is a Caretaker 400-19-105-40-15

(Revised 11/1/19 ML #3562)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-63)

 

A caretaker who is in receipt of SSI or presumptive SSI is an ineligible caretaker who cannot have their needs met by TANF. Their income and assets are not considered in the determination of the household’s eligibility or benefits.
