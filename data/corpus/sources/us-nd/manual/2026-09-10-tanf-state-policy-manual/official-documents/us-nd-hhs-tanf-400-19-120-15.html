Completion of Payment 400-19-120-15

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-71(1))

 

TANF benefits transmitted electronically are transferred into accounts on all days other than Saturday, Sunday, and federal holidays. Funds will be available on the recipient’s debit card within approximately 48 hours following issuance.  

 

For cases where payment is being made under protective payee provisions:

  1. If the payment is an initial or supplemental benefit, a TANF benefit is considered complete as of 12:01 a.m. on the day following the date authorized.
  2. If the payment is the regular monthly benefit, a TANF benefit is considered complete as of 12:01 a.m. on the first day of the month.

 

In the event of the death of a payee after that time, the payment is still considered completed.
