(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-86)

 

Tribal Native Employment Works (NEW) programs are sister programs to the State JOBS program and are available to TANF eligible enrolled or enrollable members of tribes. An individual who participates in a Tribal New Programshall meet all work requirements described in this section.

 

When individuals are required to participate in the Tribal NEW Program, the Tribal NEW Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) along with the policy defined in this section should be referenced.  (See Section 400-19-165-165, Tribal NEW Memorandum of Understanding.)
