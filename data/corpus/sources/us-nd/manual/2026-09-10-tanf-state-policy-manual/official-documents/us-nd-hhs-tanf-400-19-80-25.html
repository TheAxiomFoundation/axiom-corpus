Transferring Individuals from State JOBS to Tribal NEW 400-19-80-25

(Revised 9/1/2021 ML #3629)

View Archives

 

 

When creating a referral to the Tribal New Program in ongoing TANF cases, a referral is made in the current calendar month the individual is requesting to participate in Tribal NEW.

Example: An individual begins post-secondary education in August and is required to be referred to Tribal NEW. In the calendar month of August, a Tribal NEW referral must be created.

 

Tribal NEW participants are excluded from the work participation rate requirements for federal reporting purposes.
