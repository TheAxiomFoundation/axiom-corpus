Post-TANF Supportive Services for Tribal NEW Participants 400-19-80-60

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

Following case closure, a former Tribal NEW participant may be eligible for Post-TANF Supportive Services. Tribal NEW does not determine eligibility for Post-TANF Support Services. Instead, determining eligibility for and issuing payment of Post-TANF Supportive Services is the responsibility of the eligibility worker. (See Section 400-19-155, Post-TANF (Formerly Transitional) Supportive Services.)
