Payee 400-19-120-10

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-70)

 

Each TANF household must have a designated payee. That payee may be:

  1. A natural or adoptive parent or other caretaker relative, eligible or ineligible, designated as the primary individual with whom a deprived child lives;
  2. A stepparent when the natural or adoptive parent or any other caretaker relative, eligible or ineligible, is not present in the household;
  3. A minor parent residing in the parental home where there is no TANF eligibility for anyone other than the minor parent and the minor parents’ child(ren).

Note: In this situation the minor parent should be listed as the primary individual); or

  1.  A protective payee under conditions described in this chapter.
