Good Cause for Non-Participation - Approved 400-19-75-20-20

(Revised 9/1/2021 ML #3629)

View Archives

 

 

When a request for good cause for non-participation in the JOBS Program is approved, the reason approved and the date for the next review must be documented in the casefile.  

 

Upon receipt of a determination from the State Review Team (SRT), the State Exemption Determination Team (SEDT) or when the determination is made by the eligibility worker:

  • If a JOBS referral has not been made, the individual must be sent notification which includes:
  • The decision;
  • The good cause expected end date; and
  • The information that will need to be submitted and the due date by which the information must be submitted should they wish to continue their claim of good cause.
  • If a JOBS referral has been made, the eligibility worker must inform the JOBS Employment Contractor of the approval by using the SFN 323, JOBS Status of Change form or another means of electronic or hard copy notification. The individual must be sent notification which includes:
  • The decision;
  • The good cause expected end date; and
  • The information that will need to be submitted and the due date by which the information must be submitted should they wish to continue their claim of good cause.
