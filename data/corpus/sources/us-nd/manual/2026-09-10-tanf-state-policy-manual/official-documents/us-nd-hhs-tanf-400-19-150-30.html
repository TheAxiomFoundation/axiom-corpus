TANF special items of need (e.g. reimbursement for child restraint seats, essential services, health insurance premiums, health tracks and GED/HS diploma incentives, housing allowance, etc.) may also be paid to recipients of Transition Assistance. (See Section 400-19-60, Special Items of Need.)
