Facilitation of Household and Agency Efforts 400-19-15-05-15

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

At times it may be appropriate to arrange for household members outside agency staff, and the eligibility workers to gather as a group to coordinate the efforts different agencies are making in working with a household. In these cases it is important to note that this must be done with the informed consent of the household, and signed releases of information must be in place before such a meeting is arranged. (See Section 400-19-15-15, Confidentiality)
