Overview 400-19-65-05

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-90)

 

Within the limits described in this section, supportive services may be made available to a participant of the Job Opportunities and Basic Skills (JOBS) Program who would be unable to enter into or remain in an allowable work activity. Supportive service cannot be provided without approval from the JOBS Employment Contractor or eligibility worker and cannot be provided to a referred individual until the individual’s TANF case has been approved.

Exception:  Advance Transportation Assistance Payments may be paid to individuals who are sanctioned and who would be unable to complete a proof of performance without that assistance. (See Section 400-19-65-15, Types of JOBS Supportive Services.)

 

All approved JOBS supportive services are paid directly to the JOBS participant or vendor, as applicable. Please refer to the specific supportive service category below for additional information.
