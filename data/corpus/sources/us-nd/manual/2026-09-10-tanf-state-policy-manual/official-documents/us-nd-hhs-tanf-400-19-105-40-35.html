Budgeting When an Individual Returns Home From a Special TANF Living Arrangement 400-19-105-40-35

(Revised 4/1/2026 Release 26.1)

View Archives

 

Individuals receiving a reduced TANF benefit (clothing and personal needs allowance) as a result of a special living arrangement are not subject to a prorated TANF benefit upon return to the household.

  • The individual is eligible for the full TANF Basic Standard of Need as of the first day of the month of return if the household reports the change timely.
  • If the change is not reported timely, the individual's eligibility for the full TANF Basic Standard of Need begins the first day of the month following the month reported.
