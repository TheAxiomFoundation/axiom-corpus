Budgeting for Minor Parents Living In an Adult-Supervised Supportive Living Arrangement (Other Than Living with Parents) 400-19-45-25-25

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

When a minor parent under age 18 resides in an Adult-Supervised Supportive Living Arrangement, the minor parent’s parents’ income will not be a factor when determining eligibility.

 

In the month the minor parent residing in an Adult-Supervised Supportive Living Arrangement has a baby, her benefit will be based on a full TANF Basic Standard of Need for herself and a prorated TANF Basic Standard of Need for the baby from the date of birth. If the minor parent and newborn return to live with the minor parent’s parents, no minor parent budgeting procedures or benefit adjustment will be made for the month of return.

 

When a minor parent does not reside with a caretaker within the 5th degree of relationship, the minor parent’s earned income is countable in determining the TANF Benefit.
