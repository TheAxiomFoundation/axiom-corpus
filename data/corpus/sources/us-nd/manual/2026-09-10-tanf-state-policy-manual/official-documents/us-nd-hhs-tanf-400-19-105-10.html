Definition of Need 400-19-105-10

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

The TANF benefit for the initial month may include current need only, and cannot include need from prior months. The Total TANF Standard of Need represents amounts of income, by household size, considered by the North Dakota Department of Health and Human Services (NDHHS) to be necessary for a standard of living compatible with decency and health.

 

The six basic items of need, which represent 100% of the TANF Basic Standard of Need as currently defined by the department, are: Shelter, food, clothing, personal needs, household supplies, and fuel and utilities.
