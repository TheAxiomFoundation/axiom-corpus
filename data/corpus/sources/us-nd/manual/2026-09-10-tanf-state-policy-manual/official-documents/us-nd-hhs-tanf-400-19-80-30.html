Transferring Individuals from Tribal NEW to State JOBS 400-19-80-30

(Revised 9/1/2021 ML #3629)

View Archives

 

 

When an individual is no longer participating in the Tribal New Program and needs to be referred to the State JOBS Program, the referral is made in the current calendar month the individual is required to participate in State JOBS.

Example: An individual completes post-secondary education in May and is now required to be referred to State JOBS. In the calendar month of May a referral to State JOBS is created.

 

Tribal NEW participants are excluded from the work participation rate requirements for federal reporting purposes.
