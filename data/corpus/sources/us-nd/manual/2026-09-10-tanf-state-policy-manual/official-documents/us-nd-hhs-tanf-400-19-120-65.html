Periodic Review of Protective Payment Cases 400-19-120-65

(Revised 6/1/10 ML #3218)

View Archives

 

 

(N.D.A.C. 75-02-01.2-69(3)(d))

 

Because of the temporary nature of protective payments, the status of each household receiving them must be reviewed as often as necessary but at least every six months to determine if the protective payee is performing satisfactorily and if:

  1. The household should be restored to unrestricted money payment status because of improved ability to manage funds; or
  2. Protective payments should continue.

 

In instances in which the appointment of a protective payee is a result of disqualification or sanction of the primary individual, the use of a protective payee shall be terminated upon verification that the disqualification or sanction of that individual has ended.
