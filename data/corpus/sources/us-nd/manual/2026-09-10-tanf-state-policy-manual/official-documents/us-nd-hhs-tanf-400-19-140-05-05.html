Foster Care Case Manager Role 400-19-140-05-05

(Revised 1/1/2024 ML #3772)

View Archives

 

The foster care case manager has the primary role in determining whether a child is placed in Foster Care or TANF Kinship Care. When pursuing TANF Kinship Care, the foster care case manager must:

  1. Provide families potentially eligible for TANF Kinship Care Assistance with SFN 405, Application for Assistance or Electronic Application, program information and the verification requirements under the TANF Program.
  2. Forward, to the appropriate eligibility worker, a copy of:
  1. The SFN 423, Kinship Placement/Agreement, and
  2. The approved SFN 399, Unlicensed Caregiver Home Study, and
  3. A copy of the court order, and
  4. A signed SFN 405, Application for Assistance (unless applicant has filed an Electronic Application).
  1. Participate in an interview at the time of application for TANF if necessary.
  2. Review pertinent aspects of placement with the eligibility worker, to include providing the eligibility worker with all subsequent Court Orders addressing the care, custody, and control of the Kinship child.
  3. Determine the need for supportive services and forward to the eligibility worker along with a payment recommendation.
  4. Communicate the status of the placement and that placement in TANF Kinship Care remains appropriate.
  5. Notify the eligibility worker of the date and time of quarterly Permanency Planning meetings.
  6. Notify the eligibility worker of any changes that may affect ongoing eligibility such as but not limited to:
  7. A change in the child’s residency,
  8. A change in the child’s school status,
  9. A change in individuals living in the home,
  10. A change in the legal status of the Kinship Care child, and
  11. A change or addition of income of a child on TANF Kinship Care.
