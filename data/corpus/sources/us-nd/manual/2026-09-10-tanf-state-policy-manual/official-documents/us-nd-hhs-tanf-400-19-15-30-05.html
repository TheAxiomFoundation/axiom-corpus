JOBS Program Legislative and Regulatory Authority 400-19-15-30-05

(Revised 4/1/2026 Release 26.1)

The Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Act of 1996, Public Law 104-193, created a block grant that provides Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) benefits. Section 407 of The Act mandates work requirements for non-exempt individuals. Work requirements are further defined in North Dakota Administrative Code chapter 75-02-01.2, Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) Program, and in TANF policy section Exempt Individuals 400-19-75-10-05.

 

This chapter also addresses the Tribal Native American Employment Works (NEW) Program. Additional information including referral criteria for each of the three Tribal New Programs in the State is contained in the Tribal NEW Memorandum of Understanding and in TANF policy chapter Tribal Native Employment Works (New) Programs 400-19-80.
