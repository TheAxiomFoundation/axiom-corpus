Welcome to 07-2026 KDHE MKEESM

What is MKEESM?

The Kansas Department of Health and Environment (KDHE) Medicaid Kansas Economic Employment Services Manual (MKEESM) is the source of medical assistance Policy effective August 1, 2017.

Policy documents and medical assistance program materials are now available at the KDHE Eligibility Policy Page in KanCare.

This page contains hyperlinks to various policy document sites.

 

These include:

  • Policy Manuals,
  • Appendix,
  • Forms,
  • Policy Memos,
  • Policy Directives,
  • Policy Clarifications,
  • Policy Log (which is searchable by Keyword).
  • and the Summary of Changes (SOC).

Navigation in the Policy Manual

Navigating the Policy Manual can be done by using the Table of Contents, clicking on links through the Manual content, clicking within the path name at the top of a page, or searching for a word and then clicking on the relevant search result. 

 

To narrow a search in the Policy Manual, put a word or phrase in quotation marks. Searches using quotation marks search for the phrase verbatim. Utilizing quotation marks indicates to the search engine that the words belong together and therefore won't search for those words separately. 

Example:

"contact log"- including the quotation marks, returns 14 results of the phrase 'contact log'.

 

contact - no punctuation or quotations, returns 89 results which include contact, contacts, contacting, contacting log, contact summary, contact type, etc.

 

contact log - no punctuation or quotations, returns 150+ results which include contact, log, contact log, contact summary, contact type, logging, log, etc. 

Continued Issues Viewing Content

If you have problems using the "Search" option or viewing the Table of Contents, try clearing your internet cache. If available, launch MKEESM in Chrome rather than Edge and vice versa.
