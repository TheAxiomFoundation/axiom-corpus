Medical Assistance for People Who Are Incarcerated

2.5.5.3 Financial Eligibility

This subchapter provides financial policy information that applies to Medical Assistance (MA) for hospitalized people who are incarcerated. This includes eligibility factors that involve a person’s income or assets.

A person must meet the financial eligibility requirements that match their situation. People are in one of the following eligibility groups:

  • MA for Families With Children and Adults (MA-FCA)

  • MA for People Who Are Age 65 or Older and People Who Are Blind or Have a Disability (MA-ABD)

  • MA for Long-Term Care Services (MA-LTC)
