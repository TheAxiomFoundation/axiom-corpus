Medical Assistance Northstar Kinship Assistance

2.5.6.2.4 Post-Eligibility

This subchapter includes policies that apply to Medical Assistance (MA) for children receiving Northstar Kinship Assistance (NKA) and links to policies that apply to all MA programs and all Minnesota Health Care Programs (MHCP).

Topics covered in this sub-chapter are:

  1. MA-NKA Basis of Eligibility

  2. MA Benefit Recovery

    1. MA Third Party Liability

      1. Children who receive NKA are not required to cooperate with TPL requirements as a condition of eligibility for MA.

    2. MA Cost Effective Insurance

    3. MA Medical Support

      1. A medical support referral is not required for children who receive NKA.

  4. MA-NKA Health Care Delivery

  5. MA-NAA, MA-NKA, and MA-FC Title IV-E and Medicare

  6. MA Benefit Recovery

  7. Ma Referral for Other Benefits

    1. Children who receive NKA are not required to apply for other benefits as a condition of MA eligibility.

  9. MA-NKA Renewals

    1. Published: January 1, 2023
