Minnesota Health Care Programs

1.3.2.2 Cooperation

Applicants and enrollees must comply with various requirements for Minnesota Health Care Program (MHCP) eligibility. This includes general requirements, such as providing information and mandatory verifications, and specific requirements such as applying for Medicare. Clients who fail to cooperate with requirements may lose their health coverage.

See the Medical Assistance Cooperation policy and the MinnesotaCare Cooperation policy for more information.
