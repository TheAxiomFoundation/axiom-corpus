Medical Assistance

2.1.4 Post-Eligibility

These policies apply to MA enrollees. This subchapter includes policies that apply to all Medical Assistance (MA) programs and links to policies that apply to all Minnesota Health Care Programs (MHCP) programs. Post-eligibility policy that applies to a specific type of MA is located in the relevant MA chapter.

Topics covered in this sub-chapter are:

  1. MA Benefit Recovery

    1. MA Estate Recovery

    2. MA Liens

    3. MHCP Overpayments

    4. MA Third Party Liability

      1. MA Cost-Effective Insurance

      2. MA Medical Support

      3. MA Other Third Party Liability

  2. MHCP Change in Circumstances

  3. MA Cooperation

  4. MA Cost Sharing

  5. MHCP Fraud

  6. MHCP Inconsistent Information

  7. MA Qualifying Health Care Coverage

  8. MA Referral for Other Benefits

  9. MA Renewals
