Minnesota Family Planning Program

4.1.1.2 Mandatory Verifications

Full Eligibility

Mandatory verifications must be verified through an available electronic data source or by paper proof, if electronic data sources are unsuccessful or unavailable. The following are mandatory verifications for Minnesota Family Planning Program (MFPP) eligibility:

  • Current income

  • Immigration status

  • Social Security number

  • U.S. Citizenship

Presumptive Eligibility

Verification is not required for presumptive eligibility.
