Medical Assistance for Children in Foster Care

2.5.6.3.1 General Requirements

This subchapter provides general policy information that applies to Medical Assistance for children in foster care (MA-FC), and where applicable, links to policies that apply to all MA programs and all Minnesota Health Care Programs (MHCP).

Topics covered in this subchapter are:

MA-FC Mandatory Verifications

MA Benefit Recovery

MHCP Change in Circumstances

MHCP Inconsistent Information

MHCP Rights

MHCP Appeals

MHCP Authorized Representative

MHCP Civil Rights

MHCP Data Privacy

MHCP Notices
