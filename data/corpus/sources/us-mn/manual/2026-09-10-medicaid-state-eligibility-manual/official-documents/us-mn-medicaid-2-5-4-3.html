Program IM

2.5.4.3 Financial Eligibility

This subchapter provides financial policy information that applies to Program IM. This includes eligibility factors that involve a person’s income or assets.

A person must meet the financial eligibility requirements that match their basis of eligibility to be eligible for Program IM. People are in one of the following eligibility groups:

  • Medical Assistance for People Who Are Age 65 or Older and People Who Are Blind or Have a Disability (MA-ABD)

  • MA for Employed Persons with Disabilities (MA-EPD)

  • MA for Families With Children and Adults (MA-FCA)
