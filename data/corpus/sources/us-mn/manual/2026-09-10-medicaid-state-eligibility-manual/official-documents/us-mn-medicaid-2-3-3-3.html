Medical Assistance for People Age 65 or Older and People Who Are Blind or Have a Disability

2.3.3.3 Income

This subchapter provides policy about a person’s income for Medical Assistance for People Who Are Age 65 or Older and People Who Are Blind or have a Disability (MA-ABD). The income methodology policies also apply to Medicare Savings Programs (MSP) with some exceptions. See the MSP chapter for more information.
