Minnesota Health Care Programs

1.3 Client Rights and Responsibilities

All Minnesota Health Care Programs (MHCP) applicants and enrollees have rights and responsibilities under various laws. Application forms, notices, and brochures include information on rights and responsibilities.

This subchapter provides policy information that applies to all Minnesota Health Care Programs. Topics covered in this sub chapter are:

  1. MHCP Rights

    1. MHCP Appeals

    2. MHCP Authorized Representative

    3. MHCP Civil Rights

    4. MHCP Data Privacy

    5. MHCP Notices

  2. MHCP Responsibilities

    1. MHCP Change in Circumstances

    2. MHCP Cooperation

    3. MHCP Fraud

    4. MHCP Inconsistent Information

    5. MHCP Overpayments
